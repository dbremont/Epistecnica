# Léon Faucher

> **Léon Faucher** (1803–1854) was a French economist and politician, a liberal advocate of free trade, public works, and economic modernization.

## Life and work

Faucher was a journalist and deputy who served briefly as minister of public works and later as minister of the interior. He wrote on political economy, railways, and social reform, and belonged to the liberal camp that favoured free trade and limited government intervention.

His career illustrates the connection between economic journalism and public policy in the July Monarchy and the Second Republic.

## Selected works

- *Études d’économie politique* (1845).
- Political and economic articles.
