# T. K. Whitaker

> **T. K. Whitaker** (1916–2017) was an Irish economist and civil servant, best known for *Economic Development* (1958), the plan that shifted Irish policy toward outward-looking industrial development.

## Life and work

Whitaker served as secretary of the Department of Finance and later as governor of the Central Bank of Ireland. *Economic Development* diagnosed the weaknesses of the protected Irish economy and recommended a shift to export-oriented industry, foreign investment, and economic planning.

The plan prepared the ground for the 1960s opening of the Irish economy and is regarded as a turning point in modern Irish economic history.
