# Friedrich List

> **Friedrich List** (1789–1846) was a German-American economist and political writer, the leading theorist of infant-industry protection and national economic development. His *The National System of Political Economy* (1841) was a systematic alternative to classical free trade.

## Life and work

List was born in Reutlingen, emigrated to the United States after political trouble, and returned to Germany as a promoter of railway construction and the Zollverein. His experience of American protectionism shaped his critique of Smith and Say.

List argued that free trade was the policy of the already-industrialized nation, while developing countries needed tariffs and state support to build productive powers. His national system emphasized manufactures, infrastructure, education, and the integration of the national economy.
