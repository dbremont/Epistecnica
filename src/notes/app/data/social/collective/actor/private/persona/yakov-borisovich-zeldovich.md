# Yakov Borisovich Zeldovich

> Soviet theoretical physicist and cosmologist who made foundational contributions to nuclear physics, shock-wave theory, and the physics of the early universe. He developed the Zel'dovich approximation in cosmology and helped pioneer the Soviet nuclear programme.
