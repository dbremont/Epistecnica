# Igor Yevgenyevich Tamm

> Soviet theoretical physicist who, with Ilya Frank, explained Cherenkov radiation and shared the 1958 Nobel Prize in Physics. He also made foundational contributions to nuclear theory and proposed the tokamak approach to controlled fusion.
