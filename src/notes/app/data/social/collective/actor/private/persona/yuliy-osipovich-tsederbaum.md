# Yuliy Osipovich Tsederbaum

> Known as Julius Martov; Russian revolutionary and leader of the Menshevik faction of the RSDLP after the 1903 split with Lenin. He opposed the Bolshevik seizure of power in 1917 and died in exile in Berlin in 1923.
