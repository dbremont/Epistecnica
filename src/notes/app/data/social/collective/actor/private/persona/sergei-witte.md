# Sergei Witte

> **Sergei Witte** (1849–1915) was a Russian statesman and economic modernizer, the architect of the gold standard, the Trans-Siberian Railway, and the industrialization drive of the 1890s.

## Life and work

Witte rose through the railway administration and became minister of finance in 1892. He stabilized the ruble on the gold standard, attracted foreign capital, and promoted heavy industry and railway construction.

He later served as prime minister and negotiated the end of the Russo-Japanese War. His economic programme was a state-led industrialization strategy designed to close the gap with the advanced European powers.
