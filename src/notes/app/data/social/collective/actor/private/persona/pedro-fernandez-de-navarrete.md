# Pedro Fernández de Navarrete

> **Pedro Fernández de Navarrete** (1564–1632) was a Spanish economist and royal official, author of *Conservación de Monarquías* (1626), a wide-ranging programme of productivity and reform.

## Life and work

Navarrete wrote as a counsellor concerned with the fiscal and economic weakness of the Spanish monarchy. His *Conservación de Monarquías* discussed population, agriculture, manufactures, trade, and the reform of taxation and administration.

He belongs to the seventeenth-century Spanish literature of economic reform, which sought to reverse decline by improving productivity and restraining unproductive expenditure.

## Selected works
