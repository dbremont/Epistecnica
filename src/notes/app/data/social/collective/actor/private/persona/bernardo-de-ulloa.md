# Bernardo de Ulloa

> **Bernardo de Ulloa** was an eighteenth-century Spanish merchant and economic writer, author of *Restablecimiento de las fábricas y comercio español* (1740). The book argued for the restoration of domestic manufactures and Spanish commercial capacity.

## Life and work

Ulloa wrote from the perspective of a merchant concerned with the competition of foreign manufactures. His *Restablecimiento* examined the causes of Spanish industrial weakness and proposed measures to support domestic production, improve commercial organization, and reduce dependence on imports.

He belongs to the Bourbon-era literature of economic reform that prepared the ground for the more systematic policies of Campomanes and his generation.

## Selected works

- *Restablecimiento de las fábricas y comercio español* (1740).
