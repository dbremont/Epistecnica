# Anders Berch

> **Anders Berch** (1711–1774) was a Swedish economist and the first professor of economics at Uppsala University. His *Inledning til almänna hushållningen* (1747) was the first Swedish handbook of general economic administration.

## Life and work

Berch taught at Uppsala from 1741 and helped establish economics as an academic subject in Sweden. His *Inledning* covered population, agriculture, manufactures, commerce, and public finance, and was written for officials and students of the cameralist sciences.

He combined the Swedish improvement tradition with the wider European literature on political economy, and his work trained a generation of Swedish administrators in the principles of national housekeeping.
