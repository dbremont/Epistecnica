# Rosa Luxemburg

> Polish and naturalised-German Marxist theorist and revolutionary. A leading theorist of the Social Democratic Party of Germany, she co-founded the Spartacus League and the Communist Party of Germany and was murdered during the Spartacist uprising in 1919.
