# Veit Ludwig von Seckendorff

> **Veit Ludwig von Seckendorff** (1626–1692) was a German jurist, statesman, and cameralist, author of *Teutscher Fürstenstaat* (1656), one of the foundational manuals of German cameralist administration.

## Life and work

Seckendorff served several German princes and became chancellor of the University of Halle. His *Teutscher Fürstenstaat* described the organization of a territorial state: the prince’s household, the administration of justice, the police, agriculture, commerce, and the fiscal management of the domain.

The book was widely used in German universities and helped establish cameralism as a practical science of state administration. It combined legal, economic, and administrative knowledge in a single account of the well-ordered principality.
