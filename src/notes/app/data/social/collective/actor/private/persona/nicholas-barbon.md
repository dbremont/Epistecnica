# Nicholas Barbon

> **Nicholas Barbon** (c. 1640–1698) was an English physician, builder, fire-insurance pioneer, and economic writer. His *A Discourse of Trade* (1690) defended free trade, consumption, and the productive role of cities and building.

## Life and work

Barbon studied medicine at Leiden and Utrecht and became an honorary fellow of the College of Physicians. After the Great Fire of 1666 he helped rebuild London and established one of the first fire-insurance offices, the Fire Office, in the 1680s. He also sat in Parliament and promoted land-bank and currency schemes.

His economic writings argued that trade and consumption, rather than bullion accumulation, were the sources of national wealth. He defended the free movement of goods and people, attacked restrictions on building, and developed an early account of value, interest, and the division of labour.

## Selected works

- *Apology for the Builder* (1685).
- *A Discourse of Trade* (1690).
- *A Discourse Concerning Coining the New Money Lighter* (1696).
