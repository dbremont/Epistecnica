# Vitaly Lazarevich Ginzburg

> Soviet theoretical physicist who formulated, with Lev Landau, the Ginzburg–Landau theory of superconductivity. He shared the 2003 Nobel Prize in Physics for contributions to the theory of superconductors and superfluids.
