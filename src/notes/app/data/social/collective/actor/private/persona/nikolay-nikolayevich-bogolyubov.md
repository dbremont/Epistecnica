# Nikolay Nikolayevich Bogolyubov

> Soviet mathematician and theoretical physicist known for the BBGKY hierarchy in statistical mechanics, the microscopic theory of superfluidity, and work in quantum field theory. He directed the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research at Dubna.
