# Technical Element

> **Technical Element** is anything that occupies a role in a purposeful transformation of reality: the reality being transformed; the operation that transforms, constructs, controls or maintains it; or anything that enables, constrains, regulates or evaluates that operation.

> The term denotes the category; the elements it covers are its instances. Its boundaries are broad by design — abstract kinds such as standards, principles and parameters sit beside material objects — but the role test admits and refuses cases, and the types order what it admits.

A technical element is the root of the technica decomposition tree: it is never decomposed by an arbitrary list of parts, but through the licensed link forms — `Kind`, `Has`, `Part`, `Instance`, `Governed By`, `Evaluated By`, `Maintained By`, `Enables`, `Produces` — and every leaf must resolve to an instance, never to a bare type.
