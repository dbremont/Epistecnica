# Research Entity

> **Research Entity** is the organizational unit that carries research: an individual investigator, a chair, a laboratory, an institute, a research group, a firm's R&D centre or a national agency.

The organizational-model essay treats these forms as positions in an institutional design space — individual investigator, chair/laboratory, institute, and the firm-centred forms — each with its own governance model, funding horizon and relation to teaching or production.

The entity is not the research: it is the durable arrangement through which research is funded, staffed, evaluated and reproduced.
