# Technology Indigenization

> **Technology Indigenization** is the reproduction, adaptation, improvement and proprietary development of an introduced technology until the domestic capability no longer depends on the external source.

> Indigenization is the difference between operating a foreign plant and being able to build, adapt and improve the next one. The artefact may remain foreign for a long time; the capability does not.

It follows the introduction of foreign technology: the technology is acquired and assimilated, then reproduced, adapted and improved until the domestic organization can originate its own variants. The technical activity set names this as the outcome of Introduction.

See [Technology Acquisition](/view.html?p=notes/social/dynamic/production/technology-acquisition.md), [Technical Element Assimilation](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-assimilation), [Technical Element Absorption](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-absorption) and [Technical Adaptation](/glossarium/?t=adaptation).
