# Large Technical System

> **Large Technical System** is a networked system of artifacts, organizations, rules and users — a railway, an electrical grid, a telecommunication network — whose scale makes its management a distinct technical and political problem.

Hughes's cases show that such systems are not built by a single inventor or firm: they are assembled by system builders who align machines, capital, law and users into a working whole. Their history is one of load growth, momentum, and the transfer of control from inventors to managers and regulators.

The concept links the technical facet to the political and economic ones: a large technical system is where engineering, institution and market become one object of analysis.
