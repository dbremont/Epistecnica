# Technological Regime

> **Technological Regime** is the rule-set, practices, beliefs and competences shared by a technical community, which defines what counts as a problem, a solution and a legitimate move within its field.

The regime is the social carrier of a technological trajectory: the paradigm says which heuristics apply, the regime says who applies them and under which rules. It is reproduced through education, standards, journals, firms and professional bodies, and it is what a transition must destabilize.

The concept is distinct from a political regime: it names the governance of a technical field, not of a state.
