# Pilot Plant

> **Pilot Plant** is a small-scale production facility built to test and demonstrate a process under near-industrial conditions before full-scale construction.

It is the scale-up instrument of technical research: the laboratory proves that a transformation is possible, the pilot plant proves that it survives real materials, real throughput and real operating conditions. Its output is not only product but operating knowledge — parameters, tolerances, failure modes.

The pilot plant is also an institutional device: demonstration facilities and technology-validation centres use it to make a process credible to the firms expected to adopt it.
