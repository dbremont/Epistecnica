# Technical Facet

> The **Technical Facet** is the dimension of social reality whose dominant causal mechanism is the transformation of nature and society through material and knowledge systems — the practices, techniques, objects and systems by which a collective acts on its world, and the institutions, agents and competences that carry them.

> It is a facet, not a field: assigned by the mechanism that does the explanatory work, not by sector or actor. A hospital, a bank and a farm all have a technical facet, and none of them *is* the technical facet.

## Facet, not field

The [facet](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/facet.md) catalog defines a facet as an analytical dimension fixed by its dominant causal mechanism — market coordination, rule enforcement, meaning-making, technical transformation — and never by location, ownership or membership. The Technical Facet is the row for technical transformation: what explains an instance under this facet is the load path, the process, the technique or the instrument, not the industry code the instance happens to carry.

That is why the facet cross-cuts sectors. A workshop, a hospital and a bank each have a technical facet, and none of them is exhausted by it: the same firm is technical in its production, economic in its pricing and legal in its compliance. The facet scopes the inquiry; it does not partition the instance.

## Boundaries

The adjacent facets are close enough to be confused with it, and the distinction is by mechanism, not by subject matter:

- **Scientific** — the production and certification of warranted knowledge. A laboratory is Scientific by its dominant mechanism even though its instruments are technical elements.
- **Innovation** — invention, applied knowledge and development brought into use. A new product is Innovation by its mechanism even though it is also a technical object.
- **Economic** — allocation and exchange. A factory is both, but *how does this material become that product?* is technical while *who gets it, and at what price?* is economic.

## Where the facet is developed

The facet has its own note — [Technical Facet](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/facet/technical.md) — which states the mechanism test, the boundaries against the Scientific and Innovation facets, and where its elements live in the corpus. The vocabulary that parses the facet is collected in [Meta Tecnica](/view.html?p=notes/technica/meta/tecnica.md), and the element side of the same mechanism is stated in [Meta Technical Element](/view.html?p=notes/technica/meta/technical-element.md).

The pair of notes is deliberate: the facet note answers *through which mechanism is social reality technical?*, and the element note answers *what is the technical element that mechanism acts through?*. [Technical Mediation](/glossarium/?t=technical-mediation) and [Sociotechnical System](/glossarium/?t=sociotechnical-system) are the two concepts that hold the answers together.
