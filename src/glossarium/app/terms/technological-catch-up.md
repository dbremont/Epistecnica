# Technological Catch-Up

> **Technological Catch-Up** is a latecomer's acquisition of the capabilities needed to close a technical gap: not merely the import of machines, but the construction of the skills, institutions and research capacity that let a society use, adapt and eventually produce technology.

Catch-up is a learning process, not a transfer event. It runs through absorption, adaptation and improvement, and it depends on the receiving system's social capability — education, firms, finance and the state's capacity to coordinate. Some countries move from imitation to innovation; others remain dependent importers.

The concept links the technical facet to development economics: the gap that matters is not the distance to the frontier in artifacts, but in the capability to make the next artifact.
