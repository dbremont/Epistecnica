# Validation

> The principle of validation holds that models should be tested against available data or empirical evidence to reflect the modeled system accurately. This may involve comparing model outputs to observed data or testing the model under different conditions to see if it behaves as expected.
>

## Technical reading

Read as **technical validation**, the term names the establishment that a technical object, method or system satisfies its intended requirements and conditions of use — the judgement built on [technical testing](/glossarium/?t=technical-testing) and feeding [technical evaluation](/glossarium/?t=technical-evaluation). Validation is about fitness for purpose; verification is about conformity to specification, and the two can diverge.
