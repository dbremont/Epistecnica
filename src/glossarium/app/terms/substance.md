# Substance

> **Substance** is that which exists independently and underlies properties or changes — the bearer that persists while its attributes vary.

### Broken down:

- **Independence**: A substance does not depend for its existence on the properties it carries.
- **Persistence**: It is what remains the same thing through change (the falling apple through its trajectory).
- **Bearer role**: Properties inhere in substances; processes happen to them.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy (esp. Aristotle, early moderns)**:
Contrasted with accidents (changeable properties); materialists identify substance with matter, others with structure or process.
- **Epistemica**:
Substance-talk marks the relatively invariant pole of a segment — what a model holds fixed while state variables evolve.
