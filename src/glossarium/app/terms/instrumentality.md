# Instrumentality

> **Instrumentality** is the relation between technical means and intended ends — the structure by which something serves as a means, and the reduction of the world to means that follows when that relation becomes exclusive.

> The term is descriptive and critical at once: every technique is instrumental, and a rationality that recognizes only instrumentality is the pathology the critical tradition names.

Instrumentality is not a defect of technique; it is its form. The critical point is about scope: when means–end reasoning claims the whole of reason, ends themselves are treated as given and withdrawn from scrutiny, and persons and things alike appear as resources. That is [technical rationality](/glossarium/?t=technical-rationality) in its ideological form.

See [Technical Mediation](/glossarium/?t=technical-mediation) and [Technical Rationality](/glossarium/?t=technical-rationality).
