# Explanation

> **Explanation** is an account that makes something understandable by clarifying cause, mechanism, or relation — a very special form of description, maybe the ultimate form.

### Broken down:

- **Beyond description**: A description states what is the case; an explanation says *why* — citing the mechanism producing it.
- **Forms**: Causal (what brought it about), mechanistic (how the parts produce it), unificatory (how it follows from general principles).
- **Test**: An explanation earns its status by supporting counterfactuals — what would differ if the mechanism were altered.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy of science**:
Covering-law, causal-mechanical, and unification accounts compete; all agree explanation outranks mere prediction.
- **Epistemica**:
Explanation is the highest-purpose artifact: the DCESA is complete when its domain is not just predicted but understood.
