# Technical Knowledge

> **Technical Knowledge** is knowledge of how to make, transform, maintain or evaluate technical elements — as distinct from scientific knowledge of what the world is.

The facet note parses the knowledge system as `{ technology, know-how, know-what }` and distinguishes first-order knowledge (tacit technical knowledge) from second-order knowledge (how to create new technical knowledge). The distinction matters for policy: a country can hold the descriptions of a technique and still lack the capacity to create the next one.

Technical knowledge is the output of technical research and the input of technical practice; it is what a technique carries when it is transmitted beyond the case that produced it.


## Technical reading

The knowledge dimension has three companions: the [technical method](/glossarium/?t=method) that organizes inquiry and construction, the [technique](/glossarium/?t=technique) that carries the reproducible way of performing an operation, and the [tacit knowledge](/glossarium/?t=tacit-knowledge) that is held in practice. Technical knowledge is the genus; the other three name its organized, procedural and unarticulated forms.
