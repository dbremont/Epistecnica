# Technical Effectiveness

> **Technical Effectiveness** is the degree to which the intended technical objectives are achieved.

> Effectiveness asks whether the element does the job it was for; efficiency asks what it costs to do it. The first is a question about purpose, the second about proportion.

The term is the bridge from [technical function](/glossarium/?t=technical-function) to [technical evaluation](/glossarium/?t=technical-evaluation): a function is ascribed, and effectiveness is the measured degree to which the ascription holds in service. It is where the technical and the purposive meet, and where a technically elegant element can fail — because the objective was mis-specified rather than unmet.

See [Technical Efficiency](/glossarium/?t=technical-efficiency).
