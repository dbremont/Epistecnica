# Technical Element Industrialization

> **Technical Element Industrialization** is the establishment of the technical and organizational conditions for industrial-scale production of a technical element.

> Industrialization is the passage from *can be made* to *can be made at volume, repeatedly, at a cost, with a supply chain* — a change in the production system, not in the product's principle.

The step is where the element acquires a production system around it: tooling, process capability, quality control, supplier networks, workforce. Its characteristic difficulty is that scaling changes the element — tolerances, materials and processes that worked at pilot scale often do not, and the redesign that follows is a technical change, not a commercial one.
