# Know-how

> **Know-how** is the procedural knowledge of how to do something — carried in practice, skill and technique rather than in propositions.

It is first-order technical knowledge in the facet note's vocabulary: the tacit capacity that lets a practitioner perform a technique without being able to fully state its rules. It travels through demonstration, apprenticeship and shared work far more reliably than through documents.

Know-how is not the same as knowledge *that*: the description of a process can be complete and the know-how to run it still absent.
