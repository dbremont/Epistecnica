# Technical Research

> **Technical Research** is systematic investigation aimed at discovering technical knowledge, principles, methods, materials or capabilities.

It is a canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Knowledge), and it is the bridge from foundational research to technical objects, operational techniques and products: it transforms knowledge into the means of transformation.

The boundary between foundational and technical research is a matter of goal, not of method — the same laboratory, the same instruments, the same publication may serve either.
