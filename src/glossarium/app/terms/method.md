# Method

> **Methodology**: A set of methods to archieve some goals.
> 

> **Method**: Method (Ancient Greek: μέθοδος, methodos) literally means a pursuit of knowledge, investigation, mode of prosecuting such inquiry, or system. In recent centuries it more often means a prescribed process for completing a task.
>

## Technical reading

Read as a **technical method**, the term names an organized procedure for investigating, constructing, testing or solving a technical problem — the methodological dimension that a [technical practice](/glossarium/?t=technical-practice) carries and a [technical mechanism](/glossarium/?t=technical-mechanism) does not. A method is more than a technique: it includes the order of operations, the checks and the stopping rule, which is what makes it transferable between practitioners.
