# Technical Skill

> A **Technical Skill** is the learned ability to execute a particular technique or technical operation with a given level of proficiency.

> Skill is the personal carrier of technique: the technique can be written down, but the ability to perform it is acquired, maintained by practice, and lost when it is not used.

Skill is narrower than competence and wider than habit. Competence is the capacity to do a class of technical work, including judging when a technique applies; a skill is a specific executional ability. The distinction matters for training and for automation: a skill can be automated task by task, while competence is what remains when the tasks change.
