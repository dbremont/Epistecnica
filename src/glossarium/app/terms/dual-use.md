# Dual-Use

> **Dual-Use** is the property of a technology, component or body of knowledge that can serve both civilian and military purposes, and the governance problem that follows: the same capability that heals can harm, and controls that target the harm also target the capability.

Dual-use is not a marginal case but a structural feature of technical knowledge: the distinction between civil and military application is drawn at the level of use, not at the level of the technique. Its governance therefore relies on export controls, licensing, publication review and institutional oversight — each of which trades capability against risk.

The concept is the clearest case of a technical element whose social reading cannot be separated from its technical one.
