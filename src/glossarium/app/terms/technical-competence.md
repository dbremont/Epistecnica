# Technical Competence

> **Technical Competence** is the acquired capacity to reliably execute technical processes and techniques.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Agency). Competence is acquired — learned, trained and maintained — and it is distinct from the technique (the procedure) and from labor (the expenditure): the technique is what is done, competence is the capacity to do it, labor is the doing.

Competence decays when it is not exercised; that decay is one of the ways a technique disappears from a society even when its description survives.


## Technical reading

Competence is the capacity to do a class of technical work, including judging when a technique applies; a [technical skill](/glossarium/?t=technical-skill) is the narrower executional ability, and [technical judgment](/glossarium/?t=technical-judgment) is the capacity to decide in the cases the procedure does not cover. Competence is what survives when the tasks change.
