# Epistemic Tool

> **Epistemic Tool** is any technique, method, or formal mechanism used by an observer to construct, represent, and validate descriptions of reality — either as a guiding structure (concepts, assumptions, constraints) or as a resulting artifact.

### Broken down:

- **Guiding structures**: Concepts, priors, constraints, and representational forms that shape inquiry before results exist.
- **Resulting artifacts**: Models, datasets, and diagrams produced by applying tools — tools crystallized into objects.
- **Procedural core**: Procedures like Modeling → Model, inference, simulation, and measurement.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy / Methods**:
Tools are often invisible in finished science; making them explicit is the job of methodology.
- **Epistemica**:
Epistemic Tool is the operator category of the taxonomy: whatever participates in an epistemic activity without being its mere target.
