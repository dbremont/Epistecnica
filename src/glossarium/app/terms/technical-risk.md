# Technical Risk

> **Technical Risk** is the possibility and consequence of an undesirable technical outcome under uncertainty.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Control), beside hazard, trade-off and failure. The hazard is the condition, the risk is the possibility and its consequence, the trade-off is the price of reducing it, and the failure is its realization.

Technical research manages risk by choosing what to test: the prototype and the pilot plant exist to convert an unbounded uncertainty into a measured one.
