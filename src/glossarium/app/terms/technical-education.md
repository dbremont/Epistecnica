# Technical Education

> **Technical Education** is the formation of technical competence through formal instruction, vocational training, apprenticeship and practice.

It is the institutional path by which a society reproduces its technical agents: schools, polytechnics, vocational training centres, apprenticeships and in-firm training. Its forms range from general technical literacy to the certified mastery of a specific technique.

Technical education is a diffusion mechanism and a condition of absorptive capacity: a country can import a machine, but it cannot import the people who can run, maintain and improve it.
