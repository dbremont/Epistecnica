# Robustness

> A model should be robust, meaning that its predictions should be relatively insensitive to changes in its input parameters. This helps to ensure that the model is reliable and can be used to make accurate predictions even when the input values are uncertain or variable.
>

## Technical reading

Read as **technical robustness**, the term names the capacity of an element to maintain acceptable behaviour under variation and disturbance — insensitivity of performance to the conditions the specification did not fix. It is not [reliability](/glossarium/?t=technical-reliability): robustness is behaviour under perturbation, reliability is the probability of performing a function over time.
