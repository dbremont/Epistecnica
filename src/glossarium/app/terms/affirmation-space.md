# Affirmation Space

> **Affirmation Space** is the full set of possible affirmations that can be made about something — the space of candidate claims over a segment, of which artifacts affirm a subset.

### Broken down:

- **Possibility, not actuality**: The space contains every statable claim, true or false, affirmed or not.
- **Bounding inquiry**: Validation is navigation in this space — ruling regions out, concentrating density on survivors.
- **Relation to artifacts**: An artifact is a located commitment inside the space; a DCESA maps the explored region.

### In different fields:

- **Logic / Decision theory**:
Hypothesis spaces and state spaces are formal cousins; affirmation space stresses the semantic, claim-level view.
- **Epistemica**:
Making the space explicit turns vague curiosity into a searchable inquiry: what *could* be affirmed here?
