# Enterprise Research Lab

> **Enterprise Research Lab** is a research centre maintained by a firm to advance knowledge, technology and innovation for the firm and its industry.

Its typology follows the corporate R&D spectrum: adaptation and localization centres, development centres, applied research centres, and fundamental or transformative research labs. The further from the market, the longer the horizon and the higher the uncertainty the lab is expected to carry.

The enterprise lab is the institutional form in which industrial research becomes durable — a place, a budget and a career, not only a project.
