# Being

> **Being** is that which exists or has existence, in any form or mode — the most general notion of the ontic, prior to any distinction between entities, processes, or properties.

### Broken down:

- **Generality**: Being covers everything that is, independently of how it is parsed into entities or described by observers.
- **Mode-neutrality**: It applies equally to physical things, processes, social structures, and abstract objects with causal effects.
- **Ground**: Every epistemic practice presupposes being as its target; ontology articulates it, epistemology accesses it.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy (esp. ontology)**:
Distinguished from *beings* (particular existents); the question of being concerns what it means for anything to exist at all.
- **Epistemica**:
Being is the unsegmented presupposition behind every Segment of Reality — what remains when all epistemic cuts are lifted.
