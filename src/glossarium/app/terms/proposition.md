# Proposition

> **Proposition** is a statement that expresses a judgment that can be true or false — the bearer of truth values in reasoning and modeling.

### Broken down:

- **Judgment**: A proposition asserts something about reality (the sensor is saturated; demand exceeds supply).
- **Truth-aptness**: Unlike questions or commands, it can be evaluated against reality — correct or incorrect.
- **Composition**: Propositions combine into theories, hypotheses, and model claims.

### In different fields:

- **Logic / Philosophy**:
The shared content of synonymous sentences across languages; formalized in propositional and predicate calculi.
- **Epistemica**:
Propositions are the semantic atoms carried by Concrete Epistemic Artifacts; affirmation spaces enumerate them.
