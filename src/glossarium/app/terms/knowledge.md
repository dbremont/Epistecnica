# Knowledge

> **Knowledge** is justified true belief or reliably produced true belief — what remains of belief after validation against reality.

### Broken down:

- **Belief**: The agent holds the proposition true.
- **Truth**: The proposition corresponds to its target segment.
- **Justification / reliability**: The belief was produced by a process that tracks truth (experiment, sound inference, calibrated model) rather than luck.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy (esp. epistemology)**:
Gettier cases show justification + truth + belief may still fall short; reliabilism and virtue epistemology refine the schema.
- **Epistemica**:
Knowledge is the output state of validated inquiry: artifacts that survived standards and feedback become knowledge.
