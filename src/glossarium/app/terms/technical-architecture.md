# Technical Architecture

> **Technical Architecture** is the fundamental organization of a technical system — its components, their interfaces, their dependencies and the principles that hold them together.

> Architecture is what remains stable while components are replaced: the arrangement of roles and interfaces that a system's parts must honour, and the design principles that justify it.

Architecture is the invariant counterpart of [configuration](/glossarium/?t=technical-configuration). A configuration is one arrangement of parts; the architecture is the set of constraints that any admissible arrangement must satisfy. That is why architectural decisions are expensive to reverse: they fix the interfaces through which every later component must work, and they outlive the components that motivated them.

See [Technical Interface](/glossarium/?t=technical-interface) for the boundaries the architecture fixes.
