# Technical Safety

> **Technical Safety** is the condition and set of properties of a technical element by which unacceptable harm to people, property or environment is prevented.

> Safety is defined by its negative: it names the harms that are ruled out, the mechanisms by which they are ruled out, and the residual risk that is accepted.

Safety is not the absence of failure but the containment of consequence. The technical vocabulary is therefore layered: **inherent safety** (the hazard is designed out), **safeguarding** (a protective function intervenes), and **information** (warnings, procedures, training). The hierarchy is a claim about reliability of control — the further the measure is from the hazard, the more it depends on someone or something behaving correctly.
