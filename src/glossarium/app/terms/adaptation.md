# Adaptation

Adaptation is how organisms or systems adjust to environmental changes to enhance survival and reproduction.

## Technical reading

Read as **technical adaptation**, the term names the modification of an existing technical element to accommodate different conditions or requirements — the element-level answer to a changed [technical environment](/glossarium/?t=technical-environment). Adaptation preserves the element's identity while changing its configuration; a change that alters the architecture is a [transformation](/glossarium/?t=technical-transformation).
