# Technical Equipment

> **Technical Equipment** is an assembly of instruments, machines and apparatus used together in technical activity.

> Equipment is the plural, organized form of the implement: not one tool but a working set, whose components are chosen to fit one another and the task.

Equipment is where tool, machine and instrument are combined into a capability. The distinction from a [technical system](/glossarium/?t=technical-system) is purpose and typing: equipment serves an activity and is typed as production support, while a system is the organized whole whose interaction realizes a capability in its own right.

See [Tool](/glossarium/?t=tool), [Machine](/glossarium/?t=machine) and [Instrument](/glossarium/?t=instrument).
