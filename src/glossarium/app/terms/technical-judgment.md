# Technical Judgment

> **Technical Judgment** is the capacity to evaluate a technical situation and determine an appropriate action under uncertainty.

> Judgment is what remains when the procedure runs out: the case the rule did not cover, the trade the specification did not price, the failure that does not match the model.

Technical judgment is learned through practice and case exposure rather than through instruction alone, which is why apprenticeship survives in fields that have otherwise been formalized. It is not arbitrary: a judgment can be given reasons, checked against outcomes, and improved by reflection — the difference from a rule is that the reasons are weighed rather than applied.
