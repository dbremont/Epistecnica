# Technical Element Maturation

> **Technical Element Maturation** is the achievement of a relatively established and stable configuration, with its dominant design settled and its performance improving incrementally.

> Maturation is the state in which the element stops being redesigned and starts being refined: the architecture holds, and the changes move inside it.

The concept is the element-level equivalent of the dominant-design argument: once a configuration wins, competition shifts from *which architecture* to *how well executed*, and the rate of improvement becomes a function of process knowledge rather than of invention. Maturation is not stagnation, but it is a change in what kind of work produces progress — and it is why mature fields are displaced more often by a new architecture from outside than by a better version of themselves.
