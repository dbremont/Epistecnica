# Infrastructure

> **Infrastructure** is the substrate of a technical system — the networks, standards, protocols and facilities — that is backgrounded in use and becomes visible only when it breaks or when someone is excluded from it.

Infrastructure is relational: the same object is infrastructure for one practice and a tool for another. It is also learned as part of membership: to use a system competently is to know, without noticing, which substrate to rely on.

Studying infrastructure means studying the conditions of possibility of other technical work — and the distribution of who can take those conditions for granted.
