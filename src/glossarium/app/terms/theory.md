# Theory

> **Theory** is a systematic framework of propositions explaining phenomena, supported by evidence — an organized artifact that unifies many descriptions under shared principles.

### Broken down:

- **Systematicity**: A theory is not a heap of claims; its propositions constrain and support each other.
- **Explanatory ambition**: It answers why phenomena occur, not merely what was observed.
- **Evidence-bounded**: Its authority extends exactly as far as validation (prediction, replication, intervention) reaches.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy of science**:
Theories are compared on scope, parsimony, falsifiability, and novel predictive success.
- **Epistemica**:
A theory is a high-level Concrete Epistemic Artifact, often a whole DCESA organized around core principles plus auxiliary models.
