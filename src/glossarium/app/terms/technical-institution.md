# Technical Institution

> **Technical Institution** is a durable social structure that organizes, governs or sustains technical practice and knowledge.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Knowledge): universities and research institutes, standards bodies, firms and their R&D laboratories, vocational schools and technology-transfer offices.

The institution is a technical element because it carries technical practice, not because it is made of it: it is the durable form through which practices are taught, funded, standardized and reproduced.
