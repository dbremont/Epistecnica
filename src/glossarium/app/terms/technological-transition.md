# Technological Transition

> **Technological Transition** is a shift from one sociotechnical regime to another, through niche experimentation, regime destabilization and landscape pressure.

The multi-level perspective distinguishes three levels: *niches* where novelties are protected and tested, the *regime* of incumbent practices and rules, and the *landscape* of slow structural pressures. A transition occurs when niche innovations, regime tensions and landscape pressures align — not when a better technology simply appears.

Transitions are the practical form of the lock-in problem: the task is not to invent the alternative but to reconfigure the regime that would otherwise absorb or reject it.
