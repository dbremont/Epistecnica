# Technical Division of Labor

> **Technical Division of Labor** is the allocation of technical tasks across agents, roles and organizations — which operations are separated, which are joined, and who is entitled to perform each.

The division is technical and social at once: it follows the structure of the transformation (casting, machining, assembly, inspection), and it distributes competence, discretion and status along that structure. Durkheim read it as the source of organic solidarity; Marx and Braverman read it as the site where the worker is separated from the conception of the work.

The concept is indispensable for parsing the technical facet of an organization: the org chart is a technical decomposition with authority attached.
