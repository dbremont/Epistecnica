# Technical Investigation

> **Technical Investigation** is systematic inquiry into the properties, behaviour, mechanisms or possibilities of technical elements.

> Investigation is the genus of which analysis, experimentation and testing are species: each is an organized way of finding out, and they differ by what they hold fixed and what they vary.

An investigation is technical rather than scientific when its aim is the constitution or performance of an element rather than a general claim about nature — although the two overlap, and the same laboratory can host both. Its output is a warranted statement about what the element does, can be made to do, or fails to do.
