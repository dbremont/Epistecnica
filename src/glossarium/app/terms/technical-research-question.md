# Technical Research Question

> A **Technical Research Question** is an interrogative formulation specifying what must be investigated to address a technical research problem.

> The question is the operational form of the problem: it fixes the object, the property and the condition of the inquiry, and it is answerable — in principle — by evidence.

A research question is well formed when its answer would change what the investigator does: which experiment to run, which parameter to vary, which case to compare. Questions that cannot fail — *what are the properties of this material?* — are topics, not questions, and the discipline of the form is what separates an investigation from a survey.

See [Technical Investigation](/glossarium/?t=technical-investigation) for the inquiry the question opens.
