# Technical Facility

> A **Technical Facility** is an established physical installation supporting technical operations or research.

> A facility is a place with a purpose: the building, the services and the fixed equipment that make a class of technical work possible at that site.

Facility is the site-level concept, between the single [equipment](/glossarium/?t=technical-equipment) assembly and the [infrastructure](/glossarium/?t=technical-infrastructure) network. A laboratory, a fab, a test hall and a foundry are facilities; the distinction from equipment is that a facility is not moved to the work — the work comes to it, and its siting, services and access are part of what it offers.
