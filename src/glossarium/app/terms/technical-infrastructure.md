# Technical Infrastructure

> **Technical Infrastructure** is the durable material and organizational arrangement that supports technical activities and systems, and that is backgrounded in use.

> Infrastructure is what a practice can take for granted until it breaks: the network, the standard, the supply chain, the maintenance depot, the trained workforce.

The technical-facet reading of [infrastructure](/glossarium/?t=infrastructure) insists on two properties: durability (it outlives the systems it serves) and backgrounding (it is learned as part of membership and noticed only on failure or exclusion). The two together explain why infrastructure is both the cheapest thing to rely on and the most expensive thing to replace.

See [Technical Platform](/glossarium/?t=technical-platform) and [Large Technical System](/glossarium/?t=large-technical-system).
