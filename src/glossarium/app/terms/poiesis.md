# Poiesis

> **Poiesis** is production — the bringing-forth of something into existence — as the mode of activity whose end lies in the product rather than in the activity itself.

> The contrast is with *praxis*, whose end is the acting: making a table is poiesis, keeping a promise is praxis, and the two are judged by different criteria.

The distinction is technical before it is philosophical: it separates the activity that is assessed by its outcome from the activity that is assessed in its performance. Most technical work is poiesis with a praxis inside it — the making is judged by the artifact, the collaboration that makes it is judged by how it is done — and confusing the two criteria is a common source of misplaced evaluation.
