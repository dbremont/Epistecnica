# Technical Accumulation

> **Technical Accumulation** is the progressive accumulation of technical knowledge, components, methods and capabilities.

> Accumulation is a property of the stock, not of the flow: it names what has been retained — in firms, in institutions, in the element space — rather than what is currently being produced.

Accumulation is why technical change is path-dependent at the level of a firm or a country: each addition raises the potential for the next combination, and the loss of a stock (skills, suppliers, documentation) is not repaired by importing its output. The concept is the technical core of [technological catch-up](/glossarium/?t=technological-catch-up) and the reason [absorptive capacity](/glossarium/?t=absorptive-capacity) takes time to build.

It is not the same as growth. A firm can grow output while accumulating nothing, and a workshop can accumulate a capability that outlives its market.
