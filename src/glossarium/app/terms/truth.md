# Truth

> **Truth** is the correspondence of a proposition with reality or fact — what makes a description correct rather than merely coherent or useful.

### Broken down:

- **Correspondence**: A true proposition says of what is, that it is; falsity is mismatch with the segment it targets.
- **Not coherence alone**: A model can be internally consistent yet false; validation tests truth, not elegance.
- **Gradation**: Point estimates are true/false; distributions and intervals carry graded truth-like content (calibration).

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy**:
Correspondence, coherence, pragmatist, and deflationary theories compete; practice here assumes a working correspondence tested by feedback.
- **Epistemica**:
Truth is what Epistemic Feedback reports on: residuals, failed interventions, and replications are truth signals from reality.
