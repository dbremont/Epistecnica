# Foundational Research

> **Foundational Research** is research aimed at generating new scientific knowledge, theories or principles without immediate practical application.

It forms the base from which technical research draws. The boundary between the two is a matter of goal, not of method: the same laboratory, instruments and publication may serve either, and the distinction is useful precisely because the goals diverge.

The corpus treats foundational research as one term of the material-technology relation — foundational research, technical research, technical objects, operational techniques, products — rather than as its opposite.
