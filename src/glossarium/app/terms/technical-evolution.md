# Technical Evolution

> **Technical Evolution** is historical change in technical artifacts, processes, knowledge and capabilities over time.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Lifecycle). The corpus reads the same change through three related mechanisms: technical change (the emergence, improvement and discarding of techniques), technique diffusion (their spread across agents and societies), and capability decay (their loss).

Evolution is not progress by definition: obsolescence, fossilization and atrophy are its degenerate directions as much as improvement and replacement are its generative ones.
