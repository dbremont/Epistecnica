# Technical Operation

> A **Technical Operation** is an individual action or transformation performed within a technical process or system.

> The operation is the unit of process. A technique is the pattern an operation follows, an activity is the situated bundle of operations, and a process is the ordered sequence that composes them.

The term earns its place because the guide's decomposition speaks of operations without naming them: a technical element is anything that occupies a role in an operation, and the operation is what the role is relative to. Operations are the smallest units that can be decomposed, timed, assigned and repeated — which is why they are also the unit of division of labour.

See [Technical Process](/glossarium/?t=technical-process) for the sequence, and [Technical Division of Labor](/glossarium/?t=technical-division-of-labor) for the allocation of operations to agents.
