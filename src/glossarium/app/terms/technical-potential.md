# Technical Potential

> **Technical Potential** is the latent capacity of existing knowledge, materials, techniques and components to enable new technical configurations.

> Potential is a property of the stock, not of a single idea: it asks what the accumulated elements could be combined into, not what one designer has already thought of.

The concept is the combinatorial reading of the element space: every new element is built from existing ones, so the richness of the stock is itself a technical resource. That is why the same invention can be made in several places at once — the potential was there, and the possibility was waiting for someone to see it — and why a field that stops accumulating stops generating.

Potential is realized through [combination](/glossarium/?t=technical-combination); it is constrained by compatibility (whether the interfaces meet), by knowledge (whether anyone can see the combination) and by resources (whether it can be built).
