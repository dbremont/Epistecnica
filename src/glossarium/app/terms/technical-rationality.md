# Technical Rationality

> **Technical Rationality** is reason reduced to means–end calculation: the selection of the most efficient means to a given end, with the end itself withdrawn from rational scrutiny.

In the Weberian account it is the form of rationality that accompanies bureaucratization and calculability; in the Frankfurt School account it becomes *instrumental reason*, a rationality that treats every object — including persons — as a resource or an obstacle. The critique is not that calculation is wrong, but that a society which recognizes only calculation loses the capacity to ask what the calculation is for.

The concept is the diagnostic counterpart of the technical facet: it names what happens when the technical mechanism expands beyond its scope and claims the whole of reason.
