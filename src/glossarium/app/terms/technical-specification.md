# Technical Specification

> **Technical Specification** is a precise statement of requirements, parameters, performance and interface conditions used to define and verify a solution.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Requirements). The specification is the verifiable form of a requirement: it says what will be measured, under which conditions, and what counts as conforming.

Specification and blueprint are complementary: the blueprint says how to make, the specification says what the made thing must satisfy.
