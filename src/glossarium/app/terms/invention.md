# Invention

> **Invention** is the first conception or realization of a new technical element — a device, process or method — before it is adopted in production or markets.

Invention and innovation are not the same event: the invention is the new thing, the innovation is its first successful application and diffusion. An invention that is never applied remains a possibility in the technical element space; an innovation is a change in what is actually done.

The distinction is analytical, not chronological in every case: invention and innovation may be interleaved in the same project, but they answer different questions — *can it be made?* and *does it work in use?*


## Technical reading

Read as **technical invention**, the term names the creation of a previously nonexistent technical principle, arrangement, method or object — the step that produces a possibility rather than a product. Invention is where [emergence](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-emergence) becomes an element; commercialisation and diffusion are later and separate.
