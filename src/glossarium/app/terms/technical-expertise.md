# Technical Expertise

> **Technical Expertise** is demonstrated or certified competence to speak and decide on technical matters — the capacity to make judgments that others have reason to trust.

Expertise is not the same as knowledge: it is knowledge plus the standing to use it in a collective decision. Its forms range from contributory expertise (the ability to do the work) to interactional expertise (the ability to converse with practitioners), and it is distributed across institutions, instruments and records as well as persons.

The expert–lay divide is not a simple hierarchy: lay actors often hold local knowledge that the expert lacks, and the boundary between them is itself a political achievement.
