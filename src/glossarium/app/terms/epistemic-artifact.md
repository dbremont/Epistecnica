# Epistemic Artifact

> **Epistemic Artifact** is a deliberately constructed structured object, product of epistemic activity, that encodes a set of affirmations about a segment of reality — a very special kind of epistemic tool, externalized to embody a description.

### Broken down:

- **Deliberate construction**: Artifacts are made, not found — fitted, calibrated, drawn, written.
- **Affirmation carrier**: It encodes claims evaluable as correct or incorrect against its target segment.
- **Materiality**: It lives on a substrate (paper, file, circuit), which constrains how it can be manipulated and shared.

### In different fields:

- **Science / Engineering**:
Models, datasets, instruments readings-as-records, blueprints, and simulations all qualify when they carry claims.
- **Epistemica**:
The Concrete Epistemic Artifact is the primary semantic unit; the DCESA is its domain-level collection.
