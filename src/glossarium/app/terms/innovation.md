# Innovation

> **Innovation** is the process through which a new or improved product, process or organizational form is brought into use and diffuses through a production system or market.

Innovation economics treats it, rather than capital accumulation alone, as the primary driver of growth, competitiveness and structural transformation. It is studied as a system: firms, universities, agencies and institutions interact through research partnerships, transfer agreements and funding.

Innovation is not invention repeated: it is the social and technical work of making a novelty usable, reproducible and adopted.
