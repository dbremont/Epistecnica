# Evidence

> **Evidence** is information that supports or refutes a claim — observations, measurements, and traces enlisted in validation.

### Broken down:

- **Relational**: Nothing is evidence in itself; it is evidence *for or against* a claim under an interpretation.
- **Forms**: Raw signals, calibrated measurements, replications, failed interventions, residuals.
- **Weight**: Evidence varies in strength with quality, independence, and severity of the test it passed.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy of science / Statistics**:
Likelihoods, Bayes factors, and severity formalize evidential support; all presuppose a claim and a contrast.
- **Epistemica**:
Evidence is Epistemic Feedback domesticated: signals from reality routed into for-or-against bookkeeping.
