# Technical Genesis

> **Technical Genesis** is the process through which a technical possibility originates and becomes an identifiable technical element.

> Genesis is the process; [emergence](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-emergence) is the observable state it produces. The distinction matters because a genesis can be reconstructed after the fact — from prototypes, patents, notebooks, lineages — while the state is what a record or a decomposition sees.

Genesis spans the first three phases of the [realization chain](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-realization-chain) — conception, invention and research — and stops when the configuration is identifiable enough to be designed. It is not a single act: a possibility is recognized, a principle is formulated, and the knowledge needed to realize it is assembled, often over years and across several agents.

The term is deliberately neutral about genius. Genesis can be a deliberate programme, a recombination of existing elements, or an accident that was recognized in time; what it is not is instantaneous, and the retrospective story that compresses it into a date is a narrative convenience.
