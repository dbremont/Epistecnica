# Technical Change (Δ)

> **Technical Change (Δ)** is a difference between two technical states or configurations.

> Change is a relation, not an event: it names the difference between a before and an after, and it is classified by where the difference falls — in a parameter, a configuration, a structure, a function or a capability.

The symbol Δ is used here in its plain sense — the difference between two states — not in the thermodynamic sense of a path-independent quantity, and not as a synonym for innovation. Innovation is a social and economic process; technical change is what happened to the element.

Magnitude gives the working vocabulary: an **adjustment** changes parameter values within a configuration, a **modification** changes the configuration while keeping the structure, and a **transformation** changes the structure itself. The three are not points on a single scale — a small structural change can matter more than a large parameter change — but they keep the question *what kind of difference is this?* askable.
