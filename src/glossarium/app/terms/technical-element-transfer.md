# Technical Element Transfer

> **Technical Element Transfer** is the movement of a technical element — or of the knowledge and capability to make it — between agents, organizations or contexts.

> Transfer is not transport: an element that arrives without the capability to operate, maintain and adapt it has not been transferred, only delivered.

The technical-facet term is deliberately narrower than [technology transfer](/glossarium/?t=technology-transfer), which names the institutional and policy relation between organizations and countries. This term names what moves and what must move with it: the artifact, the documentation, the technique and the tacit competence. The usual failure is the last one — the artifact arrives and the capability does not.

See [Technical Element Absorption](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-absorption) and [Technical Diffusion](/glossarium/?t=technical-diffusion).
