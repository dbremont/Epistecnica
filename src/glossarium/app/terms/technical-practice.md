# Technical Practice

> **Technical Practice** is a repeatable, organized pattern of technical work integrating activities, techniques, principles, standards and tools.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Technique). A practice answers *what is carried on*; it decomposes into technical activities and tasks, then into general, operative and constitutive techniques, and finally into technical acts and the interface through which an agent actuates on reality.

Discipline notes — geotechnical engineering, structural mechanics — are practices in this sense: they are not the techniques they apply, nor the objects they design.
