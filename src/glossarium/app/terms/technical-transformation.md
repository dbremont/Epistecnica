# Technical Transformation

> A **Technical Transformation** is a substantive alteration of the structure, properties, functions or capabilities of a technical element.

> It is the large end of [technical change](/glossarium/?t=technical-change): not a parameter moved or a part swapped, but a change in what the element is organized to be and do.

Transformation is a matter of degree and of kind, and the two are often confused. A change is transformative when it alters the element's architecture — the interfaces, the decomposition, the principle by which it works — so that earlier descriptions of it no longer apply. A change that leaves the architecture intact is a modification, however large the effort.

The concept is the technical counterpart of the social notion of structural change: the same period can transform an element and leave its institutions untouched, or the reverse.
