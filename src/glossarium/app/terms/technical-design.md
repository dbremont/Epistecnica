# Technical Design

> **Technical Design** is the deliberate specification of the structure, properties and organization of a technical object or process.

> Design is the step that turns a requirement into a generative description: a blueprint, a specification or a model from which the element can be built, checked and changed.

Design is where constraints are traded rather than merely obeyed. A design that satisfies every requirement individually can still be infeasible as a whole, and the designer's work is the search for a configuration in which the requirements hold together. That is why design knowledge is largely knowledge of trade-offs, and why design rationale is a technical artifact in its own right.

See [Technical Blueprint](/glossarium/?t=technical-blueprint) and [Technical Specification](/glossarium/?t=technical-specification) for the descriptions design produces.
