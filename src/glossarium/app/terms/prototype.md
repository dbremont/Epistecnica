# Prototype

> **Prototype** is a first working model of a technical object, built to test a design hypothesis before production.

A prototype is an epistemic instrument as much as a technical one: it makes a design answerable to reality before the cost of production is committed. Its fidelity is chosen to match the question — low-fidelity for structure and flow, high-fidelity for performance and interface.

The prototype is the technical-research counterpart of the experiment: the place where a specification is tested against a mechanism.
