# Technical Authority

> **Technical Authority** is the standing of a person or institution to decide technical questions and have the decision accepted — the social form in which expertise becomes binding.

Authority is not competence: a competent engineer may lack the mandate, and a mandated body may lack the competence. Technical authority is constituted by credentials, office, law, standards and reputation, and it is what turns a technical judgment into a decision that others must follow.

The concept explains why technical controversies are rarely settled by evidence alone: the question of *who is entitled to decide* is part of the controversy, not external to it.
