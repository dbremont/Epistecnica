# Technical Element Assimilation

> **Technical Element Assimilation** is the integration of an acquired technical element into the adopter's routines and knowledge, turning access into durable use.

> Assimilation is the step between absorption and reproduction: the organization can operate the element, and now it makes the element part of how it works — its procedures, its people, its maintenance and its accounting of failure.

A technology can be acquired, transferred and even understood without being assimilated. The test is whether the element survives the departure of the seller's engineers and the end of the first project.
