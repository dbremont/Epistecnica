# Technical Externalization

> **Technical Externalization** is the transfer of human capacities, knowledge or operations into external technical arrangements.

> Externalization is not automation in the narrow sense: it includes the memory stored in a record, the judgment stored in a rule, the calculation stored in an instrument — anything a person did that an arrangement now does or holds.

The concept names the direction of the relation that [technical mediation](/glossarium/?t=technical-mediation) and [technical agency](/glossarium/?t=technical-agency) describe from the other side. Externalized capacities do not disappear; they change carrier, and with the carrier change their properties — a rule is applied more consistently and less contextually than a judgment, a record remembers more and understands less.

The technical stakes are in the loss of the unexternalized remainder: what was not transferred stays with the person, and when the person goes the capability may go with them — which is why documentation and apprenticeship are technical, not administrative, concerns.
