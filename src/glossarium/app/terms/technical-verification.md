# Technical Verification

> **Technical Verification** is the determination of whether a technical element conforms to its specified design requirements.

> Verification asks *did we build it right?*; [validation](/glossarium/?t=validation) asks *did we build the right thing?* The first compares the element to its specification, the second compares the specification to the need.

The pair is standard in systems engineering and routinely collapsed in practice, with consequences: an element can be fully verified and useless, or validated and out of specification. Verification is the technical, checkable half — inspection, analysis, demonstration and test against stated requirements — and it is only as good as the requirements it checks against.

See [Technical Testing](/glossarium/?t=technical-testing) for the empirical method and [Technical Evaluation](/glossarium/?t=technical-evaluation) for the judgement that follows.
