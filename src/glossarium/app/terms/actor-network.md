# Actor-Network

> **Actor-Network** is a heterogeneous association of human and non-human actants that holds together because each enrols the others: the laboratory and its instruments, the engineer and the standard, the firm and its contracts, are the same network described from different points.

The term is not a metaphor for "social network"; it is a methodological instruction: follow the actors, human and non-human alike, and describe how the association is built, maintained and undone. Analysis proceeds by *translation* — the process through which an actor's interests are redefined so that they become compatible with the network's.

An actor-network is unstable by construction: it exists only while the enrolments hold, which is why maintenance, repair and the work of keeping the network aligned are part of its description.
