# Appropriate Technology

> **Appropriate Technology** is technology matched to local resources, needs and capabilities rather than to the global frontier: small-scale, repairable, skill-intensive and adapted to the context of use.

The concept is a critique of the assumption that the most advanced technique is the right one. Its criterion is fit — to materials, energy, labor, maintenance capacity and culture — and its characteristic failure mode is paternalism: a technology designed *for* a community by outsiders who do not share its constraints.

Appropriate technology is the development-policy counterpart of technical mediation: what a technique does depends on the world it is inserted into.
