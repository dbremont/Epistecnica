# Technology Transfer

> **Technology Transfer** is the movement of technical knowledge, machinery, methods and organizational capabilities from one setting to another, and the adaptation that makes them work there.

Transfer is not delivery: the receiving setting must be able to manufacture, operate, train for and integrate what arrives. The same invention can reach two countries and produce different capabilities, because the difference lies in the receiving system.

Channels include licensing, equipment sale, contract research, spin-offs, consulting, training and the migration of skilled workers — each transferring a different mix of codified and tacit content.


## Technical reading

For the element-scoped reading — what moves when a technique or artifact crosses a boundary, and what must move with it — see [technical element transfer](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-transfer). This term keeps the institutional and policy sense: the organized relation between organizations, sectors and countries through which technology is made available.
