# Applied Research

> **Applied Research** is research aimed at solving practical problems or developing new products, processes or techniques.

It sits between foundational research, which produces the knowledge, and development, which turns that knowledge into a reproducible object. Its success criterion is a working solution, not a general law.

The same investigation may be applied in its aim and foundational in its method: the distinction classifies the goal, not the discipline.
