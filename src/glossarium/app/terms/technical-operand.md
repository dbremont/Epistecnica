# Technical Operand

> A **Technical Operand** is the entity, material or phenomenon upon which a technical operation acts.

> The operand is the *that which is acted upon*: the workpiece under the tool, the signal in the filter, the tissue under the scalpel, the data in the transform. It is what the operation is about, and it is distinct from the instrument that acts and from the result that issues.

The term fills a gap in the operation vocabulary: an [operation](/glossarium/?t=technical-operation) has an agent, an instrument, an operand and a result, and the corpus named the first three without naming the fourth. Naming it makes the typing of an operation determinate — what is being transformed is part of what the operation *is*, and two operations with the same instrument and different operands are different operations.

The concept is the technical reading of the Aristotelian *that which undergoes change*: the material cause of the transformation, as distinct from its efficient cause (the agent and instrument) and its final cause (the function).
