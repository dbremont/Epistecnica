# Technology Assessment

> **Technology Assessment** is the anticipatory evaluation of the social, technical and environmental consequences of a technology, undertaken before or during its diffusion, to inform collective choice.

Assessment is not the same as technical evaluation: technical evaluation asks whether an artifact meets its specification, technology assessment asks what the artifact will do to the system that adopts it. Its methods range from cost–benefit and risk analysis to scenario construction, participatory assessment and foresight.

The characteristic difficulty is the Collingridge dilemma: the consequences of a technology are hardest to predict when it is still easy to control, and easiest to observe when it is already locked in.
