# Technical Artifact

> A **Technical Artifact** is an intentionally produced material or symbolic technical entity — the element read as something made, carrying the intention of its maker.

> Artifacthood is not a material property. A protocol, a program, a drawing and a specification are artifacts in the same sense as a bridge: each is a structure someone produced to do something.

The concept contrasts with the natural object, which is given rather than made, and with the mere physical residue of action, which is produced but not intended as a technical element. In the corpus the artifact is the *made* reading of a [technical object](/glossarium/?t=technical-object): the same instance can be typed by its function and read as an artifact, and the artifact reading is what makes questions of design, authorship and maintenance askable.
