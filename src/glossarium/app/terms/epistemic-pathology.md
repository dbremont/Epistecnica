# Epistemic Pathology

> **Epistemic Pathology** is the study of failure modes in epistemic activities — how inquiry breaks, misleads, and compounds error.

### Broken down:

- **Failure taxonomy**: Overfitting, confounding, p-hacking, sensor saturation, model divergence, affirmation without validation.
- **Diagnosis**: Pathologies are identified by their signatures (suspiciously perfect fits, non-replicating effects, drifting calibrations).
- **Prophylaxis**: Standards (pre-registration, holdouts, replication) exist largely as pathology prevention.

### In different fields:

- **Science / Statistics**:
The replication crisis literature is applied epistemic pathology; quality regimes are its immune system.
- **Epistemica**:
Pathology is the shadow of scaffolding: every tool that enables knowing enables a characteristic way of going wrong.
