# Technical Lineage

> **Technical Lineage** is the historically traceable relationship between technical elements derived from earlier configurations.

> Lineage is the family relation, not the chronology: two elements are in a lineage when one is derived from the other — by modification, combination or descent — and the derivation can be traced, not merely dated.

The concept is what makes [technical element trajectory](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-trajectory) a history rather than a list: a trajectory is one element's sequence, a lineage is the branching structure of elements derived from a common ancestor. Lineages can fork (variants), merge (recombination) and terminate, and the structure explains why a design's constraints often predate its current formulation.

Lineage is also the technical form of ancestry claims in disputes about priority and origin — which is why a lineage claim needs the derivation to be shown, not asserted.
