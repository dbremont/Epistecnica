# Skill

> **Skill** is the acquired capacity to perform a class of tasks with a given level of proficiency, from novice to expert.

A skill is characterized by its nature (cognitive, physical or interpersonal), its level of proficiency, its relevance to a task or domain, and the degree of mastery it requires. The Dreyfus model describes the progression from rule-following novice to intuitive expert.

Skill is the human counterpart of technical competence: competence names the capacity to execute techniques reliably, skill names the graded mastery through which that capacity is acquired and exercised.
