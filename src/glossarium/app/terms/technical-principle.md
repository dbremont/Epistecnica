# Technical Principle

> **Technical Principle** is a general rule governing the construction or operation of technical systems.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Knowledge). A principle is not a technique: a technique is a procedure applied to a case, while a principle governs the member and is applied *as* a technique inside a practice.

The Euler–Bernoulli beam equation is the worked case: it is a rule the member must satisfy, applied as a constitutive technique by the structural practice, and readable as technical research once the question becomes whether its assumptions hold.
