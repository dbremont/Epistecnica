# Characteristic

**General sense**: As a noun, a characteristic is a distinguishing trait, quality, or feature by which a thing is recognized — what marks it as itself and sets it apart from others. As an adjective, *characteristic* means typical or distinctive of a kind, as in *characteristic behavior* or *a characteristic color*.

**Mathematics**: A characteristic property of an object is one that characterizes it: held by it and by nothing else (see Characterization). The characteristic of a ring or field is the smallest positive integer *n* such that adding the multiplicative identity to itself *n* times yields zero — or zero if no such *n* exists.
