# Technical Mediation

> **Technical Mediation** is the relation in which technique stands between humans and their world, shaping what is perceived, what is possible and what is required — rather than remaining a neutral instrument between them.

Mediation has several forms: embodiment (a tool becomes part of the body schema), hermeneutic relation (an instrument renders the world readable), alterity (the artifact is interacted with as a quasi-other), and background (infrastructure recedes from attention). The point is not that technology is good or bad, but that it is non-neutral: it transforms the relation it mediates.

Technical mediation is the analytical companion of *technical code*: the code names the social interest inscribed in the artifact, mediation names the way that inscription reaches experience.
