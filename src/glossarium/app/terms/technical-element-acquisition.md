# Technical Element Acquisition

> **Technical Element Acquisition** is the obtaining of a technical element, or of the right and capability to use it, through purchase, licence, transfer or in-house development.

> Acquisition is the transaction, not the commitment. The element may be bought, licensed, copied or developed; adoption is what the organization does with it afterwards.

Acquisition is distinct from [Technical Element Transfer](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-transfer), which names the movement itself, and from [Technical Element Absorption](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-absorption), which names the capability to understand and operate what arrived. A licence can be acquired without either transfer or absorption occurring.

See [Technical Adoption](/glossarium/?t=technical-adoption), [Technical Element Adoption](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-adoption) and [Technical Element Assimilation](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-assimilation).
