# Technical Feedback

> **Technical Feedback** is the return of information about an operation's results or current state, used to influence its subsequent behaviour.

> Feedback is a loop, not a report: the information must re-enter the operation and change what it does next. A measurement that nobody acts on is monitoring, not feedback.

The concept is the mechanism of [technical control](/glossarium/?t=technical-control) and the reason a process can hold a state against disturbance. Negative feedback corrects deviation, positive feedback amplifies it, and the difference between the two is a property of the loop's sign, not of the information.

Feedback also names the organizational loop — the user's experience returning to the designer, the failure returning to the specification — which is where [technical learning](/glossarium/?t=technical-learning) and [maintenance](/glossarium/?t=technical-maintenance) connect to the element's evolution.
