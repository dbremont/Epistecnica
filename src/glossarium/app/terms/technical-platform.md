# Technical Platform

> A **Technical Platform** is an underlying technical arrangement that supports multiple systems, objects or operations.

> A platform is infrastructure with a product logic: it is designed to be built upon, and its value lies in what others can do with it rather than in what it does alone.

The concept names a specific kind of reuse: an arrangement that is stable enough to serve as a base, general enough to serve several purposes, and governed enough that the things built on it remain compatible. Operating systems, machine-tool bases, vehicle chassis and cloud services are platforms in this sense; the technical question is always where the platform's boundary should sit, because every capability it offers is also a constraint it imposes.
