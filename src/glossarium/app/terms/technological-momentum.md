# Technological Momentum

> **Technological Momentum** is the inertia a maturing technical system acquires from the capital, institutions, skills and users invested in it — so that the system begins to shape the society that built it.

The concept is a middle path between technological determinism and social construction: a young system is plastic and shaped by social choices; a mature system is heavy, and the choices that made it are now constraints on what comes next. Momentum is not inevitability; it is the cost of change made visible.

Momentum is why the same society can appear to control its technology at one moment and to be controlled by it at another without either claim being simply true.
