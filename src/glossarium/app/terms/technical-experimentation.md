# Technical Experimentation

> **Technical Experimentation** is controlled intervention designed to investigate or establish technical relationships and outcomes.

> The intervention is the point: an experiment does not observe an element as it happens to be, it puts the element in a condition the investigator chose, so that the effect of one variable can be attributed.

Technical experimentation differs from scientific experiment less in method than in purpose: it aims at a working relationship — a setting, a tolerance, a failure threshold — rather than at a law. Its characteristic products are curves, envelopes and limits: the conditions under which the element behaves, and the conditions under which it stops.
