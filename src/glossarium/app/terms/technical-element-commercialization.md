# Technical Element Commercialization

> **Technical Element Commercialization** is the transformation of a technical development into a commercially exploitable product, service or process.

> Commercialization is the step that submits the element to a market test: whether anyone will pay for it, at a cost that covers its production.

The step is technical as well as economic, because the market test rewrites the design: cost targets, manufacturability, support, liability and packaging become requirements. An element that is technically excellent and commercially unviable is not a failed element — it is an element that has not passed this step, and the distinction is what keeps the life cycle from reading as a single success criterion.
