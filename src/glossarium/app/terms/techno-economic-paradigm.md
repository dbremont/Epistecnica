# Techno-Economic Paradigm

> **Techno-Economic Paradigm** is a cluster of techniques, organizational forms and infrastructures that reshapes an economy's productivity frontier for a long period — the steam-and-railway paradigm, the mass-production paradigm, the information-and-communication paradigm.

The concept generalizes the technological trajectory from a field to a whole economy: a paradigm is not one technology but a family of mutually reinforcing ones, together with the management models, labor relations and infrastructures that make them work. Its diffusion is uneven and its installation is turbulent.

Perez's contribution is the mismatch: the paradigm installs faster than the institutional frame can absorb it, and the resulting tension is what turns a technological revolution into a social crisis.
