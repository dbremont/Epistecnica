# Technical Production Capacity

> **Technical Production Capacity** is the ability of an agent or organization to produce specified technical outputs at a given scale and level of quality.

> Capacity is a triple, not a number: how much, of what quality, and at what cost. A plant that can make many units and few good ones has capacity in one sense and not in the other.

Capacity is where the technical and the organizational meet: it is a property of the production system — equipment, workforce, supply, process capability — not of the product. It is also what [industrialization](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-industrialization) establishes and what [technical element absorption](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-absorption) builds when a firm learns to make what it used to buy.

Its limits are usually not the machine but the constraint in the chain — the bottleneck, the skill, the supplier, the quality yield — which is why capacity planning is a technical problem before it is a financial one.
