# Technical Adoption

> **Technical Adoption** is the organizational decision and process by which an external technique, element or system is taken into use — including acquisition, adaptation, assimilation and routinization.

> Adoption is not acquisition. Acquisition obtains the right or the artefact; adoption commits the organization to its dependencies, its maintenance and the changes in practice that make it work.

Adoption is the general process; [Technical Element Adoption](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-adoption) is its element-specific form. The distinction matters because a firm can acquire a technology and never adopt it, and can adopt a technology without owning it.

See [Technical Element Acquisition](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-acquisition), [Technical Element Assimilation](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-assimilation) and [Absorptive Capacity](/glossarium/?t=absorptive-capacity).
