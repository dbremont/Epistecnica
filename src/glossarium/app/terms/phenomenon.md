# Phenomenon

> **Phenomenon** is an observable event or occurrence — in Epistemica, a coherent bundle of events and/or processes within a segment treated as a unified explanatory object.

### Broken down:

- **Observability**: A phenomenon shows up under some observational regime; it is what inquiry starts from.
- **Coherence**: It hangs together as one thing to be explained (superconductivity, inflation, attention) rather than a mere heap of events.
- **Explanatory role**: Phenomena are the *explananda* — artifacts explain them, standards judge those explanations.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy (esp. Kant, phenomenology)**:
Distinguished from noumena (things-in-themselves); phenomena are things as they appear under conditions of observation.
- **Epistemica**:
Phenomenon sits between raw Event Stream and formal Artifact: the stabilized object that models target.
