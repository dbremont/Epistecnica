# Technical Mechanism

> **Technical Mechanism** is the physical, procedural or logical arrangement through which a function or transformation is produced.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Capability). Function is what is achieved; mechanism is how it is achieved; process is the organized sequence of transformations or events that may constitute part of the mechanism.

The same mechanism can realize different functions depending on how it is organized or operated, and the same process may participate in different mechanisms.
