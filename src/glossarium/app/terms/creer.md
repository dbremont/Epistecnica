# Creer

Related Glosarium: (Ismo) Ism , Ideology

> La palabra **“creer”** proviene del latín **“credere”**, que significa “confiar, tener fe”, derivado de la raíz indoeuropea *kred-* relacionada con la creencia y la confianza.
> 

> Muy relacionado con ‘Truth’ en ingles - ver [Verdad](index.html?t=verdad).
> 

> More accurately, **“Believe”** denotes a **dispositional state**: a tendency or readiness of the mind to endorse or accept a proposition as true, even if it is not actively being considered at a given moment.
> 

> Note: This is a horrible construct to adhere to  - we should be able to question everything - have a rigorous epistemic self  evaluation of every idea - that we hold.
> 

> Note: The  ‘confiar’ state - is not bad itself - the bad part is when we ground our epistemic on it. But let’ say grounding our relations with other humans it not bad  -probably is required -or even grounding our relation with our own future.
