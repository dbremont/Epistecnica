# Apprenticeship

> **Apprenticeship** is the transmission of a craft or technique through sustained supervised practice under a master.

It is the institutional form that carries tacit knowledge: the apprentice learns by doing, under correction, in the presence of someone who already performs the technique well. Its modern relatives are the internship, the residency and the cognitive apprenticeship of school and workplace.

Apprenticeship is a diffusion mechanism as much as an educational one: it is how a technique crosses from one person, firm or country to another when it cannot be written down.


## Technical reading

Read as **technical apprenticeship**, the term names the organized transmission of a technique through supervised participation in technical practice — the institutional form that carries [tacit knowledge](/glossarium/?t=tacit-knowledge) and [technical judgment](/glossarium/?t=technical-judgment) when they cannot be written down. It is the complement of [technical education](/glossarium/?t=technical-education), not its older synonym.
