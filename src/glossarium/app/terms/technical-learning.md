# Technical Learning

> **Technical Learning** is the acquisition and transformation of technical knowledge and competence by an agent or organization.

> Learning is not the receipt of information: it is a change in what an agent can do, and it is only visible in performance.

The concept covers three mechanisms that are often conflated: **learning by doing** (competence improves with repetition), **learning by using** (the user's experience feeds back into the design), and **learning by interacting** (the producer and user jointly discover what the artifact should be). Each has a different carrier — the firm, the user, the relation — and a different limit, which is why the three are named separately in studies of technical change.

See [Technical Transmission](/glossarium/?t=technical-transmission) and [Absorptive Capacity](/glossarium/?t=absorptive-capacity).
