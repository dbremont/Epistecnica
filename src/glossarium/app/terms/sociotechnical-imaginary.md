# Sociotechnical Imaginary

> **Sociotechnical Imaginary** is a collectively held, institutionally stabilized vision of a good society, realized through and with technology, that makes some technical futures thinkable and others not.

Imaginaries are not forecasts and not ideologies in the narrow sense: they are the shared background that lets a society answer *what kind of future is worth building?* — the nuclear family of the atom age, the smart city, the green transition. They are performed in policy, design and everyday practice, and they can be contested.

The concept is useful precisely because it links the technical facet to the ideational one: the same artifact can be read as an engineering solution and as a carrier of an imaginary.
