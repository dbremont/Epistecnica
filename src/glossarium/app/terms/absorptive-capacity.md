# Absorptive Capacity

> **Absorptive Capacity** is an organization's ability to acquire, assimilate and apply new knowledge effectively.

The concept answers a precise question: is firm *x* capable of adopting technology or process *y*? Access to knowledge is not the same as the capacity to use it — the receiving organization must already have the routines, the trained people and the complementary assets that let foreign knowledge become local practice.

Absorptive capacity is built, not bought: it is the cumulative result of prior research, training and production experience.
