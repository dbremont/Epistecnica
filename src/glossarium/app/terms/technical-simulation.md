# Technical Simulation

> **Technical Simulation** is the execution of a model to reproduce or investigate the behaviour of a technical element under specified conditions.

> Simulation is experiment on a representation: the conditions are chosen, the model is run, and the result is evidence about the element only to the extent that the model is adequate for those conditions.

Simulation extends [experimentation](/glossarium/?t=technical-experimentation) into cases that cannot be tested directly — too large, too hot, too expensive, too dangerous, or not yet built. Its epistemic status depends on two things that are often conflated: the fidelity of the [model](/glossarium/?t=technical-model) and the correctness of the numerical execution, and a simulation can fail at either.

Its use in design is where the [verification](/glossarium/?t=technical-verification) of an element begins, but a simulation is not a test: it produces evidence about a representation, and the element still has to be tested.
