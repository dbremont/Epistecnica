# Technical Constraint

> **Technical Constraint** is a bound on technical action imposed by reality, resources, regulations or other conditions.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Context). The constraint is what the solution must respect rather than achieve: material limits, available energy, admissible tolerances, law and standard.

Constraints are not obstacles to design; they are part of its definition. A solution that ignores them is not a solution to the stated problem but to a different, easier one.
