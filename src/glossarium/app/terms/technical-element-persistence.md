# Technical Element Persistence

> **Technical Element Persistence** is the continuation of a technical element across time, including its maintenance, repair and institutional support.

> Persistence is active: an element stays in existence because someone keeps it in repair, in supply and in the rules that permit its use.

The state is the one most easily mistaken for inertia. Elements persist for different reasons — because they are maintained, because they are locked in, because their replacement is more expensive than their repair, or because a community's practice is built around them — and the reasons matter for any claim about how long the element will last. Persistence without support is a countdown.

See [Technical Maintenance](/glossarium/?t=technical-maintenance) and [Lock-in](/glossarium/?t=lock-in).
