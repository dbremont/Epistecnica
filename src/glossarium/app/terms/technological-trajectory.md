# Technological Trajectory

> **Technological Trajectory** is the direction a technical field follows under a shared paradigm: the problems it regards as worth solving, the heuristics it applies, and the solutions it treats as obvious.

A trajectory is not a plan; it is the path a community walks because its paradigm makes certain moves natural and others unthinkable. It is bounded — trajectories exhaust themselves when the heuristics stop yielding — and it is cumulative, because each step is taken from the position the previous one left.

The concept comes from the economics of technical change, where it explains why firms and countries that share a paradigm converge on similar designs even without coordinating.
