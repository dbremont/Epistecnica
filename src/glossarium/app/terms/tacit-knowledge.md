# Tacit Knowledge

> **Tacit Knowledge** is knowledge held in practice and experience that is difficult to codify, articulate or transfer in explicit form.

It is the part of a technique that a practitioner can exercise but cannot fully state — the feel of a material, the timing of an operation, the judgment that a result is acceptable. Documentation can capture its traces (shadowing, think-aloud observation, standard operating procedures), but not the whole.

Tacit knowledge is why technology transfer is not delivery: what moves through a manual is the codified part, while the rest must move through people, training and shared work.


## Technical reading

Read as **tacit technical knowledge**, the term names the part of technical competence that is held in practice and cannot be fully codified — the feel of a tool, the reading of a symptom, the judgment that a tolerance is about to be lost. It travels with persons and with supervised practice, which is why [technical transmission](/glossarium/?t=technical-transmission) has an irreducibly social component.
