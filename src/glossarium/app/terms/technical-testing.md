# Technical Testing

> **Technical Testing** is the empirical examination of whether an element satisfies specified requirements or exhibits expected behaviour.

> Testing is comparison: a measured behaviour against a stated requirement, under stated conditions. Without the requirement there is no test, only an observation.

The concept is the empirical half of [technical validation](/glossarium/?t=validation), and the input to [technical evaluation](/glossarium/?t=technical-evaluation). Its design problem is coverage — a test demonstrates behaviour in the conditions it exercises, and the interesting failures occur outside them — which is why accelerated, environmental, destructive and non-destructive tests are distinguished by the conditions they impose rather than by what they measure.
