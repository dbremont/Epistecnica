# Technical Input

> A **Technical Input** is material, energy, information or another element introduced into a technical operation or process.

> Inputs are typed by what they become: a material that ends up in the product is a constituent, one consumed in making it is a resource, and one that carries control is a signal.

The concept makes the operation describable as a transformation: inputs, an operand, a process, outputs. That description is what allows a process to be analysed for yield, loss and mass balance — and what allows it to be transferred, since the specification of inputs is part of what has to travel with the process.

Inputs are where a technical system meets its environment: supply, quality and availability of inputs are environmental constraints on the process, not properties of it.
