# Technical Quality

> **Technical Quality** is the degree to which the properties of a technical element satisfy specified requirements.

> Quality is conformity, not excellence: an element is of high quality when it meets its specification consistently, and a modest element that does so is of higher quality than a superb one that does not.

The concept is deliberately relative to a specification, which is what makes it auditable. It splits into the quality of **design** (does the specification name the right requirements?) and the quality of **conformance** (does the product meet the specification?) — a distinction that keeps a well-made bad design from being called good.
