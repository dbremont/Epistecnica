# Technical Reverse Engineering

> **Technical Reverse Engineering** is the systematic analysis of an existing technical system, product or process to identify its underlying components, techniques, structures and operational principles, in order to replicate, improve or integrate it.

It is a research mode, not a copy: the analysis reconstructs the technique from the object, which is the inverse direction of ordinary design. Its outcome is knowledge — a blueprint, a parameter set, a diagnosis — that may then support replication, adaptation or improvement.

Reverse engineering is one of the principal channels of technical diffusion, and it is distinguished from replication: the first recovers the technique, the second reproduces the object.
