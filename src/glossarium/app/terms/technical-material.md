# Technical Material

> A **Technical Material** is matter selected or transformed for technical purposes — a substance together with the processing history and the specification that make it usable.

> Material is not the same as substance. A substance is a natural kind; a material is a substance plus a route, a grade and a set of properties the maker can rely on.

The distinction is why materials science is a technical discipline rather than a branch of chemistry: the object of interest is not what the substance *is* but what a process can make it *do*. Steel, cement, a semiconductor wafer and a polymer grade are all materials in this sense, and each is defined as much by its processing window as by its composition.
