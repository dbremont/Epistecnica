# Technical Element Absorption

> **Technical Element Absorption** is the acquisition of the knowledge and capability needed to understand, operate, adapt and further develop an external technical element.

> Absorption is the difference between using a technology and owning the capability: the first requires the artifact, the second requires the knowledge that produced it.

The concept is the element-level form of [absorptive capacity](/glossarium/?t=absorptive-capacity), which is defined for organizations. Its stages are recognizable: operate (use it as given), adapt (change it to local conditions), improve (change it on technical grounds), and generate (produce the next one). Each stage requires more of the knowledge that was not transferred with the artifact, which is why absorption is slow and why it is the binding constraint in catch-up.

See [Technological Catch-Up](/glossarium/?t=technological-catch-up) and [Technical Element Transfer](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-transfer).
