# Tool

> **Tool** is an implement that bears directly on the workpiece or target — shaping, holding, cutting or joining it.

Canonically the `Production Tool`, a kind of `Production Support`: it enables a producer's operation without itself transforming reality into the delivered good. Ordinary usage extends the word to any instrument of action; the technical reading keeps the direct-bearing test.

The tool is the simplest technical object, and the one in which the technical facet of humanity is most visible: it is the extension of the hand that changes what the hand can do.


## Technical reading

Read as a **technical tool**, the term names an implement that bears directly on the workpiece or target — it is the element whose function is contact with the material being transformed. It is one of the three implement kinds, beside the [machine](/glossarium/?t=machine) that supplies the drive and the [instrument](/glossarium/?t=instrument) that measures or regulates.
