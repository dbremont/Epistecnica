# Technical Production

> **Technical Production** is the organized realization of technical processes to produce specified outputs.

> Production is not process: a [technical process](/glossarium/?t=technical-process) is the ordered sequence of operations, production is the organization that runs it — with resources, capacity, quality control and a specified output.

The term names the activity that turns processes into outputs at a scale. Its organization is where the technical and the economic meet: the same process can be run in a workshop, on a line or in a plant, and each organization imposes different constraints on tolerances, throughput and cost.

Production is the phase in which most of an element's cost is determined and most of its quality is fixed, which is why design for manufacture is a technical discipline rather than a commercial afterthought.
