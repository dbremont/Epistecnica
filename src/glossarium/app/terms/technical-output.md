# Technical Output

> A **Technical Output** is material, energy, information or another result produced by a technical operation or process.

> Outputs are typed by their role in the next operation: a product, a by-product, a waste, a measurement or a signal. The same stream can be several of these at once, depending on who receives it.

Output specification is what makes production a technical activity rather than an undirected transformation: the output is stated as a requirement — composition, tolerance, rate, quality — and the process is organized to meet it. By-products and waste are outputs too, and their treatment is part of the process's technical description, not an externality of it.

See [Technical Input](/glossarium/?t=technical-input) for the other side of the transformation and [Technical Performance](/glossarium/?t=technical-performance) for how the output is judged.
