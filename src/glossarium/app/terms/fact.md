# Fact

> **Fact** is an objective state of affairs in the world — what is the case in a segment of reality, independently of whether anyone affirms it.

### Broken down:

- **Objectivity**: Facts do not depend on observers; descriptions of facts do.
- **Segment-relativity**: A fact is always a fact *of* some segment (this sensor saturated at t; this cohort converted at 3%).
- **Role**: Facts are what true propositions correspond to and what artifacts encode.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy**:
Facts are invoked to ground truth; debates concern whether they are sparse (fundamental physics only) or abundant.
- **Epistemica**:
Facts are the ontic side of the artifact relation: the artifact affirms, the fact confirms or resists.
