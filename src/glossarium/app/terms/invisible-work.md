# Invisible Work

> **Invisible Work** is the maintenance, repair, articulation and cleanup work that keeps technical systems running but goes uncounted, uncredited and often unrepresented in the formal decomposition of the work.

The concept names a systematic blind spot: the org chart and the process diagram show the work that produces the output, not the work that keeps the conditions of production intact. When the invisible work is removed, the system does not degrade gracefully; it fails.

Invisible work is a corrective to decomposition by output: a complete technical account of a system must include its reproduction, not only its production.
