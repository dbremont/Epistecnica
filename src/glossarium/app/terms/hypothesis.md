# Hypothesis

> **Hypothesis** is a proposed explanation made on the basis of limited evidence, subject to testing — a candidate artifact awaiting validation.

### Broken down:

- **Provisional status**: A hypothesis is entertained, not yet endorsed; it guides the next observation or experiment.
- **Testability**: It must risk observable consequences — a hypothesis compatible with everything explains nothing.
- **Lifecycle**: Hypotheses graduate into models and theories, or die into gaps and pathologies.

### In different fields:

- **Science**:
Null and alternative formulations discipline testing; pre-registration fixes hypotheses before data arrive.
- **Epistemica**:
A hypothesis is a scaffold-status artifact: its job is to structure inquiry, not yet to carry settled claims.
