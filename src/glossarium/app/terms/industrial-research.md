# Industrial Research

> **Industrial Research** is research and development conducted by firms to develop new products, improve existing ones or enhance production processes.

The corporate R&D spectrum runs from adaptation and localization centres, through development centres, to fundamental and transformative research — the position on the spectrum fixes the time horizon, the risk and the distance from the market.

Industrial research is where technical research meets the firm: it is funded as an investment, organized as a project, and judged by its contribution to production and competitive position.
