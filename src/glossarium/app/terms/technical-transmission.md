# Technical Transmission

> **Technical Transmission** is the communication and reproduction of technical knowledge between agents — by instruction, demonstration, text or artifact.

> Transmission is the social mechanism that makes technique cumulative: what one agent learned can become what the next agent starts from.

Transmission is where the tacit and the codified meet. Codified knowledge travels in documents and standards; tacit knowledge travels in persons and in supervised practice, and the two routes fail differently — a document can arrive without its reader having the practice to use it, and an apprenticeship can reproduce a technique without the account of why it works. The pair is why [apprenticeship](/glossarium/?t=apprenticeship) and [technical education](/glossarium/?t=technical-education) are distinct institutions rather than alternative names for training.

See [Tacit Knowledge](/glossarium/?t=tacit-knowledge) and [Technical Learning](/glossarium/?t=technical-learning).
