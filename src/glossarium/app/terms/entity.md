# Entity

> **Entity** is a thing that exists as a distinct and identifiable unit — in Epistemica, a relatively stable invariant cluster of properties or relations within a segment of reality, treated as a "thing" for modeling and inference.

### Broken down:

- **Distinctness**: An entity can be picked out from its background; it has boundaries, however fuzzy.
- **Identifiability**: It can be re-identified across observations — the same entity at different times.
- **Invariance**: Its stability is relative: a cluster of properties holding together long enough to model.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy**:
Entities are the bearers of properties and the relata of relations; debates concern which entities are fundamental.
- **Epistemica**:
Entity is an emergent structure of a Segment of Reality — e.g. electron, firm, institution — fixed by an epistemic cut, not given raw.
