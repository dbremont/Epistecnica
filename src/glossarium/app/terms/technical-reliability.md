# Technical Reliability

> **Technical Reliability** is the capacity of an element to perform its required function consistently, for a stated period, under stated conditions.

> Reliability is a probability statement about time: it is always *reliability over a mission, under an environment*, never reliability in the abstract.

Reliability is not robustness, though the two are often used interchangeably. **Reliability** is the probability of performing the function over time; **robustness** is the insensitivity of behaviour to variation and disturbance. A robust element can be unreliable (it behaves consistently, then fails suddenly); a reliable element can be fragile (it performs well until the conditions leave the narrow band it was designed for).

See [Technical Robustness](/glossarium/?t=robustness) and [Technical Failure](/glossarium/?t=technical-failure).
