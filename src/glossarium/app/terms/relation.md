# Relation

> **Relation** is the way in which two or more entities are connected — dependence, interaction, comparison, or structure shared between relata.

### Broken down:

- **Relata**: A relation always involves two or more entities; it never exists alone.
- **Varieties**: Causal (sensor → controller), comparative (hotter than), structural (part of), semantic (refers to).
- **Model role**: Relations become edges in networks, couplings in equations, and joins in datasets.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy**:
Debates concern whether relations are real (ontic) or imposed by description; structural realists take relations as fundamental.
- **Epistemica**:
Relations are first-class citizens of segments: Technical Interaction, Technical Dependency, and every graph edge in the ontologies.
