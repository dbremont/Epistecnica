# Laboratory

> **Laboratory** is an equipped space for controlled inquiry: a place, or an organizational unit, where instruments, materials and procedures are arranged so that a question can be put to reality under specified conditions.

The word comes from *laboratorium*, "a place of work", from Latin *laborare*, "to work". In the technical corpus the laboratory is the characteristic research facility: it is where technical evaluation becomes possible because the conditions are controlled, and it is the institutional unit around which a research entity is often built.

A laboratory is not only a room: it is a configuration of instruments, standards, trained agents and routines — a technical system whose product is warranted knowledge.
