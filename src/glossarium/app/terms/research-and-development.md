# Research and Development

> **Research and Development** (R&D) is the organized activity that spans foundational research, applied research and development: the institutional form in which a society produces new technical knowledge and turns it into technical objects.

The corpus's research note distinguishes the positions on this spectrum — basic, applied, development, exploratory, incremental, breakthrough and industrial R&D — each with its own horizon, risk and relation to the market.

R&D is not a single technique but a practice family: it is carried by research entities, funded by firms and states, and evaluated through technical evaluation rather than through production output.
