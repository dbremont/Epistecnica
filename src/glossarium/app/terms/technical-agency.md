# Technical Agency

> **Technical Agency** is the capacity of technical arrangements to condition or influence action and outcomes — the distributed efficacy that makes an artifact a participant in the course of events.

> Agency here is not intention. A seat belt does not decide anything; it redistributes what can happen to a body in a collision, and that redistribution is its efficacy.

The concept is the ontological counterpart of [technical mediation](/glossarium/?t=technical-mediation): if technique mediates the relation between agents and world, then artifacts do work in that relation and the vocabulary must be able to say so without animism. The actor-network reading supplies the discipline — describe what the arrangement does, which actants it enrols, which actions it makes easy, hard or impossible — and leaves the question of intention where it belongs.

See [Actor-Network](/glossarium/?t=actor-network) and [Affordance](/glossarium/?t=affordance).
