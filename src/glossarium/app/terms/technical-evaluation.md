# Technical Evaluation

> **Technical Evaluation** is the systematic determination of properties, performance, adequacy or conformity.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Control). Its two canonical sub-kinds are verification — whether an artifact conforms to its specification or blueprint — and validation — whether it fulfils its intended purpose or solves the intended problem.

Evaluation is what closes the research cycle: it turns a claim about a technical object into a warranted one, or refuses it.
