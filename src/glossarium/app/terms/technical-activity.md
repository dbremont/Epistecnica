# Technical Activity

> **Technical Activity** is a situated bundle of technical doings carried on within a practice.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Technique). Practice answers *what is carried on*; activity answers *what is happening now*; the act answers *what is being done*. The activity is the bundle in time — which tasks, techniques and acts were performed together, in which sitting, toward what joint result.

The distinction is load-bearing: without it, a decomposition can name a practice's units but not what a set of technical acts jointly constitutes.
