# Craft

> **Craft** is skilled making governed by a body of practical knowledge, carried by a practitioner and transmitted through apprenticeship.

Craft is the human form of a technique when the technique is still held in the practitioner rather than in a document: the knowledge is real, but its medium is the trained hand and the trained eye. Its traditional organization is the workshop and the guild, its transmission the apprenticeship, its standard the master's judgment.

Craft is not the opposite of technical knowledge; it is one of its carriers — the first-order, tacit form that the facet note calls technical know-how.
