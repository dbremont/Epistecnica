# Concept

> **Concept** is a mental representation or general idea of something — the unit of thought by which recurring aspects of reality are grasped, named, and reused across descriptions.

### Broken down:

- **Generality**: A concept abstracts away from particulars (this fall) to a reusable form (falling bodies, price momentum).
- **Role in compression**: Fundamental descriptive concepts (motion, mass, opportunity cost) directly describe recurring structures of reality and therefore compress it.
- **Boundaries**: Concepts have vague edges; explication sharpens them into variables, parameters, and formal definitions.

### In different fields:

- **Philosophy / Cognitive science**:
Debates concern whether concepts are definitions, prototypes, or abilities; what matters here is their function as reusable grasp-points.
- **Epistemica**:
Concepts are the fundamental descriptive artifacts on which aggregated descriptions are built — the *what* that scaffolding tools help represent.
