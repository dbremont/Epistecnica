# Technical Labor

> **Technical Labor** is the purposive expenditure of human cognitive or physical effort in technical activity.

A canonical Kind of `Technical Element` (Classification; Agency), beside technical agent and technical competence: the agent is *who* acts, competence is the acquired capacity, labor is the expenditure itself.

Labor is what connects the practice to the person: it is where technique becomes effort, and where the cost of a technique is paid.
