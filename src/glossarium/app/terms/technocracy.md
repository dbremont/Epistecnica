# Technocracy

> **Technocracy** is rule by technical experts, justified by technical competence rather than by political mandate.

The classical formulations — Veblen's engineers, Burnham's managers — treat technical rationality as the proper basis of social order: those who understand the system should run it. The critical response is that technical competence does not settle questions of value, and that a technocratic order tends to redefine political problems as engineering ones.

Technocracy is a permanent temptation of the technical facet: every large technical system needs expert administration, and every expert administration risks confusing *can be done* with *should be done*.
