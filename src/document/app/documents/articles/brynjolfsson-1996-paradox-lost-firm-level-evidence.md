> The **“productivity paradox”** of information systems **(IS)** is that, **despide enourmous** improvements in the underlying technology, the benefits of IS  have not been found in the aggregate statistics. One explanation is that IS spending may lead to increases in the product quality or variety which tend to be overlooked in the aggregate statistics, even if they increase output at the firm-level.
