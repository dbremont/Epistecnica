## Notes

---

> Nanbeige4.1-3B, a unified 3B generalist that claims to be the first
> open-source small language model to combine agentic behavior, code
> generation, and general reasoning in a single model. Preference
> alignment mixes point-wise and pair-wise reward modeling for
> human-aligned responses; code generation uses complexity-aware rewards
> in reinforcement learning to optimize correctness and efficiency
> together; deep search relies on complex data synthesis plus turn-level
> supervision, sustaining stable long-horizon tool interaction of up to
> 600 tool-call turns. Reported to outperform prior models of similar
> scale such as Nanbeige4-3B-2511 and Qwen3-4B, and to rival much larger
> models such as Qwen3-30B-A3B.
>
