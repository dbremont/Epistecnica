## Introduction

### The Complex World

> Complexity Science is about how systems are different from physics and chemistry; but dependent on them (all reality is).
> 

> All systems obeys the laws of physics and chemistry -> but describing only in terms of those basic laws seems imposible.
> 

> Open: Implies positioned whthing a chemical or electrochemical gradient.
> 

> Self-Organizing: refers to rules amplifying fluctuations into coordinated space-time patterns.
> 

> Dissipative: Refers to dynamics that are irreversable and that braek time-reversal symetry.
> 

> Uncertain: Describes an open-ended space of possible states not enumerable  in initial conditions,
> 

> Adaptive: Describes the capture of information by a system from environment that promotes persistance and increases multiplicy.
> 

> Teleology: rfers to a goal-oriented explanation, interpreting structures or dynamics in terms of their functions.
> 

> Note: Complexity is really a paradigm; it can not be reduced to a set of techniques; and it does not apply to every system; only to those in which reductionist techniques fall short.
> 

Two main types of systems:

- A: Closed, reversible, symetry-dominated, and predictable classical domains (aka reductionism apply),
- B: Open, self-organizing, dissipative, uncertain, and adaptive domains are the subject of this book (aka reducctionism does not apply).

Descriptions:

- A: Great regularities; that give rise to short descriptions, and few initial conditions.
- B: Emergent regularities; constructed from "contigent histories" ???, describe using coarse-grained rules and nested boundaries conditions.

Systems Spectrums:

- [Equilibruim] - [Non Equilibrium and Self Organizing] - [Self Adaptative Self Synthesizing organizations with long evolutioary histories].

Origins of the Complexity Paradigm:

> A new paradigm will emerge;  when the old stop working (it's can explain new datasets of interest).
> 
- New Games; New Rules: Science does not merely the process of fitting new data into old models or a constellation of them; is only creating new ones to feet the data; sinse the old ones are not capable..
- Hermeneutics: in order to understand the parts we need the whole; in order to understand the whole we need the parts (chikend-egg problem).

Constructing the complexity paradigm:

- New paradigms are creating by a combination of New phenomena, New methods, Re-organization / unifications of fields.
- Complexity is an integrative science; one of it's first public manifestos was make by Norbert Weiner in 1948: Cybernetics.
