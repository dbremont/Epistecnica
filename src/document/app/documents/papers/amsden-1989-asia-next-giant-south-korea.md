### The Dynamics of Growth

> …
> 

**Late industrialization** is characterized by three facets of growth, as Korea exemplifies. 

- The first relates to diversification, or entrepreneurial decisions penetration of new industries, which ones to penetrate, when, and with that size investment.
- The second relates to stabilization, or short-run macroeconomic policies to maintain the level of economic activity.
- The third relates to the growth momentum itself. Once under way, growth gains a momentum whose properties are distinct, depending on the presence or absence of new technological discoveries.
