## Notes

> [**Origins of the guild examination**] The earliest notice of guild examinations in Spain dates from 1389, when the municipality of Barcelona required potters to pass an examination before the guild consuls in order to be qualified as masters. The practice spread to the other guilds between 1480 and 1562. The article follows the institutional development of examinations, licences, finances, and elections in Spain and Spanish America.

> [**Relevance**] High. It supplies the long-run institutional background for the examination regimes studied in the other papers.
