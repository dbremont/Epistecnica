## Terminology

> Note: We need to research on how Freud uses these terms.
> 

> **Psyche**:  The totality of the human mind, encompassing **conscious and unconscious processes**, thoughts, emotions, desires, and personality.
> 

> **Psychoanalysis:** A specific theoretical and therapeutic approach founded by Sigmund Freud that seeks to understand and resolve unconscious conflicts underlying thoughts, emotions, and behaviors through methods such as free association, dream analysis, interpretation of defense mechanisms, and transference work.
> 

> **Neurosis**: A mental or emotional disorder in which a person experiences chronic distress, anxiety, or maladaptive behaviors **without a loss of reality testing**. Unlike psychosis, the person remains aware of reality but struggles to manage internal conflicts, impulses, or stress. - Note This is  a dead-term in modern “Psychiatry” - In order to describe **mental disorders**.
