## Notes

> A short course on symplectic geometry: symplectic forms and manifolds,
> cotangent bundles, symplectomorphisms and Lagrangian submanifolds, local
> forms and the Moser trick, contact geometry, compatible almost complex
> structures and Kähler manifolds, Hamiltonian dynamics, and the moment map,
> including the Marsden–Weinstein–Meyer reduction theorem and its use in gauge
> theory.
