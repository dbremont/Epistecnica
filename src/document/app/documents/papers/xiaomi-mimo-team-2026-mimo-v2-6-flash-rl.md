## Notes

---

> Efficiency-balanced checkpoint of the MiMo-V2.6 series, built to scale
> reinforcement learning toward self-improvement — scaling RL compute,
> environment diversity, and grader compute together. Sparse MoE backbone
> (309B total / 15B activated), 1M-token context, text/image/video/audio
> modalities. Key ingredients: You Only RL Once (one mixed RL run across
> coding, agents, visual, cybersecurity), fully asynchronous GRPO at large
> batch scale, groupwise agentic grading (Groupwise Reward Synthesis +
> Groupwise Advantage Redistribution) closing a self-improvement loop,
> aligned RL with environment hardening, and Multi-Prefix Multi-Teacher
> On-Policy Distillation (MOPD2).
>
