## Notes

---

> The application of **mathematics to biological problems** has been used successfully several times; by D’Arcy in Growth and Form; Voltera Works, etc.
> 

> Modelling Strategy: Describe **phenomena** (elements, relations/interactions) without describing their structure; à la **thermodynamics**.
> 

> On the used of **oversimplified abstraction** to build a theory: in the early days of the **Kinetic Theory  of Gases**; the concept of a molecule as an  elastic billiard ball was used; the authors of the theory well are that this analogy was far from the truth; but still useful to start the project and describe certain phenomena.
> 

> In the preface of the book, there is an explanation of the use of **certain models** and approaches to **study biological phenomena** and their **shortcomings**.
> 

> Biology in the XX century is still a descriptive and empiral science; not a mathematical science.
> 

> It hard to study biological systems like the cell in complete way; we must pick an idealized system abstracted from the cell (some very specific phenomena).
