## Notes

---

> Nanbeige4.2-3B, a compact general agentic model with 3B non-embedding
> parameters, pretrained from scratch on 28T tokens with a Looped
> Transformer that reuses the layer stack to raise capacity without adding
> parameters. Agentic SFT data and trajectories are built from real-world
> deployment plus large-scale synthesis, expanding executable
> environments, task assets, and agentic scaffolds. The RL pipeline
> combines mixed-mode RLHF over Think and Non-Think responses,
> length-controlled reasoning RL to balance accuracy against reasoning
> efficiency, and agentic RL with outcome and process rewards to stabilize
> long-horizon training. Reported to beat larger models such as Qwen3.5-9B
> and Gemma4-12B on agentic benchmarks while staying competitive on
> reasoning and alignment; performance with OpenClaw supports its use as a
> compact local personal assistant.
