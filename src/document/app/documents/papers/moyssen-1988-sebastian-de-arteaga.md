## Notes

> [**The documentary biography**] Moyssén reconstructs Arteaga's career from archival documents: the Sevillian painters' guild examination of 19 April 1630, the workshop and apprentices he kept in Seville, the 1640 voyage to New Spain with viceroy Diego López Pacheco, the 1642 ephemeral arch for García Sarmiento de Sotomayor, his admission as notary of the Inquisition, and the 1652 duel wound that killed him.

> [**The works**] The study is the reference for *La incredulidad de santo Tomás* (c. 1643), *Los desposorios de la Virgen*, the lost portraits of the Inquisitors, and the Rubens-influenced *La caída de los ángeles rebeldes*.
