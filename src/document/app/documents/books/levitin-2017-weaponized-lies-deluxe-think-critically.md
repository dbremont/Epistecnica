### Fun with Averages

> What is the epistemic role of a  central tendency metric?   How to evaluate the capacity for this to capture parts of the affirmation space about an underlying piece of reality?
> 

We have a recording of piece of reality - in the form of a dataset -  and then we try to characterized - or map the data set to a number - we want to **capture the central tendency -  this assumes several things:**

- It’s has a central tendency   (a typical case),
- The mass of the central points - is larger than the mass of the outliers.
- …

A central tendency metric cannot be applied blindly and assumed to yield meaningful insight. Its epistemic validity depends on whether the underlying data-generating process satisfies the structural and distributional assumptions implicit in the metric. Only under those conditions does the statistic reliably capture a meaningful aspect of reality.
