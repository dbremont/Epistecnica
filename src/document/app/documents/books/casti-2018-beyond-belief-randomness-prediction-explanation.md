## The anthropic cosmological principle: philosophical implication of self-reference

> This chapter  explores  the consequences of the  “anthropic cosmological principle"; homo sapients has a privileged position observing the universe.
> 

> he **anthropic principle**, also known as the **observation selection effect**,  is the proposition that the range of possible observations that could  be made about the universe is limited by the fact that observations are  possible only in the **type of universe that is capable of developing 
intelligent life**.
>
