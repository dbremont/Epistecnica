**Which are the consequences of the (Commutative Law)?**

Commutativity gives you:

- **Symmetry** → no order dependence
- **Reorderability** → flexible transformations
- **Parallelizability** → scalable computation
- **Canonicalization** → easier normalization
- **Compatibility** → shared structure (e.g., eigenvectors)

But at the cost of:

- losing **direction-sensitive information**

Which are the consequences of the ‘Associative Law’?

- **Associativity = invariance under tree shape**
- **Commutativity = invariance under permutation**
