> **GEB** explores the emergence of **'animate things'** from **'inanimate things'** by drawing an analogy between **inanimate molecules** and **meaningless symbols**, examining how certain **formal systems** give rise to **meaningful patterns**.
> 

> **Reader:** The physics analogy to meaning is quite limited and static, whereas a biological, dynamic, evolutionary, and adaptable perspective is far more powerful.
> 

> **Reader:** **Hofstadter** is not a biological fundamentalist (whatever that means); he delves deeper in search of the source of meaning.
> 

> **Russell** believed that for a mathematical system to refer to itself in any way was the kiss of death, as **self-reference** was a sufficiency condition and would lead to **self-contradiction and inconsistency**. However, **Gödel shattered this illusion, showing that these problems arise regardless of whether self-reference is present or not.**
