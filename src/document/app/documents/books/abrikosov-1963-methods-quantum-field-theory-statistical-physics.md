## Notes

---

> Classic monograph on the application of quantum-field-theory methods to
> statistical physics and many-body problems: diagram technique
> (Feynman diagrams), Green's functions, perturbation theory, and
> applications including superconductivity; the standard reference for
> diagrammatic methods in many-body theory.
>
