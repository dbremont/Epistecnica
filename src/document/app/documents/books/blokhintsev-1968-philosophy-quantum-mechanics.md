## Notes

---

> Monograph on the conceptual and philosophical foundations of quantum
> mechanics — the interpretation of the wave function, measurement and the
> role of the measuring instrument, complementarity and causality —
> growing out of Blokhintsev's Fundamentals of Quantum Mechanics course.
>
