## Complexity Economics: An Introduction

> **Complexity economics** views the **economy** as a **system** not necessarily in **equilibrium**, but rather as one where **agents** constantly change their **action** and **strategies** in response to the outcomes they **mutually create**.
> 

> **Complexity Economics** uses  mathematics and computation to understand the economy.
> 

> **Complexity Economics** recognizes that increasing and diminishing returns may be present in economic situations.
> 

> **Complexity Economics** recognizes that the economy is not something given that forms from a constantly developing set of actions, arrangements and technological innovations.
> 

> **Complexity Economics** sees change as endogenous phenomenon, not simply the result of **unexplained shocks** from outside system.
> 

> The **Economy** is thus compromised of evolving networks of interacting agents, institutions, and technologies - network of networks.The macro-level pattern of the economy —growth, innovation, business cycle, market booms and busts, inequality,  and carbon emissions —then emerge from the dynamics micro— and meso— level interactions.
>
