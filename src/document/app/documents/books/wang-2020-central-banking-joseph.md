### The Moneys Creators

> FED Mandades: Full Employment and stable prices.
> 

> The ***R* Neutral Interest Rate*** is the theoretical real interest rate at which the economy is balanced, with full employment and stable inflation, neither stimulating nor restricting economic growth.
> 

The “Open Market Desk“

> The **Open Market Desk operates from the New York Fed** because of Wall Street's central role in global finance.
> 

The **Open Market Desk** is a key operational unit of the **Federal Reserve Bank of New York** that conducts **open market operations (OMO)** to implement U.S. monetary policy. It plays a crucial role in managing liquidity in the financial system and influencing interest rates.

**Functions of the Open Market Desk:**

1. **Buying and Selling U.S. Treasuries** 📈📉
    - **When the Fed buys Treasuries**, it injects money into the banking system, increasing liquidity and lowering interest rates.
    - **When the Fed sells Treasuries**, it removes money from the system, decreasing liquidity and raising interest rates.
2. **Managing the Federal Funds Rate** 💰
    - Ensures that the overnight lending rate between banks stays within the Fed’s target range.
3. **Controlling Inflation and Economic Growth** 📊
    - Adjusting money supply to maintain price stability and promote employment.
4. **Implementing Quantitative Easing (QE) or Tightening (QT)** 🔄
    - **QE**: Large-scale asset purchases to stimulate the economy.
    - **QT**: Selling assets to reduce excess liquidity.
