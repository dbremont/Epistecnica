## Notes

---

> Problem collection with answers and hints, widely used as a companion to
> theoretical-physics courses: Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics,
> small oscillations, canonical transformations and Hamilton-Jacobi theory,
> mechanics of continuous media, and special relativity.
>
