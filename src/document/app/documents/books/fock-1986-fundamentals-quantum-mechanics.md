## Notes

---

> MIR English edition of Fock's quantum-mechanics course, based on his
> Leningrad lectures: the Schrödinger equation, operators and observables,
> perturbation theory, the hydrogen atom, identical particles, and
> relativistic wave equations, presented with Fock's characteristic
> conceptual care.
