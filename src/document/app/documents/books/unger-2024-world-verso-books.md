## Finitude and  Transcendence in Human Experience

> How can we abandon the project of a philosophical super-science without resigning ourselves to the exclusive rule of the specialized disciplines? The solution to this apparent dilemma is to understand philosophy as a bearer of our residual powers of insight: those that the specialized sciences fail to exhaust and that we identify with the imagination.
> 

> Two moves define the aspect of our mental experience that we call the imagination.  The first move — the one emphasized by Kant — is distancing from the phenomenon. The second move—which Kant neglected — is the subsumption of the actual under a range of variations in the realm of the adjacent possibilities: what the actual can become next.
