### Basic Properties of Numbers

> Examining the properties of the operators $(+, \cdot)$ over $\mathbb{R}$ —namely, **associativity**, **commutativity**, **distributivity**, **inverses**, and **identity**—reveals deeper aspects related to the **'structural invariance'** of associativity and commutativity.
> 

> Since **multiplication** is defined in terms of addition, the **‘distribution property’** emerges naturally. This leads to what is referred to as the **'distributive law'** in axiomatic formalism.
> 

> **Distribuir**: viene del latin ‘distributere’ y significa “dividir y repartir entre varios”.
> 

> **Factorization**: Transform an expression into its distributive form.
> 

> **Factor**: A **factor** is an element or component that contributes to the production or existence of a larger whole.
> 

> The concept of a number was not defined; but the properties of numbers were stated.
> 

- **(P1) (Associative law for addition)           ⇒ a + (b + c) = (a +  b) + c**
- **(P2) (Existence of an additive identity)    ⇒ a + 0 = 0 + a   = a**
- **(P3) (Existence of addictive inverses)      ⇒ a + (-a) = 0**
- **(P4) (Commutative law for addition)         ⇒ a + b = b + a**
- **(P5) (Associative law for multiplication)   ⇒ a * (b * c) = (a * b) * c**
- **(P6) (Existence of multiplicative identity)  ⇒ a * 1 = 1 * a  = 1;  a  <> 0**
- **(P7) (Existence of multiplicative Inverse)  ⇒ a * a^-1 = 1; a <> 0**
- **(P8) (Commutative law for multiplication) ⇒ a * b  =  b * a**
- **(P9) (Distribute law)                                     ⇒ a * (b * c) = (a * b) * c**

- **(P10) (Trichotomy law) For every a in P; a = 0, a > 0, a > 0.**
- **(P11) (Closure under addition) For every a, b in P a + b in P.**
- **(P12) (Closure under multiplication) For every a, b in P a * b in P.**

**Absolute Value:**

The **absolute value** of a real number $x$, denoted  $|x|$, is defined as:

$$
|x| =
\begin{cases}
x, & \text{if } x \geq 0 \\
-x, & \text{if } x < 0
\end{cases}
$$
