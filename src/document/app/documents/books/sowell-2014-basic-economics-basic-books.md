> Let say $x$ is a social phenomena, **Why does economics have a say about $x$**? Which are the limits of economics?
> 

> **What is economics?** **Thomas Sowell** describes the economy as the use of scarce resources that have alternative uses. This definition, while useful for mathematical modeling, is limited when it comes to understanding real-world phenomena, such as the role of innovation. This definition may seem about local constraint optimization;  but have innovation,  failed to capture productive knowledge (which is  a good inner of itself, …)
