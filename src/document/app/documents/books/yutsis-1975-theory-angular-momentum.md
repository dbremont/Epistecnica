## Notes

---

> English translation (A. Sen & R. N. Sen) of the 1960 Vilnius monograph
> on the mathematical apparatus of angular-momentum theory: addition of
> two and many angular momenta, graphical methods, J-coefficients and
> transformation matrices, and the theory of non-commuting tensor
> operators — the standard systematic treatment behind the usual
> Edmonds-style reference.
>
