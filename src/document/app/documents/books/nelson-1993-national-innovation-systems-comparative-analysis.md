## Notes

---

> **Techo-Nationalism**: "Techo-Nationalism" refers to a government's policy or sentiment that prioritizes and promotes the advancement and dominance of its own technological capabilities, often through protectionist measures or strategic economic and industrial policies.
> 

> **Innovation Ecosystem**: The set of actors that play a major role in influencing innovation performance.
> 

> This books **study institutions and mechanisms** supporting **technical innovation** across across 15 countries.
> 

> The goal of a **national innovation system** is to localize and master existing technologies or to develop new ones.
> 

> How **technical innovation** happens?
> 

> **R&D Facilities**: staffed with scientists and engineers attached to business firms, universities, or government agencies,  are the principal vehicles thought which technological advance proceeds,  in fields such as electrical equipment and systems, chemical products and processes, and aviation.
> 

> Pilot Plans (Testing Production):  Helps  to reduce technological uncertainties in innovation.
> 

> **Technology Parks**: Areas designed to promote research and development through collaboration among businesses and institutions.
> 

> **Cumulative Incremental Technological Advanced**:  is key for certain kinds of innovaltions; the feedbacks mechanics, trial and error, etc.  Which innovation have being radical (phase transition) or incremental (small changes at at time)?
> 

> Profesionalization of Research: …
> 

> **Technological Localization**: (aka reverse engineernig) the way to go in underdevelop economies.
> 

> R&D is only one **part** of innovation;  a lot of improvements happens in process innovation (learning by doing), etc.
