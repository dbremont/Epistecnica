## Newton’s Laws

> Mechanics: Is the study of motion/displacement (position) of bodies and it’s causes.
> 

> Dynamics:  Models the causes of movement. “Why movement’.
> 

> Kinematics: Models of trajectories without the causes of movements.
> 

> What makes a theory scientific? Replication + Experiments.
> 

> **Formulations**:  A formulation of a physical theory is the mathematical and conceptual framework used to describe and predict the behavior of physical systems. A Physical Theory May have several formulations.
> 

> **Niels Bohr**:  Correspondence Principle.
> 

> **Einstein** notions of space, time and frames of reference.
> 

> **Geometric structure** of classical mechanics.
> 

> Time as **parameter** or **coordinate**.
> 

> **Aristotelian Mechanics**:  **Aristotelian mechanics** is a pre-Newtonian framework that explains motion as objects seeking their natural state, requiring continuous force for movement, but was later disproven by observations and replaced by Newtonian mechanics.
> 

> **Mass**: Inertial Mass vs Gravitational Mass.
> 

> Inertial Frame: ..?
>

Laws

> Note one can formulate the laws in different ways;  for example the second law; can be formulated only in terms of mass, force and acceleration and take out **momentum**.
> 
1. **Inertia Law**: A body remains at rest or moves with a constant velocity, $v$ ,  when no external force acts on it.
2. **Movement Law**: The rate of change of **momentum** of a body is **proportional** to the **force** on the body.
3. **Action Reaction Law**: When two bodies interact, they exert on each other equal, but opposite, forces: $F_{12}$ = - $F_{21}$.

- Reference Frame:  ‘Absolute’  the same for everyone.
