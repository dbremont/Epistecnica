## Mental Model Space

- **[Conceptual Descriptor] Critical mass** is a systemic **threshold property** — a condition marking the **transition** from one regime of behavior to another.  Signature `Variable, Condition, Emergent Effect`. -  This is by not means  a model - but it’s concept to denote a special value - that antecedent a transition in the regime of behavior of a system.
- [Inverse Thinking - Problem Inversion] A **technique** (or **metatechnique**) used by an **agent** to **reformulate a problem space** by inverting its guiding objective or constraints, in order to reveal alternative formulations, hidden assumptions, or neglected solution paths.
- **Unforced Error** is a **diagnostic concept** — specifically, a **type of agentic error** that denotes a **failure originating from the agent’s own actions or decisions**, not from external pressures or uncontrollable conditions. A mistake that can be prevented - with high probability - Again, not a model.
- **Antifragility** is a **systemic property concept** — a concept describing how a **system responds to stressors, variability, or disorder - if a system get’s better when in stress then It’s Antifragile**. — Again, not a model.

| **Inversion (Problem Inversion)** | Metatechnique | Reversing a problem’s formulation to expose hidden assumptions or failure modes. |

| **Confirmation Bias** | Cognitive Bias | Tendency to favor information that confirms preexisting beliefs. |

| **Hanlon’s Razor** | Diagnostic Heuristic | “Never attribute to malice what can be explained by incompetence.” |
| **Map–Territory Distinction** | Epistemic Concept | Models are representations, not the reality itself. |
| **Circle of Competence** | Self-Reflective Concept | Operate within areas where one has deep understanding. |
