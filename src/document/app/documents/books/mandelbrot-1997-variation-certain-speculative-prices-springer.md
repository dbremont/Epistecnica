## Notes

> Mandelbrot found that the underlying **power law** that was evident in **random patterns in nature** also
applies to the positive and negative price movements of many financial  instruments. The movement of stock prices followed a power law rather than a 'Bell', 'Gaussian' curve or 'Normal Distribution'.
