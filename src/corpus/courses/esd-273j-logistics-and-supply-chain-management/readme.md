# ESD.273J Logistics and Supply Chain Management

> **Graduate · Fall 2009 · Engineering Systems Division and Civil and Environmental Engineering.** Instructor: Prof. David Simchi-Levi. MIT OpenCourseWare.

> A survey of the operations-research models and techniques developed for logistical planning in multi-echelon systems, with the emphasis on planning models for production, inventory and distribution strategy in general multi-echelon, multi-item systems. Topics include vehicle routing problems, dynamic lot-sizing inventory models, stochastic and deterministic multi-echelon inventory systems, the bullwhip effect, pricing models, and the integration problems arising in supply-chain management. Probability and linear programming are assumed.

## Topics

- Vehicle routing
- Dynamic lot-sizing inventory models
- Stochastic and deterministic multi-echelon inventory systems
- The bullwhip effect
- Pricing models
- Integration problems in supply-chain management
