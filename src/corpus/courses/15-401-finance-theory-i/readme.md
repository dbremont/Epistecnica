# 15.401 Finance Theory I

> **Graduate · Fall 2008 · Sloan School of Management.** Instructor: Prof. Andrew Lo. MIT OpenCourseWare.

> The core theory of modern financial economics and financial management, with a focus on capital markets and investments. The course builds from the time value of money to the pricing of assets: present value and its applications, fixed-income securities, common stocks, forward and futures contracts, options, risk and return, diversification and portfolio selection, the equilibrium pricing of risky assets (CAPM and APT), capital budgeting, and the theory of efficient markets. The mathematics is kept to what the argument needs — the point is the economic logic of valuation, not the technique of pricing.

## Topics

- Functions of capital markets and financial intermediaries
- Asset valuation and present value relations
- Fixed-income securities: bonds, yields, duration and term structure
- Common stocks and equity valuation
- Forward and futures contracts
- Options: payoffs, boundaries and the binomial and Black–Scholes frameworks
- Risk and return: means, variances and the portfolio frontier
- Portfolio theory and diversification
- Equilibrium pricing: the CAPM and the APT
- Capital budgeting and the cost of capital
- Efficient markets and the empirical record
- Derivatives as instruments of risk management
