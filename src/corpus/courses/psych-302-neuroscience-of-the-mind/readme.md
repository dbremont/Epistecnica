# PSYCH 302 — Neuroscience of the Mind

> A course resource site for **PSYCH 302: Neuroscience of the Mind**: the study of how neural systems produce perception, attention, memory, emotion and decision — the bridge course between the physiology of the nervous system and the cognitive science of the mind.

## What the course is

> The course's position in a curriculum is the interesting part. It is neither a physiology course (the anatomy and signalling of neurons) nor a cognitive-psychology course (behaviour and its models), but the place where the two are put in correspondence: a cognitive phenomenon is introduced, the neural evidence bearing on it is examined, and the question is whether the neural account constrains the cognitive one.
