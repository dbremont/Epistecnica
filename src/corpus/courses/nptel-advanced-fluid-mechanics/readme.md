# NPTEL — Advanced Fluid Mechanics

> NPTEL course (IIT Kharagpur, Prof. Suman Chakraborty), 12 weeks. The Indian-engineer's counterpart to the graduate fluids course: the continuum formulation, the equations of motion, exact solutions, potential flow, boundary layers and turbulence, taught with the worked-problem density that NPTEL courses are known for.

## Why both this and 2.25

> The two courses cover the same field with different emphases, and the corpus keeps both on purpose. The MIT course is organised around the **viewpoint** — what regime the equations are in, and how to pass between them. The NPTEL course is organised around the **worked problem** — the same theory, applied at a density that makes the technique visible. For a corpus that reads science as technique, that is a difference of method, not of subject.
