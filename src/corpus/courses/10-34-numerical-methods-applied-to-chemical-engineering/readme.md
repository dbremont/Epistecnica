# 10.34 Numerical Methods Applied to Chemical Engineering

> **Graduate · Fall 2015 · Chemical Engineering.** Instructors: Prof. William Green, Jr. and Prof. James W. Swan. MIT OpenCourseWare.

> Numerical methods for solving problems arising in heat and mass transfer, fluid mechanics, chemical reaction engineering, and molecular simulation. Topics: numerical linear algebra, solution of nonlinear algebraic equations and ordinary differential equations, solution of partial differential equations (for example Navier–Stokes), and numerical methods in molecular simulation (dynamics, geometry optimization). The methods are presented inside chemical-engineering problems rather than as mathematics in the abstract, and familiarity with structured programming is assumed.

## Topics

- Numerical linear algebra
- Nonlinear algebraic equations and ordinary differential equations
- Partial differential equations, including Navier–Stokes
- Numerical methods in molecular simulation: dynamics and geometry optimisation
