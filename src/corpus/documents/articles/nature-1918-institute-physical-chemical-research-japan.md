## Notes

> A contemporary *Nature* notice from December 1918, reporting that the long-discussed Japanese national institute had been partly realised. It records the institute's legal establishment in March 1917 and the shortfall in its endowment, and it makes the contemporary expectation explicit: the institute was supposed to serve both the progress of science and the progress of Japanese industry.

> The piece is short but valuable as a dated external view. It confirms the founding chronology and the financial weakness that the later RIKEN histories describe from the inside.
