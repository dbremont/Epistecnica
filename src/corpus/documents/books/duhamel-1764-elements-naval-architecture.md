## Notes

> Henri-Louis Duhamel du Monceau's elements — ship construction as one mathematical discipline: the hull geometry, the tonnage and the calculation of displacement that gave the French naval academy its theory, translated into English in 1764 for the trade Chapman's generation read. Naval architecture's theory book before Chapman's system — the built-hull sciences' French side.
