## Notes

> The third volume of Leupold's machine theatre — mills and their machinery: the mill construction and mechanical power transmission of flour, ore and the century's milling trades. Posthumously issued, it closes the engineer's machine science where practice was moving from the mill to the factory; the archive's German-scans carry both the printings and a 1767 reissue.
