## Notes

> Daniel Kinnear Clark's Tramways — the street railway designed and worked: the permanent way and the rail, the conduit and the underground supply, the horse and the steam and the cable traction, the cost and the municipal regulation, and the history of the system from the American streets. The reference in which the tramway became a municipal engineering system, by the same Clark who wrote the railway machinery and the roads and streets.

> The 1878 first edition and the French 1880 edition are scanned.
