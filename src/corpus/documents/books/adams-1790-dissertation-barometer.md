## Notes

> George Adams's short dissertation — the mathematical-instrument maker's account of the barometer, thermometer and the other meteorological instruments of the eighteenth-century philosophical cabinet: what they were, how read, how made. The meteorological-instrument branch's earliest document — the apparatus as a trade's product before observatories catalogued it.
