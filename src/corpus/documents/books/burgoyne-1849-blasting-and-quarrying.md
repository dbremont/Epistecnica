## Notes

> Sir John Fox Burgoyne's blasting treatise — the Quartermaster-General's own Rudimentary volume: drilling, charging and firing for building stone and siege works, written where the military engineering the guide's classrooms taught met the quarry face. Blasting's founding English instruction, in the same Weale series the collection's Rudimentary volumes run.
