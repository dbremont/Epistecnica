## Notes

> Thomas Savery's Miner's Friend — the steam-pump engine's founding manual, before Newcomen's cylinder and Watt's condenser: the "engine to raise water by fire" whose design the steam branch's founding economy opened. The steam engine's own purpose-built instruction, written before its successors the collection holds.
