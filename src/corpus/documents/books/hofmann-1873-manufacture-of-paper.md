## Notes

> Carl Hofmann's treatise — the papermaking machinery, pulp preparation and chemical processing of all the branches the trade ran: rag, wood, straw and the material economy written by the German-American papermaking engineer. The paper-manufacturing literature's process manual between Herring's materials account and the chronology the Munsell register kept.
