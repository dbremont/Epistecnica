## Notes

> George Adams's essays — the King's Mathematical Instrument-Maker's general account of the instruments of geometry, geography, navigation and perspective, with graphical and optical devices beside: the trade's own literature continued from his 1790 meteorological dissertation into the whole mathematical instrument cabinet. Public instruction in the instrument arts from the maker's side — the same Adams whose barometer treatise the collection holds.
