## Notes

> Xerxes Addison Willard's complete dairy treatise — the milk, the dairy cows, the butter and the cheese, the farm dairies and the beginnings of the factory system, written by the New York State dairy commissioner in the decade the factory organization arrived. Dairying treated as a documented production system: animal, process and product under one practical roof.
