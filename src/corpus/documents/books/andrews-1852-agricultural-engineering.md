## Notes

> G. H. Andrews's rudimentary treatise — the agricultural machinery, farm buildings and works engineering of the Weale series applied to the field: the threshing, lifting and drainage machines, the barns and their construction, written for the practical reformers of the farming economy. Agricultural engineering enters the Weale's rudimentary form the engineering trades had owned — the farms' own construction literature.
