## Notes

> Francis Home — military surgeon turned professor of materia medica — brought the chemical balance sheet to the field: experiments burning plants to their ashes to read what soils give, an early statement that the plant gathers matter different in kind from the soil it stands in. The work stands between Tull's mechanics and Davy's agricultural chemistry: the question is already chemical, the instrument still crude.

> Edinburgh printings ran through the century — the scanned copies below carry 1756 (the first edition), 1759 and 1762 — evidence of the demand the little book found before Davy reorganized the field.
