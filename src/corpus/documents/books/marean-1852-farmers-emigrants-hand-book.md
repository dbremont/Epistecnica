## Notes

> The farmer's and emigrant's hand-book — farm practice, food processing and the emigrant's agricultural guide in one 1852 volume, written for the westward reader's table. Food preservation and agricultural processing sit here in their most practical American form — the farm-side aliment of the same literature the dairy and brewing shelves carry.

> Note on the authorship: the outline attributes the book to William Beaumont; the 1852 scans' own title page carries the Marean identification, and the entry follows the printed source.
