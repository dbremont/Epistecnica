## Notes

> Frederick Walter Simms's instrument treatise — theodolites, plane tables, levelling instruments, sextants, artificial horizons, reading microscopes, the construction, adjustment and operation of the surveyor's instruments explained by the family that made them (father and son at Troughton & Simms). The guide's geodesy branch anchors on it for instruments that measured and surveyed — the manual that carries instrument construction into field practice.
