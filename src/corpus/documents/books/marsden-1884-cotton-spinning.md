## Notes

> Richard Marsden's cotton spinning — the mill floor's own machinery manual: development, principles and practice of the spinning trade, written where the Lancashire mills ran. The spinning-preparation literature beside Nasmith's carding engine and the American cotton volumes — one industry's floor-side instruction.
