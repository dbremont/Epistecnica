## Notes

> John Claudius Loudon's Encyclopaedia of Agriculture — the whole of the subject surveyed in one alphabet: soils and manures, crops and rotations, implements, farm buildings, livestock and the rural economy, with the plates and the citations that made it a working reference. The book the improving farmer and the land agent owned, standing beside the chemical treatises as the practice's own compendium.

> The 1825 first edition is scanned, and the later enlarged edition is held too.
