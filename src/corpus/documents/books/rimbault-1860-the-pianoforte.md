## Notes

> Edward Francis Rimbault's The Pianoforte — the instrument's origin, progress and construction: the predecessors and the invention, the action and the iron frame, the strings and the soundboard, and the makers from Cristofori to Broadwood, with the specifications and the trade detail. The piano trade's own account at the moment the instrument became an industrial product.
