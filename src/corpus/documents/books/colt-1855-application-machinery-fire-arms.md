## Notes

> Samuel Colt addressed the Institution of Civil Engineers on his Hartford manufacture: the rotating chambered-breech revolver produced by fixtures and gauges on general-purpose machine tools, the arms made interchangeable. The printed lecture is the American System stated at the gun plant by its manufacturer.
