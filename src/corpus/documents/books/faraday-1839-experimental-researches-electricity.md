## Notes

> Michael Faraday's researches in electricity — the three volumes of his serialized papers, reprinted as they appeared: electromagnetic rotation, induction, the identity of the electricities, lines of force, the field intuitions that Maxwell would later write in mathematics. The Royal Institution's laboratory record at its serialization: the experimental foundations beside which the telegraphy and engineering literature of the second half of the century defined itself.
