## Notes

> Bernoulli's Hydrodynamica bound pressure, velocity and the level of flow in one proposition — the theorem every later hydraulic computation assumes — and worked the efflux of water from a vessel, the jet's reaction and the motive forces of fluids as a mathematical subject. It is the founding treatise of hydrodynamics, the analytical layer beneath the channel engineering Guglielmini began and the conduit engineering Darcy later measured.
