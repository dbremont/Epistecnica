## Notes

> Abu al-Fazl of the Mughal court of Akbar recorded the empire as an administration: the revenues and the offices, the imperial mint, the cannon, the arts and crafts and the textile trades — the technical administration of one of the pre-modern empires carried in its own book. The English translations of Blochmann (1873) and Jarrett (1948) carried the Ain into the European library last century.

> The scanned copies run as the digitised Dalas volumes of the Asiatic Society edition.]
