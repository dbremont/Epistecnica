## Notes

> William Savage's dictionary — the materials, equipment and processes of printing alphabetized: the press's vocabulary, its foundry and ink trades, documented by a working printer of thirty years. The print-trade's technical reference between Johnson's instructor and Hansard's history, in the same year the print shops' mechanization entered its own equipment decade.
