## Notes

> The Aeronautical Annual, edited by James Means — the movement's yearbook: Lilienthal's and Chanute's results digested, Langley's aerodromes described, the bibliography of the flying literature, and the programme of experiments the editor urged on his readers. The volume in which aeronautics appears as an organized research programme rather than a series of isolated attempts.
