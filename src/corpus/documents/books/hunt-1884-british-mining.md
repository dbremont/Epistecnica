## Notes

> Robert Hunt's British Mining — the metalliferous mines of the United Kingdom documented in history, discovery, development and prospects: the mining-records surveyor's own national account, from the Cornwall and Devon mines his Royal Cornwall reports ran. The mining-literature branch's national survey beside the engineering and machinery treatises it collected.
