## Notes

> Henry de Graffigny's manual — gas-engine principles, petroleum engines, generators and carburetted-air engines in maintenance-oriented instruction: the French engineering literatures' own gas-engine book, translated by A. G. Elliott into English where the motor industry gathered. The gas-engine branch's maintenance-side manual, at the century's boundary.
