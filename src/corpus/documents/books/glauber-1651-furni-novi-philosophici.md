## Notes

> Johann Rudolf Glauber, the Amsterdam chemical shopman, set down the furnaces and their product as a technical book: the fiat of the retort, the furnaces of the mineral merchants and the aqua regia, the salt of common cooking water and the practical chemistry of the first shops. The Furni Novi is the microscope of the earliest German chemical technology.

> The scanned copy is the 1651 first Latin edition; the German BSB printing of 1650 is also scanned.
