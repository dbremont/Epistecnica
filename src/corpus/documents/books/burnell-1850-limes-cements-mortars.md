## Notes

> George Rowdon Burnell's rudimentary treatise — the limes, cements, mortars, concretes, mastics and plastering of the trade: construction materials and hydraulic binders documented as the engineer's own material sciences, in the Weale series whose well of civil engineering instruction his Rudiments continued. Roman cements, the water-lime, the making of mortars — the materials branch of the construction literature.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1850 treatise is verified here; the later cement literatures of the 1920s do circulate, and the 1850 treatise is catalogued untouched.
