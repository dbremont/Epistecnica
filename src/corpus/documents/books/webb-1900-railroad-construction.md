## Notes

> Walter Loring Webb's textbook at the century's boundary: railroad construction — location, earthwork, track, ballast and the economic returns — as completed theory-and-practice, taught as a discipline inside technical schools rather than learned through apprenticeship. The road-and-rail literature's engineering-science close, one book behind McAdam's roads and Wood's first railroads in the same branch.
