## Notes

> William Pole's treatise on the Cornish pumping engine — the high-pressure pumping engine Cornish mines developed by Trevithick, the pumping machinery whose work was measured in duty by the Cornwall Polytechnic Society and printed from it in 1844: the mining district's sophisticated literature of steam pumping, where the engine's mechanisms ran against the mines' own accounts.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1844 treatise is verified here; the Polytechnic Society's reports hold the printings and the chase is open.
