## Notes

> Travels in the Air — the balloon used as an observing platform: Glaisher's ascent to seven miles with the instruments and the readings, the physiological effects recorded, and the French aeronauts' accounts of the siege balloons and the high flights, with the plates that show the apparatus and the country below. The aeronautical practice of the century at the point it served science, and the upper air measured from a wicker car.
