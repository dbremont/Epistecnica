## Notes

> Hippolyte Dussauce's matches treatise — the phosphorus-match manufacture, the safety compounds and the colored fires and fulminating powders of the trade: the lucifer economy, documented beside the tanning manual the author's Roret inheritance carried. The match industry's own manufact-uring chemistry, before the safety-match decade closed its phosphorus era.
