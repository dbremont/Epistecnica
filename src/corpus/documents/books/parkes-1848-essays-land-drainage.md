## Notes

> Josiah Parkes's essays on land drainage — drainage argued as a science and an art: the theory of the water in the soil, the depth and spacing of the drains, the pipe-trench and the outfall, and the yields that followed the works. The point at which drainage became a designed system rather than a remedy for wet spots, and the engineering layer above Elkington's practice.
