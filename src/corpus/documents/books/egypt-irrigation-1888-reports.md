## Notes

> The Egyptian Irrigation Department's 1888 reports — the Nile's water as a state service: the canals and the barrages, the distribution of the flood, the staff and the budgets, the works undertaken and the works needed, and the Sudan alongside Egypt. The reports document irrigation as an administration, with the engineer's works embedded in a system of water rights and state finance.

> The 1888 reports are scanned.
