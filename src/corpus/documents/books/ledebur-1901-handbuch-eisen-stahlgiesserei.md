## Notes

> Adolf Ledebur's handbook of iron and steel founding — the foundry side of the metallurgy the treatises theorized: the melting, the moulding, the casting of iron and steel, and the defects and their causes, written from the works. The handbook that carried the nineteenth-century foundry into the new century, entered at the boundary because the verified edition is 1901.

> The Getty copy of the 1901 edition is scanned; the earlier editions the critique dates to the 1880s were not resolved in the archive search, so the row carries the verified year.
