## Notes

> Edward Livingston Wilson's cyclopædia — the photographic apparatus, processes, chemicals and practical procedures alphabetized for the profession: the trade's support-cyclopædia by the editor of the Photographic Mosaic, in the decade the dry-plate trade had industrialized. The photography branch's encyclopedic close — the trade's accumulated manuals digested into one reference.
