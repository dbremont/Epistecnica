## Notes

> Francis Trevithick's Life of Richard Trevithick — the engineer's career documented from his own letters and papers: the high-pressure engine and the portable boiler, the Penydarren locomotive and the railway experiments, the Cornish pumping engine and the mineral railways, the patents and the struggles. The 1872 biography is the documentary record of the engineer who made steam portable, and the source book for the high-pressure branch of the steam revolution.

> The 1872 edition is scanned (two copies).
