## Notes

> Louis Vicat reduced the constitution and setting of hydraulic limes to measurement: the calcination states of the stone, the proportion of clay that makes a lime set under water, the times of setting, the proportions of beton and mortar. His Recherches is the founding experimental science of hydraulic binders — the test methods and the classification that prepared Portland cement, the Vicat needle still carrying his name.
