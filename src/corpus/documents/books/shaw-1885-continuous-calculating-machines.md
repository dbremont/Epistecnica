## Notes

> H. S. Hele Shaw's 1885 paper — the theory of continuous calculating machines and a mechanism of the class: the integration of a product by wheel and disc, the geometry of the continuous product, and the conditions under which a machine can calculate a flowing quantity rather than a number. The paper in which the calculating machine is extended from arithmetic to the calculus, and the analogue tradition beside the digital one.
