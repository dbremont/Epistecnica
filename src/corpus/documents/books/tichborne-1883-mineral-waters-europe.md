## Notes

> Charles Tichborne's Mineral Waters of Europe — the natural springs surveyed and the artificial waters made to match them: the analyses of the famous spas, the gases and the salts, the apparatus and the carbonation, and the trade that bottled a chemistry. The book where the mineral-water business became an analytical manufacture, and the beverage branch of the food-processing corpus.

> The 1883 edition is scanned; Struve's 1823 remarks on the artificial-water institution are the earlier record.
