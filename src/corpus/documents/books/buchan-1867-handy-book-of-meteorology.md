## Notes

> Alexander Buchan's Handy Book of Meteorology — meteorology taught for the observer and the sailor: the instruments and their correction, the pressure and the wind, the clouds and the storms, and the climate statistics of the British stations gathered into tables. The classroom form of the forecasting service, and the manual that carried the observatory's practice to the reader.

> The 1867 and 1868 printings are scanned.
