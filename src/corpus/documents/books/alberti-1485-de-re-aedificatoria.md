## Notes

> Leon Battista Alberti's De re aedificatoria — architecture theorized from the Vitruvian text: the lineament and the material, the site and the composition, the private and the public building, and the restoration of the ancient discipline on rational principles. The first modern architectural book, printed in 1485, and the text that set the discipline's scope for the next four centuries.

> The 1485 first edition and the 1512 edition are scanned.
