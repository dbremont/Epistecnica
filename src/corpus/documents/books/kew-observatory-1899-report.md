## Notes

> The Kew Observatory Committee's report for 1899 — the observatory's year: the magnetic and meteorological observations, the solar photographs and the sunspot record, the standardizing of barometers and thermometers and watches, the instruments tested and the results printed. The annual reports document the observatory as an instrument of national measurement, the place where the weather and the magnetic field were made comparable across the country.
