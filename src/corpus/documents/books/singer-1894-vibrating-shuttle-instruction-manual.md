## Notes

> The Singer Manufacturing Company's instruction manual for the 27-2 vibrating-shuttle machine — threading and winding, the tension and the stitch, the feed and the needle, the cleaning and the oiling, and the fault-finding table. The manual is the sewing machine as the first mass-produced consumer durable: a standard machine delivered with its own operating text and maintained by its owner.

> The 1894 manual (Singer form 7160) is scanned.
