## Notes

> Alberto Castigliano carried the energy idea into the bare structure: the strain stored in a stressed system gives its equilibrium, the displacements obtained from the derivative of the strain energy with respect to the forces — the two theorems by which a statically indeterminate truss or frame is, for the first time, a solved system by a general method. Read here in the German translation printed in 1886 — the original of 1879 was printed in Turin.

> The 1886 German translation is scanned; no edition of the 1879 Turin original is verified here.
