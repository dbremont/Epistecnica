## Notes

> Charles Knight's results of machinery — the publisher's tract of 1831, written from the Society for the Diffusion of Useful Knowledge's statistics arm: cheap production and increased employment traced through the fifty-five years of machinery's diffusion. The machinery question's best-documented advocacy — the economical argument documented against both the frame-breaking and the handloom discontent that made it a public question.
