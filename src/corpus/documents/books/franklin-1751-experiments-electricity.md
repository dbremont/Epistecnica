## Notes

> Benjamin Franklin's experiments — the electrical branch's founding manual from the American colonies: the kite-fire economy, the Leyden jar and the one-fluid theory that ran the science before Priestley organized it into a history. Priestley's tradition explicitly stands behind — the electricity tradition's own founder a generation before.
