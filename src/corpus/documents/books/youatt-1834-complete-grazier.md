## Notes

> William Youatt's complete grazier — livestock production and the agricultural practice of the grazing economy: breeding, feeding, the cattle doctor's own accounts, written by the veterinary writer whose animal-science teaching the farming press ran. The livestock branch of the agricultural literature between the grasses of Woburn and the farm operations of Stephens.
