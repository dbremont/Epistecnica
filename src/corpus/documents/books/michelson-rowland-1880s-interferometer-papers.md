## Notes

> The Michelson and Rowland papers — the interferometer and the ruling engine: the wavelength of light used as a ruler, the diffraction grating ruled to a precision no dividing engine had reached, and the standard metre measured in light waves. The instrument branch's arrival at the precision the standards offices later institutionalized.
