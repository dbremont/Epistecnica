## Notes

> William Humber's bridge treatise — the fabrication and erection of cast and wrought iron bridges documented project by project: the designs, the foundations and the practice of the fabricators the railways employed. The iron-bridge literature's complete form, standing beside the English bridge volumes and the water-supply treatise the same author's collection carries.
