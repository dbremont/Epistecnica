## Notes

> John Fincham's historical survey — ship construction's development documented as one naval-history record, prefaced by the mathematical-science argument itself: survey to theory, written by the master shipwright whose dockyard career the drawings ran. The naval-architecture literature's history book between Murray's treatise and Scott Russell's modern system.
