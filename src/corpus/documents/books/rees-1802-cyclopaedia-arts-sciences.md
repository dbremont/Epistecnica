## Notes

> Rees's Cyclopaedia documented the arts of a manufacturing Britain article by article, with the technical plates of John Farey and the Lowry firm — the steam engine worked out in section by John Farey, the textile machinery drawn as built, the mines, mills and manufactures described from the works. It is the English encyclopedia of the technical arts, the counterpart of the Encyclopédie's plates.

> Individual volumes are scanned; the series runs complete on the archive.
