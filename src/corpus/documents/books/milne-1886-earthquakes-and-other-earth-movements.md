## Notes

> John Milne's earthquakes and other earth movements — the seismology of the instrument era: the pendulum systems, recording mechanisms and observatory practice that made earthquake movements continuously measurable, written by the engineer whose Tokyo instruments founded the modern seismograph. Mallet's investigation preceded it; the standing instrument answered it — the book reads the whole earth's movement through its equipment.
