## Notes

> Edward Charles Frome's Outline of the Method of Conducting a Trigonometrical Survey — the survey from the base to the map: the measurement of the base, the triangulation and its adjustment, the theodolite and the level, the topographical detalles, and the computation and plotting of the sheet. The 1873 manual is the surveyor's method book behind the national maps, and the practical companion of Craig's projection theory.

> The 1873 edition is scanned.
