## Notes

> Iltyd Redwood's treatise — the oils, greases and lubrication arithmetic of the machinery economy, written by the petroleum technologist whose testing the oil trade ran. The lubrication literature's professional reference, holding the friction economy the machine shops and railways consumed.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1898 treatise is verified here; the neighbouring friction-and-lubrication treatises do circulate.
