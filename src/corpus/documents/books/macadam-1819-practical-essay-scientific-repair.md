## Notes

> John Loudon MacAdam's 1819 essay — road repair made a science of drainage and materials: the raised crown and the side drains, the broken stone of a uniform size, the subsoil kept dry, and the argument that a road is built from below rather than patched on top. The first statement of the method the 1820 Remarks then generalized, and the technical basis of the turnpike road at its best.

> The 1819 essay is scanned in the Canadian Institute for Historical Microreproductions set.
