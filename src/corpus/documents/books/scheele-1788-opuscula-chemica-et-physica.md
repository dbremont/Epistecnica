## Notes

> Carl Wilhelm Scheele's Opuscula Chemica et Physica — the bench papers collected: the discovery of oxygen and chlorine, the preparation of manganese and the mineral acids, the tartaric and citric acids, the analysis of air and the reactions of the metals. The 1788 Latin collection is the working chemist's papers, the record of an experimental craft carried out with simple apparatus and exact observation, and the foundation of the chemical analysis the industrial century inherited.

> The 1788 collected edition is scanned.
