## Notes

> Boussinesq reduced the elastic body to potentials: the stresses and displacements of the half-space beneath a surface load, closed in three dimensions — the theory from which soil engineers later computed the settlement and stress distribution beneath their foundations, and the mechanics of the load-bearing ground was given its elasticity form.

> The 1885 Gauthier-Villars edition is scanned.
