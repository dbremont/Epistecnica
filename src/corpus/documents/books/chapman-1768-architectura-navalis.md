## Notes

> Fredrik Henrik af Chapman's mercantile naval architecture — the Stockholm plate book of 1768: one hundred and sixteen plates classifying the ships and boats of the merchant and naval marine, from the Nordic lacia to the ocean-going vessels the shipping trade built. The naval-architecture literature's founding classification — the record the ship type-readings found their lineages through.

> Honest record: the 1768 plate book itself is not scanned at archive.org verified here (a 1971 facsimile edition circulates), and the 1781 French translation of Chapman's ship-building treatise is linked as its companion.
