## Notes

> Claude-Louis Navier's lectures at the École des Ponts et Chaussées — applied mechanics carried into engineering calculation: the strength of materials, the elasticity the Navier equations would carry into theory, and the bridge-building arithmetic the French corps of engineers ran on. The textbook that made école mechanics an engineering practice — suspension bridges above all — and the engineering-science literature's French side beside Tredgold's English cast-iron tract.
