## Notes

> Lazarus Ercker's treatise on ores and assaying — the imperial mint-master's systematic account of how gold, silver, copper and lead ores are assayed and their metals extracted and refined, in the service of a sixteenth-century metal economy that needed trust between mine, coin and ledger. Its apparatus predates the period the guide collects and stays with Agricola at the head of the metallurgical literature.

> No archive.org scan is held here; the specialist digitizations of the sixteenth-century edition live in institutional collections (the Hoover-era English translation of 1912 appears in American collections). Honest record: the treatise is catalogued; the digitization chase is open.
