## Notes

Boserup inverted Malthus: population pressure is what drives the intensification of agriculture, from long fallow to short fallow to annual cropping. The book made land-use intensity a function of demographic pressure rather than of technical choice alone, and it remains the alternative to the Malthusian reading.
