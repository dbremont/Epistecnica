## Notes

> The first of William J. M. Rankine's profession-building manuals: applied mechanics — statics, dynamics, strength of materials, the elasticity and the stability the civil and mechanical engineer carried onto the site. With the civil-engineering (1862), steam-engine (1859) and millwork (1869) manuals, it made the Rankine system the textbooks of Victorian engineering.
