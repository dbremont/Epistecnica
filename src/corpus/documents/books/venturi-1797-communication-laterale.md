## Notes

> G. B. Venturi measured the tube that bears his name: at the constriction the stream accelerates and the side branches draw; at the expansion the pressure falls before it recovers. The lateral communication of motion in fluids is the founding principle of the Venturi tube — read later in meters, aspirators and carburettors.

> The 1797 Paris edition is scanned.
