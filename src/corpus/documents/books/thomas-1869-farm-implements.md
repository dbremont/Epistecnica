## Notes

> John Jacobs Thomas's farm implements — the machinery and the mechanical principles of its construction carried to the American farmer by the author of the held Fruit Culturist: ploughs, harrows, drills, reapers and the implements trade, treated as mechanics for the field's own use. The agricultural-operations literature in its machinery register through the Orange Judd press — the same readership the dairy and fruit volumes served.
