## Notes

> Michael Faraday's candle lectures — the combustion chemistry's public demonstration: one flame teaching combustion, capillary action and the scientific method's own economy, printed as the Royal Institution Christmas lectures' own record. The chemistry instruction's most famous volume, standing beside the industrial shelves the collection holds.
