## Notes

> Sigismund Friedrich Hermbstädt's technology and technical-chemistry writings — the French chemistry naturalized into German: his translations of Lavoisier's system and of Guyton de Morveau's theoretical and practical principles, with the compendia and the technological writings that carried the new chemistry to the German manufactures. The translator as the period's real transmission channel, and the German counterpart of Chaptal's programme.

> Honest no-scan: Hermbstädt's own compendia were not resolved as scans; the verified record is his Lavoisier translation (1792) and his Guyton de Morveau (1796–97), both linked.
