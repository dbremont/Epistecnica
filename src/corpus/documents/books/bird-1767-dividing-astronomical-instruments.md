## Notes

> John Bird's The Method of Dividing Astronomical Instruments — the instrument-maker's own account of the work: the dividing of the quadrant and the circle, the beam compass and the micrometer, the method of setting off the degrees and the correction of the errors, and the tools with which the observatory's precision was made. The 1767 book, published by the Commissioners of Longitude, documents the hand-and-engine craft of the dividing room and the instrument that the national almanac depended on.
