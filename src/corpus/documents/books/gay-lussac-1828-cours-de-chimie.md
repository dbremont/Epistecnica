## Notes

> Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac's Cours de chimie — the chemistry of the 1820s taught as a course: the combining volumes of gases and the atomic proportions, the acids and the salts, the metals and their preparation, and the industrial processes that depended on them. The course documents chemistry as a science of manufacture as well as of the laboratory, by the chemist whose gas laws and industrial work made it one.
