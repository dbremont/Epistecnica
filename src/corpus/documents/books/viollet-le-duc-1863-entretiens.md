## Notes

> Eugène-Emmanuel Viollet-le-Duc's Entretiens sur l'architecture — construction reasoned from the Gothic and the classical alike: the stone and the iron, the vault and the truss, the wall and the buttress analysed as structure, and the argument that a rational architecture must follow its materials rather than imitate a style. The theory that turned the medieval fabric into a constructional lesson, and the book the iron builders read.

> The 1863 first volume and the atlas are scanned.
