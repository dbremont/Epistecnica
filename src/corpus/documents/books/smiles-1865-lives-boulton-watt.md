## Notes

> Samuel Smiles's Lives of Boulton and Watt — the engine and the works from the firm's own manuscripts: the separate condenser and the partnership, the Soho manufactory and its division of labour, the engine shop and the erectors, the mining trade and the letters that negotiated it. The 1865 book is the documentary account of the steam engine as a manufactured product, and the workshop beside the engine treatise.
