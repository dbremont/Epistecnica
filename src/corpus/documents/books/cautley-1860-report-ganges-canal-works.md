## Notes

> Proby Cautley's Report on the Ganges Canal Works — the canal from its commencement to the opening in 1854: the head works and the regulating bridge, the main channel and its falls, the masonry aqueducts and the distributaries, the levels and the discharge, and the labour that dug it. The founding document of British Indian irrigation engineering, and the model of the canal as a designed water system.

> Volumes 1 and 3 of the 1860 report are scanned; the plates and volume 2 are not verified here.
