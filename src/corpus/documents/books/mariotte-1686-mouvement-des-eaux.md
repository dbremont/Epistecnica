## Notes

> Edme Mariotte tested what the philosophers had only assumed: the resistance of water in channel and pipe, the resistance of wind, and — in the treatise on the strength of timber printed with it — the line of no strain set inside the bending beam, correcting Galileo and founding the neutral axis on measurement. The posthumous 1686 treatise is the founding experimental hydraulics of the French tradition.

> The scanned copy is the 1700 printing.
