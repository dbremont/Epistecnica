## Notes

> Benjamin Dean Wyatt's observations — the design for the new Theatre Royal, Drury Lane defended as built: the theatre's size, form, sight lines and hearing, the constructive case of the architect whose house dominated the early nineteenth century's patent theatre. The acoustic dimension enters here as an architectural position — a theatre built to hold an audience heard as well as seen, written before a science of acoustics existed to judge it.
