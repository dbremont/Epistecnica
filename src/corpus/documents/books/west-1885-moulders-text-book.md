## Notes

> The moulders' companion — the trade's bench-side instruction: the moulding operations, ladle practice and the casting-floor economy taught as a textbook the ironworks' apprentices ran. The foundry-engineering literature's own part-two — the moulder's register beside the practice volume the same author carried.
