## Notes

> John Bourne's screw-propeller treatise — screw propulsion's design arithmetic and the paddle-wheel comparison that settled the marine adoption question in the 1850s: ship performance read across the tables the transition measured. The propulsion literature's founding English treatise beside the marine-steam-engine volumes that powered it.
