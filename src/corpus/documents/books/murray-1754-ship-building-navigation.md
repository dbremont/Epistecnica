## Notes

> Mungo Murray's treatise — shipbuilding, navigation and the nautical instruments in three parts, written from the Clyde shipwright's own practice: the construction, the seas' theory and the instruments' use carried as one builder-navigator's account. The Scottish side of the naval-architecture literature beside Duhamel's French theory and Chapman's Swedish system.
