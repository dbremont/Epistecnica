## Notes

> The Tredwell & Pell illustrated catalogue of agricultural and horticultural implements — the American implement trade in its own prices and specifications: the ploughs and the cultivators, the harrows and the rollers, the horse hoes and the garden tools, and the machinery the farm and the nursery bought. The 1859 catalogue documents the implement as a manufactured commodity rather than a local smith's product, and the trade literature behind the agricultural revolution of the century.

> The 1859 catalogue is scanned.
