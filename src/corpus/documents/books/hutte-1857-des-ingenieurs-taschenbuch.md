## Notes

> The engineering students of Berlin organized their whole profession into one desk-book compiled by their association, taking the name Hütte — the smelting house — as its own. From the 1857 first edition through every revision of the century, Des Ingenieurs Taschenbuch carried the rules, tables, standards and forms of the German trades in one volume.

> The 1857 first edition is scanned.
