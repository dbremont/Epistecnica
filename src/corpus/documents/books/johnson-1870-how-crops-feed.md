## Notes

> The second half of Samuel W. Johnson's pair — *How Crops Grow* treated the plant's life here; *How Crops Feed* treats its food: the atmosphere and the soil as related to the nutrition of agricultural plants. Liebig's mineral theory, Hellriegel's nitrogen question and the American field's own measurements are carried to the reading farmer — the Connecticut experiment-station director's synthesis of what plant nutrition knew in 1870.
