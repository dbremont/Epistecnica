## Notes

> William Slingo and Arthur Brooker's teaching book for the new trade — the dynamos, lamps, wiring and testing of electric lighting carried to the working man the illumination trade was then making. Its formal instruction is an artifact in itself: electricity engineering at the point where the trade had to train its own artificers, not the readers of telegraphy manuals.
