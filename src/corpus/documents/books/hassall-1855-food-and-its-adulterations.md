## Notes

> Arthur Hill Hassall's Food and Its Adulterations — the microscope turned on the shop: the coffee cut with chicory, the bread with alum, the milk with water, the pickles coloured with copper, each sample examined and the results published in the Lancet's Analytical Sanitary Commission. The founding text of food analysis as a regulatory science, and the reason the Adulteration Act followed.
