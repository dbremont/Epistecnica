## Notes

> John Thomas Fanning's treatise — waterworks, hydrology, distribution and construction as one engineering discipline: the reservoirs, pipes, pumps and supply arithmetic of the American water-supply systems written by the engineer whose Manchester, New Hampshire works set the region's standard. Water-supply engineering's first professional reference in the United States — construction materials and hydraulics beside the waterwheels it powered.
