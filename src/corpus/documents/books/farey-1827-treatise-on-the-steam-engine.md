## Notes

> John Farey's steam-engine treatise — historical, practical and descriptive: the engine's invention and development, the boilers and the machinery by which it made its power, and the working detail of the engines the 1820s industry actually ran. The power-engineering shelf's great descriptive volume before the applied science of Carnot and Rankine carried the engine into thermodynamics — and the guide's reading priority #10.
