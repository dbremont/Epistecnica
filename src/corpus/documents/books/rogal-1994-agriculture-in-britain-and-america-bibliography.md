## Notes

> A modern annotated bibliography of the eighteenth-century agricultural literature of Britain and America — **more than a thousand titles**, arranged and annotated for scholars of eighteenth-century social, economic, and cultural history. Compiling what appeared between the Restoration and the close of the agricultural improvement era, from husbandry manuals and periodicals to society transactions and estate instruction, it serves as a compass into the corpus's agrarian-history stratum: the literature that carried the agricultural revolution's practices into print as they spread across England and across the Atlantic.
