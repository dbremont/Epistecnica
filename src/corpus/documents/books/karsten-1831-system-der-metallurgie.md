## Notes

> Carl Johann Bernhard Karsten's System der Metallurgie — metallurgy given the form of a science: the historical, statistical, theoretical and technical divisions, the classification of ores and processes, and the furnace arithmetic that the German school taught from. The systematizing layer between the eighteenth-century manuals and Percy's English treatise, and the reason the branch reads as a discipline rather than a collection of shop notes.
