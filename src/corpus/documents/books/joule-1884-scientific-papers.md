## Notes

> The collected papers of James Prescott Joule — energy conversion's own experimental record: the paddle-wheel determinations of the mechanical equivalent of heat, the galvanic and electromagnetic work, the experiments whose arithmetic Rankine's and Thomson's thermodynamics taught. The energy-conversion literature's paper source, printed 1884 by the Physical Society's memorial edition.
