## Notes

> Francisco Balaguer y Primo's treatise on the manufacture of vinegars — from wine, from alcohol, from wood, and the acetates beside them — the Spanish-language industrial-fermentation reference of the 1870s. Vinegar as a manufacturing industry rather than a household accident: wine-producing lands' practice, put beside the same decade's German-origin technology books.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of this specific title has been verified here. The same author's related industrial treatises do circulate in digitized form; the vinegar treatise is catalogued but untouched.
