## Notes

> Bélidor's Science des ingénieurs taught the engineer's arithmetic as a discipline: stones and timbers sized by rules from experiment, the retaining wall and the earthworks computed, the practice of fortification and civil building carried in one course. It is the founding French textbook of the profession — the book the Ponts et Chaussées taught from before Gauthey and Navier.

> The scanned copy is the 1754 printing of the 1729 treatise.
