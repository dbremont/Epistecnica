## Notes

> Jonathan Hornblower's compound-engine patent — steam expanded successively in two cylinders, the principle that the nineteenth century's engine design worked out once the pressures and the materials allowed it: a patent that anticipated the compound engine by half a century and was fought by the Boulton and Watt monopoly. The patent as the record of an idea ahead of its means.

> Honest no-scan: the specification is a patent-office record; the entry collects it and the litigation it entered.
