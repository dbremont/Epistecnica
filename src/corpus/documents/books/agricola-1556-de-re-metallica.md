## Notes

> Georgius Agricola's twelve books of mining and metallurgy — the fountainhead of the technical literature of metals: mine survey and drainage, the raising of ores, assaying, smelting, the whole extraction economy of the Saxon mines in its woodcuts. It predates the guide's stated period and stays as the foundational reference alongside Ercker: the practical knowledge from which later mining and metallurgical engineering developed.

> The Hoover translation (1912, mining-engineering annotated) and the Latin original of 1621 carry the scans below; the Scholars' Mine digitization carries the annotated Hoover edition pages.
