## Notes

> Émiland-Marie Gauthey's Traité — bridge construction's masonry arithmetic and the works' economy edited posthumously by Claude Navier: the practice the Burgundian engineer-general built, systematized by the theorist of elasticity the École ran. The bridge-construction manual between Tredgold's materials and the engineering profession's manuals — France's teaching beside Smeaton's reports.
