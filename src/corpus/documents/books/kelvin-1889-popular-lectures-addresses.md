## Notes

> Lord Kelvin's lectures — the measurement-and-instrument science of the electrical age delivered to the public lecture platform: units, standards, the Kelvin scale, the electric instruments — the physical measurement of the late century and the culture of exactness under which its instrumentation worked. The guide's instrumentation shelf lands on Kelvin as its largest platform voice.
