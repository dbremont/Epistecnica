## Notes

> Edward Wegmann's *Design and Construction of Dams* — the dam as an engineering structure, gathered into one volume: the distribution of pressure in a masonry wall, the theoretical and practical profiles, the Spanish, French, Algerian, European, Asian, Australian and American masonry dams, the earthen, rock-fill, hydraulic-fill and timber dams, and the principal types of movable dam — frame, shutter and bear-trap. The first part appeared in 1888 as *The Design and Construction of Masonry Dams*, grown out of Wegmann's calculations for the proposed Quaker Bridge Dam and the high masonry dams built for New York's water supply; the 1899 edition enlarges it to the whole subject, with the New Croton Dam specifications and a worked profile calculation in the appendix.
