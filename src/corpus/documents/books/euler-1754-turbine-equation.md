# Leonhard Euler (1754–66). Théorie Plus Complète des Machines qui sont mises en mouvement par la Réaction de l Eau. [Prussian Memoires / E226].

```bibtex
@article{euler1754turbine,
  title={Principes généraux du mouvement des fluides},
  author={Euler, Leonhard},
  year={1754 (E226 of 1757)},
  publisher={Berlin: Académie},
}
```

## Notes

> Euler analysed the water-wheel as a momentum machine: the change of motion inside the wheel computed by loss and gain, the foundations of Euler’s turbine equation. Also the Euler equation of the machine applied to turbines, pumps, fans and every later turbomachinery treatise.
