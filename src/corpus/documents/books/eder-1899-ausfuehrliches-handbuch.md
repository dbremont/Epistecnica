## Notes

> Josef Maria Eder's Ausführliches Handbuch der Photographie — the whole photographic art and its history documented: the chemistry and the optics, the processes from the daguerreotype to the gelatine plate, the apparatus and the literature, and the historical chapters that make the handbook a bibliography as well as a manual. The book in which photography became a documented science, and the reference the twentieth century's photographic history still cites.

> The 1899 volume and several part-issues of the second edition are scanned.
