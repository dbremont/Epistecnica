## Notes

> John Playfair's Illustrations of the Huttonian Theory of the Earth — Hutton's argument made readable: the demonstrations restated, the field evidence assembled, the objections answered, and the prose that carried the theory into the nineteenth century. The book that saved Hutton from his own style, and the reason the uniformitarian programme had a text to teach from.
