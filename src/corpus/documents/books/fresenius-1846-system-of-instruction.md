## Notes

> Carl Remigius Fresenius's instruction in quantitative analysis — the Wiesbaden laboratory's teaching distilled into one system: gravimetric procedures, separations and the cation-and-anion order that made analytical chemistry a teachable discipline. Translated into English in its first year, it became the century's laboratory method-book through generation after generation of editions.
