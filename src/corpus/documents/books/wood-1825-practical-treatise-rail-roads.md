## Notes

> Nicholas Wood's practical treatise on rail-roads — the colliery engineer's 1825 account of railway engineering at the moment the Stockton and Darlington opened: rails and carriages, the locomotive question and the interior-communication economy, written by the Killingworth colliery's own engineer. The railway-engineering literature's first systematic document, in the year the railway became a public system instead of a waggonway.
