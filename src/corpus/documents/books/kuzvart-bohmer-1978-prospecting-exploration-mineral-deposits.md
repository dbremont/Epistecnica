## Notes

> The standard method manual of its era: the **sequence** from regional reconnaissance through target definition to reserve estimation, with the decision rules that govern each stage. It is a book about what to do next, and what evidence justifies spending the next tranche of money — which is the actual content of exploration, as distinct from the geology it is applied to.

> Its structure is the discipline's: **prospecting** (finding a mineralisation signature — geological, geophysical, geochemical) → **exploration** (establishing size, grade and continuity by drilling and sampling) → **evaluation** (resources and reserves, with the classification scheme and the error attached to each). The staged character is the point: each stage is designed to kill the project cheaply if the evidence is not there.

> Read with [Lindgren](/view.html?p=documents/books/lindgren-1919-mineral-deposits.md) and [Solovov](/view.html?p=documents/books/solovov-1987-geochemical-prospecting.md), the trio is the complete literature of finding a deposit: the classification says what kind of thing you are looking for, the survey says how to see its trace, and this manual says how to prove it at a price.
