## Notes

> Christoph Andreas Schlüter's thorough instruction in hütten-works — the first systematic German manual of the smelting works, with the plates of furnaces, hearths, bellows, stamps and the whole works layout that the Harz and Erzgebirge practice ran on. The German manual layer the guide's Agricola and Ercker opened, printed two centuries later in the same trade.

> The Wellcome and BSB copies are both scanned; the plates are the reason the book matters beside the text.
