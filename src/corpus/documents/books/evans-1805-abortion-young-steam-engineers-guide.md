## Notes

> Oliver Evans's Abortion of the Young Steam Engineer's Guide — the American high-pressure engine argued for and described from the works: the boiler, the valve, the mill plant and the applications, written by the inventor who had already built the automated flour mill and the amphibious dredge. The eccentric title is the sign of a practical inventor writing against the established engine, and the book the American high-pressure line started from.
