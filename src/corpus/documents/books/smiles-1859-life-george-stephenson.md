## Notes

> Samuel Smiles's Life of George Stephenson — the railway documented from the engineer's own life and letters: the colliery engines and the safety lamp, the Stockton and Darlington and the Liverpool and Manchester, the Rocket and the Rainhill trial, the gauge and the surveys, and the organisation of the locomotive works. The 1859 biography is the documentary account of the railway as a working system, and the book that made the railway engineer a public figure.
