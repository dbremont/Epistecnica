## Notes

> Roderick Murchison's Silurian System — a formation defined by its fossils and mapped across a region: the strata of the Welsh borderland described bed by bed, the fossils that identified them, and the correlation that let the same rocks be recognized elsewhere. The model of the fossil-based system the century's stratigraphy ran on, and the book that gave the geological survey its method and its first great division.
