## Notes

> Euler's three papers on map projection, reissued in 1898 in Ostwald's Klassiker der exakten Wissenschaften — the conformal projection and its analysis, the general conditions a map projection must satisfy, and the construction of a projection from a given distortion. The 1898 edition put the eighteenth-century mathematical papers back into the mapmaker's hands, and shows projection theory as a continuous mathematical tradition from Euler to Craig.

> The 1898 Ostwald edition is scanned.
