## Notes

> James Joseph Manley's treatise — the cane-sugar extraction and industrial manufacture of 1884: the machinery and process economy of a colonial industry read at the decade the beet economy pressed it. The sugar manufacture's own cane-side reference beside Burgh's 1863 machinery treatise and Evangelista's 1895 Spanish cane-and-beet pairing — the cane industry's getting-the-scale documentation three decades apart.
