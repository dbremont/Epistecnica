## Notes

> Alfred Vail — Morse's partner, the builder of the working apparatus and the dot-dash alphabet — published the American telegraph as a manual: the register and the relay, the line construction, the reports of Congress and the Baltimore demonstration printed with the apparatus as built. That 1845 handbook is the primary account of the first working telegraph system in an intelligible apparatus form.

> Vail's 1847 Description of the American Electro Magnetic Telegraph is a further printed account.
