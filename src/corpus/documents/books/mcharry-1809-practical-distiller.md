## Notes

> Samuel McHarry's practical distiller — the mashing, brewing and still-house economy of the American distilling trade: whiskey, gin and brandy from the pot stills the grain economy fed. The distilling branch's founding American manual, printed in the decade the monasteries' arts became an industry.
