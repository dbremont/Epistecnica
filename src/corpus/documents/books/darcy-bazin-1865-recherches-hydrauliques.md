## Notes

> The Recherches hydrauliques are the experimental corpus behind the century hydraulic arithmetic: the Dijon canal and pipe experiments carried through channel by channel — wood, lime, the walls and beds measured, the coefficients fitted for the open-channel flow and for the pipe resistance that Darcy had already formulated in 1856. Bazin pressed the work after Darcy death, and the formulas were carried into Ganguillet-Kutter, into the American textbooks and into Fanning.
