## Notes

> Charles Edgar Fritts's adjuster's manual — the chronometer and watch adjusting carried to the trade's own practical guide: springing, timing and the adjustments for isochronism, positions and temperature. The horological branch's adjusting register beside the trade's treatise and dictionary — the outline's chronometer row entered honestly.
