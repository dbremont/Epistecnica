## Notes

> Robert Bunsen's Gasometrische Methoden — gas analysis and measurement systematized as one discipline: the eudiometry, absorption methods and the apparatus of the Heidelberg laboratory whose burners and burettes carry his name. Gas analysis's founding manual — the instrumentation the chemical-manufacturing and mine-air literatures would measure by.
