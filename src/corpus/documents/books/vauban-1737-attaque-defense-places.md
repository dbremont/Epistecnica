## Notes

> The posthumous treatise of the man who took a hundred fortified places for Louis XIV and rebuilt the fortress on the frontier: the attack by trenches and parallels, batteries and mines, the defence classified by works — the method of the fortress army that commanded Europe for two centuries. The Woolwich and West Point traditions descend from this book.

> The scanned copy is the 1742 edition of the 1737 posthumous treatise.
