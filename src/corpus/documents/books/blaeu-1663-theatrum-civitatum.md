## Notes

> Joan Blaeu's theatre of Italian cities — the two-volume atlas of 1663 showing the towns and developed areas of Italy: engraved town views, city plans and the admired monuments (*admiranda*) of the peninsula, drawn as much for the cities' built works as their situation. The chorography beside the physical descriptions of the Telluris section: the built world mapped city by city by the great Dutch atlas house, whose town volumes the European libraries bought.
