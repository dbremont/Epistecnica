## Notes

> Thackrah surveyed the trades of Leeds as a physician: the dust of the flax mill, the lead of the painter, the posture of the shoemaker, the hours of the factory child. The book carried Ramazzini's inventory into the industrial town and gave the sanitary movement its clinical evidence.
