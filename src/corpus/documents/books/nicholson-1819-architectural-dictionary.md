## Notes

> Peter Nicholson's Architectural Dictionary — the terms, the trades and the materials of building in one alphabet: the joinery and the masonry, the instruments and the machines, the classic orders and the modern practice, with the plates that defined each term. The reference that let an architect specify materials and trades he did not himself practise, and the dictionary form the technical encyclopaedias then took up.
