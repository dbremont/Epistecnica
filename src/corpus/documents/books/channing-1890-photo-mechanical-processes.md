## Notes

> Charles Frederick Channing's Photo-Mechanical Processes — the photographic image turned into a printing surface: the letterpress block and the halftone, the photo-lithograph and the collotype, the photogravure and the woodburytype, with the camera work and the etching and the press. The book in which photography entered the printing trade as a process rather than a picture, and the technical base of the illustrated press.
