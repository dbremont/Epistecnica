## Notes

> The instrument-house treatise — Negretti & Zambra's account of the meteorological apparatus as one commercial family: barometers, barographs, thermometers, hygrometers, rain gauges, anemometers and the instruments of atmospheric electricity, their principles, construction and practical use. Barometers rendered as apparatus economics: the manufacturer as the author of the science's own instruments manual.
