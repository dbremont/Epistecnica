## Notes

> Augustus Voelcker's agricultural-chemistry papers — the consulting chemist's work as the farms bought it: soils analysed, manures valued by their constituents, feeding stuffs checked against their adulterants, and the reports that told a farmer what his purchase actually contained. The commercial face of the agricultural chemistry beside Rothamsted's experimental programme.
