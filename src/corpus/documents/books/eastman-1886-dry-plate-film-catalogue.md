## Notes

> The Eastman Dry Plate and Film Company's illustrated catalogue — the dry plate and the roll film sold as manufactured goods: the plates and the films, the cameras and the holders, the chemicals and the papers, with the prices and the descriptions that turned a laboratory process into a catalogue commodity. The document in which photography became an industry rather than a craft, and the trade literature the guide's photographic manuals assume.
