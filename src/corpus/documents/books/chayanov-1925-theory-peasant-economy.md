## Notes

Chayanov modelled the peasant household as a labour–consumption balance rather than a profit-maximising firm: the family works until the marginal drudgery outweighs the marginal product it needs. It is the classic statement that the farm is not always a capitalist enterprise, and a permanent reference for agrarian economics.
