## Notes

> Floris Osmond's allotropy papers — the iron allotropes identified and measured, the microscopic examination of steel made a method, and the heat treatment of steel given its physical explanation: the metallography that Howe and Roberts-Austen were writing into their treatises at the same moment. The paper layer beneath the metallography books the guide holds.

> Honest no-scan: the papers live in the French journal series rather than in a scanned monograph; the entry is a collector for them, and the chase is open for the specific numbers.
