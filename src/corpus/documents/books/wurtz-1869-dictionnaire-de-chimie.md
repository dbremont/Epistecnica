## Notes

> Adolphe Wurtz's dictionary — the French reference behind Muspratt's and Wagner's English treats, written from the Sorbonne's teaching tradition. Beside Thorpe's applied chemistry and Lunge's industrial treatises — the pure and applied reference vocabulary.
