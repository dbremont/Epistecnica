## Notes

> William Crookes's dyeing handbook — the editor of the *Chemical News* and discoverer of thallium turned industrial-chemistry author: dyeing and printing carried onto the trade's own chemistry, its instruments and its machinery, eleven coloured plates at hand. A decade after O'Neill's dictionary, the synthetic dyes and the analysis had transformed the craft; the handbook documents the change from the chemist's side.
