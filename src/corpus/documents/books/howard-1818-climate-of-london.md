## Notes

> Luke Howard's Climate of London — systematic urban weather observation: daily readings of temperature, pressure and wind kept for years, the city compared with the surrounding country, and the first clear demonstration that a great town makes its own climate. The beginning of urban climatology and of the long instrumental record the later meteorological services built on.

> The 1818 first volume and the 1833 second edition are scanned.
