## Notes

> James Bicheno Francis's Lowell experiments — the hydraulic motors, weir flows and the great turbines of the Lowell mills measured with the precision the canals' economy demanded: the Francis formula for weirs is the report's own arithmetic. The experimental hydraulics literature's American foundation — water power measured where it worked.
