## Notes

> Milton Thomas Richardson's blacksmithing series — the smith's bench economy collected from the trade press's own practitioners: each volume an account of the fires, stock, hammers and shop arithmetic. The forging trade's own printed notebook, three volumes over 1889–91, entered honestly for the smithing branch.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the period printing is verified here; one modern republication circulates, and the originals are catalogued but untouched.
