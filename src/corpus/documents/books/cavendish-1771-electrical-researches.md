## Notes

> Henry Cavendish's electrical researches — the quantitative experiments kept in manuscript and published a century later: the Leyden jars compared by capacity, the conductivity of solutions measured, and the laws of electric force established by experiment before the instruments to name them existed. The founding quantitative layer between Franklin's observations and Coulomb's torsion balance.

> Maxwell's 1879 edition is the form in which the trade read them, and the 1771 Philosophical Transactions paper is the printed part. Both are scanned.
