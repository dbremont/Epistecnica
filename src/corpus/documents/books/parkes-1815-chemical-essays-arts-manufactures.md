## Notes

> Samuel Parkes, the London chemist and industrialist, put the chemical arts of British manufacture into essays: the kinds of alkali and soda, the soap, the bleaching arts of the textile trades, the vitriol and the glasshouse, each stated with its processes and its commercial form. The essays ran through many editions and were read by a generation before Ure and the dictionaries of the 1830s stood in their place.

> The scanned copies are the 1815 Baldwin first edition and the 1819 printing.
