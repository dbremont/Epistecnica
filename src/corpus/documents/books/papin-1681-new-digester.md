## Notes

> Denis Papin's new digester — the pressure vessel built, used and described: the safety valve he devised for it, the bone-softening and the cookery, and the experiments that showed what a confined steam could do. The first working pressure machine in print, and the book in which the piston driven by steam is sketched as a possibility beside the digester.

> The 1681 first edition and the 1687 continuation are both scanned from the early-English collection.
