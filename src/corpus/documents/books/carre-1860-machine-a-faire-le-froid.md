## Notes

> Ferdinand Carré's absorption-refrigeration apparatus — the machine that made cold by the ammonia-water cycle, patented and commercially described in the 1860s through the description documents its machines' construction carried. Absorption refrigeration's founding apparatus, beside Twining's compressed-air ice-making and the iron-machinery decade the Cibaria refrigeration rows collect.
