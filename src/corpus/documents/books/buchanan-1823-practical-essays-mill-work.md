## Notes

> Robertson Buchanan's Practical Essays on Mill Work and Other Machinery — the millwright's work examined with the engineer's arithmetic: the water wheel and its effect, the teeth of wheels and the gearing, the shafts and the bearings, the millstones and the machinery they drove, and the calculations by which the mill was proportioned. The 1823 book fills the gap between the millwright's rule of thumb and the engineer's theory, and it was reprinted with additions in 1841.

> The 1823 first edition is scanned; the 1841 edition is also held.
