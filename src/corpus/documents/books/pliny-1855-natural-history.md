# John Bostock & Henry T. Riley, tr. (1855). The Natural History of Pliny. London: Henry G. Bohn.

```bibtex

```

## Notes

> The translation carries the five industrial books — gold, silver, copper, iron, lead, tin, the alloys, the glass furnaces of Sidon and Alexandria, the pigments and the papyrus. Pliny is the one ancient encyclopedia in which the arts of the workshop are treated at book length.
