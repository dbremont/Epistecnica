## Notes

> Schmidt edited the Greek text of the *Pneumatica* and the *Automata* with a German translation: the force pump, the water organ, the reaction steam sphere, the temple-door automata and the five machine laws. The volume is the working edition of the Alexandrian mechanics behind the Renaissance machine books.
