## Notes

> Thomas Tredgold's rail-roads treatise — the early railway's construction principles and carriage power theory, written by the cast-iron engineer in the same year Wood's practical treatise appeared: the two founding texts of railway engineering's printed economy. The traction-to-corrective account whose carriage arithmetic the Inspectorate's quarters ran against Stockton.
