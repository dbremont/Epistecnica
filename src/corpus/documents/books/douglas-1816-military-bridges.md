## Notes

> General Sir Howard Douglas's essay — the founding English doctrine of military bridges and river passages, written from the Peninsular war's proving grounds three years after Waterloo. The military-bridging literature's original work, of which the held Gregory treatise of 1857 is the later generation — the outline's row fills the branch's own founding slot.
