## Notes

> Agostino Ramelli's book of diverse and ingenious machines — the founding printed machine-book: 195 engraved plates of pumps, mills, hoists, presses and the turning-machines that the mechanica branch's machine-book lineage descends from. The sixteenth-century source Veranzio, Böckler and Leupold's plates all stand behind.
