## Notes

> The earthquake laboratory John Milne and Fusakichi Mori equipped for the World's Columbian Exposition — the instruments of the Tokyo observatory carried to Chicago as a public exhibit of the seismological programme: pendulums, recorders, and the descriptive matter the *Nature* note printed. An instrument catalogue rather than a treatise, but the primary document of what seismometry's toolkit looked like in 1893.
