## Notes

> The Astronomer Royal's treatise on atmospheric sound — propagation through an atmosphere whose wind, temperature and motion the meteorological sciences were themselves learning to measure, with the mathematical elements of music appended. Sound's medium treated as the object: the atmosphere as an instrument laboratory, written for the same century's forecasting and observatory sciences.
