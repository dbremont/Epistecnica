## Notes

> The country-study handbook: a single reference volume covering the Soviet Union's geography, history, population, political system, economy, foreign policy and defence, written for a general readership and compiled from official and academic sources. Its value is breadth and standardisation rather than argument — it is the volume to check a date, an institution's name, or the structure of a ministry against.

> The economy chapters are the most useful part for the corpus: they describe the planning apparatus, the industrial ministries, agriculture, transport and the reform debates of the late Soviet period in the same vocabulary the specialist literature uses, which makes it a good entry point before [Nove](/view.html?p=documents/books/nove-1977-soviet-economic-system-allen-unwin.md) or [Kornai](/view.html?p=documents/books/kornai-1992-socialist-system-princeton.md).
