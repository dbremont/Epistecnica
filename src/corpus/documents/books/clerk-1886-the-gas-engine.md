## Notes

> Dugald Clerk's gas-engine theory — the cycle's thermodynamic analysis measured at the inventor's own engines, printed in the Institution's proceedings and expanded into the gas-engine treatise the 1880s trade ran: internal combustion's founding English literature, the working theory whose Otto-cycle economy the two-stroke engine (his own patent) carried.
