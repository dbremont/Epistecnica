## Notes

> Elwood Mead's 1899 paper — the rise of irrigation in the United States and its future: the diversion works and the ditches of the mountain states, the water rights and the arid-land acts, the colonies and the settlement they carried, and the federal programme he argued for. The American irrigation movement's statement at the century's close, and the western counterpart of the Indian and Egyptian canal reports.
