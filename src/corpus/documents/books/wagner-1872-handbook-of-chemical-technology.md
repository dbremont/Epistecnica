## Notes

> Rudolf Wagner's handbook of chemical technology — the systematic account of industrial chemical processes (the German *Technologie* of the Leipzig school), translated into English by William Crookes for the British industrial reader. More systematic than the dictionary format: the processes follow from their chemistry, and the manual to the packed industrial reader of the 1870s.
