## Notes

> James Atkinson's gas-engine papers and patents — the cycle variations worked out against Otto's patent: the differential piston, the over-expansion and the scavenging arrangements, each an attempt to get more work from the same charge. The inventive space the Otto patent left open, documented by the engineer who tested its boundaries.
