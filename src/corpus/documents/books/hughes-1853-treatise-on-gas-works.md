## Notes

> Samuel Hughes's gas-works treatise — the manufacture, purification, retorts and the water-gas question at the works' construction economy: the gas-engineers' manual a decade behind Clegg's, reading the supply industry's plant as its own built works. The gasworks literature's second systematic treatment, alongside the same Lockwood series' engineering titles.
