## Notes

> Hermann von Helmholtz's theory of tone-sensations — resonance, combination tones, beats and the physiology of hearing measured with the resonators he built for the purpose: the acoustic-analysis side of the sound sciences, written between physics and the concert hall. Where Rayleigh's later treatise would compute, this book instrumented — and the 1875 Ellis translation carried both to English readers.
