## Notes

> John Percy's metallurgy series — the Royal School of Mines chair's exhaustive treatment of materials, furnaces, ores and extraction: the indispensable metallurgical reference of the nineteenth century and the guide's priority #4. The first volume treated the art of extracting metals generally; its treatment of furnaces, ores and production is unusually detailed alongside the industrial journals the period's metallurgy ran through.
