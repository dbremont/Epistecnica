## Notes

> The Great Trigonometrical Survey of India — the subcontinent triangulated from the coast to the mountains: the baseline measured with the compensation bars, the theodolites and the signals, the arc carried north, and the survey made a geodetic instrument rather than a map-making convenience. The largest trigonometrical operation of the century, and the measurement that gave the Himalayas their height.

> Honest no-scan: the survey volumes are official publications in scattered sets; the entry collects them and names the survey.
