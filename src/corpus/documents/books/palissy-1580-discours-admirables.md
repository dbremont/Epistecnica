## Notes

> The Huguenot Huguenot potter Bernard Palissy worked his craft — the clot of the clay, the tin-lead glazing, and stone corals turned into art. His Discours Admirables founded notably the hats of the Renaissance in moving both the hydroil and the general clay glaze apparatus of the shop.
