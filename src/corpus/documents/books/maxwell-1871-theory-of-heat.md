## Notes

> James Clerk Maxwell's Theory of Heat — the heat doctrine taught with the statistical argument inside it: the thermometer and the expansion, the mechanical equivalent, the reversible cycles and the entropy, and the molecular theory that made the second law a statement about probabilities. The teaching form of the thermodynamics the engine builders needed, and the book that carried Maxwell's demon into the ordinary reader's hands.

> The 1872 printing and the 1899 edition are scanned; the first edition is 1871.
