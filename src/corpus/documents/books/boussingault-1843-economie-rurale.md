## Notes

> Jean-Baptiste Boussingault's Économie rurale — the farm weighed as chemistry: the crops and the animals entered in balance sheets of carbon, nitrogen and mineral matter, the field experiments at Bechelbronn reported, and the feeding of livestock given its first quantitative basis. The French experimental agriculture beside Liebig's programme, and the source of the balanced ration the later feeding manuals assume.
