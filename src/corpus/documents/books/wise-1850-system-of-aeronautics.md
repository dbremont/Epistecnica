## Notes

> John Wise's system — the aeronaut's own documented economy of ballooning: envelope making, hydrogen generation, the captive and free ascents the pioneer balloons flew. The aeronautics literature's founding American work, entered here beside the French dirigible greats the guide's flight trades only sketched — the branch's missing domain, opened.
