## Notes

> Goldsworthy Gurney's 1854 specification — the steam carriage's boiler and engine laid out as a road vehicle: the compact boiler, the blast and the draught, the engine mounted on the carriage, and the drive to the wheels. The steam carriage was the first practical motor vehicle, and its specification records the road-vehicle problems — weight, steering, braking and the road surface — that the automobile inherited.

> The specification is scanned as an eight-page pamphlet with plates.
