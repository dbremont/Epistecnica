## Notes

> Richter arranged the notebooks by subject and reproduced the machine drawings with transcriptions: the elements of machines, the lifting and driving devices, the water works and the flying machines. The edition made the notebooks readable as a technical corpus rather than as a curiosity of manuscripts.
