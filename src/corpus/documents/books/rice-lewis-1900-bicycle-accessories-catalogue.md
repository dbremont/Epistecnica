## Notes

> Rice Lewis & Son's 1900 bicycle accessories catalogue — the Canadian mail-order trade in the parts and fittings of the safety bicycle: hubs and bearings, chain and sprockets, tyres and rims, saddles, lamps and bells, and the tools of the repair shop. The catalogue in which the bicycle appears as a maintained machine with a component economy, at the close of the century that made it.

> The 1900 catalogue is scanned.
