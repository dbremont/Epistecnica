## Notes

> Henry Darcy, Inspecteur Général des Ponts et Chaussées, carried Dijon from the Rosiere spring to the tap through a whole water-supply engineering: the pipe sections and their friction (the law of head lost per length and velocity that bears his name), the aqueduct, the filter beds — and, in the appendix, the experiments on the passage of water through sand that founded the theory of flow in porous media. Les Fontaines publiques is the complete document of a nineteenth-century municipal water supply, computed as it was built.
