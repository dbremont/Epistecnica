## Notes

> The engineers of Bern reduced the friction of the channel to one general formula: a roughness coefficient scaled from Swiss stream measurements that took the whole range of beds and slopes into a single law. The 1869 formula reached the English-speaking world through the 1889 translation of Hering and Trautwine — the standard on which American and British channel computations ran until Manning.

> The scanned copy is the 1889 English translation.
