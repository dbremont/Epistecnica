## Notes

> Henri-Louis Duhamel du Monceau's grain treatise — the 18th-century preservation economy where grain storage, ventilation and granary design were a documented agricultural science. The food-preservation branch's own founding document before Appert's sealed-jar chemistry, and the Saumur school's agriculture—commerce connection the plough-and-grain economy carried.
