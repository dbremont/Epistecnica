## Notes

> Isabella Beeton's Book of Household Management — the whole household entered as a system: the cookery beside the management of servants, the stores and the accounts, the medical and the legal advice, and the encyclopaedic range that made the book a fixture of the Victorian middle-class home. The form in which the household became an object of technical description, and the domestic counterpart of the trade encyclopaedias the guide holds.

> The 1880 printings are scanned; the first edition is 1861.
