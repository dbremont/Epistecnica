## Notes

> Nicholas Culpeper's Pharmacopoeia Londinensis — the London pharmacopoeia translated into English, expanded and modernized for the apothecary: the simples and the compounds, the preparations and the uses, the astrological additions, and the plain instruction the Latin formulary withheld. The vernacular dispensatory carried the official pharmacopoeia into the shop, and the scanned edition is a later seventeenth- or eighteenth-century printing.

> The scanned copy is an undated later edition.
