## Notes

Walter Blith's treatise is the classic statement of the mid-seventeenth-century English improvement programme: drainage, enclosure, new crops and better tillage as a national duty. It belongs on the list because it turns husbandry into a programme of deliberate improvement, the frame the later experimental agriculture inherits.
