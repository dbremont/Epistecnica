## Notes

> Octave Chanute's Progress in Flying Machines — the century's attempts at flight collected and judged: the balloon, the ornithopter, the aeroplane and the glider, each inventor's apparatus described and its results tabulated, the experiments of Lilienthal and Langley weighed, and the braced biplane recommended as the form with a future. The synthesis before the Wrights, and the book they read as a manual.
