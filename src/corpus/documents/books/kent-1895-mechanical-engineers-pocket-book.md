## Notes

> William Kent — consulting mechanical engineer of the ASME generation — compiled the pocket-book the profession carried as "Kent": rules, tables, data and standards of the American mechanical engineering desk. The scanned copy is the 1901 printing of the 1895 first edition; the book stands across the guide's 1900 boundary as the entr reference of the desk tradition.

> It is the measuring generation of the desk tradition beside Haswell and Hütte.
