## Notes

> William Harvey Birkmire set the tall office buildings into their method: the steel skeleton carrying the whole building above its own frame — the boxed columns, the riveted girders, the wind bracing, the fireproofing of the steel, and the floors hung upon the frame rather than borne by the masonry. Skeleton Construction is the defining manual of the first skyscraper decade, printed as the taller buildings were still being raised.
