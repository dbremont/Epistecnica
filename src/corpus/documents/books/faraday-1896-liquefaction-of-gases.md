## Notes

> The Alembic Club's 1896 reprint of Faraday's liquefaction papers — the apparatus and the method: the sealed tube and the generating flask, the cooling baths of ice and salt, the gases condensed one after another, and the pressures and temperatures recorded at each success. The papers in which a permanent gas was first made a liquid, and the experimental foundation on which Linde, Hampson and Dewar built the low-temperature machines.

> The 1896 Alembic Club reprint is scanned; it also carries Thomas Northmore's earlier compression papers.
