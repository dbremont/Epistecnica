## Notes

> George Atwood's Disquisition on the Stability of Ships — the stability of a ship defined and measured: the metacentre and the centre of gravity, the righting moment at small and large angles, the model experiments on the water, and the rules a builder could apply. The paper that made stability a quantity a naval officer could test, and the experimental companion of the theoretical treatises.

> The 1798 paper and the 1796 geometrical propositions are scanned.
