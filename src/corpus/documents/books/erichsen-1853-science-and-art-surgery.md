## Notes

> John Eric Erichsen's surgery — the treatise of surgical methods, instruments and operations that carried the profession's teaching through generations of editions: science and art in the title's own pairing, the operative discipline beside the instrument trades that supplied its knives. The medical-technological literature's surgical reference, standing beside the microscope branch's medical microscopy as the guide reaches the operating professions.
