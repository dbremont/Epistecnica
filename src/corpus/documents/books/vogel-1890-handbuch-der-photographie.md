## Notes

> Hermann Wilhelm Vogel's Handbuch der Photographie — photography taught as applied chemistry: the silver salts and the collodion and the gelatine, the sensitizers and the colour sensitivity he discovered, the densitometry and the sensitometry, and the practice of the plate and the print. The book in which the photographic process became a measurable science, by the chemist who taught the dry plate to see colour.

> The 1890 edition is scanned; the handbook appeared in successive editions from the 1870s.
