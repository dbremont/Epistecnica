## Notes

> Under Park Benjamin's editorship the Cyclopaedia of Applied Mechanics assembled the American shop's applied mechanics in two volumes — steam, machine tools, structures, motive machinery and the industrial arts — described and drawn for the office desk. The scanned copy is the 1895 edition.

> It is the American reference of the 1880s, read beside Knight and the British dictionaries.
