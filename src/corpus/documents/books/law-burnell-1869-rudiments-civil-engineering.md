## Notes

> Henry Law's Rudiments, continued and rewritten by George Rowdon Burnell — civil and hydraulic engineering's groundwork carried in the Weale teaching series through its editions: setting out, earthworks, masonry, water power and water supply for the practical engineer. The rudimentary-civil text the water-supply branch reads beside Fanning's professional treatise.
