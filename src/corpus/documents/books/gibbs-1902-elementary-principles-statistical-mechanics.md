## Notes

> Josiah Willard Gibbs's Elementary Principles in Statistical Mechanics — statistical mechanics formulated as a general theory: the canonical and grand-canonical ensembles, the partition function and the thermodynamic analogies, written with the economy that made the book hard and permanent. The boundary text where the heat doctrine becomes the statistical mechanics of the new century, entered at 1902 because that is the verified publication.

> The 1902 first edition and the modern reprint are both scanned.
