## Notes

> Nicolas Bion's instrument treatise — the Paris maker's own account of the construction and principal uses of the mathematical instruments, translated into English by Edmund Stone in 1723 for the trade the London shops could buy. The eighteenth-century instrument-maker's literature at its most traded form — construction, adjustment and operation in one trade book both languages carried.
