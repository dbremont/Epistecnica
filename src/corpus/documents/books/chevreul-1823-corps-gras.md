## Notes

> Chevreul dissociated the fats and their soap trade: the animal and plant gangs made into formularies for a chemistry of fats, with stearic and oleic acids isolated. The Recherches then carried the candle trade from tallow into stearin, and the soap trade into its chemical soap-glycerine base.
