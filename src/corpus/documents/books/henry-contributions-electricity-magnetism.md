## Notes

> Joseph Henry's contributions — the experimental series on electromagnets and induction: the magnetizing power of the coiled wire, the apparatus of the Princeton laboratory, from the 1830s through the 1850s. The electromagnet's experimental science carried as published record beside Faraday's London work — the American side of the electromagnet technology the telegraphy books built.
