## Notes

> Oliver Heaviside's Electrical Papers — the transmission-line theory, the operator calculus and the distortionless circuit, collected from the journal papers: the mathematical layer between Maxwell's treatise and the engineering handbooks, written largely outside the institutions and adopted by them slowly. The theory the telephone and the submarine cable ran on before the textbooks taught it.

> The two-volume 1892 collection is scanned.
