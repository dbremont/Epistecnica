## Notes

> William Theodore Brannt's vinegar treatise — the manufacture of vinegar, acetates, cider, fruit-wines, pickles, preserves and the preservation of meats, fish, oysters and eggs under one practical roof: the preservation industries as one apparatus-economy rather than separate crafts. Its scope makes it the guide's food-preservation shelf reference beside Appert's founding treatise.
