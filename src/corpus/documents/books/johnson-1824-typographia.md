## Notes

> John Johnson's Typographia — the printing machinery, typography and operations of the press carried into the printer's own instructional form: the trade's elements from letter-founding to the composed page, written for the apprentice printing house. The English print-trade literature beside Moxon's century-older Mechanick exercises — printing documented as the documented trade of trades.
