## Notes

> Gustave Eiffel's La Tour de 300 mètres — the tower described by its engineer: the wind load and the curve of the profile, the foundations and the caissons, the ironwork and the riveting, the elevators and the platforms, the construction sequence and the cost. The 1900 book is the engineer's own account of the century's tallest structure, and the monument of the iron construction that the exhibition architecture carried to its limit.

> The 1900 edition with its plates is scanned.
