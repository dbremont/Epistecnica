# Marin Mersenne (1636). Harmonie Universelle, contenant la Theorie et la Pratique de la Musique. Paris.

```bibtex

  publisher={Paris: S. Cramoisy},
```

## Notes

> Mersenne worked the study of vibrations: the actual tone of bells, strings, pipes and the boundary between tone and machine usual trade. The bell dimensions, the string weights and the pipes measured.
