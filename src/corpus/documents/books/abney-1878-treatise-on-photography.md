## Notes

> Captain William de Wiveleslie Abney's treatise — photographic chemistry, optics and practice in one systematic text at the dry-plate revolution: the gelatin processes the officer-scientist helped found and the instruction the profession ran through its editions. The photography branch's engineering-science close — the treatise the trade's teaching standardized on into the twentieth century.
