## Notes

> Robert Francis Fairlie's Railways or No Railways — the gauge controversy argued as engineering: the narrow gauge against the broad, the curves and the gradients, the double-bogie locomotive and its adhesion, the cost of construction and the economy of the light railway. The 1872 book is the technical core of the gauge controversy that Brunel's broad gauge had opened, and the locomotive designer's case for the railway the terrain could afford.
