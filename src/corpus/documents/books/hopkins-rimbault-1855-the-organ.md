## Notes

> Edward John Hopkins and Edward Francis Rimbault's The Organ — the instrument's history and construction in one treatise: the pipes and their scales, the wind supply and the bellows, the chests and the actions, the stops and their voicing, with the builders' memoirs and the specifications of the great instruments. The organ builder's own reference, and the largest acoustic machine of the century documented in full.

> The 1855 first edition and the 1870 reissue are scanned.
