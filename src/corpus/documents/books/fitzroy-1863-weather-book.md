## Notes

> Robert FitzRoy's Weather Book — forecasting argued as a practical service: the barometer and its fall, the storm warnings and the signals, the weather charts and the rules for reading them, and the case for a public forecast made from observations telegraphed to a centre. The founding text of public weather forecasting, written by the man who ran the Board of Trade's service.

> Two 1863 printings are scanned.
