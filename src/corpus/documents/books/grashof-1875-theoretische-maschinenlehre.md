## Notes

> Franz Grashof's Theoretische Maschinenlehre — the machine taught as applied mechanics: the kinematics of the parts, the forces and the friction, the strength of the members, and the theory of the engine and the mill, in the systematic German form. The theory behind the drawing office, and the German counterpart of the English engine manuals the guide holds.

> Several volumes of the 1875 work are scanned; the set is one publication project and one entry.
