## Notes

> Alexander Ross Clarke's Geodesy — the Clarendon Press volume of the Ordnance Survey's principal geodesist: triangulation, the measurement of base lines, the figure of the earth from an empire's networks, and the geodetic reference curves the mapping sciences worked from. The practical geometry beside Helmert's physical theories.
