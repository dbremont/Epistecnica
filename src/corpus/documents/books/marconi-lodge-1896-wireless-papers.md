## Notes

> The Marconi and Lodge wireless papers and patents of 1896–99 — syntonic telegraphy worked out: the tuned circuit, the aerial and earth, and the selectivity that let several stations share the ether. Lodge's syntonic work and Marconi's practical system, printed at the boundary of the guide's window and marking where the electrical branch left it.

> Honest no-scan: the papers are journal and patent records; the entry collects them and names the series.
