## Notes

> Samuel Charles Brees's Railway Practice — the working plans and the contracts of the new lines: the cuttings and the embankments, the bridges and the viaducts, the stations and the tunnels, with the specifications and the quantities as the engineers drew them. The pattern book the railway contractor and the engineer actually used, and the working-drawing layer beneath the railway treatises.

> The 1839 appendix, the 1840 second series and the 1859 collection are scanned.
