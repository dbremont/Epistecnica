## Notes

> Charles Wyville Thomson's account of the Challenger voyage — the deep sea sounded, sampled and mapped: the sounding and the dredge, the temperatures at depth, the deposits and the creatures, and the physical and biological results of a voyage designed to measure an ocean. The preliminary account of the expedition whose reports founded oceanography as a measurement science.

> The 1878 preliminary account and the 1895 report volume are both scanned.
