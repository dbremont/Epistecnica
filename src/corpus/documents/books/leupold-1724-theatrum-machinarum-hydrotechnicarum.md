## Notes

> The second volume of Leupold's machine theatre — hydraulic machinery, waterworks, mine drainage and the whole arts of raising water: pumps, wheels and the lifting systems of the German mines, carried into the plate-and-prose form the century's mine engineers could buy. The volume belongs with Bélidor's hydraulic architecture as one of the hydrotechnical science's early systematic accounts.
