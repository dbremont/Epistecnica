## Notes

> Maria Rundell's New System of Domestic Cookery — the domestic kitchen organized by economy and method: the receipts arranged by kind, the marketing and the preserving, the quantities and the seasons, and the household management that surrounded the cooking. The book that set the pattern the nineteenth-century household manual followed, and the domestic side of the food processing the industrial books describe at scale.

> The 1814, 1818 and 1860 printings are scanned; the first edition is 1806.
