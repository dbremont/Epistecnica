## Notes

> John Fowler's steam-ploughing patents — the cable-and-winch system that made steam cultivation a field practice: the two engines working a balance plough across the field, the tackle and the windlass, and the patents that fixed the arrangement the trade then ran. The patent layer of the agricultural machinery the guide's implement books describe from the maker's side.

> Honest no-scan: the specifications are patent-office records rather than a scanned treatise. The surviving company record is the John Fowler & Co. steam-cultivating machinery catalogue of 1909, entered at the boundary elsewhere in this section.
