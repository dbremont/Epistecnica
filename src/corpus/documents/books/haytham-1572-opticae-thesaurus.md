## Notes

> Ibn al-Haytham, the Arab physicist who reasoned about vision and lenses, carried the camera obscura and the spherical mirror build, and took the apparatus-tray of the Arabic optics into the lens trades and the Kepler optical science. The printed Risner edition carried the Arabic optics into European instrument practice.
