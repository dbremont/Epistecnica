## Notes

> Pierre-Simon Fournier's Manuel typographique — type founding and printing described by the man who cast the letters: the punches, the matrices and the mould, the founts and their proportions, and the point system that gave printing its first rational measurements. The typefounder's own book, beside Moxon's English exercises, and the continental side of the printing trades the guide holds.

> The 1764 first volume and the 1766 second are scanned.
