## Notes

> Viollet-le-Duc's Dictionnaire raisonné de l'architecture française — the medieval building explained by its construction: the vault and the rib, the timber roof and the truss, the iron tie and the hinge, the window and the flue, and the reasoning by which each form answered a structural problem. The 1875 volume is part of the dictionary that gave the Gothic revival its rational construction manual, and the architectural counterpart of the engineer's treatise.

> Volume 5 of the 1875 edition is scanned.
