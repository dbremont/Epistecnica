## Notes

> Robert Grimshaw's The Miller, Millwright and Millfurnisher — the mill trade in one volume: the miller's practice with wheat and flour, the millwright's construction of the gearing and the shafting, the millfurnisher's catalogue of stones, silks and machines, and the new roller mills that were displacing the millstone. The book documents the mill at the moment its machinery was being rebuilt around the roller process.
