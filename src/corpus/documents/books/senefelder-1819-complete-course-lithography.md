## Notes

> Alois Senefelder's Complete Course of Lithography — the process described by its inventor: the Solnhofen stone, the greasy ink, the etching and the press, with the workshop detail and the history of the invention he had patented and then taught. The rare case of an inventor's own manual becoming the trade's founding text, and the technical base of the pictorial printing the century ran on.

> Three 1819 copies are scanned, including the Wellcome and the Getty sets.
