## Notes

> Antonio Neri wrote down the glasshouse as a book of recipes: the cristalline frits of the Florentine and Murano shops, the enamelled and glass of diamonds, the arts of tin and gold glasses, the making of coloured glasses by the calcined chemical stones — each set with its furnace state and its effect. The first printed treatise of the glass trade, it carried the Murano methods into the later European shops, Kunckel's German and Merrett's 1662 English versions among them. The scanned copy is the 1663 Rome reprint of the 1612 first edition.
