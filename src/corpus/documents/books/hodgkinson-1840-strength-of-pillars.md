## Notes

> Eaton Hodgkinson's researches — the pillar-strength experiments that the Royal Institution ran, written up in the Philosophical Transactions and carried into the British engineering tradition: the columns that Fairbairn's rails and bridges were built on. The experimental-founding literature closed the empirical pillar economy the iron structures ran.

> Honest record: the Hodgkinson researches live in the Royal Institution tables and Philosophical Transactions rather than a dedicated archive.org scan; the branch is entered honestly through Fairbairn's manual.
