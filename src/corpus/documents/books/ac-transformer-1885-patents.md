## Notes

> The alternating-current transformer patents of 1885–88 — the closed-core transformer and the parallel-connection distribution system, patented almost simultaneously by Gaulard and Gibbs, by the Ganz works trio of Zipernowsky, Bláthy and Déri, and by Tesla: the invention that made alternating current distributable and the central station economic. Three patent families for one problem, and the technical hinge of the electrical century's last decade.
