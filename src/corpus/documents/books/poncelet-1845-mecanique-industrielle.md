## Notes

> Jean-Victor Poncelet put the work principle — force through distance, the machine a ledger of its exchanges — at the centre of an industrial mechanics taught to Metz and published across France: the motions of mechanisms, the work spent and gained, the efficiency of waterwheels and mills, and the inward-flow turbines he re-designed around curved blades. His course is the missing middle between the work calculus of Coriolis and the engineering curricula that Rankine and Weisbach printed.

> The 1845 Traite (second edition) is scanned in two volumes.
