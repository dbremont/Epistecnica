## Notes

> William Crookes's handbook of analytical methods — the apparatus and procedures of the chemical laboratory collected by the editor of the *Chemical News*: inorganic analysis above all, the methods the analytical chemistry of the 1870s actually ran. Standing beside Fresenius's systematic instruction as the English side of the decade's laboratory literature.
