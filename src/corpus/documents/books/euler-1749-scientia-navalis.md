## Notes

> Leonhard Euler's Scientia Navalis — the ship made a problem of rational mechanics: the equilibrium and the stability, the resistance of the water, the action of the wind on the sails, and the motion of the hull under both, worked out as analysis rather than rule of thumb. The point where naval architecture became applied mathematics, and the treatise the French and Spanish schools then translated.
