## Notes

> Charles W. Hearn's practical printer — photographic printing practice as one complete manual: the printing-room's papers, baths and economy, in the same Philadelphia trade the skylight manual served. The photographic-printing literature beside Abney's silver printing — the profession's second workshop documented.
