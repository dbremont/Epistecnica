## Notes

> Sir William Fairbairn's treatise in two parts (1861 and 1863): the construction and machinery of mills — the mill building, its machinery and motors, water wheels and turbines, gearing and shafting, the whole mechanical economy of Victorian productive building. Written by the engineer whose iron ships and bridges sat beside his mill consultancy, it stands as industrial milling machinery's systematic reference beside Rankine's millwork manual.
