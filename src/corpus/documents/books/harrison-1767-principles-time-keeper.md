## Notes

> Harrison's Principles of the Time-Keeper is the published account by the Board of Longitude: the marine chronometer in its principles — the countersprung remontoire, the escapement and the temperature compensation — drawn into plates the longitude credit was finally paid to. The device carried the sea-time to the navy, and it carried the longitude into solution.

> His Description concerning such Mechanism of 1775 is an unscanned pamphlet beside the 1767 book.
