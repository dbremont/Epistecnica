## Notes

> John Cresson Trautwine's pocket-book — the field and office handbook of civil-engineering practice and calculation: mensuration, trigonometry, surveying and hydraulics carried in the coat pocket, through the editions the profession ran into the twentieth century. The civil engineer's own pocket instrument — between Rankine's manual and Fairbairn's collected practice notes.
