## Notes

> Charles Tomlinson's cyclopædia — the mechanical and chemical manufactures, mining and engineering with their illustrations delineated article by article: the useful-arts reference between the Ure tradition and the century's applied-chemistry encyclopædias. Industrial economy's own alphabet, illustrated — the successor reference the 1850s trade readers bought.
