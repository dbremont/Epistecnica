# Charles-Augustin de Coulomb (1821). Théorie des Machines Simples, en ayant égard au Frottement de leurs Parties. Paris: Bachelier.

## Notes

> The volume collects Coulomb's friction and rope-stiffness papers with the 1773 *Essai* on the maximis and minimis applied to statics — the earth pressure of the retaining wall and the friction of the arch. The edition is the engineer's access to the two laws the eighteenth century added to the machine: friction proportional to the load, and the pressure of loose earth.
