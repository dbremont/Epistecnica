## Notes

> Robert Fulton's canal treatise — the improvement of canal navigation through the small-boat system and its inclined-plane machinery, published in London by the young engineer whose steamboats the next few years would make. The canal-engineering literature's founding American document — locks, water management and machinery argued as inland communication's own system.
