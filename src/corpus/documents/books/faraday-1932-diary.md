## Notes

> Faraday's Diary — the bench notes of forty years printed in full: the experiments and the failures, the apparatus sketched in the margin, the daily record of what was tried and what happened, from the first electromagnetic rotations to the last researches. The 1932 edition is the printed form of the notebooks Faraday kept at the Royal Institution, and the raw record behind the published Experimental Researches — the closest a reader can come to the laboratory itself.

> The 1932 edition is scanned. It is a modern publication of nineteenth-century notebooks and is included as the bench record, not as a period treatise.
