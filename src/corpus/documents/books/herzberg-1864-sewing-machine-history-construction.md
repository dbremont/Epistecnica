## Notes

> Rudolph Herzberg's The Sewing Machine — the machine's history and its mechanism in one volume: the lock stitch and the shuttle, the needle and the loop, the feed and the tension, the several makers' arrangements, and the applications to the clothing trades. The first systematic account of the sewing machine as a mechanism, translated from the German and published in the decade the machine became a factory tool.

> The 1864 English edition is scanned (two copies).
