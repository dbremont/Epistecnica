## Notes

> William Sturgeon's Annals of Electricity — the first electrical journal: the electromagnet and its windings, the instruments and the batteries reported as they were made, and the experimental notices that the textbooks of the 1840s distilled. Sturgeon's own electromagnet, the soft-iron core wound with wire, was the apparatus the telegraph and the dynamo were built from.

> The 1836 first volume and the 1840 fourth are scanned.
