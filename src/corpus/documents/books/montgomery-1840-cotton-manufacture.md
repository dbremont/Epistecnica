## Notes

> James Montgomery's practical detail — the American cotton manufacture's processes documented machine by machine and contrasted with the British state of the trade: the mills' machinery, the management and the manufactures the transatlantic comparison demanded. The cotton-manufacturing literature's American counterpart to Baines's history — one industry's two economies compared.
