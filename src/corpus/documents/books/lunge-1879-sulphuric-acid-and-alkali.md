## Notes

> George Lunge's treatise — the manufacture of sulphuric acid and alkali as one industry's theoretical-and-practical record: the chamber process, the soda manufacture and the alkali trades whose processes carried the chemical industry's heaviest plant. The outline's "Manufacture of Soda" row is the alkali half of this treatise — one entry carries both claims through its volumes; the definitive later edition covered the ammonia-soda verdict explicitly.
