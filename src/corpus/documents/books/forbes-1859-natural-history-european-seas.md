## Notes

> Edward Forbes's natural history of the European seas — posthumously edited and published in 1859, the marine provinces, dredging stations and life-zones of the European seas rendered as one natural system. The marine survey sciences' founding document on the European side — its depth zones read against Maury's surface physics in the oceanographic branch.
