## Notes

> William Henry Cole's Notes on Permanent-Way Material — the plate-laying and the points and crossings described from the maintenance gang: the rails and the chairs, the sleepers and the ballast, the laying and the gauging, the crossings and the wear, and the inspection that kept a line open. The permanent-way engineer's working manual at the end of the century, and the maintenance layer beneath the railway construction books.
