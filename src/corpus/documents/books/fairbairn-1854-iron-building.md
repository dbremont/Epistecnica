## Notes

> Sir William Fairbairn's iron-in-buildings volume — the structural engineering of cast and wrought iron for buildings: the columns, beams and roofing forms whose tests the Britania tubular-bridge experiments fed, taught as building economy. The structural-iron literature beside the mills-and-millwork volumes the same author's collection carries — one engineer's construction and machinery in two books.
