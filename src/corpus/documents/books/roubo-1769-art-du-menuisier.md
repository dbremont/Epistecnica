## Notes

> Roubo put the joiner trade into four volumes in the Arts et Metiers tradition: building joinery, cabinet work and marquetry, the bench, the tools and the timber drawn across several hundred plates. The monument of the woodworking literature, read here in the 1828 Roret printing.
