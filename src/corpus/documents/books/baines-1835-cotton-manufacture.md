## Notes

> Edward Baines's history of the cotton manufacture — the machinery, the spinning and the textile manufacturing of Great Britain's greatest industry documented in the year the Factory Acts took hold: from Hargreaves and Arkwright through the power-loom economy, with the wages and the regions beside the machines. The cotton industry's own documented record inside the same literature Ure's factory philosophy ran.
