## Notes

> Johann Schreck — the Jesuit Terrentius — and the Ming scholar Wang Zheng gathered the machines of the far west into a Chinese book: the mechanical devices drawn and signed off in the synthesis of the machine book tradition — Ramelli, Zonca, and the Italian machine books rebuilt  in the Chinese reading and desk trade of the Ming.

> The 1627 collection is scanned from a deposit of the KADS book formes.
