## Notes

> Henry Marion Howe's Metallurgy of Steel — the first American systematic treatise on the subject: the blast furnace, the converter and the open hearth, the chemistry of the metal, and the testing and classification of steels, written as the Bessemer and open-hearth plants were settling into their modern form. The American counterpart of Percy's iron and steel volume, a generation later and with the metallography inside it.
