## Notes

> Georg Andreas Böckler's *theater of new machines* — plates and descriptions of water-raising machines, mills, fountains, pumps, and the mechanical arts of the German household and estate — a machine book of the seventeenth century in the Veranzio tradition but aimed at the wide German reading world: practical hydraulic engineering as a public spectacle, the mill and the pump carried to Radviller and the German lands.

> The scans below include the 1661 first edition and the expanded 1703 printing that carried the work through the century's demand.
