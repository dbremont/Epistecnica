## Notes

> Friedrich Robert Helmert's two volumes on the mathematical and physical theories of higher geodesy: the mathematical reductions of the geodetic network in the first, the physics of the gravity field — the figure of the earth from gravitation — in the second. The German discipline's great treatise, standing beside Clarke's Geodesy as the quantitative basis the gravimetry and geodesy branches share.
