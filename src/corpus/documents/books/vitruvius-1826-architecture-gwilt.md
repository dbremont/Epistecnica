## Notes

> Vitruvius in Joseph Gwilt's translation — the ten books that made architecture a technical discipline: the materials and their preparation, the orders and the proportions, the temples and the houses, the water supply and the machines, and the education of the architect. The founding text every later builder's manual cites, and the reason architecture keeps a technical canon rather than only a stylistic one.

> The 1826 Gwilt translation is scanned; the translator is the same Gwilt whose encyclopaedia the guide already holds.
