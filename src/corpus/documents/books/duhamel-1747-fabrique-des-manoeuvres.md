## Notes

> Duhamel du Monceau carried the rope trade from the hemp field to the navy cable: the cultivation and retting of hemp, the heckling and spinning, the ropewalk laid with its tension and tarring, and the cables break-tested as a matter of science. The engraving plates sit to the description.
