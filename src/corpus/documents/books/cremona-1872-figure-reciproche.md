## Notes

> Luigi Cremona gave graphical statics its geometry: the reciprocal figures — truss and force polygon as dual drawings, each fitting projectively against the other — so that the structure's form is read off its equilibrium and the drawing computer-free. His Hoepli pamphlet is the Italian form of the method Culmann founded, standard in the Italian schools and, in translation, in English offices.

> The 1872 Milan Hoepli edition is scanned (two Google copies).
