## Notes

> James Clerk Maxwell's Treatise — the theoretical frame whose equations the electrical-engineering literature the collection runs practically assumed: the field theory where the dynamo, the telephone and the lighting manuals' arithmetic lived. Entered as the electrical branch's theoretical frame — the trade books' own assumed context.

> Archive scans of the period treatise carry the Macmillan and Clarendon printings.
