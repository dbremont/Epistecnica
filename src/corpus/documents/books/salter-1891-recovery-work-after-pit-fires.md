## Notes

> Charles Salter's recovery work — the apparatus and methods of re-establishing workings after fires, explosions and accidents: respiratory apparatus, brattice and re-entry done by the mining engineers' rescue economy. The mine-safety branch's own document beside the accident-prevention literature the same decade carried. The outline's Bagot *Accidents in Mines* (1878) and Chatard *Salt-making Processes* (1888) rows fold here honestly — no archive scans verified for either; the accidents-prevention and salt-manufacture literature is catalogued but untouched.
