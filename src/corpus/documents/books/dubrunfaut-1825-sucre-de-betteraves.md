## Notes

> Augustin-Pierre Dubrunfaut's Art de fabriquer le sucre de betteraves — the manufacture of beet sugar from the root to the loaf: the rasping and the pressing, the clarification and the lime, the evaporation and the crystallization, the charcoal and the refining, with the apparatus and the yields. The 1825 book is the technical statement of the French beet-sugar industry, the manufacture that the Napoleonic blockade had forced into existence and the chemistry of sugars carried forward.
