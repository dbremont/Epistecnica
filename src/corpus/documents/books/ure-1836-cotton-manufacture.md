## Notes

> Andrew Ure's cotton treatise — the industry systematically investigated: spinning, weaving and the machinery of the cotton manufacture carried into two volumes of original engravings a year after his Philosophy of Manufactures. The factory's own machinery documentation in Ure's system form — the second book the guide's cotton shelf needed beside Baines's history and Montgomery's American detail.
