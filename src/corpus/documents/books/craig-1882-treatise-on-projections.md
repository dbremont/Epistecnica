## Notes

> Thomas Craig's A Treatise on Projections — the map projections derived as mathematics: the conical, cylindrical and azimuthal classes, the conditions of conformality and equal area, the formulae for each projection, and the comparison of their distortions. The book in which map projection became a mathematical subject in English, written for the U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey, and the theoretical companion of the surveyor's plane table.
