## Notes

> William Henry Perkin's mauveine papers and patents — the first aniline dye reported and then manufactured: the oxidation product of crude aniline, the dyeing trials, and the patents and works process that turned a laboratory accident into the synthetic colour industry. The industrial hinge of the coal-tar chemistry Hofmann systematized.

> The 1856 report is scanned as a journal article; the patent specifications are the works-side record and are not separately scanned here.
