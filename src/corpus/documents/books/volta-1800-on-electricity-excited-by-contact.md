## Notes

> The letter that opened the battery age: Alessandro Volta's announcement to Joseph Banks of the *"tension" produced by mere contact of conducting substances* — the pile of alternating zinc and copper discs whose sustained current reorganized electricity from a science of static charge into one of continuous flow. Read in London in June 1800 and published in the *Philosophical Transactions* before the year ended, it is the moment from which Priestley's history becomes an engineering literature.
