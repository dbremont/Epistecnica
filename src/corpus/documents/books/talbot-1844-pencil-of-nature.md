## Notes

> William Henry Fox Talbot's *Pencil of Nature* — the first photographically illustrated book, issued in fascicles 1844–46: twenty-four calotype plates pasted into each copy — the inventor's Lacock estate, the open door, the haystacks — each with Talbot's own text on what photography was for. The photographic processes' first published form; the paper-negative invention whose patent documents the trade ran had its public idiom here.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of an original fascicle is verified here; Talbot's 1839 Phil. Trans. disclosure of the photogenic-drawing process is scanned below, and the *Pencil of Nature*'s plates circulate in museum digitizations (one item linked).
