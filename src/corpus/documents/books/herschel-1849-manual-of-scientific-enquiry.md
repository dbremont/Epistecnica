## Notes

> John Herschel's Admiralty manual — scientific enquiry prepared for the use of the navy and travellers: the observation instructions the empire's officers carried to sea, geology and hydrography and terrestrial magnetism among the disciplines each expert contributor explained for the non-specialist instrument-handler. The geophysical branches' observation method circulated as a naval handbook — the distributed instrument program the Cosmos observatories ran ashore, carried afloat.
