## Notes

> The Descriptions des arts et métiers — each trade described by an academician sent into the workshop: the sugar boiling, the tanneries, the glassworks, the forges, the fisheries and the manufactures of France, with the plates that showed the tools and the operations better than the trade itself could draw them. The series that made the trades describable objects, and the model every later technical encyclopaedia copied, from the Encyclopédie methodique to Ure.

> Honest no-scan: the Descriptions is a large series of separate fascicules rather than one scanned set. The verified sample in the archive is Duhamel's sugar-boiling volume in its German translation (1765), linked here; the individual fascicules remain a chase.
