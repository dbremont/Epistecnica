## Notes

> Bernard Forest de Bélidor's four-volume treatise on hydraulic engineering — the machinery of waterworks, mills, fountains and pumps, computed as well as catalogued: theory and application joined with military and civil engineering practice. Bélidor trained the eighteenth-century engineering profession before there were polytechnics — the lieutenant-général's consultant, the professor whose *La Science des Ingénieurs* and the hydraulic architecture carried French engineering into textbooks.
