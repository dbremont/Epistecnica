## Notes

> Archibald Cochrane's present state of the manufacture of salt — the salt works analysed and a new method proposed: the brine, the pans and the fuel, the duties and the prices, and the refining process he had tried on his own works. The chemical manufacturer as reformer, by the same Earl of Dundonald who wrote on agriculture and chemistry, and the pamphlet layer of the alkali-and-salt branch the guide's Lunge covers at industrial scale.
