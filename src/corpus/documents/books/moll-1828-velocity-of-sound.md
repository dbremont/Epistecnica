## Notes

> Gerrit Moll's paper on the polar sound-velocity experiments — Parry's and Foster's measured velocities from the Arctic expeditions examined against the theory that they were meant to certify: the temperature and wind corrections the phase disturbance demanded. The earliest sound-velocity literature of the planet's extremes, and the reason its three numbers carried instrument corrections into the century's acoustics.
