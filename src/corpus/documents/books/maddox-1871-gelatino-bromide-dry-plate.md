## Notes

> Richard Leach Maddox's 1871 communication — the gelatino-bromide plate proposed: silver bromide suspended in gelatine, coated on glass and dried, so that the photographer no longer had to carry a dark tent and a wet plate into the field. The short paper on which photography became portable and industrial, and the process the plate manufacturers turned into the dry plate of the 1880s.
