## Notes

> John Herschel's 1840 paper — the action of light on silver and other substances mapped: the sensitiveness of the salts, the effect of the spectrum's several colours, the fixing agents that made an image permanent, and the processes he named photography, positive and negative, and the cyanotype. The paper in which photography got its vocabulary, and the experimental foundation on which the fixing and the iron processes rested.

> The 1840 paper is scanned in two copies.
