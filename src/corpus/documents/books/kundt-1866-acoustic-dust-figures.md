## Notes

> August Kundt's dust-figure paper — the tube method: standing waves made by fine dust in a vibrating tube, from which the speed of sound in solids and gases was determined, 1866. Acoustic measurement's own method invention — the Kundt tube, whose apparatus the physics laboratories bought as instrument and whose procedure the experimental acoustics texts taught.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1866 Annalen paper is verified here; it is catalogued in the journal and the chase is open.
