## Notes

> James Dewar's 1900 paper on liquid hydrogen — the production of the liquid by the regenerative cooling of hydrogen under pressure, the vacuum-jacketed vessel that held it, the boiling point and the behaviour of substances at the lowest temperatures then reached, and the apparatus that made the quantity. The paper in which the low-temperature engineering that Faraday's tubes began reached liquid hydrogen, and the flask that carries Dewar's name became an instrument of the laboratory.

> The 1900 Science article is scanned (JSTOR). It stands at the guide's 1900 boundary and records the 1898 liquefaction.
