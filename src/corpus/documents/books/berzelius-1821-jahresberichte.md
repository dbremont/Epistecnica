## Notes

> Berzelius's annual reports — the yearly survey of the physical sciences that made a fast-growing literature followable: the papers abstracted, the results weighed, and the critical judgments that gave the field its direction. The review instrument the applied chemist used to keep up, and the model the later abstract journals copied.

> Honest no-scan: the series is a periodical run rather than one scanned book; the entry names the instrument and leaves the volume chase open.
