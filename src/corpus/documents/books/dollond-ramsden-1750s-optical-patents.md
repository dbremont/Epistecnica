## Notes

> The Dollond and Ramsden patents and papers — the achromatic lens and the dividing engine: the crown-and-flint combination that removed the colour from the telescope, and the engine that divided circles and rulers to a precision no hand could reach. The two instruments the optical and mathematical trades were rebuilt on, and the point at which the instrument maker appears as an inventor.
