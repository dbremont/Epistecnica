## Notes

> Xu Guangqi's Nong Zheng Quan Shu — the administration of agriculture as a technical subject: the irrigation works and the water control of the northern plains, the cultivation of grain and cotton, sericulture, the tools and the works, the famine relief and the food plants, and the astronomical calendar's place in the farming year. Written in the late Ming and printed after Xu's death, it is the statecraft-half of the Chinese technical literature beside the Tiangong Kaiwu's workshop-half, and the treatise whose scope the European farm manuals of the same decades did not attempt.

> The 1909 Shanghai printing (eight volumes) is scanned.
