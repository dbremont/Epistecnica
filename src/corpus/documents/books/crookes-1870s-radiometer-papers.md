## Notes

> William Crookes's radiometer and vacuum papers — the light-mill and the discharge tube: the vacuum pump pushed further, the phenomena at low pressure described, and the instrument that made a residual gas visible. The vacuum-technology layer of the instrument branch, and the apparatus the later lamp and tube trades inherited.

> Honest no-scan: the papers are society reports; the entry collects them.
