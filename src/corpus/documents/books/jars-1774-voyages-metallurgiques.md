## Notes

> Gabriel Jars's metallurgical voyages — the European mines and works inspected in person and reported for the French administration: the German forges, the English steel and tin-plate works, the furnaces and the processes recorded as they were run. The comparative survey the French metallurgical corps built its practice on, and the travelling counterpart of the desk manuals.

> All three volumes are scanned; the plates carry the works the text describes.
