## Notes

> William Mitchell Gillespie's Manual of Road-Making — the road and the railway taught to the American engineer: the location and the gradients, the macadam and the paved and the plank road, the drainage and the bridges, and the European practice transferred to American conditions with the costs and the specifications. The American textbook that made road-making a branch of civil engineering, and the classroom form of the Telford and MacAdam method.

> The 1847 first edition and the 1855 printing are scanned.
