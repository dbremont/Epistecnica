## Notes

> De Volson Wood's Thermodynamics, Heat Motors, and Refrigerating Machines — the heat engine and the refrigerating machine worked as one subject: the Carnot cycle, the properties of the working fluid, the coefficient of performance, the ammonia and carbonic-acid machines, and the calculation of the plant. The 1889 treatise gives the refrigeration engineer the cycle arithmetic the ice works and cold stores depended on, and the theoretical bridge from the engine to the low-temperature machine.
