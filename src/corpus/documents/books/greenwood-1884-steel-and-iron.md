## Notes

> William Henry Greenwood's steel-and-iron book — the practice and theory of every manufacturing method its production ran on: blast furnace through the Bessemer converters and open-hearth plants, with the testing and the mill practice in one account. Written in the decade the basic process settled the phosphorus question, it is the period's iron-and-steel production reference on the American side.
