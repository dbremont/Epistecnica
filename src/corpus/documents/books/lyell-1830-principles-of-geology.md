## Notes

> Charles Lyell's Principles — the three volumes of 1830–33 whose uniformitarian argument restructured the earth sciences: geological processes documented through their present-day physics, stratigraphy and field observation set as a discipline's method rather than its catalogues. The geological-surveying branch's great theoretical reference — standing beside Phillips's and Ansted's manuals as its own science's argument.
