## Notes

> Christian Friedrich Schönbein's gun-cotton disclosures — the cellulose nitration he announced from Basel in 1846: the chemistry that made guncotton industrially significant, its preparation publicized through the learned societies in the same year it was patented. The first industrial cellulose chemistry, whose derivatives the later fibre and dye economies would inherit.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1846 contributions has been verified here; the announcement lived in the Naturforschende Gesellschaft's reports and the Philosophical Magazine disclosures, and the chase is open.
