## Notes

> Robert Mallet's 1846 paper on the dynamics of earthquakes — the observed phenomena reduced to wave motion in solids and fluids: the velocity estimated from the damage and the time of shaking, the direction inferred from the fallen walls, and the first attempt to make seismology a measurement rather than a chronicle. The methodological founding of the branch, sixteen years before the Neapolitan report the guide already holds.
