## Notes

> Joel Munsell's chronology — paper and paper-making's historical development dated as the industry's own register: the origin, progress and the American mills' record kept by the Albany printer-historian whose publishing house printed the book. The historical side of the papermaking literature beside Herring's practical account — one trade, its history and its process in two books.
