## Notes

> Albert J. T. Morris's treatise on meteorology — the instruments of atmospheric science explained as one branch of the physics: the barometer, thermometer, hygrometer, anemometer and the rest of the observatory's equipment, carried into the general meteorology of the instrument decade. An intermediate document between the trade's manuals (Adams, Negretti & Zambra) and the observatory methods treatises (Abbe).
