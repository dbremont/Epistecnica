## Notes

> Rankine's civil-engineering manual — earthwork, foundations, masonry, roads, railways and waterworks, the surveying and the estimating that the profession's site work ran on, computed and systematized for the men building Victorian Britain's infrastructure. The companion to the applied-mechanics manual and the millwork and steam-engine manuals' civil cousin.
