## Notes

> Squire Whipple, canal builder of Utica, made the truss a computed structure: the members of his bowstring arch and the Whipple truss determined as clean tensions and compressions by the statics of the outline, the strains traced panel by panel to their diagonals and chords. His Work is the founding American bridge analysis — the general structural theory of trusses, followed by his enlarged treatises and by Haupt's —

> The 1847 Utica first edition is scanned; the 1873 enlarged treatise carries the theory to professional form.
