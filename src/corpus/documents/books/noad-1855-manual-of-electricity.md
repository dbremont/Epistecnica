## Notes

> Henry Minchin Noad's manual — the electrical sciences and their apparatus in one teaching volume: galvanism, magnetism, diamagnetism, electro-dynamics and the electric telegraph as one mid-century literature, written by the chemist whose demonstrations ran inside the Royal Polytechnic Institution. The instruction volume between the 1840s demonstration tradition and the telegraph machinery manuals the next generation bought.
