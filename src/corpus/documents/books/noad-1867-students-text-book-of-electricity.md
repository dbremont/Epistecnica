## Notes

> Henry Minchin Noad's student text-book — the electricity instruction renewed for the telegraph trade's post-war decades, the Lockwood series' own electrical register beside the held 1855 Manual. Two generations of Noad's teaching — one manual from the galvanism decade, one text-book from the telegraph commerce.
