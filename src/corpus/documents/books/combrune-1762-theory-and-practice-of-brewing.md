## Notes

> Michael Combrune's brewing treatise — the first systematization of the brewer's art against its thermometer: mashing, boiling, fermentation and the body of the process tied to temperatures the instrument could certify rather than rules the hand could only estimate. Its theory-practice pairing is the earliest form of what food technology would later call process control, written while brewing was still a London common-brewery craft.
