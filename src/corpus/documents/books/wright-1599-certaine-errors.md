## Notes

> Edward Wright worked out the Mercator chart as mathematics: the meridians of the chart spaced by the mathematical table of meridional parts he computed, the secant method printed with the demonstration that the navigator could follow. Certaine Errors is the founding printed account of the Mercator projection — and it carried from the manuscript of Mercator the plate to the English sea trade.

> The 1599 Dawson edition is scanned.
