## Notes

> Wheaton B. Kunhardt's account — the mineral processing and ore concentration machinery of the European mining districts written for the American engineer: the crushing, jigging and dressing practices as a documented comparative economy. The ore-dressing literature beside the mining-machinery shelf — concentration as its own documented trade.
