## Notes

> James Finlay Weir Johnston's Durham chair asked geology to sit beside chemistry in the field account: soils read from the rocks beneath them and from the chemistry on top of them. The first edition of 1842 ran through editions into the 1850s (the year the guide's priorities table cites) — the link between geology, soil composition and agricultural production that the agricultural-chemistry stratum needed between Liebig's polemic and Johnson's crop treatises.
