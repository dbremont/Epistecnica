## Notes

> Robert Henry Thurston's manual — boiler engineering as a teachable branch: design, construction and operation carried by the Sibley College professor whose engineering-teaching system the American schools adopted. The boiler branch's own professional manual — where Barr's practical economy became engineering instruction.
