## Notes

> David Brewster's The Stereoscope — binocular vision explained and the instrument built: the theory of the single image from two, the lenticular and the reflecting stereoscope, the camera and the mounting, and the applications to art and education. The book in which the stereoscope became both a theory of vision and an industry, and the optical companion of the photographic studios.

> Three 1856 copies are scanned.
