## Notes

> Charles Wheatstone's Scientific Papers — the bridge, the rheostat, the chronoscope and the telegraph instruments collected in one volume: the measuring apparatus that made the telegraph workable and the laboratory's electrical quantities comparable. The collection that shows the instrument and the system as one trade.
