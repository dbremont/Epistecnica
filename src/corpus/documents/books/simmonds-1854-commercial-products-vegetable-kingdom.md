## Notes

> Peter Lund Simmonds's compendium of the vegetable kingdom's commercial products — the sugars, starches, oils, fibres and food-stuffs of the world's plant economies carried into one reference: what each raw material was, where produced, what its manufactures and trades were. Sugar and the agricultural commodities read as a single international economy in the decade of the repeal and free-trade commerce.
