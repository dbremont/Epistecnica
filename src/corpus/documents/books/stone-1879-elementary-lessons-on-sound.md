## Notes

> William Henry Stone's lessons — science instruction in the Macmillan elementary series: the experiments and laws of sound for the ordinary student, written within a year of Rayleigh's treatise and translating it into a boy's and teacher's program. The sound discipline's own teaching tradition, seen from the syllabus rather than from the laboratory bench or the mathematics.
