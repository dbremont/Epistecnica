## Notes

> George Cayley's Practical Remarks on Aerial Navigation — the principles of the 1809–10 Nicholson's Journal papers restated for the 1830s reader: the cambered wing and its lift, the separation of the sustaining surface from the propelling power, the tail for stability, and the fixed-wing glider as the machine to build. The pamphlet was the aeroplane's founding statement in the years before experiment caught up with it.

> Honest record: the 1809–10 papers themselves are not verified in an archive scan here; this 1837 pamphlet is the scanned statement of the same principles.
