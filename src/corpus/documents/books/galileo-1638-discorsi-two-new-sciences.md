## Notes

> The Discorsi is the book in which the strength of materials was born: the cantilever beam loaded and broken, the imagined cross-section taken along its length, the greater strength of a shorter timber compared, the same beam laid and held so its weight is one of pure length — the first sustained attempt to make a structural member a computed proposition rather than a craft rule. Its second new science is the law of uniformly accelerated fall, the kinematics the whole machine theory later assumed.

> The Leiden Elzevir edition of 1638 is scanned. Its strength of materials descends through Mariotte, Bernoulli and Coulomb to the profession manuals of the 19th century.
