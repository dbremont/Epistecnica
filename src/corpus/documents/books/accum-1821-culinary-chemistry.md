## Notes

> Friedrich Accum's Culinary Chemistry — cookery explained by the chemical principles: the changes that heat and water work on food, the fermentation and the preservation, the pickles and the conserves, and the reasons behind the recipe. The first book to give the cook the chemistry of the kitchen, by the same Accum who exposed the adulterations of the London trade.

> Two 1821 copies are scanned from the Wellcome set.
