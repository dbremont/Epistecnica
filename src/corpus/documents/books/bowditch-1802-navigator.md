## Notes

> Nathaniel Bowditch's practical navigator — the epitome of navigation and nautical astronomy whose table corrections made the American trade's own standard: arithmetic run at sea by the Salem mathematician, correcting the Hamilton Moore original until the tables became the profession's certainty. The *Bowditch* the American merchant service still names for its standard — navigation's grounding arithmetic beside Norie's English trade book.
