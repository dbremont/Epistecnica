## Notes

> Samuel Clegg's treatise — the manufacture and distribution of coal-gas as one supply economy: gasworks, retorts, purification, gas holders and the distribution arithmetic, written by the gas engineer whose installations the industries ran. The gasworks branch's founding treatise beside Accum's gas-light reform treatise the collection holds.
