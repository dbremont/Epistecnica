## Notes

> Robert Hooke's Micrographia — the microscope turned on the small world and reported in plates: the point, the flea, the cork's cells, the needle's edge, and the instrument itself described as it was used, with the illumination and the preparation that made the observations possible. The instrument branch's founding document, and the book that taught a trade how to see.

> The 1665 first edition and the 1667 printing are scanned; the 1745 Micrographia restaurata carries the plates.
