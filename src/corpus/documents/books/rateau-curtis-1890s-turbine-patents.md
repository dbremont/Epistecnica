## Notes

> The Rateau and Curtis steam-turbine patents — the multi-stage pressure turbine and the velocity-compounded wheel: the stage theory made practical, the steam expanded in steps so that the blade speeds stayed within the strength of the rotor. The design layer that turned the turbine of the 1880s into the efficient prime mover of the twentieth-century station.
