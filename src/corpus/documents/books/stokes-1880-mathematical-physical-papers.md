## Notes

> George Gabriel Stokes's Mathematical and Physical Papers — the collected papers of the Cambridge physicist: the wave theory of light and the diffraction, the motion of fluids and the viscosity, the pendulum and the elastic solid, and the acoustic papers on the communication of vibrations and the resonance of the air. The collected papers give the acoustics of the century its mathematical foundation, and the theoretical voice beside the instrument makers.

> The first volume (1880) is scanned; the collection runs to several volumes.
