## Notes

> John W. Urquhart's Sewing Machinery — the practical manual of the sewing machine: its construction and adjustment, the shuttle and the bobbin, the tension and the feed, the care and the repair, and the varieties of machine for the factory and the household. The book in which the sewing machine is treated as a maintained tool, with the fitter's knowledge the trade needed.

> The 1881 edition is scanned (two copies).
