## Notes

> Thomas Frederick Hardwich's manual — the photographic chemistry of the collodion decade as one laboratory instruction: the silver baths, the sensitized chemistry and the manipulation, written by the King's College demonstrator whose process editions the trade ran. The photographic-chemistry literature's professional textbook between Le Gray's French traite and the 1870s manuals.
