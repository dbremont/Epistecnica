## Notes

> Edward Knight's three-volume dictionary described and drew the American machine economy alphabet by alphabet: every tool, instrument, machine, process and patent device of the 1870s carried in the text and several thousand engravings — the Centennial generation's record of its own apparatus, built from the patent documents the office held.
