## Notes

> Friedrich Georg Wilhelm Struve's Arc du Méridien — the great meridian arc measured across Russia: the triangulation from the Danube to the Arctic, the baseline and the azimuth, the stations and the observers, and the figure of the earth determined from the ground over a quarter of the globe's circumference. The largest geodetic measurement of the century, and the model of the international arc the geodesists then proposed.

> Two volumes of the 1857–60 work are scanned.
