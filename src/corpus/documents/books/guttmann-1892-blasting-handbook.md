## Notes

> Oscar Guttmann's blasting handbook — the use of the explosives whose manufacture his held treatise documented: charging, firing and the rock-work economy of mines, tunnels and quarries, carried for the engineers who bought the powders. The blasting trade's own professional handbook, beside the Rudimentary founding volume of 1849.
