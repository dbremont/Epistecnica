## Notes

> Augustin Bernard Mouchot's La chaleur solaire et ses applications industrielles — solar heat collected and applied: the concentrating mirrors, the boiler and the still, the pumping and the cooking, and the arithmetic of what the sun could deliver against coal. The first systematic solar-power treatise, written when the question was already the cost of fuel, and the honest beginning of a branch that waited a century for its industry.

> The 1869 first edition is scanned.
