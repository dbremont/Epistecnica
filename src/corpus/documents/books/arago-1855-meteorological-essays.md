## Notes

> François Arago's Meteorological Essays — the atmosphere and its phenomena explained: the composition and the pressure, the winds and the trade winds, the storms and the waterspouts, the dew point and the rain, the barometer and the thermometer, and the observations on which the account rested. The 1855 English translation by Edward Sabine is the French observatory's meteorology made a public science, and the meteorological volume beside the astronomical and magnetic work of the century.
