## Notes

> Thomas Ewbank's account — the pumps and water-lifting mechanisms of the ages described and placed in history: from the ancient machines to the patent-era devices the author himself invented, written by the United States patent commissioner whose own hydraulic engine made the machines practical. Hydraulic devices carried one literature — the descriptive-patent tradition the water-developments branch runs on.
