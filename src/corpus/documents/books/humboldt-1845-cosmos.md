## Notes

> Alexander von Humboldt's Cosmos — the five-volume sketch of a physical description of the world, the terrestrial magnetism, meteorology, geography and their instruments set in one physical system. The geophysical instrument sciences of this collection all descend from its programmatic chapters: observatories, magnetic surveys and the comparative physics of the earth as a whole. The century's magnetic observatory campaigns were its direct product.
