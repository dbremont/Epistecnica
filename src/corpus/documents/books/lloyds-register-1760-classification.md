## Notes

> Lloyd's Register of Shipping — ships surveyed and classed: the hull and the rig inspected by the surveyor, the character assigned by condition and construction, and the class printed in a book the underwriter and the charterer both read. The institution that standardized ship construction from the outside, turning a wooden hull's condition into a public record and a price, and the classification rules the iron ship then had to satisfy.

> Honest no-scan: the early Register Books are rare and not resolved as scans; the entry stands on the series and the chase is open.
