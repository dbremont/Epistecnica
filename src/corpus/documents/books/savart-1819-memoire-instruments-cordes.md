## Notes

> Félix Savart's memoir on the construction of stringed and bowed instruments — the violin body and the string measured: the resonances of the plates, the bow's action and the contact point, and the instrument analysed as an acoustic system rather than a craft tradition. The first scientific account of instrument construction, and the paper the violin makers ignored and the physicists quoted.

> Honest no-scan: the memoir is an Academy publication; the entry stands on the bibliographic record and the chase is open.
