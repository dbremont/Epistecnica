## Notes

> William Benjamin Carpenter's microscope — the revelations as the century's largest-selling microscopy reference: microscopic investigation taught from instrument to observation to the religious and philosophical reading of the small world, run through edition after edition across Carpenter's lifetime. Optical investigation as literature — the microscope's own place in the period's sciences and its public.
