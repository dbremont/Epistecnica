## Notes

> Joseph Gwilt's encyclopædia — architecture historical, theoretical and practical in one reference: the orders, the construction and the architects' practice carried for the profession. The architecture-literature's own dictionary beside Cresy's civil-engineering volume — the built-works art in one alphabet.
