## Notes

> Edwin Chadwick surveyed the labouring population at its suppurations: fever, damp, cellar dwellings, stagnant courts — and drew the conclusion that made modern civil engineering: that drainage and water supply are public works, best performed by glazed tubular sewers carrying the waste from the house. The Report is the founding sanitary-administrative document behind the water-carriage systems Latham and the metropolitan boards of the second half of the century built.

> The 1842 Poor Law Commissioners Report is scanned; a second copy from the Scottish inquiry also resolves.
