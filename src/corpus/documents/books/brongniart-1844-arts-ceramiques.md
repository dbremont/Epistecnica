# Alexandre Brongniart (1844). Traité des Arts Céramiques, ou des Poteries. Paris: Béchet jeune.

```bibtex

  title={Traité des arts céramiques ou des poteries considérées dans leur histoire, leur pratique et leur théorie},
  author={Brongniart, Alexandre},

  publisher={Paris: Béchet jeune},
}
```

## Notes

> The director of Sèvres wrote the ceramic treatise as chemistry, kiln practice and history at once: the pastes, the glazes, the firing curves, the kilns and the potteries of Europe and Asia. It is the book from which the nineteenth-century ceramic industry argued its materials.
