## Notes

> Sir Henry Parnell's Treatise on Roads — road construction argued with the evidence of the survey: the principles of the road, the plans, specifications and contracts Telford used, the costs and the administration, and the case for a national road policy. The book that made road building a question of public administration as well as engineering, and the parliamentary companion of the Telford programme.

> The 1833 first edition and the 1838 second are scanned.
