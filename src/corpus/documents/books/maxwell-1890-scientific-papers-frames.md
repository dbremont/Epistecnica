# James Clerk Maxwell (1864). On the Calculation of the Equilibrium and Stiffness of Frames. [In The Scientific Papers, 1890].

## Notes

> Maxwell worked the framework in a single paper: the statics of a truss, its determinacy counted by its members and joints, the forces traced by a reciprocal diagram, the stiffness of the elastic members obtained by the energy of the frame. He gave the counting rule behind Cremona's reciprocal figures and the strain-energy idea Castigliano generalized.

> The scanned copy is the 1890 Scientific Papers volume containing the paper.
