## Notes

> The British Pharmacopoeia of 1864 — the first national formulary of the United Kingdom: the materia medica and the preparations, the weights and measures, the processes for the tinctures, extracts and solutions, and the tests by which each article was to be known. The pharmacopoeia is the legal standard that made the pharmaceutical shop a chemical works, and the book against which the manufacturing pharmacist's output was judged.
