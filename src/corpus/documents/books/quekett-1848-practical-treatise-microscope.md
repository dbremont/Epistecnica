## Notes

> John Quekett's practical treatise — microscope construction, preparation and operation taught as laboratory discipline: the object-preparation, the illuminations and the management of the instrument written by the Royal College of Surgeons's own conservator of histology. The microscope as a documented working practice — the optical instrumentation branch's bench side beside the atlas's optical treatises.
