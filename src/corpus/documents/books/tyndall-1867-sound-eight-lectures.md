## Notes

> John Tyndall's eight lectures — the Royal Institution's experimental acoustics carried into print: sound waves, flames and fountains of light in sound production, the sensitive flame and the apparatus of the demonstration theatre. The experimental tradition the guide's acoustic-instrument rows repay: where Rayleigh computes and Helmholtz measures the ear, Tyndall shows the discipline working before its audience.
