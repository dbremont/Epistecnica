## Notes

> Charles Hutton Gregory's treatise — military bridges and river passages as engineering science: the principles of construction applied where speed and military command governed the bridge as much as load and span. Bridge construction carried the structural engineer's arithmetic into its expedition form, the treatise of the Royal Military Academy generation.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of Gregory's treatise is verified here; the neighbouring military-bridging literature does circulate and the treatise is catalogued untouched.
