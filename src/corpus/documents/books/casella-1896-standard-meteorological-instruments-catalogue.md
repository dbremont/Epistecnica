## Notes

> The Casella trade catalogue — the instrument-maker's illustrated list for observatories, travellers and explorers: the meteorological, surveying and philosophical instruments a field expedition bought, with specifications and prices the trade promised. The guide's instrumentation rows read trade catalogues as the primary documents of actual commercial instruments, construction and applications.
