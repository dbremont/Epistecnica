## Notes

> Alexander Melville Bell's Visible Speech — the sounds of speech written as a universal alphabet: each letter drawn from the position of the organs that make it, the consonants and the vowels classified by articulation, and the notation that let a reader pronounce an unknown language. The beginning of speech science as an engineering discipline, and the system his son Alexander Graham Bell taught before the telephone.

> The 1867 first edition is not among the scans; the 1881 Sounds and Their Relations and the 1895 English Visible Speech primers are linked, and the entry states the edition.
