## Notes

> Andrew Ure's industrial dictionary — the alphabetized account of an industrial economy's technological composition: arts, manufactures, mines, the processes and the machines under one reference for the early-Victorian reader. The dictionary that reconstructed the technological capabilities of an industrial society for its own readers, and that the guide pairs with the *Encyclopédie* plates as the library's foundational reference pair.
