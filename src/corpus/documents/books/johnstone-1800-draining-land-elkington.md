## Notes

> John Johnstone's account of Elkington's drainage — the system by which springy and boggy land was drained with the auger and the deep cut, described so that other estates could repeat it: the survey of the wet ground, the positions of the drains, and the results on the land. The transmission form of a practice its inventor never wrote himself, and the beginning of the systematic land drainage the nineteenth century made routine.

> The 1800 first edition, the 1808 account and the 1841 mode are all scanned.
