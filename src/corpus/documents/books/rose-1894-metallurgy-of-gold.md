## Notes

> Thomas Kirke Rose, assayer of the Royal Mint, set out the gold metallurgy as a treatise: the extraction by amalgamation and chlorination, the workings of the cyanide dissolution opened by the MacArthur-Forrest patents of 1887–90, and the assay of the bullion. The scanned copy is the 1898 printing of the first edition.
