## Notes

> William Henry Preece and James Sivewright's Telegraphy — the telegraph as a teachable discipline in the Text-Books of Science series: circuits, apparatus, working practice, written by the two Post-Office engineers whose administration ran the British network. The outline's "Telegraphy of the Future" title did not verify; this is the actual Preece telegraphy text the archive holds, with the authorship the print itself carries.
