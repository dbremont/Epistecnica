## Notes

> Fausto Veranzio's Latin book of forty-nine engraved plates proposing mechanical inventions and civil-engineering works — bridges, mills, wells, the famous parachute and the first cable-stayed bridge concept — with multilingual descriptions in Latin, Italian, Spanish, French and German, aimed at the European political elite of Venice and its court networks. The first mechanical inventions publication of its kind in the Adriatic context: the proposal as a technical genre, waiting for the century's printers and patrons to carry the ideas into practice.
