## Notes

> Wallace Clement Sabine's Collected Papers on Acoustics — reverberation measured and the concert hall designed from the measurement: the relation of reverberation time to volume and absorption, the materials tested in the pipe and the chamber, and the corrective work in the halls that founded architectural acoustics as an exact practice. The boundary collection that explains the nineteenth-century halls the guide's building books describe.

> The 1922 collection is scanned; the papers themselves date from the 1890s and 1900s, which is why the entry is marked at the boundary.
