## Notes

> William Fothergill Cooke and Charles Wheatstone's telegraph patents — the needle telegraph specified as a working system: the deflecting needles, the wire and the railway installations that carried the first commercial electric signalling between Paddington and West Drayton. The specifications are where the laboratory's deflection became an instrument a railway would buy.

> Honest no-scan: the specifications are patent-office records; the entry collects them and the installations they covered.
