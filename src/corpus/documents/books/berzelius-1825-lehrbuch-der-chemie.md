## Notes

> Jöns Jacob Berzelius's Lehrbuch der Chemie — the systematic teaching the German and Scandinavian trades learned their chemistry from: the elements and their atomic weights, the notation and the analysis, the mineral and organic chemistry organized as one course. The teaching layer between Dalton's atomism and the industrial handbooks, and the reference the applied chemists cited when they needed the number.

> The archive holds several editions of the Lehrbuch; the 1825 first volume and the 1835 fifth are the copies linked.
