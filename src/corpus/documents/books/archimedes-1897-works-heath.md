## Notes

> Heath carried the Archimedean corpus into English: the *Equilibrium of Planes* with the law of the lever and the centres of gravity, the *Floating Bodies* with the stability of the ship hull, the *Measurement of the Circle* and the *Method*. The edition is the working reference of the mathematical mechanics the Hellenistic engineers inherited.
