## Notes

> The Annual Reports of the Aeronautical Society of Great Britain — the world's first aeronautical society, founded in 1866, reporting its papers and experiments: Francis Wenham's wind-tunnel work and the cambered-wing experiments, the society's tests and models, and the annual account of what the members had tried. The reports in which flight was argued as an engineering subject with apparatus and measurements.

> The scanned volume collects the 7th–12th reports (1872–77).
