## Notes

> Ernst Abbe's microscope papers and the Zeiss catalogues — the microscope designed from diffraction theory rather than adjusted by eye: the numerical aperture, the resolution limit, the apochromatic objectives computed and then made, and the catalogue that listed the resulting series. The instrument-maker's catalogue become a scientific document, and the model the optical industry followed.

> Honest no-scan: the papers and catalogues are trade and journal printing; the entry collects them.
