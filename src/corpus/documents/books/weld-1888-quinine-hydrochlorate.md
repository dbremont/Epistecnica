## Notes

> Loretta Barton Weld's 1888 patent specification for improvements in the manufacture of hydrochlorate of quinine — the alkaloid salt claimed as a chemical process: the solution and the decolourizing, the crystallization and the separation from the cinchona extract, and the yield the process promised. The specification documents the alkaloid as an industrial product rather than a galenical, and the patent literature of the quinine trade beside the pharmacopoeia.
