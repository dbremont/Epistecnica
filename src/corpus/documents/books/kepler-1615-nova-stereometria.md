# Johannes Kepler (1615). Nova Stereometria Doliorum Vinariorum. Linz.

```bibtex
@book{kepler1615stereometria,
  title={Nova stereometria doliorum vinariorum},
  author={Kepler, Johannes},
  year={1615},
  publisher={Linz: Blaeu},
}
```

## Notes

> Kepler wrote for the Austrian wine trade and its gaugers: the barrel volumes computed for the cooper and the excise officer, the volumes of the solid figures at the best proportions of the cask. It is founding mathematics of the gauging trade and the spirit trade, done at the barrel.
