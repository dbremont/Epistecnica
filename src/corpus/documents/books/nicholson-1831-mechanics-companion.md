## Notes

> Peter Nicholson's mechanic's companion — the elements and practice of the trades: tools, timber, iron, the mechanical operations from measurement to finished works, written by the architect whose carpentry textbooks the trade already owned. The mechanics' apprentice's pocket teacher at the era the mechanics' institutes organized the trade's own instruction.
