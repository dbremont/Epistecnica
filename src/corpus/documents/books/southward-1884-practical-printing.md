## Notes

> John Southward's practical handbook — the printing machinery and workshop practice of the 1880s trade carried to the art of typography: machine-presses, type-setting, the composing room and the works' economy, through the editions the trade's printers kept buying. The print-trade literature at its professional end — instruction as employment in the century's largest information machine.
