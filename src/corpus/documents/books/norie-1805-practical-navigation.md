## Notes

> John William Norie's epitome — practical navigation carried into the commercial trade's own reference: the rules, the tables and the nautical instruments' use through the editions the British merchant service ran. The navigation literature's English handbook beside the American navigator the Bowditch work established — one discipline, two trade books, both entering Ars Navalis as the shipping trade's instruction.
