## Notes

> Carl Koppe's Photogrammetrie und internationale Wolkenmessung — the photograph used to measure the ground and the sky: the camera and its orientation, the intersection and the resection, the plotting of the survey from the plates, and the international cloud measurement that put the same geometry to meteorology. The book in which photogrammetry became an engineering method, and the photographic branch that served the surveyor rather than the portrait studio.
