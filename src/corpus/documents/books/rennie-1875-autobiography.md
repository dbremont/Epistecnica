## Notes

> Sir John Rennie's Autobiography — the works of the Rennie office from inside: London Bridge and Waterloo Bridge, the docks and the harbours, the drainage and the waterworks, the contracts and the committees, and the profession's practice in the first half of the nineteenth century. The 1875 book is the engineer's own account of the great works, and the memoir beside the Smeaton and Telford reports.

> The 1875 edition is scanned.
