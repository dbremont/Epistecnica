## Notes

> Matthew Fontaine Maury's physical geography of the sea — the currents, winds and depths of the ocean charted from the logbook network the depot collected: the first synoptic oceanography, run on merchantmen's and naval vessels' observations as its instrument base. The hydrographic-measurement branch's surface-side founding text, the seas' physics read for both science and commerce.
