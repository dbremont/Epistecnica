## Notes

> Torricelli's treatise on motion carried the law that all jets issue from a vessel at the speed free fall would give from the height of the surface — the orifice law that makes the fountain, the sluice and the watermill calculable. It stands at the head of analytical hydraulics, from Mariotte and Bernoulli through the pipe and channel engineering of the later century.
