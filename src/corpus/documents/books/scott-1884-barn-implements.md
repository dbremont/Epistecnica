## Notes

> John Scott's barn-implements treatise — farm machinery and the mechanized agricultural operations of the barn economy: grinding, cutting, elevating, hauling and the mechanical power applied, in the Orange Judd format the American farm's own instruction paper ran. Written at the close of the machinery's own mechanization decade — the barn as an engineered workshop.
