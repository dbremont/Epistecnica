## Notes

> Pierre Duplais's treatise — the alcoholic liquors' manufacture and distillation systematized: European brandies, spirits and the industrial still-houses in one chemistry reference, translated by the trade's own English-language workshop. The distilling branch's industrial-chemistry reference beside McHarry's practical manual.
