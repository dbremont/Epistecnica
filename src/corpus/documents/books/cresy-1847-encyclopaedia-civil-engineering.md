## Notes

> Edward Cresy's encyclopædia — civil engineering historical, theoretical and practical, the profession's own reference beside Gwilt's architecture: surveying, construction, waterworks and the whole works alphabet carried into the encyclopædia decade. The civil profession's dictionary between Smeaton's reports and the manual series the volumes grew into.
