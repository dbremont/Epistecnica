## Notes

> Andrea Palladio's I quattro libri dell'architettura — the buildings measured and drawn by their architect: the orders and the materials, the private palaces and the villas, the public buildings and the temples, with the plates that let a builder reproduce the proportions and the details. The model of the architect's own published drawings, and the book that carried the Venetian practice across Europe.

> The 1581 and 1790 printings are scanned; the first edition is 1570.
