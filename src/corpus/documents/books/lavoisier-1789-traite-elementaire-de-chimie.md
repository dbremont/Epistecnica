## Notes

> Antoine-Laurent de Lavoisier's Tra\u00e9mentaire — the chemical revolution's founding text: oxygen against phlogiston, the conservation of mass and the nomenclature the applied sciences would carry. The chemistry instruction the industrial revolution's own laboratory ran against, standing before Davy the collection's first summation.
