## Notes

> Guilford Molesworth, railway engineer in India then surveyor general of Ceylon, packed the British civil and mechanical engineer's field needs into one pocket-book — formulas, tables and memoranda compiled for use at the works, from the 1862 first edition through many revisions. The British counterpart of Trautwine. The scanned copy is the 1886 edition.

> It sits beside Molesworth's own Indian railway work in the desk tradition.
