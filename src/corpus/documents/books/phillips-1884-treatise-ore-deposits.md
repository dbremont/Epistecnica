## Notes

> John Arthur Phillips's ore deposits — the ore bodies and their geological occurrence systematized as mining geology's own treatise: the vein structures, the lodes and the mineral deposits' formation, written by the author whose Elements of Metallurgy the collection holds. The ore-deposit literature beside the extraction sciences — the geological side of the mining branch.
