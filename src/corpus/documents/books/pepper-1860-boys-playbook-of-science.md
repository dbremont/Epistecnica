## Notes

> John Henry Pepper's playbook — the demonstration apparatus of Victorian science education, sound among the planet, chemistry and light: the lecture theatre's manipulations reduced to things a boy could build, in the Regent Street Polytechnic tradition that made Pepper's Ghost a household phrase. The demonstration trade's own literature — where acoustic apparatus sits inside a general instrument culture.
