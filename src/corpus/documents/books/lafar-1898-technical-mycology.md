## Notes

> Franz Lafar's technical mycology — the industrial utilization of micro-organisms carried into reference form at the century's close: the yeasts, moulds and bacteria of brewing, wine, vinegar, cheese and the new fermentation industries, organized as the technology of a discipline rather than its physiology. Its first volume appeared in 1898 (the guide labels it a transition into twentieth-century industrial microbiology); the English translation ran to 1910.
