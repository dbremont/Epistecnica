## Notes

> Langley of the Smithsonian Institution measured the air itself: the flat plate and the inclined plane in the steam-driven whirling table, the lift and the drag of the flying body set against the speed that proves the possibility of greater weights than air-born. The Experiments are the founding measured aerodynamics and their Smithsonian desk carried into the Wrights and the machine.

> The 1891 Washington edition is scanned.
