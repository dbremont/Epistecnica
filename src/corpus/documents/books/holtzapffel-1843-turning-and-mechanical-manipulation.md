## Notes

> The Holtzapffel turning series — five volumes issued 1843–84 from the London tool-making house itself: materials and their values, the varieties and principles of cutting, the lathe's construction and uses, hand-turning and manipulation, and the principles and practice of the mechanical trades. Workshop practice documented by the firm whose lathes defined the trade — the reference the whole artisan tradition ran its bench on.
