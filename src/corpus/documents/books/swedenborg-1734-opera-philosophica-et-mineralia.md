## Notes

> Emanuel Swedenborg's Opera philosophica et mineralia — the Swedish mineral kingdom documented as a working system: iron, copper and tin, the mines and the works, with the plates of furnaces and machinery that the Swedish board of mines had behind it. The Swedish counterpart of Schlüter's German manual, published one year apart, and the metallurgical volume of a corpus that otherwise reads German and English.

> The archive holds the Opera in the 1734 printing; the metallurgical volumes are the ones the guide cites.
