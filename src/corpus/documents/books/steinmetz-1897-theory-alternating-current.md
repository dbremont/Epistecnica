## Notes

> Charles Proteus Steinmetz, the GE alternating-current engineer, set the alternating circuit down as complex algebra: the sine wave as a vector, the impedance law, the hysteresis losses of his magnetic researches, the whole of the AC theory stated in the phasor form the electrical engineering school room would teach. The scanned copy is the 1900 printing of the 1897 first edition.
