## Notes

> Napier's Mirifici published the log table: the correspondence of numbers and the logarithms that turned multiplication into addition, the computation of the astronomer and navigator reduced to an arithmetic. Its companion Rabdologia of 1617 carried Napier's bones and the calculating rods the desk used for centuries.

> The 1614 Edinburgh edition is scanned.
