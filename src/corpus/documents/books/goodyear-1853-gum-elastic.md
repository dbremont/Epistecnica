## Notes

> Charles Goodyear's gum-elastic — natural rubber and vulcanization in the inventor's own account: the discovery, the applications and the manufacture's uses, published as the patent-holder's documented claim to the process the rubber trades ran. The elastomer literature's founding document — the materials branch's rubber economy, patent wars and all.
