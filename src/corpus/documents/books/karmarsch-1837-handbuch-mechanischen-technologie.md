## Notes

> Karl Karmarsch, founder of the Hannover Polytechnic, made mechanical technology a teaching subject in German: the machine trades, the manufactures and the tools each stated with their theory and their workshop form. The Handbuch is the German systematic technology of the mid-century, read where the French lectures and the English manuals were read.

> The scanned copy is an 1875–77 printing of the 1837 first edition.
