## Notes

> Euler's Methodus founded the calculus of variations, and its appendix De Curvis Elasticis derived the elastica — the curve of the bent elastic strip, the shape the metre-stiff member carries before it fails. His 1757 paper Sur la force des colonnes gave the buckling load of the slender column, the formula the pillar tests of Hodgkinson later measured from. Structural engineering arrived, and its uses end — no — began beneath these two works.

> The 1744 Bousquet edition is scanned; the 1757 column paper is available in the Euler archive.
