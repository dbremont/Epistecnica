## Notes

> Charles Haynes Haswell of the New York navy yard packed the rules, tables and strengths the American engineer carried into one pocket-book — the first of its line, running through editions to the end of the century. It is the American engineer's desk as a book, the reference line that ran to Trautwine and Kent.

> The 1844 first edition is scanned.
