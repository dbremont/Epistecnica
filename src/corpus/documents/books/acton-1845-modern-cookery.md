## Notes

> Eliza Acton's Modern Cookery — the receipts reduced to a system of easy practice: the tested quantities, the plain instructions, the foreign dishes introduced to the English household, and the preface that argued cookery deserved to be taught as a subject. The reforming household book between Rundell's economy and Beeton's encyclopaedia.

> The 1859, 1861 and 1864 printings are scanned; the first edition is 1845.
