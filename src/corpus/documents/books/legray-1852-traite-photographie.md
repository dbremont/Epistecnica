## Notes

> Gustave Le Gray's new treatise — the photographic chemistry and techniques of the wet-plate decade: the paper and glass processes, the waxed-paper improvement and the metallic plates, taught by the painter-photographer whose studio trained the medium's first generation. Photography's technical self-definition as one French teaching text, beside Talbot's founding English book.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1852 traite is verified here; the photographic process literatures of the following decades circulate, and the treatise is catalogued but untouched.
