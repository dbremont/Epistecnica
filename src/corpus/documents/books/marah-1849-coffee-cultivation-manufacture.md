## Notes

> W. H. Marah's essay on the cultivation and manufacture of coffee — the plantation process described from the colony: the soils and the shade, the picking and the pulping, the drying and the sorting, and the preparation that decided the price in London. The agricultural and processing side of a commodity the European trade only roasted, written where it was grown.
