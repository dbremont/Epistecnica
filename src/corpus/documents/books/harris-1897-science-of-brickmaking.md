## Notes

> George F. Harris's The Science of Brickmaking — the clay and the kiln made a science: the composition and the plasticity, the drying and the firing, the structure and the physical properties of the finished brick, and the tests that told a builder what he was buying. The materials-science text the building manuals assume, and the brick trade's own account of its product.
