## Notes

> Isaac Lowthian Bell — the Cleveland ironmaster whose firm fed the railway age — turned his blast-furnace chemistry into print: an experimental and practical examination of the furnace's capacity, written from inside the industry's largest ironworks. The chemistry of the furnace carried to the industrial scale and read against the professorial metallurgy of the day.
