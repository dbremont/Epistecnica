## Notes

> John Tyndall's atmosphere paper — sound transmission through fog, rain and temperature-surface air, studied from the fogs of the South Foreland with the trumpets and whistles that carried the result into the Royal Society's 1875 Transactions. The atmospheric section of the acoustic literature: the medium probed as an instrument in its own right, joining Airy's 1868 mathematics to the fog-signaling trade's demands.
