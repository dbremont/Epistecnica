## Notes

> The Official Descriptive and Illustrated Catalogue of the Great Exhibition — the world's industry classified under the Crystal Palace's glass roof: the raw materials and the machinery, the manufactures and the fine arts, the colonies and the foreign nations, each exhibit described with its maker and its place. The 1851 catalogue is the technical inventory of the first world's fair, and the book in which Paxton's building appears as the exhibition hall its contents required.

> The 1851 catalogue is scanned. The building itself is represented through the catalogue and the guide's Architectura section rather than by a Paxton construction account.
