## Notes

> Apsley Pellatt's curiosities — the glass trade's composition, furnaces and processes disclosed by a proprietor of the Crown Glass Company: the crown-and-plate furnaces, the chemistry of the metallic mixtures, the history of the invention to the manufacturer's own working documentation. Glasceramics' own trade-manufacturing literature at mid-century, materials-engineering's glass branch beside the metallurgies.
