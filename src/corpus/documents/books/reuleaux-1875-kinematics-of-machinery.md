## Notes

> Franz Reuleaux's theory of machines — mechanism analysis rebuilt from paired links and their constraints: the machine as a chain of kinematic pairs rather than a catalogue of devices, the notation that made machine design systematic for its time. The Berlin professor's *Kinematics* (the German *Theoretische Kinematik* of 1875) was translated by Alexander Kennedy in 1876 and reorganized an engineering discipline it had been waiting for.

> The scans below carry the 1876 English translation; the treatise sits in the same boom of systematic engineering the Rankine manuals were writing for Britain.
