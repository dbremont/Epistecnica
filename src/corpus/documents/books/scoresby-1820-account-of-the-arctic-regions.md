## Notes

> William Scoresby's account of the arctic regions — the whale-fishery captain's own record: ice, currents, meteorology and the natural history of the northern seas observed instrument by instrument through a working trade. Marine and environmental observation at its earliest systematic European form, two generations before the Challenger expedition made oceanography institutional.
