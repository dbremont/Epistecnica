## Notes

> Samuel William Johnson — Yale's professor of agricultural chemistry, later director of the Connecticut experiment station — wrote the first half of the pair that reorganized American agricultural science: *How Crops Grow* (1868) on the plant's composition, structure and life, *How Crops Feed* (1870) on the soil's part in feeding it. Published by Orange Judd — founder of the *American Agriculturist* and the era's great publisher of agricultural science for the working farmer — it is the maturing of the genre Stöckhardt's field lectures had popularized, on the American experiment.
