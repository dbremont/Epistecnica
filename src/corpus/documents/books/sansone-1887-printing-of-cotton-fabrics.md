## Notes

> Antonio Sansone's manual — the bleaching, printing and dyeing of cotton fabrics carried into Heywood's technical series: the finishing trades' processes as one Manchester economy, at the decade the steam calico print works ran the industry. The textile-finishing literature's late-century manual beside Parnell's chemistry and O'Neill's dictionary.
