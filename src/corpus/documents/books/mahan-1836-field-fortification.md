## Notes

> Dennis Hart Mahan's field fortification — the American military-engineering teaching text of the West Point chair: laying out, constructing, defending and attacking field works, the tactical site engineering the academy's engineers carried into practice. The fortification literature's American branch beside Muller's Woolwich arithmetic.
