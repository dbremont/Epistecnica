## Notes

> I. D. Ware's illustrated hand-book — the complete history and construction of the carriages and wagons the American coach trade ran, documented for the shop's own builders. The road-vehicle construction literature beside Burgess's treatise, covering the trades the wheel-carriage treatises only sketched.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1875 hand-book is verified here; a later copy circulates in specialized libraries, and the 1875 original is catalogued untouched.
