## Notes

> The working catalogue of the small parameter. Nayfeh writes the techniques as recipes with the failure mode each one repairs: straight expansion where it holds, the strained coordinate and Lindstedt–Poincaré where the expansion produces secular terms, multiple scales where several time scales coexist, averaging where a fast oscillation rides on a slow drift, and matched asymptotic expansions where the small parameter multiplies the highest derivative and a boundary layer appears.

The book's value is diagnostic rather than foundational: it teaches the reader to look at an expansion, see which term diverges, and know which technique the divergence calls for. That is why it stands behind the corpus's [perturbation method](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/mathematical/method/approximation/perturbation-method.md) note, and why it is cited for the techniques rather than for a theory of asymptotics — the theory is in the sibling [asymptotic analysis](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/mathematical/method/approximation/asymptotic-analysis.md) note.
