## Notes

> Three chapters, one design: Ramon Myers on Taiwan 1965–1981, Sun Yeung Kwack on Korea over the same years, and Tibor Scitovsky comparing the two. The design is the point — **two economies with similar endowments, similar starting dates and the same export orientation, and two different state strategies**: Korea's large conglomerates, heavy and chemical industry and foreign borrowing; Taiwan's small and medium enterprises, more cautious macro policy and higher domestic saving.

> The value for the corpus is that the comparison is **controlled rather than rhetorical**. Because the authors hold the period and the external environment constant, the differences that remain are attributable to policy and structure — land reform, the treatment of foreign capital, the credit system, the choice between chaebol-scale and SME-scale industrial organisation — which is exactly the decomposition the [developmentalism](/view.html?p=notes/social/synontic/ideational/developmentalism.md) note needs, and the one the later "East Asian miracle" literature generalised.

> Its limits are those of the genre and the moment: two successes observed after the fact (**survivorship**), a small-N design that cannot isolate causes, an emphasis on what the states did right rather than on labour, and a 1986 vantage that predates the 1997 crisis and the revisionist account of the developmental state.
