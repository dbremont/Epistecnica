## Notes

> William Whewell's mechanics — the Cambridge course's statics and dynamics, from equilibrium through machines to the physical methods by which mechanics was measured and taught, through editions across Whewell's lifetime. The mechanical-measurement content the guide's instrumentation shelf carries — less an engineering manual than a teaching discipline whose students became the century's instrument makers and its engineers both.
