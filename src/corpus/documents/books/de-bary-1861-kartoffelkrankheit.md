## Notes

De Bary demonstrated that the potato blight is caused by a fungus — the organism later named *Phytophthora infestans* — against the prevailing miasma and spontaneous-generation explanations. The book is the founding act of plant pathology: a disease assigned to a living agent that can be studied, predicted and fought.
