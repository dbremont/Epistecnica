## Notes

> Emanuel Posselt, the Philadelphia textile publisher, took the Jacquard machine apart: the punched-card loom explained card by card and plate by plate, the reader apparatus and the pattern-making described as the trade built and punched them. The book is the standard account of the punched-card loom — the machine that carries the pattern in holes and the ancestor beside which Hollerith's tabulating cards stand.
