## Notes

> Hippolyte Mège-Mouriès's margarine patent — a butter substitute designed from the chemistry of fats: the beef tallow and the milk, the churning and the crystallization, and the product offered to a French navy that needed a keeping fat. The patent record of the point where the fat economy became a formulated product, and the beginning of a manufacture the twentieth century made vast.
