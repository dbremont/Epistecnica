## Notes

> Karl Culmann of the Zurich Polytechnic turned structural computation into drawing at scale: force and funicular polygons, the equilibrium of the truss and the arch taken from the drawn line, the rotating moment — the graphical statics that became the standard bridge and framework method of the late century, rehearsed in the engineering offices and taught from this book. His method is stasis used against geometry itself.

> The 1866 Zurich edition is scanned, alongside the Google copy of the later printing.
