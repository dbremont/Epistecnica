## Notes

> Humphry Davy's safety lamp treatise — the wire-gauze flame's own chemistry turned to the miner's protection: the inventor's documented account of the fire-damp explosions the lamp prevented, revised and corrected through editions into 1825. The mine-safety branch's founding manual from the Royal Institution's own laboratory — standing beside the recovery-work apparatus the rescue economy built after it.
