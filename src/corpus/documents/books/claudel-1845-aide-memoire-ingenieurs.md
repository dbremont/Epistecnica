## Notes

> The aide-mémoire the French engineers carried across the machine and building trades: sections, tables, formulas, the strengths of stone and timber and iron, each trade's proportions in one pocket volume. First published in 1845, revised and read into the twentieth century. The scanned copy is the 1877 Dunod edition.

> It is the French answer to Molesworth and Trautwine — the desk tradition in its own language.
