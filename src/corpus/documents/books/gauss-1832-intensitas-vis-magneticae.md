## Notes

> Carl Friedrich Gauss's Intensitas — the 1832 Latin memoir reducing terrestrial magnetic intensity to absolute measure: length, mass and time as the only units the measurement needed. Geomagnetism's metrological charter — the Gauss absolute system whose magnetometers, observatory protocols and the Weber instruments the century's magnetic-network economy ran on. Sabine's English translation carried it into the Phil. Trans. and onto every observatory globe.
