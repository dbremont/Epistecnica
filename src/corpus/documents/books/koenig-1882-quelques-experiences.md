## Notes

> Rudolph Koenig's acoustical experiments — the instrument-maker of Paris's chair of acoustical apparatus describing the phenomena his tonometers, sirens and manometric flames measured: sound as both science and instrument economy at the discipline's experimental peak, the same Koenig whose catalogue of apparatus the branch's instrument rows carry. The maker-scientist's own systematic summary.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1882 Quelques expériences is verified here; the catalogue volumes beneath circulate and the experiences are catalogued completed.
