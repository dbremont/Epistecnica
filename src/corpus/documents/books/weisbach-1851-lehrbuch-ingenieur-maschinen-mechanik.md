## Notes

> Julius Weisbach of the Freiberg Bergakademie compressed the whole of engineering mechanics into the Lehrbuch: statics and dynamics of rigid bodies, the strength of materials, hydraulics with the resistance formulas, and the machine mechanics of gearing, turbines and prime movers — each worked to numerical form. The book became the mid-century standard text of mechanical engineering, carried into English through the American translations and used wherever Rankine carried.

> The 1851 scanned volume is one of the run of printings the century used. The English translation, Principles of the Mechanics of Machinery and Engineering, is not verified in an archive scan here.
