## Notes

> Johann von Lamont's handbook of terrestrial magnetism — the Bogenhausen observatory's director writing the discipline's German systematic account in 1849: magnetic intensity and direction, their instruments and observations, the whole network's measurement practices as one manual. The geomagnetic handbook between Gauss's absolute memoir and the observatory record-series Sabine carried.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1849 Handbuch des Erdmagnetismus itself is verified here; the author's related polymath volume — the 1867 Handbuch des Magnetismus of the Allgemeine Encyklopädie der Physik — does circulate, and the 1849 handbook is catalogued untouched.
