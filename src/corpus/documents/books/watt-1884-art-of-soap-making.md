## Notes

> Alexander Watt's soap handbook — the industrial manufacture of soaps carried to the trade's own practical instruction: hard and soft soaps, toilet soaps, the glycerine recovery the industry's by-product economy ran. The soap-and-detergent literature's Victorian reference, beside the vinegar and coatings manuals the same publisher's series carried.
