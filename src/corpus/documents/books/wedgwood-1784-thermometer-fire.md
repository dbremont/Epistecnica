## Notes

> Wedgwood's kiln thermometer: the shrink of a small hanging cylinder of the potter's clay and the temperature numeration of the kiln trade. The paper did for the kiln furnace and its measurement trade what the thermometer did for the body trade. It established low-temperature thermometer science and led to the invention of the Seger cone and pyrometric measurement.
