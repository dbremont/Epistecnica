## Notes

> Charles Bright's submarine telegraphs — the cables' history, construction and working written by the engineer whose Atlantic careers ran the trade: manufacture, laying and the maintenance the industry institutionalized. The submarine-telegraph literature's own professional treatise, one book behind the telegraphy instruction the land lines carried.
