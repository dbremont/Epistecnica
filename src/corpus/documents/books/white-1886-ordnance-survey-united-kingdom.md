## Notes

> Thomas Pilkington White's The Ordnance Survey of the United Kingdom — the national mapping agency described as a technical system: the principal triangulation and the base, the theodolites and the levels, the topographical survey and the contouring, the engraving and the printing, and the scales and the sheets. The book in which the Ordnance Survey appears as the chain from theodolite to published map, and the model of the national survey.
