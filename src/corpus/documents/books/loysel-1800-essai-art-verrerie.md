## Notes

> Loysel took the glass trade past its recipes: the alkali and lead compositions stated as Lavoisierian chemical analysis, the furnace heat reasoned from the chemistry of combustion. The first book of glass that reasons from chemical analysis rather than from the workshop formularies.
