## Notes

> John Sutcliffe's Treatise on Canals and Reservoirs — the canal and its reservoirs designed as a water system: the supply calculated from the rainfall and the locks, the reservoir and the feeder, the embankment and the puddle, and the estimates and the contracts for the works. The engineering text of the canal age at its height, and the water-side companion of the navigation treatises the guide holds.

> The 1816 edition is scanned.
