## Notes

> Nicholas Procter Burgh's treatise on sugar machinery — the extraction, crushing, clarification and evaporation apparatus of the sugar manufacture carried into a machinery book: mills, pumps, boiling, the vacuum pan and the whole equipment train from cane field to refinery. The sugar industry's technical monograph at the moment machinery was becoming the industry's economy.
