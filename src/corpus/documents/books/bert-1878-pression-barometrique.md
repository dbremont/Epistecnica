## Notes

> Bert carried the animal into the pressure chamber and measured what the barometer does to the body: the oxygen partial pressure, the nitrogen saturation and the decompression sickness of the caisson and the diver. The book founded the staged decompression the pneumatic foundations of the bridges and tunnels required.
