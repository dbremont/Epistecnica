## Notes

> George Richardson Porter's cabinet-cyclopaedia treatise — porcelain and glass manufacture's origin, progressive improvement and present state: the materials, furnaces and processes of the ceramic trades documented for the useful-knowledge audience, beside the same literature's cotton rail and gas. The porcelain-and-glass branch's own first systematic English account, ahead the Pellatt glass curiosities of 1849.
