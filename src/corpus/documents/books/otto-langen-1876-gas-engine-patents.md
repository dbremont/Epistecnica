## Notes

> The Otto and Langen gas-engine patents — the free-piston atmospheric engine and then the four-stroke cycle: the compression, the ignition and the exhaust strokes specified as a single working cycle, and the engine that made gas power a commercial machine. The patent layer beneath the gas-engine books, and the cycle every later motor car inherited.
