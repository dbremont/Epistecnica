# Isaac Newton (1704). Opticks; or, a Treatise of the Reflexions, Refractions, Inflexions and Colours of Light. London.

## Notes

> Newton developed the entire science of optics: the prism analysed the spectrum, the reflecting telescope designed for the trade, the theory of colour and of refractive dispersion. Optics — applicable to every optical trade instrument of the lens age.
