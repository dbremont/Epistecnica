## Notes

> Henry Stephens's book — the agricultural operations, implements and machinery of the farm documented function by function: ploughing, hedging, shepherding, the labour arrangements and the implements trade, arranged so a farm could be learned from print. The agricultural-operations literature's great reference of the 1840s — the economy of the farm beside the chemistry of Rothamsted.
