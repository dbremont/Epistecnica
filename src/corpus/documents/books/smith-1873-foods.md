## Notes

> Edward Smith's Foods — the International Scientific Series volume of 1873: food composition, the dietetical values and the processes that transformed them, written by the physician whose London dietary surveys measured bread and meat for the Poor Law. Foods as the food sciences' own reference — the composition analysis of the century's principal alimentary materials — standing beside the preservation and industrial volumes in Ars Cibaria.
