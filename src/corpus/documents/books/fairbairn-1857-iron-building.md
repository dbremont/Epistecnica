## Notes

> William Fairbairn's On the Application of Cast and Wrought Iron to Building Purposes — the iron girder and the fireproof floor made a building system: the beams and their loads, the columns and the connections, the floors and the roofs, and the tests that told a builder what an iron member would carry. The text that turned the mill engineer's iron into architecture's structural material, and the technical ground of the iron-fronted building.

> The 1857 first edition and the 1858 printing are scanned.
