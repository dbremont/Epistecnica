## Notes

> John Eddowes Bowman's Introduction to Practical Chemistry — the student's course laid out as exercises: the reagents and the blowpipe, the simple and the systematic analysis, and the manipulations graded so that a class could work through them in a term. The classroom layer between Faraday's manipulation and the analytical handbooks, and the form in which the laboratory entered the curriculum.

> The 1849 first edition and the 1864 fourth are scanned.
