## Notes

> Hans Christian Ørsted's Experimenta — the four-page Latin pamphlet reporting that a voltaic current deflects a magnetic needle: the single observation that opened electromagnetism, printed and circulated across Europe within months. The pamphlet every electrical engineer's branch descends from, and the shortest founding text in the guide.
