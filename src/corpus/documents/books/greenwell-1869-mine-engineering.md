## Notes

> George Clementson Greenwell's mine engineering — the geological surveying, ventilation and haulage machinery of the northern collieries in one practical treatise, written by the Durham mine engineer who gave the district's practice its teaching. Mine construction and the mine's machinery documented as one engineering system, standing beside the mining-machinery trade treatises of the same years.
