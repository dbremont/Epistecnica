## Notes

> J. Bucknall Smith's wire treatise — the drawing of wire, the construction of wire ropes and their applications carried into one industrial reference: the ropes whose suspension bridges and mine hoists the period ran. The wire-drawing branch's own treatise beside the metal trades' forging manuals.
