## Notes

> The Life of Thomas Telford, written by himself and edited by John Rickman — Telford's works described in his own reports: the Holyhead road and its bridges, the Menai suspension bridge, the Caledonian canal, the harbours and the churches, and the survey of the country that preceded them. The record of an engineer who built a public works system rather than a single work, and the primary account of the road and bridge programme the guide's MacAdam and Stevenson entries serve.
