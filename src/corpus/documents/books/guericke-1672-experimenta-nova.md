## Notes

> Otto von Guericke, burgomaster of Magdeburg, built his air pump and the experiment that carried the vacuum into the literature: the two hemispheres evacuated and their extraction drawn, the water barometer, the vacuum jar of the pneumatic machine tradition Boyle next developed in England. The 1672 Experimenta Nova is the founding treatise on the vacuum as instrument and art.

> The 1672 Amsterdam edition is scanned in a Leiden-Library digitisation.
