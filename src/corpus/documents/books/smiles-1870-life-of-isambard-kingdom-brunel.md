## Notes

> Samuel Smiles's life of Isambard Kingdom Brunel — the great railway, bridge, tunnel and marine engineering biography published in 1870, written around the engineer's own notebooks: the Great Western Railway, the Clifton bridge, the Thames tunnel, the ships. Victorian engineering biography at its largest documented scale, and the study of one engineer's system of projects.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1870 first edition has been verified here; the modern editions circulate, and the first edition is catalogued but untouched.
