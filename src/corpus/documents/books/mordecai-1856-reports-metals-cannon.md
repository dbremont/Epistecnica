## Notes

> The Ordnance Department set the metals for cannon to trial: iron, steel, bronze and the composite forms tested by the gun system of the army, the results printed by the department. The reports are the founding American metal-testing volumes and the cannon trade's material reference — the interchangeability precedent at its desktop.
