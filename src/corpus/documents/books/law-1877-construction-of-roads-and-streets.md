## Notes

> Henry Law and Daniel Kinnear Clark's Construction of Roads and Streets — the municipal street specified: the foundation and the drainage, the macadam and the paving, the wood and the asphalt, the tram rails and the kerbs, and the contracts and the maintenance that kept a city moving. The city engineer's manual the asphalt and tramway trades assume, and the street as an engineered surface rather than a graded way.

> The 1877 first edition and the 1890 printing are scanned.
