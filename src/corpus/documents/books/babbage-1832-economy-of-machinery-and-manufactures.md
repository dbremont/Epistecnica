## Notes

> Charles Babbage's audited machine economy — the book that carried the pin-factory example into political economy and the manufacturing system into analysis: machinery, the division of labour, production operations and cost, examined at first-hand factory scale. Pre-dating Ure's factory philosophy by three years, the astronomer turned manufacturing analyst documented production organization as a system — the discipline that would later carry his name into computing before the analytical engine was ever built.
