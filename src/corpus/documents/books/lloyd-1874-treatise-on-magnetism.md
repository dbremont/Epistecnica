## Notes

> Humphrey Lloyd's Treatise on Magnetism — terrestrial magnetism taught with the instruments and the observatory method: the declination, the dip and the intensity, the magnetometers and the corrections, the secular change and the magnetic survey, and the practice of the observatory that Lloyd had directed at Dublin. The manual the magnetic observatories trained their observers from.

> Three 1874 copies are scanned.
