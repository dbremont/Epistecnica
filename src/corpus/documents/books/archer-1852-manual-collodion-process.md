## Notes

> Frederick Scott Archer's Manual of the Collodion Photographic Process — the wet collodion process taught step by step: the collodion and the sensitizing bath, the exposure and the development, the fixing and the varnish, and the glass plate that gave a negative of a fineness the paper could not. The book that put the process into every photographer's hands, and the working medium of the next thirty years.

> The 1852 first edition and the 1854 collodion-on-glass pamphlet are scanned.
