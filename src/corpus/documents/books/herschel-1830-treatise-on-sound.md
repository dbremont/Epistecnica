## Notes

> John Herschel's treatise on sound — the *Encyclopaedia Metropolitana* account of the physical acoustics of the 1830s: waves, pipes, vibrating strings and the theory of the phase as it stood between the classical demonstrations and the later mathematical systems. Compiled in volume with his physical-astronomy and light articles, it is the sound discipline's great introductory account before Rayleigh's computation took the field.
