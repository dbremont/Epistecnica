## Notes

> Cautley's 1864 disquisition on the heads of the Ganges and Jumna canals — the water-supply problem argued in public: the discharge of the river at the head, the silt and the shifting bed, the demand of the canal, and the works proposed to secure it. The book in which the irrigation canal appears as a claim on a river's regime, and the engineering argument that followed the first years of the Ganges Canal's operation.
