## Notes

> The iron-and-steel volume of Percy's series — the pig from the blast furnace, the refinery, the conversion processes at the moment Bessemer's and Siemens's inventions were reaching industry: iron's furnaces documented furnace by furnace through the English ironmaking districts. The industrial archive of a decade in which iron was becoming steel.
