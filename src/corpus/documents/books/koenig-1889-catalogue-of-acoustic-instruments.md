## Notes

> The Koenig trade catalogue — the acoustic apparatus constructed by the Parisian maker himself: tonometers, sirens, resonators and the manometric flame apparatus, priced and illustrated for laboratories whose acoustics ran on them. The instrument-catalogue literature as primary document — what gave the discipline's laboratories their equipment and prices between the treatises.
