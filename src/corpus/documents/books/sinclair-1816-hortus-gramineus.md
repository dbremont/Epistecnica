## Notes

> George Sinclair's Woburn grass garden — the Duke of Bedford's experiments on the produce and nutritive qualities of different grasses and other forage crops, published at London in 1816: herbage measured species by species, the agricultural experimentation tradition's first systematic treatment of the meadow. The grasses' comparison where agricultural science later learned its method, and the reference the agricultural literatures of the 1820s leaned on.
