## Notes

> Basil Jackson's military surveying — the reconnaissance, plan-drawing, levelling and field-sketaching economy the armies taught their officers, in the edition series that ran through the 1850s. The military-surveying branch beside the held fortification volumes — the military engineering department's own surveying register.
