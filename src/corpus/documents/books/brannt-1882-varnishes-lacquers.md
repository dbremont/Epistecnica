## Notes

> The coatings treatise — volatile and fat varnishes, lacquers, siccatives and sealing-waxes in one practical manufacture, translated from the German by William T. Brannt whose vinegar and other technology manuals the collection holds. The varnish-and-coatings literature's founding reference — the surface trades' own documented chemistry.
