## Notes

> Friedrich Mohr's Practical Pharmacy, translated by William Procter Jr. — the pharmaceutical shop and laboratory described from the bench: the arrangements of the shop, the balances and the measures, the apparatus for distillation, filtration and crystallization, the preparation of the extracts, tinctures and plasters, and the manipulations by which the pharmacist made his stock. The American translation of 1849 documents the apothecary's craft as a manufacturing practice, and the book the pharmacopoeia's processes assumed.
