## Notes

> George Bartlett Prescott's telegraph engineering — the electrical science beside the telegraph system: lines, batteries, relays, the registering and duplex apparatus, the state of the network that had become American infrastructure by the 1870s. The technological-systems literature the guide sets beside the experimental literature of Priestley and Faraday: what the electric telegraph was, on the ground, as engineering.
