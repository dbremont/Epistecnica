## Notes

> Thomas Reid's treatise — the horological mechanisms of clocks and watches in theory and practice: the escapements, the train and the striking work, written by the Glasgow and Edinburgh clockmaker whose own trade the plates documented. Horology's trade literature in its first systematic Scottish form — the mechanism craft beside the instrument trades.
