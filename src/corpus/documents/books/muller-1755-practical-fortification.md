## Notes

> John Muller's treatise — the practical part of fortification as engineering arithmetic: trace, profile, the construction of works and their materials, written by the professor of the Royal Military Academy, Woolwich, whose mathematics the army engineers ran. Military construction carried as engineering teaching — the literature the guide's military rows stand beside.
