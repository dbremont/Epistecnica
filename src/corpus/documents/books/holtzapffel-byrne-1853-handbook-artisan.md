## Notes

> The artisans' handbook from the Holtzapffel tool firm with Oliver Byrne — practical mechanical construction and workshop technique in one handbook format: the pocket side of the Turning series, written for the man on the bench rather than the study shelf. Byrne's involvement appears the same decade before his famous Euclid's colours — the artisan literature's best-branded compact form.
