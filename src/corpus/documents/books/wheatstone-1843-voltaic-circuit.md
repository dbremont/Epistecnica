## Notes

> Charles Wheatstone's Bakerian Lecture — the rheostat, the diversified metallic circuit and the processes by which a voltaic circuit's constants became measurable: electrical measurement as instrumentation science in 1843. The telegraph trade's own starting apparatus answered there; measurement against the equilibrium of the circuit stands at the head of this branch.
