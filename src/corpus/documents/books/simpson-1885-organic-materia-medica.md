## Notes

> James Young Simpson's Organic Materia Medica and Therapeutics — the vegetable and animal drugs arranged against the sixth revision of the United States Pharmacopoeia: the source plant, the part used, the constituents and the preparations, the dose and the therapeutic use, and the alkaloids that were replacing the crude drug. The 1885 textbook documents materia medica at the point the isolated alkaloid became the pharmacist's active article.
