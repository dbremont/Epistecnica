## Notes

> The academicians examined the machine of the Montgolfier brothers: the hot air driven into a bag at Annonay and Versailles, verified by the Royal Académie to weigh and lift. The Rapport is the founding assessed account of the aerial machine — the aeronautical machine as a works of verifiable physics rather than as a marvel.

> Cavallo's own 1785 History and Practice of Aerostation gathered the trade beside it.
