## Notes

> Stevin — the mathematician of the Dutch defence and drainage — stated the physics of statics and hydrostatics as a book: the triangle of forces, the first law of statics, the Berlin theory of the inclined-plane, the hydrostatic paradox, the pressure-scale of the water trades and the floating foundation. His Spilsluysen of 1617 described the pound-lock drainage sluices. His De Thiende of 1585 introduced the decimal trade to the trades of measurement.
