## Notes

> Jean Nicolas Pierre Hachette's elementary treatise — the machine components and their elementary theory: receivers, communicators, modifiers, the classification of machine movements taught at the Polytechnic through the draughtsman's geometry. Mondragon's mine-school successor became the theory book an engineering generation of French mechanical arts first learned on, between the machine-books and the kinematics of Reuleaux.
