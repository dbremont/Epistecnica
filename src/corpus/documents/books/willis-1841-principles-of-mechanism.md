## Notes

> Robert Willis's Principles — mechanism theory designed for the Cambridge students between Desaguliers's and Zonca's generations: the kinematic vocabulary, the gearing theory and the machines' own parts in the teaching Cambridge ran. The mechanism-theory book between Hachette's French elementary machines and Reuleaux's kinematic pairs.
