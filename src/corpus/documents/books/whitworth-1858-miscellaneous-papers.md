## Notes

> Joseph Whitworth's collected papers — precision engineering's founding documents in one collection: the plane surface's true surface ("On the trueing of planed surfaces"), the measuring machine, and the screw-thread standardization that the British machine trades ran on. The same Whitworth whose 1841 thread paper made the *Whitworth thread* the standard's own name — precision as written literature, collected by the engineer's own works.
