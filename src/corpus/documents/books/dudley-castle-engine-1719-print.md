## Notes

> The Dudley Castle engine print — the first successful atmospheric steam engine drawn as installed machinery: the cylinder and piston, the boiler and the beam, the pump rods and the coal-fire beneath, with the engine that pumped the Dudley mines from 1712 set out in one plate. The engraving is the Newcomen engine's primary visual document, the pictorial record that stands beside Savery's printed manual and before the later treatises that describe the engine only from the outside.
