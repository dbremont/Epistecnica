## Notes

> The instrument-makers' catalogues of the nineteenth century — Troughton and Simms, Elliott Brothers, Repsold, Ertel, Pistor and Martins: the standard ranges listed with their prices and their descriptions, the theodolite, the level, the dividing engine and the astronomical instrument as articles a buyer could order. The trade's own record of what was made, and the layer beneath the instrument manuals the guide holds.

> Honest no-scan: the catalogues are trade printing, surviving in scattered runs; the entry collects the principal makers and leaves the individual catalogue chase open.
