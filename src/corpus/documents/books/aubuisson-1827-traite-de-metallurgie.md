## Notes

> Jean François d'Aubuisson de Voisins's Traité de métallurgie — the French mining corps' teaching treatise, written for the engineers of the mines: the ores, the preparatory work, the furnaces and the extraction processes, in the systematic order the École des mines taught. The French systematic layer between Jars's voyages and Rivot's principles.
