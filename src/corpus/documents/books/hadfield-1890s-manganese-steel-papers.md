## Notes

> Robert Abbott Hadfield's papers — manganese steel and the alloy steels that the 1880s and 1890s opened: the compositions tried, the properties measured, and the works-scale trials that turned the laboratory's alloys into founders' products. The alloy-steel counterpart of Osmond's microscopy and the industrial edge of the metallurgy the treatises systematized.
