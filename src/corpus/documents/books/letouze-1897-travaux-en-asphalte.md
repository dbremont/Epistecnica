## Notes

> P. Letouzé's Traité pratique des travaux en asphalte — asphalt paving specified and laid: the bitumen and the aggregate, the mixing and the compression, the foundations and the kerbs, and the municipal contracts that put the surface on the street. The road material as an engineered product, and the works the motor car would inherit from the bicycle and the carriage.

> The 1897 edition is scanned.
