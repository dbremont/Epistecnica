## Notes

> Norman Selfe's refrigeration-machinery paper — the compressors, absorbers, condensers and plant arrangements of the artificial-cold industry, written by the Sydney engineer whose bridges and public works sat beside his machinery consultancy. A technical paper rather than a book, but the primary engineering source the period left on refrigeration machinery's inventory.
