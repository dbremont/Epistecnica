## Notes

> Henry Stopes's treatise on malt and malting — barley, floor and kiln, the selection of the grain and the germination, drying and storage that turn it into malt: a raw-material-side account of the brewing industries whose process literature was written above it. Historical, scientific and practical in its own declared order, the malting trade's reference as the century closed.
