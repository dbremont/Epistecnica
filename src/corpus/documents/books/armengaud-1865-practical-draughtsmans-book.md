## Notes

> The practical draughtsman's book — the Armengaud brothers' French drawing-teaching treatise, translated and adapted by William Johnson in 1865: mechanical drawing and industrial design as a discipline of projections, skating the plan, section, detail and worked drawing the machine shops' draughtsmen learned. Technical drawing's founding manual in English — from the same French tradition the polytechnic literature carried.
