## Notes

> McLaughlin Leander Byrn's practical brewer — the brewery operations and equipment carried into the American industrial manual: mashing, fermenting and the brewery's whole plant, in the Baird series the trades ran. The brewing literature's American operations volume — the beer engine's own practical feed, between the theory books and the English process manuals.

> The scans below carry the 1852 first edition; later editions ran to 1867, whose date the outline carried.
