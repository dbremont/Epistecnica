## Notes

> Pierre Bouguer's Traité du navire — the ship treated as a floating body to be calculated: the displacement and the centre of gravity, the metacentre and the stability, the resistance and the sailing qualities, and the geometry of the hull reduced to problems of mechanics. The founding text of theoretical naval architecture, written by the geodesist who had measured the earth in Peru, and the book Euler and Jorge Juan built on.

> Three 1746 copies are scanned.
