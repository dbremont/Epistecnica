## Notes

> Edward James Reed's Shipbuilding in Iron and Steel — the metal hull designed and built: the plates and the frames and the rivets, the scantlings and the strength, the bulkheads and the watertight compartments, and the launch and the docking of a hull that no longer relied on the timber tradition. The hinge from the wooden ship to the metal hull, written by the Chief Constructor of the Navy who had fought the battle over the turret ship.
