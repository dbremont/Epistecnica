## Notes

> Alexander John Ellis's work on musical pitch — the history reconstructed from the surviving instruments, the tuning forks and the organ pipes, and the standard proposed from the measurements: the French diapason normal, the British attempts at a uniform pitch, and the cents system that let the comparisons be written down. The moment tuning became a metrological question rather than a local convention.

> The 1880 Nature paper and the Philosophical Transactions report are both scanned.
