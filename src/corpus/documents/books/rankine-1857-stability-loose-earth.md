## Notes

> Rankine carried the plastic interior of the earth against Coulomb’s wedge theory with an equal-plane theory of the earthen pressure: the equilibrium of loose earth mass behind a cutting plane. The paper founded the gears to form the great engineering science of the earth-pressure trade that engineers applied from the 1850s to the end of the century.
