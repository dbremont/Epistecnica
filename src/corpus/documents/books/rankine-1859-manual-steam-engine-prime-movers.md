## Notes

> Rankine's power-engineering manual — the steam engine and the other prime movers, treated from his own thermodynamic cycle (the Rankine cycle) down to the boiler, the engine frame, the fittings: the theory and the machinery of the age's prime energy converter in one teacher's hands, from Glasgow's chair into the profession's pockets. The guide's power-engineering shelf runs from Carnot through Farey into this book.
