## Notes

> Charles-Augustin de Coulomb's electrical and magnetic memoirs — the torsion balance and the inverse-square law measured: the force between charges and between poles reduced to a number by an instrument of his own design, and the measurement programme that made electricity a quantitative science. The experimental foundation the battery and the telegraph were later built on.

> Honest no-scan: the memoirs live in the Academy's volumes rather than a scanned monograph; the entry collects them and names the series.
