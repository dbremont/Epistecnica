## Notes

> The American Pharmaceutical Association's 1880 report on the revision of the United States Pharmacopoeia — the proposed changes laid before the convention: the articles added and dropped, the tests and the strengths, the nomenclature, and the pharmacists' objections and proposals. The report documents the association's role in making the national standard, and the working papers behind the sixth revision.

> The 1880 report is scanned.
