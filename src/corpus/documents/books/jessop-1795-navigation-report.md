## Notes

> William Jessop's 1795 report on the proposed line of navigation between Newcastle and Maryport — the canal surveyed and costed: the route and the levels, the locks and the aqueducts, the earthwork and the masonry, the estimate and the expected traffic. The report documents the canal engineer's survey and estimate as a public document, the arithmetic by which the canal age was financed, and the practice Jessop carried from the Calder and Hebble to the Grand Junction.

> The 1795 report is scanned.
