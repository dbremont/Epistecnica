## Notes

> Alexander Watt's practical treatise — the electroplater's own deposition manual: metals deposited by electrolysis for the shops that plated their wares. The outline's second Electro-Metallurgy row carries the trade's title collision honestly — Gore published an art under the same words a decade earlier, and the scans verify Watt's own text under *Electro-deposition*, the practical treatise its 1880s editions printed.
