## Notes

> Ira Osborn Baker's Engineers' Surveying Instruments — the instrument's own book: the theodolite and its circles and micrometers, the level and its bubble, the chain and the tape, the plane table and the compass, each described in construction, adjustment, testing and use. The 1892 volume documents the instrument-maker's trade at the point the survey became an engineering profession, and the apparatus the map's accuracy rested on.

> The 1892 volume is scanned.
