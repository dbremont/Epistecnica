## Notes

> The Modern Bicycle and its Accessories — the trade annual of the bicycle boom: the frames and forks of the leading makers, the hubs and bearings, the chain and the gearing, the tyres and the saddles, and the accessories that filled the dealer's counter. The volume in which the bicycle appears as an industry of interchangeable components rather than a single maker's machine.
