## Notes

> James Walker and John Urpeth Rastrick's Report — the locomotives compared by trial: the Rocket and the Novelty and the Sans Pareil weighed and timed, the rope-haulage alternative examined, and the evidence on which the railway chose steam traction. The primary judgment of the Rainhill competition, and the document that settled the locomotive as the railway's moving power.
