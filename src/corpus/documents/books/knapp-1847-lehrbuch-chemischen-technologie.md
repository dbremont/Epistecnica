## Notes

> Friedrich Knapp's Lehrbuch of chemical technology — the German Technologie tradition's own textbook: industrial chemical processes organized for instruction and self-teaching, written by the Hohenheim professor for the chemical industry's first professional generation. One of the tradition's books before Wagner's handbook popularized it in translation — the German literature the English technology books leaned on.
