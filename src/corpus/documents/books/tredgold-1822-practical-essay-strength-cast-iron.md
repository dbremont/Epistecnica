## Notes

> Thomas Tredgold's cast-iron essay — experimental properties behind the beam tables and the bridge calcs the early nineteenth century's iron structures ran on; the reference work that carried engineering from empirical construction practice toward quantitatively established material properties. Whatever its tables needed, the iron bridge and the mill building trusted them.
