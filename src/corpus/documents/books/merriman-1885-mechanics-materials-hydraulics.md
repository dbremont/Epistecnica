## Notes

> Mansfield Merriman of Lehigh set the strength of materials and hydraulics down as teaching texts for the American schools: beams, columns and shafts by the mechanics of materials from 1885, and the flow of channels and pipes by the Treatise on Hydraulics from 1889. The scanned copy is the 1895 printing of the first.

> Together the two volumes are the American teaching opposite of Rankine.
