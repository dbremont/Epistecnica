## Notes

> Carl Joseph Napoleon Balling's Die Gährungschemie — fermentation grounded in chemistry and applied to the trades: the sugar and the yeast, the wort and the fermentation, the attenuation and the saccharometer, the brewery and the distillery and the wine press. The 1854 book is the point at which brewing became a laboratory science, and the technical literature of the fermentation industries beside the domestic and agricultural manuals.
