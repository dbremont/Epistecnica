## Notes

> The anonymous dairy guide — the cow as the guidance unit of the dairy economy: breed, feeding, milk, butter and the smallholder's own cattle economics, written for the reading farmer rather than the factory. The literature of dairy production at its smallest scale, holding the door the Willard factory books open at the opposite end.
