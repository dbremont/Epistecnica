## Notes

> Edward Dobson's rudimentary treatise — the brick and tile manufacture's clays, moulding, kiln processes and burning: ceramic construction materials as engineering, in the Weale series whose Rudiments of civil engineering taught beside it. The brickmaker's own science of the trade's materials at mid-century, standing with the cements and concretes treatise as construction-material literature.
