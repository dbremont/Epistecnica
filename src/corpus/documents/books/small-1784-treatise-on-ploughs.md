## Notes

> James Small's treatise — the plough and wheel-carriage designs whose improved construction the Scottish farms ran: the mould-board geometry Small had refined as one implement's engineering history, written by the man held to have given Scotland's ploughs their standard form. Agricultural-implement construction's pioneering treatise beside the Horsemanship husbandry texts.
