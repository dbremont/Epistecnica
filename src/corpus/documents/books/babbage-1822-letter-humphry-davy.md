## Notes

> Charles Babbage's 1822 letter to Sir Humphry Davy — the difference engine proposed: the method of finite differences turned into machinery, the tables calculated by a sequence of additions rather than by human computers, and the errors of the printed tables eliminated at the source. The founding document of automatic calculation, written before a single engine had been built, and the programme the Difference Engine was funded to carry out.
