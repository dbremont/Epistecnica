## Notes

> William H. Burr — the New York structural engineer at the Scottish bridge works and later engineering professor — carried the experimental laws of timber, stone, iron and steel into the working formulae of the American bridge and building trades: the working stresses, the fatigue limits, the riveted and built forms of the late iron age.

> The scanned copy is the 1883 first edition.
