## Notes

> Lord Rayleigh's treatise in two volumes, published 1877 and 1878: the mathematical theory of sound from vibrations of strings and pipes to the acoustic properties of rooms, the discipline's first complete quantitative system. The quantification Helmholtz's physiology either avoided or built toward instruments; later reprinted across the century (the archive's earliest scans are of the 1894 second edition).
