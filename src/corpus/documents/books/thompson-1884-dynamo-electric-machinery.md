## Notes

> Silvanus P. Thompson's dynamo manual — the generators and motors of electrotechnics as a student discipline: the machine's action, construction and power conversion carried into the manual generations of English electrical engineers learned on through its editions. The power-conversion literature's founding student text beside the telegraphy trade manuals of the same decade.
