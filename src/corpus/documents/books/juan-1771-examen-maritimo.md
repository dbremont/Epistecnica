## Notes

> Jorge Juan's Examen marítimo — the ship analysed by mechanics for the Spanish service: the stability and the centre of gravity, the resistance and the sailing, the masts and the rigging, and the construction rules derived from the theory rather than inherited. The book that carried the new naval science into a navy's practice, written by the officer who had measured the meridian with La Condamine and brought back the methods of the Paris academy.

> The 1771 first edition and a second copy are scanned.
