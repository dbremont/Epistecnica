## Notes

> Oliver Evans's The Young Mill-Wright and Miller's Guide — the mill designed as a complete system: the water wheel and its fall, the gearing and the shafts, the millstones and the dress, the bolting and the conveyors, and the millwright's arithmetic for each. Evans's own automatic mill and his steam engine appear in the later editions, and the guide remained the American miller's manual through the nineteenth century.

> The 1850 edition is scanned (two copies); the first edition is 1795.
