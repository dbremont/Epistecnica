## Notes

> Justus von Liebig's Extract of Meat — the manufacture of a preserved food argued from chemistry: the soluble constituents of muscle, the extraction and the concentration, the nutritive claims and the analyses, and the South American works that made the product a world trade. The pamphlet record of the point where a laboratory product became an industry and a century of advertising followed.

> The 1872 edition is scanned from the Wellcome copy.
