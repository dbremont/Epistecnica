## Notes

> John W. Nystrom — the Philadelphia mechanical engineer and inventor of a calculating machine of his own — carried his tables, rules and formulas into a pocket-book compiled for the workshop and the office. The scanned copy is the 1856 edition of the 1854 first.

> It sits in the Haswell line, the American counterpart of the British and German desk-books.
