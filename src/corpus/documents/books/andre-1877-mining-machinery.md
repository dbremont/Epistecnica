## Notes

> George G. André's descriptive treatise — the mining plant documented tool by tool: excavation, pumping, ventilation and the mineral-treatment equipment the trade ran, carried in the Spon descriptive format the engineering trades bought. The mining-machinery trade's own reference beside the mining-engineering textbooks of the same decade.
