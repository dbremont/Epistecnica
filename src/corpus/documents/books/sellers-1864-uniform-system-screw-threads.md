## Notes

> William Sellers's 1864 paper — the uniform system of screw threads that became the American standard: the thread form, its pitches and the sizing table proposed as Franklin Institute's own publication, read against the Whitworth system the British machinery ran. Mechanical standardization's founding American document — the paper that kept the machine trades' fasteners interchangeable within one economy.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1864 paper is verified here; it is catalogued in the Franklin Institute's journal and the chase is open.
