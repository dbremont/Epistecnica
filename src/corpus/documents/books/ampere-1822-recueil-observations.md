## Notes

> André-Marie Ampère's Recueil d'observations électro-dynamiques — the collected memoirs that built electrodynamics out of Ørsted's deflection: the force between current elements, the equivalence of the circuit and the magnet, and the mathematical frame the telegraph and the dynamo later assumed. The theoretical companion to the four-page pamphlet, written within two years of it.

> Two 1822 copies are scanned.
