## Notes

> James Sheridan Muspratt's two-volume industrial chemistry — chemical substances documented with their manufacturing applications, the processes of bleaching, dyeing, glassmaking, soapmaking, paper production and metallurgy carried from the laboratory to the works' floor. The Liverpool industrial chemist taught, researched, and produced ammonia; the volumes document the chemical processes behind industrial products, and the Science History Institute holds both digitized originals.

> The scanned copies below carry the two volumes as the Institute and archive.org digital collections hold them.
