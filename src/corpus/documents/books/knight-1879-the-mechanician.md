## Notes

> Cameron Knight's mechanician — the construction and manipulation of the principal tools, the blacksmithing and forging arts the metalworking trades ran: hand-forging, fullering, swaging and tool-making as the shop's own documented economy. The metalworking branch's shop-side manual, entering beside the trades' dictionaries and the later blacksmithing series.
