## Notes

> Oscar Guttmann's treatise — the history, physical and chemical properties and industrial manufacture of explosives: the nitroglycerine, dynamite and blasting processes the consulting engineer documented from the works themselves. The explosives-manufacturing literature's professional reference — the Schönbein gun-cotton line's industrial descendants.
