## Notes

> Charles Russell's tannin-process pamphlet — the photographic emulsion and image-production process of the dry-plate's early claim: the tanning chemistry documented as its own process decades before the gelatin plates the later manuals ran. The process-pamphlet literature beside the wet-plate treatises, one of the dry-plate economy's first documents.
