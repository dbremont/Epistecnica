## Notes

> Arthur Young's serial — forty-five volumes over thirty-one years — is the running record of the English agricultural improvement era: experiments, farm tours, price tables, and the correspondence of a whole nation of improving farmers, edited and mostly written by the man whose tours and whose Board of Agriculture made agricultural statistics a genre. For the economic historian, the *Annals* is where the eighteenth-century English field survives in numbers.

> Archive.org's coverage of the run is sparse — the scanned volume below is vol. IV (1785) — and no single digital surrogate carries the whole set; the per-volume chase stays open.
