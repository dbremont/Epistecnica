## Notes

> Joseph Nasmith's carding-engine volume — the machine's construction and operation at the cotton economy's feed stage, documented for the trade the Manchester mills ran. The spinning-preparation literature's engine-side reference, standing with the firm machinery literatures the decade issued.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1887 carding-engine volume is verified here; Nasmith's later cotton-mill engineering works do circulate, and this volume is catalogued but untouched.
