## Notes

> Adolf Martens — director of the Charlottenburg state material-testing laboratory whose optically coupled test instruments, extensible readings, and metallographic microscope a metal phase is named for — set out the official handbook of the German state testing house: the test pieces, the testing machines, the extensometer and the metallography of the laboratory. The English translation appeared in 1899.

> The scanned copy is the 1899 English translation.
