## Notes

> Gardiner Hiscox, the Patterson engineer of Scientific American — the trade journal that chronicled the American school, brought the horseless carriage of 1900 into one desk-book: the steam, electric and hydro-carbon machines described in their running and forms, the motor cycles and the plant beside them. The turn-of-pencil automobile reference beside Worby Beaumont.

> The 1900 Munn edition is scanned.
