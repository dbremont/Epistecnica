## Notes

> Francois Coignet built factories and houses in agglomerated concrete and opened their accounts to the trade: the proportions of the mix, the shuttering, the lifting and bonding of walls and floors without mortar or brick — and the iron reinforcement embedded in his floors and beams to carry the tension the plain stone could not. The 1861 Traité is a founding book of systematic concrete construction, the material later tied together by Monier and Hennebique.
