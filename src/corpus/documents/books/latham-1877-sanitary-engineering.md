## Notes

> Baldwin Latham's guide — the construction of sewerage and house drainage as one engineered system: the public-health engineering that the sanitary acts demanded and the urban infrastructure trades ran, documented works-ward. Sanitary engineering's professional reference beside the water-supply branch — the city's drainage where Fanning's waterworks are its supply.
