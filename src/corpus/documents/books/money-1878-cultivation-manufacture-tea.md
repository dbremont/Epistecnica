## Notes

> Edward Money's Cultivation and Manufacture of Tea — tea grown, picked, rolled and fired: the gardens of Assam and the Punjab, the withering and the rolling, the firing and the sorting, and the manufacture described in the field by a planter. The book that documents the Indian tea industry at the point it displaced the China trade, and the food-processing side of a drink the century made ordinary.
