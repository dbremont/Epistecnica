## Notes

> Ferdinand Berthoud carried the French marine chronometer: the horloges marines built and described for the corps, the theory of the longitudes by time measurement — the tradition that accompanied Harrison in the Englishdeveloped form. The scanned copy is the 1775 Les longitudes par la mesure du temps; the 1787 form is also scanned.

> It is the French counterpart of Harrison, with the escapements of both traditions carried.
