## Notes

> The Patent Office's abridgments of the railway specifications — the patented development of the railway printed as a record: the early wagon-way and rail patents, the Losh and Stephenson patent of 1815, George Stephenson's locomotive specifications, Robert Stephenson's improvements and the running of the locomotive trade to 1863, each abridged from the Patent Office's own enrolments. The 1868 volume is the primary patent corpus of the railway — the specifications the biographies cite and the catalogue's treatises describe — printed in one place for the century's engineers.

> The abridgments are abridgements, not the enrolled specifications; the full texts remain in the Patent Office register.
