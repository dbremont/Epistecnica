## Notes

> Youatt wrote the anatomy, the shoeing and the management of the horse; Brunel appended the engineering of draught — the traces, the collar, the spring carriage, the wheel diameter and the road resistance measured. The volume is the bridge between the farrier's trade and the traction calculations of the road and the farm.
