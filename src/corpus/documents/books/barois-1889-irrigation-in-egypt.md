## Notes

> Julien Barois's Irrigation in Egypt — the Nile's irrigation described for the engineer: the river's regime and the flood, the barrage and the canals, the perennial system and the basin system, the pumps and the drainage, and the administration that ran the water. The technical account of the Nile works in the British decade, and the Egyptian counterpart of the Indian canal reports.
