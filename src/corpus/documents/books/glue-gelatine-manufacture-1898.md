## Notes

> The manufacture of glue and gelatine — the adhesive trades described from the works: the raw materials and the extraction, the drying and the finishing, the machinery and the uses, and the by-products of the slaughterhouse turned into a manufacture. The trade compilation that records an industry whose literature otherwise stays in the works, and the adhesive side of the joinery and bookbinding the guide's craft books assume.

> The 1898 edition is scanned from the Cornell set.
