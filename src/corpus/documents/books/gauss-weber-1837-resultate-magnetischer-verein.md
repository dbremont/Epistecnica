## Notes

> Gauss and Weber's Resultate aus den Beobachtungen des magnetischen Vereins — the magnetic observatories organized into a network: the absolute measurement of intensity and declination standardized, the instruments compared, and the simultaneous observations that let a magnetic storm be seen as one event across Europe. The first international scientific observing network, and the model the later geophysical services copied.

> The 1836 and 1837 volumes are scanned.
