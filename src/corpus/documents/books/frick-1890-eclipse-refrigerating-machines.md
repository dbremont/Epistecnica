## Notes

> The Frick Company's "Eclipse" catalogue — the refrigerating machine sold to the cold-storage depot: the compressor and the condenser, the ammonia circulation, the ice-making tank and the cold rooms, with the capacities, the dimensions and the prices. The catalogue documents mechanical cooling as an installed industrial system, the plant that kept meat, fruit and dairy produce through the season and made the cold chain a commercial reality.

> The 1890 catalogue is scanned.
