## Notes

> Benjamin Robins's New Principles of Gunnery — the founding document of scientific ballistics: the ballistic pendulum that measured the velocity of the ball, the force of gunpowder determined by experiment, the resistance of the air to swift and slow motions investigated, and the theory of the rifle and the expanded ball. Gunnery ceased to be a rule of art and became a measured science with this book; Euler's commentary carried it across Europe, and Hutton's 1805 edition is the form the next century read.
