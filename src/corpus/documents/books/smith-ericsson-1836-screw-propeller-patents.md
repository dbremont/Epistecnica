## Notes

> The Smith and Ericsson screw-propeller patents — the screw made a practicable propulsion: the helical blade in the deadwood, the gearing and the shaft, and the claims that set the propeller against the paddle wheel. The patent layer of the transition in which the screw became a naval system, documented in the specifications and the Admiralty trials that followed.
