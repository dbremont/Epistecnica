## Notes

> John Bourne's great treatise — the steam engine applied across the whole of its economy: mines, mills, steam navigation, railways and agriculture, the constructive account that preceded his catechism by a decade. The applied-steam literature's massive English reference — the engine's own systems documented where they ran.
