## Notes

> C. C. Bramwell's The Construction of a Gasoline Motor Vehicle — the first petrol cars treated as a whole machine: the chassis and its frame, the engine and its cooling, the clutch and gearbox, the steering and the brakes, the suspension and the wheels. The book in which the automobile appears as an integrated vehicle rather than an engine mounted on a carriage, written at the point the technical literature of the motor car began.

> The 1901 volume is scanned. It stands just past the guide's 1900 boundary and is included because the automobile's own literature begins here.
