## Notes

> Thomas Tredgold's warming and ventilation — the heating and ventilating systems of public buildings, houses and manufactories treated as engineering arithmetic: the fluid-flow and mechanical applications whose cast-iron materials the same author's strength treatise supplied. The building-services literature's founding English treatise, read beside the held carpentry and cast-iron volumes.
