## Notes

> John Butler Johnson — whose framed structures the engineering course already carried — composed the materials treatise: timber, stone, brick, iron and steel stated by their experimental properties, the testing data gathered for the engineer. The scanned copy is the 1898 printing of the 1897 first.

> It is the American materials reference of the end of the century.
