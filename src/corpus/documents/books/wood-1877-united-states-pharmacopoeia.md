## Notes

> Horatio C. Wood Jr.'s 1877 address — the United States Pharmacopoeia and the American Medical Association argued as a professional standard: the pharmacopoeia's authority and its revision, the relation of the physicians and the pharmacists who made it, and the need for a national formulary the trade would accept. The address documents the politics of the American pharmacopoeia in the decade before its sixth revision, and the professional settlement behind the standard.
