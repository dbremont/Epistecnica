## Notes

> Frederick Overman's metallurgy — the American practical side of the extraction economy: mining of ores, metallurgical processes, machinery and the applications of metals, carried beside the English treatises' furnaces. The mining-and-metallurgical operations manual the American works ran, standing with Phillips and Bauerman on the collection's shelves.
