## Notes

> René-Antoine Ferchault de Réaumur's art of converting wrought iron into steel — steelmaking described from the furnace and the crucible: the cementation of iron in charcoal, the crucible melt, the hardening and the tempering, and the microscopic examination of the fracture that let him distinguish the products. The founding scientific account of ferrous metallurgy, a century before Karsten systematized the field and two before the microscope became standard in the works.

> Three 1722 copies are scanned; the plates are the reason the book is cited as much for its method as its results.
