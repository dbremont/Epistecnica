## Notes

> The qualitative companion of the held quantitative system — Carl Remigius Fresenius's Anleitung zur qualitativen Analyse of 1841: the detection scheme, the group separations and the cation-and-anion order that preceded quantitative instruction. The English trade knew it as the *Elementary instruction in chemical analysis*, the teaching sheet whose sequence the Wiesbaden laboratory ran.
