## Notes

> Thomas Dixon Lockwood's practical manual for the telegraph trade — the Western Union engineer's account of electricity and magnetism as the working electrician used them: the circuits, the apparatus, the maintenance and the craft of telegraphy on a national network. The guide's electrical-technology shelf in its strongest form: instruction for operators and constructors, not lectures on principles.
