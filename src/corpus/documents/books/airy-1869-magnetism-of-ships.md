## Notes

> The Board of Trade's compilation on the magnetism of ships — the compass deviations iron ships introduced and the compensation.methods the Astronomer Royal's instruments and correspondence proposed: the geomagnetic measurement sciences applied to the century's largest moving iron masses. Part science, part regulation — the actual instrument record of a technological problem the navy and the shipowners carried.
