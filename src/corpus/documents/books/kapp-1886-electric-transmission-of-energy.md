## Notes

> Gisbert Kapp's transmission treatise — electrical power's transmission, transformation, subdivision and distribution systematized at the discipline's invention: the alternating-current economy and the machines whose arithmetic the electrical-engineering schools would teach. The power-transmission literature's founding English volume, one book behind the lighting manuals the decade carried.
