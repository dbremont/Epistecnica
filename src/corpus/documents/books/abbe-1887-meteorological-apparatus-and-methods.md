## Notes

> Cleveland Abbe's treatise on meteorological apparatus and methods — the Signal Service's observatory systems and their measurement regimes, published as the Appendix to the Chief Signal Officer's 1887 Annual Report: the apparatus tables of an American weather-service in its administrative era. The methods-end of the meteorological instrument branch before the century turned to synoptic practice.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1887 treatise itself has been verified here; the Signal Service reports circulate in institutional collections and the chase is open. The Smithsonian's meteorological tables of the same program do circulate.
