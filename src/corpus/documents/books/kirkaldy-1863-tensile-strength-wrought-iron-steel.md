## Notes

> David Kirkaldy's Experimental Inquiry into the tensile strength of wrought iron and steel — the results of his own machine: the specimens broken and measured, the elongation recorded, the different irons and steels compared, and the tabulated results printed as a record. The 1863 inquiry put the strength tables inherited from Tredgold and Barlow on the footing of experiment, and founded the testing house that Kirkaldy then ran for the rest of the century as a public instrument of materials verification.
