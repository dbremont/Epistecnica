## Notes

> Adolphe Legros's Le Soleil de la photographie — the complete photographic practice of 1863: the portrait and the landscape, the stereoscope and the monument, the collodion and the albumen, the retouching and the colouring, and the whole apparatus and chemistry of the studio set out for the beginner and the professional. The French manual that documents the state of the art between the collodion and the dry plate.
