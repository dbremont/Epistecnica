## Notes

> Alexander Blackie William Kennedy's Mechanics of Machinery — the machine taught from its constrained motion: the kinematic pairs, the chains and the turning-pair analysis, the British counterpart of Reuleaux's kinematics written by the man who had translated Reuleaux. The teaching layer between Willis's 1841 principles and the twentieth-century machine-design texts.

> The critique's "Kinematics of Machinery (1886)" does not verify as a Kennedy title — the 1876 Kinematics is Reuleaux in Kennedy's translation — so the row names the book Kennedy actually published in 1886. Both the 1886 and 1893 printings are scanned.
