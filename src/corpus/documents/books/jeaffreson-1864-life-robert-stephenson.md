## Notes

> John Cordy Jeaffreson's Life of Robert Stephenson, with William Pole's descriptive chapters on the professional works — the son's career documented from the family's own papers: the correspondence printed in the text, the locomotive and the railways described by Pole as the engine works understood them, and the Britannia and Conway bridges analysed by a contemporary engineer. The 1864 biography is the Stephensons' nearest scanned printed record beside the Patent Office's abridgments; the letters and design notebooks themselves remain in private and institutional hands, unscanned here.

> The 1864 edition (two volumes) and the 1866 one-volume printing with Pole's chapters are scanned.
