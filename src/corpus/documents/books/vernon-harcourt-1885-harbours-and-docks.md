## Notes

> Leveson Francis Vernon-Harcourt's Clarendon treatise — the physical features, histories, construction, equipment and maintenance of harbours and docks as one professional reference: the maritime-engineering branch's close-of-century volume, standing where Thomas Stevenson's family science met the university's print. The coast and dock economies in one book.
