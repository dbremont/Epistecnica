## Notes

> Thomas Curson Hansard's Typographia — the art of printing sketched historically by a printer at his own trade: origin, progress and the practice of the press, written by the parliamentary printer whose family's printing the Hansard reports made famous. The print-trade histories beside Johnson's instructor — the technology and the craft carrying one account.
