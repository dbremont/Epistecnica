## Notes

> The butter volume of Willard's dairy system — butter production, machinery and creameries, the factory and the farm dairy treated as two regimes of the same manufacture. The book documents the transition from farm-based processing to organized dairy factories in the clearest form its period produced: one technology, two scales.
