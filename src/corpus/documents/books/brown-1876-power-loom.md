## Notes

> Alexander Brown's power-loom treatise — the construction of the loom and the art of weaving as one mechanic's instruction from the Boston trade: the loom's parts, the setting and the adjustments, taught where the mills built their own machines. The weaving-mechanism literature's American practical form beside Barlow's history.
