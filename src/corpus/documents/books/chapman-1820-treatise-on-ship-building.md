## Notes

> Chapman's ship-building treatise — ship construction and engineering as one theory: the displacement, stability and fitting-out calculations that made the hull an engineering object, translated as the 1781 French *Traité de la construction des vaisseaux* whose scans the archive carries. Naval architecture's theory book holding its 1768 plate classification in one discipline the shipping trade could apply.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of an 1820 English printing is verified here; the French translation of the treatise is scanned below and it is catalogued elsewhere.
