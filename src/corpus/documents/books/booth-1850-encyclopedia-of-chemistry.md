## Notes

> James Curtis Booth and Campbell Morfit's encyclopedia — practical and theoretical chemistry in one American reference: substances, industrial applications and the laboratory practice the profession earned its living on, issued mid-century between Ure's industrial technology and the analytical systems. The American chemistry trade's shoulders beneath the later professional literature.
