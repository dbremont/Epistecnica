## Notes

> Robertson Buchanan — Glasgow engineer of the Clyde steamboat works — carried the young trade into a book: the steamboat described in its engines and paddles, the British form on which the Clyde and Mersey ran, and the American application printed beside. The first book devoted to the steamboat in any language.

> Buchanan had already written the mill work treatise of 1823 later in the century.
