## Notes

> J. J. Lagowski's four-volume Macmillan Encyclopedia of Chemistry — the general-chemistry reference of the late 1990s: elements, atoms, molecules, bonding, stereochemistry, spectroscopy, and the people and processes of the discipline, alphabetized across A–Cl, Co–I, J–P, and Q–Z with an index. The comprehensive reference between the specialized dictionaries and the teaching textbooks.
