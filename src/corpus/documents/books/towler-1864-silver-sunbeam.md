## Notes

> John Towler's sunbeam — the comprehensive practical and theoretical photographic text-book of the wet-plate decade: the operations from sensitizing to printing, written for the American studios whose manuals the trade collected. The photography literature's comprehensive operations manual, between the daguerrean and collodion generations.
