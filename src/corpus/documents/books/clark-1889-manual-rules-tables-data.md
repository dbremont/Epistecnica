## Notes

> Daniel Kinnear Clark — already the author of the Railway Machinery and the steam-engine books the profession used — compressed his data into a single manual: boiler pressures, the strengths of materials, the rules of the machine tool and the engine, gathered from the recent researches of the trade. The 1889 Manual is the British mechanical desk-book of its decade.

> The scanned copy is the 1889 first edition.
