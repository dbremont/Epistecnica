## Notes

> The Kater and Sabine pendulum papers — gravity measured by the seconds pendulum: the reversible pendulum, the clock and the comparisons carried from station to station, and the figure of the earth inferred from the rate of a swinging bob. The instrument as a geodetic tool, and the measurement line the guide's geodesy volumes assume.
