## Notes

> George James Symons's British Rainfall — the volunteer rain-gauge network organized: gauges placed and read by observers across the islands, the yearly returns collected and mapped, and the distribution of rain in space and time made a statistical object. The model of the amateur-and-office observation network, and the record the later meteorological service inherited.

> The annual volumes are a series; the 1874 report is scanned as a Nature notice, and the entry links that verified item.
