## Notes

> Daniel Kinnear Clark's Railway Machinery — the mechanical engineering of railways as one treatise: locomotives, carriages, wagons and the permanent way's equipment economy, documented by the Scottish engineer whose locomotive design the industry ran. The railway-machinery literature between Webb's construction textbook and the locomotive-engineering volumes of the following decades.
