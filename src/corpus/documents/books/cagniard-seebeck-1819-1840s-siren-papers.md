## Notes

> The siren papers of Cagniard de la Tour and Seebeck — the rotating disc that turns a blast of air into a counted frequency: the holes passing the jet, the pitch set by the speed, and the first reliable tone generator whose frequency could be read from a revolution counter. The instrument that made frequency a quantity the ear could compare with a number.

> Honest no-scan: the papers are journal reports; the entry collects them and names the series.
