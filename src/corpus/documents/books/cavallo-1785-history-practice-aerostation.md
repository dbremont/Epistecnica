## Notes

> Tiberius Cavallo — the natural philosopher of hydrogen and the balloon trade — wrote the history and the practice of aerostation as a manual: the hot-air and the hydrogen machine, their construction, the gases that fill them and the navigation of the air, described for the first balloonists. It is the founding English aeronautical manual after the Académie verified the machine.
