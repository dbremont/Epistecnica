## Notes

> Robert Smith's system — the four books of optical science and instruments: perspective, the camera's own geometry, microscopes and telescopes in their instrument condition, written by the Cambridge Plumian professor whose pupils the instrument trades ran. The instrumental-optics literature's founding system — the theory book beside which the eighteenth century's optical apparatus trades stand.
