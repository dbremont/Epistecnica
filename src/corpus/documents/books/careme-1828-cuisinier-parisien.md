## Notes

> Marie-Antoine Carême's Cuisinier parisien — the grande cuisine systematized: the stocks and the sauces reduced to rules, the kitchen brigade and its stations, the architecture of the dish and the display, and the recipes written for a profession rather than a household. The book that made cookery a designed practice before the restaurant industry formalized it.

> The 1828 first edition and the 1842 printing are scanned.
