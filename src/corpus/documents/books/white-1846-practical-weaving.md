## Notes

> George White's weaving treatise — the loom construction and weaving operation taught practically for both hands and power: the hand-loom and power-loom economy beside the trades' history volumes. The weaving-manual literature's own mid-century practical instruction, filling the gap between the American art books and Barlow's 1878 history.
