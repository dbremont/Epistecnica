## Notes

> Louis Édouard Rivot's principles of ore treatment — the French school's theoretical metallurgy: the reactions and the furnace conditions stated as principles before the processes are described, the ore dressing and the extraction taught as applied chemistry. The book the guide's French line needed between d'Aubuisson's treatise and the later industrial manuals.

> The critique's "Doctrine des essais au chalumeau" does not verify as a Rivot title; the verified work is this 1859 treatise, and the row names it. Both 1859 printings are scanned.
