## Notes

> Charles M. Percy's practical treatise — the engines, drills, pumps, crushing and concentration equipment of the metalliferous mines as one plant inventory, written for the mine's engineer and manager. The last of the mining-machinery references the branch collects: André's descriptive treatise opened the decade's plant documentation, and this closes it before the twentieth century's hoisting the new electrical power brought.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1894 Percy treatise is verified here; the neighbouring machinery treatise by Davies of 1902 does circulate, and the Percy is catalogued untouched.
