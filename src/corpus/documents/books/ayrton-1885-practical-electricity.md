## Notes

> William Edward Ayrton's laboratory course — practical electricity as first-year engineering instruction: the galvanometer economy, resistance methods, and the measurements the profession certified. The Finsbury Technical College's course-book at the electrical engineering profession's founding — measurement as employment, taught as lecture and laboratory in one.
