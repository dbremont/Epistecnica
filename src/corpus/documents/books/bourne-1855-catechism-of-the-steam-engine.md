## Notes

> John Bourne's catechism — the steam engine's construction, boiler construction and corrosion, locomotive boilers and applications taught in the question-and-answer instruction the trades' apprentices ran: engine knowledge in the catechetical form whose editions the mechanic readers bought into the twentieth century. The engine-room's own teaching literature, beside the theoretical manuals.
