## Notes

> Sydney Walker Barnaby's Marine Propellers — the propeller treated theoretically and tested: the momentum and the blade-element views, the slip and the efficiency, the experiments on models and the full-scale trials, and the design rules that came out of them. The first systematic propeller theory for the marine engineer, and the book that turned the screw from a patented shape into a calculated component.

> The 1891 first edition and the 1900 second are scanned.
