## Notes

> Robert Mallet's great Neapolitan earthquake report — the first principles of observational seismology demonstrated on the ruined provinces of the 1857 Basilicata earthquake: the investigation's instruments, the direction and violence of the shock measured from its architectural record, the earthquake made into an experiment one could read after the fact. The founding document of instrument-grounded seismology, decades before Milne's pendulums.
