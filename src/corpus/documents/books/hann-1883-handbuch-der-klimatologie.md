## Notes

> Julius von Hann's Handbuch der Klimatologie — climate systematized as a physical science: the temperature, the pressure and the moisture of the globe arranged by cause rather than by place, the climates classified, and the mountain and the tropic given their physics. The summit of nineteenth-century climatology, and the reference the twentieth century's climate science revised.

> The 1897 third edition is scanned; the first edition is 1883 and the entry states it.
