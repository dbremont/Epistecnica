## Notes

> Theron L. Hiles's ice crop — the natural-ice trade as one harvest, storage, shipping and use economy: the pond ice the American cities bought, carried in the Orange Judd format the farm trades knew. The refrigeration shelf's natural-side book beside Twining's invent-the-cold patents — both the harvest economy and the machines.
