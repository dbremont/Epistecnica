## Notes

> The technical plates of the great French encyclopedia — eleven volumes of plates issued with (and after) the seventeen text volumes — drawing Vauchy's, Goussier's and their colleagues' engravings of workshops, machines, tools, and manufacturing operations term by term: textiles, metallurgy, mining, glass, paper, printing, the whole range of eighteenth-century industrial production rendered visible. Its technical plates document workshops, tools, production equipment and manufacturing operations: the volume-record of the mechanical arts on their way to being manufactured science.

> No single-digit archive.org scan represents the plates; the collaborative Chicago project (ARTFL) and the Gallica digitizations carry the pages — particularly the plate volumes' facsimiles — and these links point at the collections, not at an invented one-page surrogate.
