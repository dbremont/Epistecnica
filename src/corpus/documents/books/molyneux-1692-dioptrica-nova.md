## Notes

> William Molyneux's Dioptrica Nova — the optics of the telescope and the microscope worked out for the instrument maker: the refraction, the lens combinations, the aberrations and the practical grinding and mounting, with the plates that show the instruments. The treatise between Descartes's dioptrics and the achromatic lens, and the optical branch's own practical book.

> The 1692 first edition and the 1709 printing are scanned.
