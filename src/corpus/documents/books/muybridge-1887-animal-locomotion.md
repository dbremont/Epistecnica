## Notes

> Eadweard Muybridge's Animal Locomotion — the animal and the human body photographed in motion: the consecutive phases of the walk, the trot, the gallop and the human gait, taken with the battery of cameras and the electric shutter he built for the purpose. The plates that settled the horse's gallop and made the frame sequence an instrument of measurement, entering the study of movement and the design of machines at once.

> The archive holds the 1880 prospectus and electro-photographic plates and the 1893 Descriptive Zoopraxography; the 1887 folio itself is not fully scanned, so the entry links the verified Muybridge material.
