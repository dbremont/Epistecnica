## Notes

> The Earl of Dundonald — industrial chemist, innovator in coal-tar and soda — addressed the cultivators of the soil directly: agriculture is a chemical art, and its improvement waits on the chemist's knowledge of soils, manures and combustion. The treatise is the era's bluntest statement of what agriculture and chemistry owe each other, a decade before Davy's Board-of-Agriculture lectures made the same claim institutional.
