## Notes

> Edward James Stone's experimental determination — the velocity of sound measured at Greenwich in the Royal Society's terms, reported in 1871 and printed in the *Philosophical Transactions*: precision acoustics as the discipline's own measurable achievement. The science of sound calibration the guide's acoustic-measurement branch anchors; where Polar sound rushed through 1828 air, this 1871 measurement timed it to the observatory.
