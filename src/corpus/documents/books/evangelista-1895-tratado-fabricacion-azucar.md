## Notes

> León Evangelista's treatise on the manufacture of cane and beet sugar — the Spanish-language reference of the moment the two sugar industries met: the extraction, clarification, crystallization and the machinery of both materials treated as one process family. Its title makes the cane-and-beet pairing explicit at the century's close, when the beet had broken the plantation's monopoly.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of this title has been verified here; the digitized Spanish sugar literature of the period is scattered, and the treatise is catalogued but not touched.
