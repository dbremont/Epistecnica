## Notes

> William Oughtred put the logarithms on the rule: the circles of proportion in which the movable logarithmic scales multiplied among themselves — the slide rule in its first published account, the instrument on which the engineer of the next three centuries computed at the desk. The scanned copy is the 1632 first edition.
