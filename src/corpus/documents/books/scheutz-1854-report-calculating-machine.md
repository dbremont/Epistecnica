## Notes

> The Royal Society committee's 1854 report on the Scheutz difference engine — the machine examined and its tables tested: the mechanism of differences, the printing apparatus, the accuracy of the tabulated results, and the verdict that the engine worked. The first independent validation of a working difference engine, and the public proof that Babbage's principle could be realized by other hands.
