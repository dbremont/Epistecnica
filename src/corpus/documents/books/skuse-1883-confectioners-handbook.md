## Notes

> E. Skuse's Confectioners' Hand-Book — sugar boiling and the confectioner's own processes: the degrees and the tests, the lozenges and the comfits, the creams and the candied fruits, and the bench practice the trade guarded. The confectionery trade's own manual, documenting the branch that the industrial sugar books serve from above.
