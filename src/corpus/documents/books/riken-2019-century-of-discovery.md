## Notes

> RIKEN's official centennial history, written for the 2017 centenary and published in English in 2019. It follows the institute through four institutional forms: the private foundation (1917–1948), the private company KAKEN (1948–1958), the public corporation (1958–2003), and the Independent Administrative Institution / National Research and Development Institute (2003–present). The English edition is deliberately an organisational history rather than a peer-reviewed academic monograph, and says so in its foreword.

> Its value for the corpus is the detail on the early institute: Takamine's 1913 proposal, Shibusawa's fundraising, Okochi's 1921 appointment and 1922 laboratory reform, the Riken Konzern's one-company-per-business model, the wartime Ni-go Project, and the destruction of the cyclotrons. Those are the facts the RIKEN actor record and the technical representation dataset need.
