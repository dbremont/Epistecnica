## Notes

> Selimo Romeo Bottone's bells book — the small electrical apparatus installed and maintained: bells, batteries, the working-and-wiring economy of houses and offices, written for practical men by the teacher of the electrical trades. The "doing" side of the small installation trade beside the big machine manuals.
