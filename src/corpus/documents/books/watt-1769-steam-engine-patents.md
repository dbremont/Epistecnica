## Notes

> James Watt's steam-engine patents — the separate condenser, the double-acting engine, the rotative drive and the parallel motion, specified one after another across thirty years: the documents by which the atmospheric pump became a machine that could drive a mill. The patent layer beneath the engine books the guide holds, and the legal frame the Boulton and Watt monopoly ran on.

> Honest no-scan: the specifications are patent-office records; the entry collects them and the engine books they governed.
