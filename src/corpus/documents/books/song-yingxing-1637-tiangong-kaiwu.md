## Notes

> Song Yingxing's Tiangong Kaiwu — the works of nature exploited by the arts of Ming China, recorded craft by craft: the cultivation and milling of grain, sericulture and the silk looms, salt and sugar, the porcelain kilns and the brick, the smelting of iron and the casting of bronze, paper and ink, shipbuilding and the art of war. The 1637 encyclopaedia is the Chinese counterpart of the technical encyclopaedia tradition — the workshop and the field drawn and described from the practice itself — and the reason this guide, otherwise an Atlantic library, carries a section of its own.

> The 1637 edition is scanned (a CADAL digitisation). The modern English translation by E-Tu Zen Sun and Shiou-Chuan Sun (1966) is scanned separately.
