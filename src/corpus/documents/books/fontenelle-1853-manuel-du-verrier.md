## Notes

> The Roret-manual of the glass trades — glassmaking, crystal, glaces and the artificial precious stones as one process instruction, the French tradition's own verrier book: furnaces, mixtures and the manufactured wares carried in the Manuels-Roret series the trades bought. The glass-manufacturing literature's French process register beside Porter's English history and Pellatt's disclosures.
