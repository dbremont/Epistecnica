## Notes

> James Harrison's refrigeration patents — the Geelong printer's ether-vapour ice-making machinery, patented through the 1850s–70s and run as the first commercial ice-making plants of Victoria and the frozen-food trials the era's trade press could read. Mechanical refrigeration and ice manufacture's founding patent literature, independent of the American and French apparatuses beside it.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the patent documents is verified here; they are catalogued in the Australian and British patent archives and the chase is open.
