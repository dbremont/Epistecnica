## Notes

> John Fry Heather's treatise — the construction, testing and adjustment of the mathematical instruments of drawing, vision, surveying levelling and practical astronomy: the Weale's rudimentary series' instrumentation volume for self-instruction, standing between the maker's trade books and the surveyor's field manuals. The instrument trade's most public teaching document at mid-century.
