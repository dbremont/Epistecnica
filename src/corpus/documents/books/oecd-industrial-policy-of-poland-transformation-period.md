# Polish Ministry of Economy. Industrial Policy of Poland During the Transformation Period and Assumptions for the Future. In OECD, *Policies for Industrial Development and Competitiveness*.

> **Bibliographic caveat.** The volume year is not recorded here and has not been verified; the item is cited as the ministry's contribution reproduced in the OECD collection. Treat the entry as a source pointer with its provenance stated, not as a certified citation.

## Notes

> The **official account** of the Polish transformation's industrial policy, written by the ministry that ran it: the diagnosis of the inherited industrial structure, the instruments used (privatisation, restructuring programmes, trade liberalisation, support institutions), and the assumptions the ministry made about the future — a document that is at once a policy statement and a primary source for the period.

> Read beside the academic treatments ([Kożuch](/view.html?p=documents/papers/kozuch-instruments-of-industrial-policy-restructuring.md), [Weresa](/view.html?p=documents/papers/weresa-2004-can-fdi-help-poland-catch-up-with-the-eu.md)), it supplies the missing voice: the **self-description** of the policy, which is what the later literature argues about. The gap between what the ministry said it was doing and what the statistics show happened is the interesting object, and it needs both texts.
