## Notes

> Hermann Wedding's Eisenhüttenwesen — the ironworks taught as one system: the ores and their preparation, the blast furnace, the puddling and crucible processes, the steelmaking of the 1870s, and the works organization that held them together. The German handbook layer between Karsten's classification and Ledebur's foundry manual.
