## Notes

> Frederick Walter Simms's Treatise on Levelling — the level and the theodolite taught with the railway works as the application: the instruments and their adjustments, the field book and the reductions, the gradients and the earthwork, and the errors that a survey must control. The survey manual the railway engineer learned the gradients from, and the measurement side of the railway construction the guide's treatises describe.

> The 1837 first edition and the 1870 printing are scanned.
