## Notes

> The engineers of the American schools — Johnson of Chicago, Bryan and Turneaure of Madison — compiled modern framed structures as a reference for the bridge and the building: the theory of the frame stated, and then the practice built with it, riveted steel, wind bracing, and the loading of the railway bridge. It is the American structural reference of the 1890s.

> The scanned copy is the microform printing of the 1893 first edition.
