## Notes

> Gaspard de Prony's Recherches made channel and pipe resistance a formula: head lost to slope, velocity reduced by the friction of the bound, coefficients fitted from engineering experiments and the École's own corpus. It is the founding engineering treatise of current-water hydraulics — the discipline which Darcy and Bazin later pressed to the measured coefficients the century's aqueducts and canals used.
