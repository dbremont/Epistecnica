## Notes

> John Slight's farmer's guide — agricultural operations, machinery and the productive practice carried with the chemistry in two volumes: the farm manual written at the moment Liebig's polemic and the Rothamsted trials were reshaping the field's science. Practical agriculture's own bridge book — the operations literature meeting the chemistry decade.
