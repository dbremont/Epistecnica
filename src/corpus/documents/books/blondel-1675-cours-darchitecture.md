## Notes

> François Blondel's Cours d'architecture — architecture taught in the academy: the orders and their proportions, the distribution and the decoration, the materials and the construction, and the rules set out as a course for students rather than a treatise for patrons. The 1675 course that began architectural education as a public institution, and the model the later schools followed.
