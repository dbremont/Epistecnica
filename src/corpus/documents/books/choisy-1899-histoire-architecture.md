## Notes

> Auguste Choisy's Histoire de l'architecture — the whole building tradition explained by its construction: the Egyptian and the Greek, the Roman and the Byzantine, the medieval and the Renaissance, each shown by the axonometric drawings that make the fabric stand visibly, and each explained by the materials and the structural problem it answered. The constructional history that gave the guide's building branch its long view.

> The 1899 first edition is scanned.
