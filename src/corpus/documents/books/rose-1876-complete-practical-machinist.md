## Notes

> Joshua Rose — editor of the American Machinist, the machinist-era journal — put the shop floor into a book: the lathe and vise work, the drill press and the planer, the tools ground and the cuts taken, the machine trades as a profession in its own manual. The 1876 first edition ran through revisions to the end of the century, and his later Modern Machine-Shop Practice (1887) carried the same trade to larger form.

> The scanned copy is the 1885 printing of the 1876 treatise; the 1887 Modern Machine-Shop Practice is scanned in a later printing.
