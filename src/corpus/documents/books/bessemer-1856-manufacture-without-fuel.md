## Notes

> Henry Bessemer's Cheltenham disclosure of 1856 — malleable iron and steel made without fuel by the air blown through the molten pig: the process announcement that reorganized steelmaking in the very vocabulary a Midland ironmaster's audience could test against their own furnaces. Bessemer steelmaking's original document, whatever the priority disputes and the refractory difficulties that followed.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1856 meeting report itself is verified here. Bessemer's own later autobiography — carried in scan below — prints the account and its chapter on the process at length, along with the patent chapters.
