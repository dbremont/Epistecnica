## Notes

> Jean-Baptiste Rondelet's Traité de l'art de bâtir — building construction taught as applied science: the stones and the mortars and their strength, the walls and the vaults and the carpentry, the iron and the roofs, and the calculation that sized each element instead of the rule of thumb that had preceded it. The hinge between the mason's tradition and the engineer's calculation, written by the architect who completed the Panthéon.
