## Notes

> Bontemps — the glass master of Choisy-le-Roi and later of Chance Brothers, the makers of light-house optics — compressed both the furnace and its commercial forms into one manual: the pot, crystal, vitrail and plate glass of the French shop. It is the standard continuation of the tradition that ran past the Neri–Kunckel line into technical glass.
