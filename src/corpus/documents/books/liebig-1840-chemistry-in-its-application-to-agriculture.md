## Notes

> Justus von Liebig's polemic — the organic chemistry of plant nutrition launched against the humus doctrine: the soil feeds the plant by minerals returned as ash, and the farmer who burns his harvest without returning them robs his fields. The mineral theory, right about what the plant needs and wrong about where its nitrogen comes from, carried agricultural chemistry from the lecture hall into industrial practice: artificial fertilizers, the chemistry of soils, and the general application of chemistry to physiology that collected the guide's agricultural period #9.
