## Notes

> The Government of Bengal's 1861 papers — irrigation in Bengal and the Maghassani Hills argued as a sanitary question: the canals and the tanks, the water and the fevers, the famine and the relief works, and the medical and engineering opinions set side by side. The papers show irrigation debated as public health and famine policy, not only as a works programme, and the administrative context the Indian canal reports assumed.

> The 1861 papers are scanned.
