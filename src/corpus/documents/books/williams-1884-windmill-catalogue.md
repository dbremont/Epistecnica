## Notes

> The B. S. Williams & Co. illustrated catalogue — the American windmill sold as a manufactured system: the wind wheel and its governing vane, the tower, the pump and the tank, with the sizes, the capacities and the prices. The catalogue documents the wind engine as a farm and railway commodity, the small wind machine that watered the plains, beside the great Dutch and English windmills of the mill tradition.
