## Notes

> Jean-Baptiste Dumas's Traité de chimie appliquée aux arts — chemistry applied to the arts across the manufactures: the acids and the alkalis, the dyes and the mordants, the soap and the candles, the glass and the ceramics, the metals and their treatment, and the processes of the factory described with the apparatus and the proportions. The 1848 treatise is the applied-chemistry sum of the French industrial tradition, and the bridge from the laboratory to the workshop.
