## Notes

> Gustav Robert Kirchhoff's Vorlesungen über mathematische Physik: Mechanik — the mechanics of continua taught as physics: the deformation of solids and fluids, the vibration of strings and rods, the propagation of sound in air, and the boundary conditions that make an acoustic problem soluble. The 1876 course gives the physicist's acoustics that the instrument makers worked from, and the German theoretical counterpart of the English papers.

> The 1876 volume is scanned.
