## Notes

> The Gurley manual — the instrument firm's own catalogue of the principal instruments of American engineering and surveying: the transits, levels and chains the Troy house built, documented as the trade's equipment inventory with specifications. The American counterpart of the Simms and Casella instrument literature — what the surveyor actually bought.
