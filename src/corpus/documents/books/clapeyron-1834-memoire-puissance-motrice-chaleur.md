## Notes

> Benoît Paul Émile Clapeyron's memoir — the first mathematical working of Carnot's theory of heat: the indicator diagram, the cycle in coordinates, and the analytic restatement that made the puissance motrice accessible to the physicists and engineers who could not read Carnot's prose. It is the bridge-work through which Carnot's *Réflexions* — already held in the corpus — reached Kelvin and Clausen and became the thermodynamic theory of the steam engine.

> No archive.org scan is held here; the memoir lives in the *Journal de l'École polytechnique* (vol. 14, cahier 23) and its reprintings. Honest record: the scan chase is open.
