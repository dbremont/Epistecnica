## Notes

> Hippolyte Dussauce's treatise — the tanning, currying and leather-dressing arts translated into one American manufacturing instruction from the French chemical-manufacturing tradition: the hides' processes as the trade's own documented economy. The leather branch's founding treatise beside Procter's later text-book.
