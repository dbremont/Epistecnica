## Notes

> Francis Galton's hearing papers — the whistle pushed above the audible range: the small pipe with its adjustable plug, the highest frequency a person could hear, and the differences between species and between individuals recorded as measurements. The instrument that made the upper limit of hearing a number, and the beginning of the audiometry the twentieth century institutionalized.

> Honest no-scan: the papers are society reports; the entry collects them and names the series.
