## Notes

> William J. M. Rankine's Glasgow chair carried a systematic engineering manual tradition into machine construction: gears, shafts, couplings, the transmission of power through the mill's working parts — the mechanics of the millwork whose applied mechanics and civil engineering volumes (1858, 1862) made the Rankine manuals the engineering profession's nineteenth-century textbooks.
