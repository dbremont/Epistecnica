## Notes

> Poinsot made the couple a free element of statics — the equal-and-parallel forces reducible to a pure moment — and rebuilt the whole science of equilibrium on forces, moments and couples alone, displacing the older composition-of-points grammar. His four memoirs on the composition of moments shrank what engineering statics had carried as its algebra.

> The Elements became the standard French teaching text and passed through editions to the end of the century; the 1803 first edition is scanned alongside a later one.
