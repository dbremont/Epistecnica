## Notes

> Joseph W. Richards — Lehigh metallurgist, affiliated with the Castner and Hallete applications — carried the new metal into engineering form: the history and occurrence of aluminium, its physical properties, the Hall-Heroult electrolysis from cryolite and iron ore, and the industrial processes of its time. The first systematic treatise of the new metal.
