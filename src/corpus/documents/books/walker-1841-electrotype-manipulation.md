## Notes

> Charles Vincent Walker's Electrotype Manipulation — the electrotype process taught as plain bench instructions: the moulds, the solutions, the battery and the deposited metal, set out so that a workshop could take the process up without a chemist. The small-shop layer beneath Smee's and Gore's treatises, and the practical manual the trade actually learned from.

> The 1849 first part and the 1852 edition are scanned; the 1841 first edition is the entry's year.
