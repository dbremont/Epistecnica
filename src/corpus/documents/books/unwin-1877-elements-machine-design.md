## Notes

> William Cawthorne Unwin — Rankine's pupil and partner, the Kelvin circle's engineer — taught machine design as a course: shafts, gearing, the strength of parts, working stresses from the material laws, each design element carried as a computed subject. The Elements is the English machine-design text between Reuleaux's theory and the modern design books; the scanned copy is the 1878 printing.

> The companion volume on machine details carried the same teaching to practical form.
