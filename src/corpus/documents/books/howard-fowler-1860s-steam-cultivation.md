## Notes

> The steam-cultivation literature of the Howard and Fowler firms — the two systems sold and compared: the tackle and the balance plough, the engines and the field arrangements, in the catalogues and the agricultural-press papers that carried the practice to farmers. The commercial layer above Fowler's patents, and the way the trade actually learned the machines.

> Honest no-scan: no single treatise collects this literature; the entry is a collector for the firms' catalogues and the survey papers. Van Alen's 1872 paper on steam cultivation in England and the 1909 Fowler catalogue are the verified surviving access.
