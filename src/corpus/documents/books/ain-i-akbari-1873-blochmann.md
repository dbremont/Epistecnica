## Notes

> The Ain-i-Akbari records the Mughal state as an inventory: the mint and its assay, the ordnance foundry and the damascened barrels, the arsenal, the textile workshops, the wages and the prices. The Blochmann translation is the standard access to the administrative and technical book of Akbar's empire.
