## Notes

> Jean Rodolphe Perronet's Description des projets et de la construction des ponts — the bridges of Neuilly and Mantes and Orléans described by their engineer: the design and the foundations, the centering and the stone, the labour and the cost, and the experiments on the strength of the materials. The 1783 book is the founding account of the modern masonry bridge, and the model of the engineer writing up his own works.

> The 1783 edition is scanned.
