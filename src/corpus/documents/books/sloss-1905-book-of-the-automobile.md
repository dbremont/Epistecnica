## Notes

> Robert Thompson Sloss's The Book of the Automobile — the motor car given its practical volume: the history of the vehicle, the engine and its systems, the chassis and the transmission, the care and operation of the car, and the trade that sold it. The American manual in which the automobile is already a manufactured commodity with a literature of its own.

> The 1905 volume is scanned. It stands past the guide's 1900 boundary and documents the vehicle the 1886–1900 patents had made.
