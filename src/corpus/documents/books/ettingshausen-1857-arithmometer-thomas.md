## Notes

> Andreas von Ettingshausen's 1857 report on the arithmometer of M. Thomas — the calculating machine examined and its operation described: the stepped drum, the setting and the crank, the multiplication and division performed by repeated addition, and the results checked. The report introduced the Thomas de Colmar arithmometer to the scientific public, and the machine that made mechanical calculation a commercial instrument rather than a cabinet curiosity.
