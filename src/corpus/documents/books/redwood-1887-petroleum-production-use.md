## Notes

> Boverton Redwood's Petroleum: its production and use — the oil industry surveyed at the point it became a world trade: the geology and the wells, the refining and the products, the transport and the lamps, and the statistics of a business that had changed scale within a generation. The book between Gesner's founding account and the twentieth-century petroleum engineering.

> The 1887 first edition is scanned, and the later treatise is held in the archive.
