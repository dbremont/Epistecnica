## Notes

> Andrew Ure's examination of the factory as a technological production system: machinery, workers, production processes, power and industrial organization — the factors of the factory system analyzed on Scottish mill floors in the year the factory workers' conditions became the question of British industry. The system defended as much as analyzed; the factory literature of the decade leans on it from both sides.
