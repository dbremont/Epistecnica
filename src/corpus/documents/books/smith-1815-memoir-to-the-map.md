## Notes

> William Smith's memoir to his map — the strata ordered by their fossils and the map explained: the succession of the formations, the identification of each by its characteristic fossils, and the practical survey that let a canal or a mine be planned from the surface. The founding document of stratigraphy as a practical mapping science, and the text that accompanies the first geological map of a whole country.

> The 1815 memoir is scanned; the map itself is a separate engraved sheet and is not part of the scan.
