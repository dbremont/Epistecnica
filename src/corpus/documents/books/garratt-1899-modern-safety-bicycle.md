## Notes

> Herbert Alfred Garratt's The Modern Safety Bicycle — the machine that displaced the high wheel, described from its parts: the diamond frame and its geometry, the ball bearings, the chain and the gearing, the wire-spoked wheel and the pneumatic tyre, the brakes and the saddle. The construction manual of the bicycle at the end of the decade in which it became the precision-engineered consumer durable.
