## Notes

> Jean-Baptiste Boussingault's Économie rurale — the farm analysed as a chemical system: the composition of the soil and the crops, the rotation and the fallow, the fertilizers and the manure, the feeding of stock and the balance of nitrogen, and the weather as a variable of the farm. The 1851 book is the field-station science applied to the farm as a whole, and the French counterpart of the Rothamsted experiments.

> The 1851 edition is scanned.
