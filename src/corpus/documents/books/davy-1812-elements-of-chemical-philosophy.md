## Notes

> Humphry Davy's summation of the chemical science his own electrochemistry had rewritten — the elements of chemical philosophy arranged for the lecture hall and the research laboratory of the Royal Institution: the voltaic battery's decomposition of matter, the nature of fire and light, and the new chemistry's systematization after Lavoisier through Davy's own experiments. The year before he delivered the agricultural chemistry lectures to the Board (also held here), the same chemical philosophy ran through both.
