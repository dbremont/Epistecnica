## Notes

> The Waygood guide — the hydraulic passenger lift's mechanism and merits described for the intending purchaser: the lift trade's own commercial literature at the decade the hydrated lifts served the new tall buildings. The building-machinery literature beside the held Robinson plant volume — the lifts as the power economy's public face.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the 1889 guide is verified here; the lift literatures of the following decade do circulate, and the guide is catalogued but untouched.
