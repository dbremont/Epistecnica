## Notes

> David Boswell Reid's illustrated ventilation theory — the theory and practice of building atmosphere: the air-movement of halls and galleries that carried sound as well as breath, the heating and lighting systems behind it. The building's environment treated as one engineering system, which the architectural acoustics branch reads beside Smith's public-buildings treatise.
