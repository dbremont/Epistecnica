## Notes

> Étienne-Jules Marey's La machine animale — the living body analysed as a machine: the graphic method applied to walking, running and flight, with the recording instruments he devised to trace the limbs and the pressures, and the comparisons with machines that move. The physiological theory of the same evidence Muybridge photographed, and the point at which the study of movement became an instrumented science.

> The 1873 first edition and the 1886 edition are both scanned.
