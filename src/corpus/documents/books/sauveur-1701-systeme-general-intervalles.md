## Notes

> Joseph Sauveur's Système général des intervalles des sons — the harmonic series named and the intervals derived from it: the overtones heard in a sounding string, the beats and the nodes, and music given a physical foundation rather than a numerical convention. The 1701 memoir that brought the overtone series into music theory, and the beginning of the acoustic measurement the guide's later books assume.
