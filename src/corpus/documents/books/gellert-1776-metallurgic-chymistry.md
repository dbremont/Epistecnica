## Notes

> Christlieb Ehregott Gellert's Metallurgic Chymistry — the mineralogy of the ores and the arts that rose from them: the classification of minerals, the furnace and the crucible, the assay and the calculation of the metal, the smelting and the refining. The 1776 English translation of the Freiberg professor's system documents the metallurgical laboratory as the place where the ore was tested before the works, and the chemical education of the eighteenth-century metallurgist.
