## Notes

> Richard Herring's paper account — the materials, the historical processes and the industrial papermaking of the mid-century trade: rag and the new fibres, the machine, the mills' economy. The papermaking literature's own mid-Victorian account — a decade whose mills the paper trade mechanized, carried in the same book-maker's form the print trades bought.
