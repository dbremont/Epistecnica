## Notes

> Charles Frederick Mitchell's Building Construction and Drawing — the whole craft taught as a course: the foundations and the walls, the floors and the roofs, the joinery and the ironwork, each detailed and drawn for the student, with the specification and the drawing office practice that made a building buildable. The 1902 textbook is the boundary record of the nineteenth-century construction it codified, entered at that year because it is the verified edition.

> The 1902 first edition is scanned.
