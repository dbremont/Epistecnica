## Notes

> Kunckel — Brandenburg court alchemist, director of the Potsdam glassworks — took the Neri tradition and worked it experimentally: the Neri and Merrett text translated and corrected, and then his own German practice set beside it, including the gold-ruby glass he observed from gold–tin chloride and the melting behaviour of lead glass. The 1679 folio is the founding experimental glass-chemistry book of the German trade.
