## Notes

> Heinrich Hertz demonstrated the electric waves with his oscillator: the spark gap made a resonant system, the wave measured and standing before the mirror, the reflection, refraction and polarization of electromagnetic radiation established in laboratory form. The Maxwell theory was received as experiment in these papers — the spark transmitter from which Marconi's radio came.

> The 1893 Jones translation is scanned; the posthumous German Untersuchungen of 1894 is scanned too.
