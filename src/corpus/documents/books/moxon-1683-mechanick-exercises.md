## Notes

> Joseph Moxon's doctrine of handy-works — printing, joinery, smithery and the practical crafts explained from inside their own shops, printed in monthly installments 1677–84: the trades' own accounts in their own trade idiom, a century before the Encyclopédie plates made crafts' documentation the century's project. The source the mechanical-arts literature traces behind everything the guide collects; it predates the stated period and stays as a foundational reference.
