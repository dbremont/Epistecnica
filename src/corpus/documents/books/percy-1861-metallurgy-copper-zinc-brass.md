## Notes

> The non-ferrous volumes of John Percy's Metallurgy — copper, zinc and brass treated with the same furnace-by-furnace detail the iron and steel volumes gave the ferrous trades: the smelting works of Cornwall and Swansea, the ore processes and the brass manufacture. The series' extractive-chemistry foundation extends here beyond iron, through the 1860s printings the archive carries.
