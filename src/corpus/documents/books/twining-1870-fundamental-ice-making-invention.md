## Notes

> Alexander Catcott Twining's ice-making papers — the artificial-ice claim that opened the refrigeration machinery era: vacuum-evaporation apparatus turning water into ice by the sweep of the vapor pump, patented and fought over in the 1870s while the ice trade still carved its product from ponds. This is the food-preservation literature's moment of transition from Appert's heat to cold's industrial economy.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of the Twining specification has been verified here; the patent literature lives in institutional collections and the chase is open.
