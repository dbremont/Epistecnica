## Notes

> Johann Beckmann's Beyträge zur Geschichte der Erfindungen — the history of inventions argued from the documents: the printing press, the compass, gunpowder, the mills and the trades, each traced through the sources rather than the legend, with the errors of the received accounts corrected. The book that made the history of technology a subject, and the ancestor of the guide the reader is holding.

> The third and fourth volumes are scanned from the BSB copy; the set is one publication project and one entry.
