## Notes

> The Useful-Information series — Sir William Fairbairn's collected notes for the profession: boiler construction, the strength of materials, the experiments and the industrial-machinery papers the consulting practice produced, issued in successive series to 1866. The engineer's workshop economy in its own collected columns — the practical side of the mills-and-millwork volumes.
