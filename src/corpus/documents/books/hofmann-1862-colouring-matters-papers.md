## Notes

> August Wilhelm von Hofmann's researches on the coal-tar colouring matters — the aniline dyes worked out systematically at the Royal College of Chemistry: the constitution of the bases, the reactions that produced the colours, and the programme that turned Perkin's accidental mauve into a research field. The science beneath the dye industry the guide's textile books assume.

> The papers are scanned as journal articles; the Philosophical Transactions and Chemical Society runs carry them.
