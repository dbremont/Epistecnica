## Notes

Malthus's essay is the founding statement of the population–subsistence problem: population grows geometrically while the means of subsistence grow arithmetically, so checks must operate. Its place on an agricultural list is the reverse of a compliment — it made the productivity of the land the central constraint of political economy.
