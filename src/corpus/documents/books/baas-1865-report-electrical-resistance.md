## Notes

> The British Association's standards-committee report — the new unit of electrical resistance its committee determined and proposed: the absolute ohm of electromagnetic measure, the work of the committee whose members — Jenkin, Maxwell, the Astronomer Royal's instruments — ran the wire-and-coil experiments the report printed. Electrical metrology's founding document. (The outline's attribution to Joseph Whitworth is corrected: the committee carried this work, not the machine-tool engineer, whose standardization belongs to the threads paper of 1841–58.)
