## Notes

> J. N. Shoolbred's volume on the permanent way and the rolling stock — the rails and the sleepers and the fastenings, the locomotives and the carriages, the signals and the working, compared across the railways of Europe and reported for the British Association. The technical comparison that standardized the railway, and the survey of the state of the art at the point the block system and the continuous brake arrived.
