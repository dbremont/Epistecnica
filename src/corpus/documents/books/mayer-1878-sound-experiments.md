## Notes

> Alfred Marshall Mayer's experiments — the phenomena of sound demonstrated with things a household could own: water glasses, clock vibrators, the simple apparatus that made acoustic physics reproducible beyond the Royal Institution's budget. The acoustic-apparatus literature of the guide in its most portable form, experimental acoustics as public practice.
