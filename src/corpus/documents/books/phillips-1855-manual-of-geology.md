## Notes

> John Phillips's manual — the Geological Survey's uncle-teacher of geology: practical and theoretical ordered as one discipline's instruction, written by the nephew of William Smith and the surveyor who gave the era its stratigraphic nomenclature. The geological-surveying branch's 1855 reference beside Ansted's manual, standing where the field sciences met the museum.
