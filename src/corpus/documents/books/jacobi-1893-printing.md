## Notes

> Charles Thomas Jacobi's treatise — typography, composition, printing operations and the technical terminology of the printer's art, carried by the Chiswick Press's own foreman of the trade. The typographic literature's professional close — the art-of-printing book whose editions the trade's composing rooms ran into the new century.
