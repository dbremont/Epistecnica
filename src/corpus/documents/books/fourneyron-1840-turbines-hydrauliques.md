## Notes

> Fourneyron turned the waterwheel inside-out: the water admitted inward through a fixed guide and discharged from a moving wheel, the whole of the fall recovered by a rotating runner — the reaction turbine, proven at Fraisans in 1833 and carried in this Memoire adapted from the prize paper. It begins the turbine tradition whose hydraulic criticism Francis's Lowell experiments later supplied.
