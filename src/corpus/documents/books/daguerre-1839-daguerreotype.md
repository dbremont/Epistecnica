## Notes

> Louis Jacques Mandé Daguerre's historical manual — the daguerreotype's own founding description: the mirror-image economy whose silver-plated copper the photography branch opened, printed exactly the same year as the Talbot Pencil. The daguerrean side's own founder, from the Paris inventor's own notebook economy.
