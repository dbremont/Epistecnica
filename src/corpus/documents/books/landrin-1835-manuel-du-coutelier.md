## Notes

> Henri Landrin's coutelier manual — the cutler's art in the Manuels-Roret series: blade forging, grinding, hafting and the assembly of the wares, the French trade's own documented economy where Sheffield's directories never printed a doing-book. The edge-tools and cutlery branch's founding process manual, beside the glass trade's verrier manual the series paired.
