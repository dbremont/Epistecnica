## Notes

> Robert Whymper's Cocoa and Chocolate — the chemistry and manufacture of a confectionery commodity: the bean and its fermentation, the roasting and the grinding, the alkalizing and the conching, and the works process written up with the analyses. The boundary record of the nineteenth-century chocolate manufacture it inherited, entered at 1912 because that is the verified edition.

> The 1912 first edition is scanned from the Cornell set.
