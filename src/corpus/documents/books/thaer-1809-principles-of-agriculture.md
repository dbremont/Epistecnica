## Notes

> Albrecht Daniel Thaer's principles — the systematic agriculture of the Möglin school: crop rotations, manure economies, cultivation and the rational management of the estate, arranged into two volumes that gave practical agriculture its first complete system. Written at the root where agricultural improvement met the sciences, the work through whose German and English reprints Liebig's polemic found readers.
