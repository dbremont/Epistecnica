## Notes

> The Overman Wheel Company's Victor Bicycles and Tricycles — the American trade catalogue of 1886: the high-wheel bicycles and the tricycles, their frames and wheels, the prices and the specifications, and the accessories the dealer stocked. The catalogue documents the bicycle before the safety frame, when the tricycle was still a serious road vehicle and the trade was learning to sell a precision machine.

> The 1886 catalogue is scanned.
