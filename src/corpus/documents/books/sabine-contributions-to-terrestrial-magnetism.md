## Notes

> The series of Edward Sabine's *Contributions to Terrestrial Magnetism* — no single book, but the run of papers carrying the magnetic survey of the globe's observatory records into the Royal Society's Transactions decade by decade: the declinations, inclinations and intensities measured at the network of stations the Cosmos program built. Geomagnetism's founding instrument record in its original serial form.

> The scans below carry single numbers of the run; the series lives across the journal's volumes.
