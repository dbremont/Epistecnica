## Notes

> Thomas Roger Smith's rudimentary treatise — acoustics as a practical architectural problem rather than a physical science: lecture halls, churches, theatres, the shape and material of the acoustical building. The work the guide's architectural-acoustics branch anchors on; its author expanded it within a few years as *Acoustics in Relation to Architecture and Building*, the edition the scan below carries.
