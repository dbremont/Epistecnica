## Notes

> Hooke set the spring as a law: the extension of a spring is proportional to its pulling force — the physisis of the strain-energy idea and of the spring balance in the account of the balance-spring watch. The first explicit elastic-law statement, printed while Hooke tested the tough ribs of the Dynamo compendium.
