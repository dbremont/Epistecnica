## Notes

> Michael Faraday's Chemical Manipulation — the bench operations taught as a craft: the balance and the glass-blowing, the filtration and the crystallization, the furnace and the blowpipe, and the small apparatus a chemist makes for himself. The book that made the laboratory a place one can work in, and the bench companion the industrial chemist kept beside the systematic treatises.

> The 1827 first edition and the 1831 second are both scanned.
