## Notes

> Friedrich Accum's gas-light treatise —coal-gas manufacture's own founding English instruction: the theory and practice of the economical uses of gas, written by the chemist whose apparatus and disclosures carried Winsor's industry into the streets. The gas-manufacturing process and its apparatus — the outline's 1812 process-description pamphlet is the treatise's own predecessor within this literature — the chemical-manufacturing branch's lighting industry.
