## Notes

> The collected papers of Joseph von Fraunhofer — optical glass, achromatic lens work and the solar spectrum's dark lines: the optical-instrument maker's own papers, published 1888 by the Bavarian academy from a Munich career whose dispersion measurements made spectroscope science possible. The optical-glass literature a direct paper source at physics' foundation — outside the 1600–1900 reading period only in print date, not in work.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan is verified here; the collected papers live in the academy's holdings and the chase is open.
