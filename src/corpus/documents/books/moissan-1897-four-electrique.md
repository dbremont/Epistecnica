## Notes

> Henri Moissan built his electric furnace and worked its high temperatures — the carbides, silicides, phosphides of his chemistry of extreme temperature, the carbon chemistry of the carbide trade, the diamond synthesis of the same period. Le Four Electrique is the founding book of electric furnace chemistry, read into the furnace engineering and dolerite works of the later century.
