## Notes

> John Arthur Phillips's elements — metallurgical processes and practical extraction as one treatise: the ores, the furnaces and the assayed chemistry of extraction written by the practical chemical examiner whose name the Assay tradition ran. Extractive metallurgy's teaching text at the start of a period the Percy series documented at scale.
