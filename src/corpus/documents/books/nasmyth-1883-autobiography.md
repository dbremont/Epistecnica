## Notes

> James Nasmyth's autobiography — the machine shop described from inside by the man who ran it: the steam hammer and its invention, the shaping and planing machines, the foundry and the drawing office, and the Patricroft works in its Maudslay-derived order. The tool-making economy that the guide's workshop manuals describe from the outside, written by a maker whose machines the manuals assume.

> The 1883 first edition and the later printings are scanned; the autobiography is the entry the steam-hammer papers are read through.
