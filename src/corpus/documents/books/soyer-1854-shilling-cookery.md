## Notes

> Alexis Soyer's Shilling Cookery for the People — good food for the poor costed and taught: the cheapest cuts and the fuel economy, the soups and the stews, the quantities priced in shillings and pence, and the public kitchens Soyer had run in Dublin and the Crimea behind the book. The rare cookery book written for the people who could not buy cookery books, and the kitchen entered into public reform.

> Two 1854 copies are scanned.
