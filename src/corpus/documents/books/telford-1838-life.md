## Notes

> The life of Thomas Telford, published on the engineer's death in 1838 largely from his own reports and documents: the bridges, roads, canals and harbours of the era's greatest British civil engineer collected as a career's record. An engineering biography as document — the profession's first great life — whose value sits in its source material as much as its narrative.
