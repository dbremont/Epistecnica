## Notes

> The short book that turns the institutional argument of [*Economics of Shortage*](/view.html?p=documents/books/kornai-1980-economics-shortage-north-holland.md) into a formal macro model. Kornai builds a small model of a socialist economy around the shortage phenomenon — the firm's demand for inputs, the investment sector's expansion, and the adjustment that takes place through quantities rather than prices — and uses it to show how growth, shortage and efficiency are jointly determined.

> Its value to the corpus is methodological. It shows that the distinctive outcomes of the socialist economy do not require irrational planners or unusually bad managers: they follow from a few institutional rules — state ownership, soft budgets, quantity signalling — run through a consistent model. The same rules predict the observed combination of rapid extensive growth, chronic shortage, and low efficiency, and predict that the three cannot be traded off against one another within the system.
