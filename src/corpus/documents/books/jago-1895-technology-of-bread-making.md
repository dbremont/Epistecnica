## Notes

> William Jago's technology of bread-making — flour, dough and bread treated with a chemist's analytical apparatus: the chemistry of flour, the aeration and fermentation of dough, and the practical examination that made bread an industrial product of measurable inputs. The baking trade's process-science book at the end of the nineteenth century, in the German Technologie tradition applied to Britain's staple loaf.
