## Notes

> Thomas Box's mill-gearing treatise — the transmission of power through wheels, shafts and the mill's riggers, written for millwrights and engineers: the intermediate power-delivery system the mills ran while the machine-shop trades were developing their own stock. Written beside the same author's heat and strength treatises, the millwright's trade book the Spon rudimentary series carried.
