## Notes

> Otto Lilienthal's Der Vogelflug als Grundlage der Fliegekunst — bird flight measured and reduced to design: the wing's curvature and aspect, the loading per unit area, the angle of attack, the stability of the outstretched wing, and the tables of observations on storks, gulls and buzzards. The book in which flight became an experimental science, and the basis of the glider flights that followed it.

> The 1889 first edition is scanned (two copies).
