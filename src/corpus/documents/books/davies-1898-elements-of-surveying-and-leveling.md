## Notes

> Charles Davies's Elements of surveying and levelling — the West Point mathematics professor's textbook carried through the American surveying schools for half a century; its 1898 printing is a posthumous reissue of the series (Davies died in 1874), keeping the original field instruments' descriptions and the arithmetic of the survey in one volume. The surveying branch's entry point for American readers.
