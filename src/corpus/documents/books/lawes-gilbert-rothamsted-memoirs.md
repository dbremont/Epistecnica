## Notes

> The Rothamsted memoirs of John Bennet Lawes and Joseph Henry Gilbert — the classical series of agricultural-chemistry and plant-nutrition experiments that ran half a century from 1843: the ash of the wheat plant, the soils' mineral supply and exhaustion, the manures' long-term field answers to the laboratory's box experiments. The experimental plant nutrition of the century's agricultural sciences, carried to the Royal Society's Transactions in serial form and collected in Rothamsted's own memoir volumes.
