## Notes

> John Loudon McAdam's remarks — the 1820 argument that founded the road-science literature: the broken-stone cambered pavement, its drainage and its maintenance economy directed against the heavy-stone and paving tradition. Road construction and materials could then be documented as an engineer's method; the turnpike trusts that commissioned him carried the theory to the whole of Britain's roads within the decade.
