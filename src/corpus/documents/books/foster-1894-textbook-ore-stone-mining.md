## Notes

> Clement Le Neve Foster's text-book — the mining operations and extraction techniques of ore and stone mining as a school text: surveying, sinking, working, dressing the ore and the assay, written by the Royal School of Mines professor whose mine-inspectorate career the district's records knew. The mining branch's institutionalized close-of-century reference.
