## Notes

> Daniel Kinnear Clark's steam treatise — the engine and boiler construction and operation the working designer wrote after the *Railway Machinery* the collection holds: Rankine's theory and Thurston's boiler manual bridged by the constructive account the plants themselves ran on. The steam shelf's late-century practical volume, beside Bourne and the Catechism.
