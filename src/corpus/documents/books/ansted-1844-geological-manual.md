## Notes

> David Thomas Ansted's geological manual — the field investigation and classification of the earth's structure carried into one instruction, written by the King's College professor and consultant whose geological practice ran from the mining districts through the Railway era. Entered as the geological-surveying branch's first manual beside Phillips's 1855 survey-era successor.

> Honest record: no archive.org scan of this exact 1844 manual has been verified here; the same author's later geological work does circulate, and the manual is catalogued but untouched.
