## Notes

> The Admiralty Manual of Scientific Enquiry, edited by John Herschel — the naval officer given the methods of the sciences: the astronomy and the magnetism, the meteorology and the tides, the geology and the natural history, the ethnology and the instruments, each chapter written by a specialist and addressed to a captain at sea. The manual by which a navy produced scientific knowledge as a by-product of command, and the instruction book the survey and exploring voyages carried.

> The 1851 first edition and the 1859 edition are scanned.
