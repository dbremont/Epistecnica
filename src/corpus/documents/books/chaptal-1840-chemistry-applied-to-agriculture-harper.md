## Notes

> Jean-Antoine Chaptal — chemist, manufacturer, Napoleon's minister of the interior — wrote *Chimie appliquée à l'agriculture* (Paris, 1823) as the statesman's accounting of what chemistry owes farming and farming owes chemistry. The Harper edition translated it for the American market and prefixed Davy's chapter on the organization and structure of plants, binding the French industrial chemistry of the age to England's agricultural-chemistry canon in one volume.
