# C. F. Cross, E. J. Bevan & C. Beadle (1895). Cellulose: An Essay on the Structure, Chemistry, and Manipulation of Cellulose. London.

```bibtex
@book{cross1895cellulose,
  title={Cellulose: an essay on the structure, chemistry and manipulation of cellulose},
  author={Cross, C. F.; Bevan, E. J.},

  publisher={London: Longmans},

```

## Notes

> Cross, Bevan and Beadle made cellulose a chemical subject rather than a thread: the xanthate manipulation with alkaline carbon disulphide — the viscose reaction that Chardonnet spun into the first man-made fibre — and the entire chemistry of the plant fibre carried in the chemical-industrial text. The treatise is the chemical reference behind the viscose rayon and the wood pulp chemistry of the paper trade.

> The authors also wrote the Text-Book of Paper-Making (1888; the scanned copy is the 1907 boundary printing).
