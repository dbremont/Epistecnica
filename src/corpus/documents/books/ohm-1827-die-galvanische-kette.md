## Notes

> Ohm set the galvanic chain down as mathematics: the current proportional to the electromotive force and proportional to its resistance, the circuit a single law. Written outside the teaching apparatus he held — the Schulmeister of Cologne — the monograph took years to be received, and then became the founding circuit law of everything the telegraphic and electrical engineering sciences computed.
