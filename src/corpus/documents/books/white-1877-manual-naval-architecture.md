## Notes

> William Henry White's Manual of Naval Architecture — naval architecture taught to the officer: the displacement and the stability, the resistance and the propulsion, the rolling and the pitching, the materials and the structure, and the design of the hull as a course for the men who would command and inspect it. The standard text in which the iron ship was taught, written by the constructor who became the Navy's chief designer.

> The 1877 first edition and the 1894 edition are scanned.
