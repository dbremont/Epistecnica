## Notes

> Frederick James Britten's handbook — horology in dictionary-and-guide form for the working maker: the mechanism terms, the escapement's varieties and the repairs' arithmetic, through the editions the trade kept buying into the twentieth century. First issued in 1881, it is the trade's own standards reference where Reid's treatise had been its theory book.
