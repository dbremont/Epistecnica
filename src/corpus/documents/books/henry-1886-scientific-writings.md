## Notes

> The Scientific Writings of Joseph Henry — the collected papers of the American experimenter: the electromagnets and the intensity and quantity arrangements, the induced currents and the self-induction, the galvanometer and the telegraph, and the meteorological and Smithsonian work. The 1886 collection documents the American experimental tradition beside Faraday's, and the papers in which the electromagnetic apparatus became a system.

> The 1886 collection is scanned.
