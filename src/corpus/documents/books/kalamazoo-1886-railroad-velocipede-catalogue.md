## Notes

> The Kalamazoo Railroad Velocipede Company's illustrated catalogue — the railway inspection velocipede and the section, telegraph, push and inspection hand cars: the light wheeled vehicles the permanent way was patrolled with, their frames and wheels, the gearing and the brakes, and the prices. The catalogue documents the velocipede's industrial niche beside the bicycle, where the same light-frame and wheel technology served the railway.
