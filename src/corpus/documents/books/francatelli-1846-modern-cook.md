## Notes

> Charles Elmé Francatelli's Modern Cook — the Carême tradition translated into English practice: the sauces and the garnitures, the bills of fare for the seasons and the occasions, the confectionery and the ice, and the professional kitchen laid out in full. The transmission of the French brigade system into the English household and hotel, by the chef who cooked for the Queen.

> The 1860 and 1862 printings are scanned; the first edition is 1846.
