## Notes

> Jacob Leupold's general machine theatre — the first volume of the three-volume *Theatrum Machinarum*, the mechanical science and machine-construction theory that its two siblings (the hydrotechnico-raising and the mill volumes) would apply. The German engineer-mechanist's own machine science: mechanisms, the foundations of machine systems, written from the Leipzig instrument-maker's practice a generation before the eighteenth century's polytechnics.
