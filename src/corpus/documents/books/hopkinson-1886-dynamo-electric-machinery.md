## Notes

> John Hopkinson's dynamo-electric machinery paper — the magnetic circuit of the dynamo worked out and the machine made calculable: the magnetization curve, the series and shunt windings, and the design rules that replaced cut-and-try with a calculation. The paper layer beside Thompson's manual, and the reason the 1880s dynamo became an engineered product.

> The 1886 Philosophical Transactions paper and its 1892 continuation are both scanned.
