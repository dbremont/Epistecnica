# Salomon de Caus (1615). Les Raisons des Forces Mouvantes, avec diverses Machines tant utiles que puissantes. Frankfurt.

```bibtex
@book{decaus1615raisons,
  title={Les raisons des forces mouvantes avec diverses machines tant utilles que puissantes},
  author={de Caus, Salomon},
  year={1615},
  publisher={Frankfurt: Jan Norton},

```

## Notes

> Salomon de Caus, engineer at the courts of the Habsburgs, published from the physics of motion and its application: the forcing of water through gardens and grottos, the automata and the sounding organs worked by the moving force, and a machine in which the steam of boiling water raised a ball — the steam force stated before the engines were built. The book is the hinge between the machine-book genre and the theoretical treatment of moving forces.

> The 1615 Frankfurt printing is scanned.
