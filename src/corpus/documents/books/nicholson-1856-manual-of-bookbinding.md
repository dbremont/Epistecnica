## Notes

> James B. Nicholson's bookbinding manual — the binder's own craft instruction in full: sewing, forwarding, gilding, finishing and the marbled papers the trade's workshops produced. The bookbinding branch's founding English-language manual, beside the printing trade the guide's types.carried.
