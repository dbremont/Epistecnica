## Notes

> Carl Gustaf Patrik de Laval's steam-turbine papers and patents — the impulse turbine and the converging-diverging nozzle: the single wheel driven by a jet at high velocity, the flexible shaft that let it run, and the cream separator that first paid for it. The turbine's first practical form, before Parsons's reaction machine made it a central-station prime mover.
