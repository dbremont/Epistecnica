## Notes

> Jean-Antoine Claude Chaptal's Chimie appliquée aux arts — the chemical operations of the manufactures set out as one applied science: the fermentations, the dyeing, the bleaching, the soap and the glass, the metals and the salts, each with the works practice and the chemistry that explained it. The 1807 book that gave the manufactures a chemistry of their own, written by a chemist who was also a manufacturer and a minister.

> All four 1807 volumes are scanned from the Wellcome set.
