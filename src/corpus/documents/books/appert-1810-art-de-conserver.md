## Notes

> Nicolas Appert's method of preserving animal and vegetable substances for years — the heat treatment of sealed bottles that preceded Pasteur's germ theory by half a century and carried the canning industry out of the cellar workshop. Preserved as a technique with no theoretical account: what worked was published in 1810 after the French government bought the method for the navy; preservation science would come to explain it in its own century.
