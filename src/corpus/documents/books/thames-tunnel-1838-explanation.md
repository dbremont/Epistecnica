## Notes

> The Thames Tunnel Company's documents — the works of the tunnel explained and the directors' reports carried to the subscribers: the shield-driven tunnelling under the river documented as public-works literature, with the flooded works and the halt the 1828 report printed before the 1838 explanation resumed. Tunnelling's first great river crossing in its own company print — reportedly the origin the 1829 Sketches volume issued the works' pictures from.

> Honest record: the company documents themselves are catalogued rather than scanned; the surviving 1829 Sketches volume of the works is linked below and the chase is open.
