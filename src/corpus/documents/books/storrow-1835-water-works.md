## Notes

> Charles Storrow's water-works treatise — aqueducts, distribution systems and the hydraulic calculations of conveyance: the American water engineer's first manual, written by the Boston engineer whose Croton-era generation the profession ran. The waterworks-design literature's founding American document, before Fanning's professional treatise half a century later.
