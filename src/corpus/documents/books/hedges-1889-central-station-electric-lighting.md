## Notes

> Killingworth Hedges's central-station volume — the generating stations, feeders, mains and the arc-and-incandescent plant economy of the lighting trade's system decade: distribution as an engineered economy beside the lamp and dynamo. The electric-lighting literature's system-side manual, standing where the Edison-era stations opened.
