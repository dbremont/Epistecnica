## Notes

> Richard Wilson and Charles L. Grey's treatise — the mechanized printing presses, machine specifications and the newspaper-and-book letterpress economy documented at the trade's machine decade: the perfecting presses whose speed the metropolitan dailies ran. The printing-machinery literature's own trade account beside the typographic manuals.
