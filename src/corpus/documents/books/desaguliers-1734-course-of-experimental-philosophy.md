## Notes

> John Theophilus Desaguliers's course — the Royal Society's demonstrator teaching experimental philosophy as practical instruction: machine theory and practical machine use mixed through the two volumes, the waterworks, the friction machines and the mechanical powers carried to the lecture demonstration from then-current science. The experimental philosophy tradition's own published course — where natural philosophy's demonstrations met the machine practice the engineers of the next generation would buy.
