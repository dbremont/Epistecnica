## Notes

> Arthur Young's Course of Experimental Agriculture — the farm run as a register: five years of crops, soils, manures and yields recorded field by field across some three hundred acres, with the comparisons that made the results arguable. The experimental method the improving agriculture brought to the farm, and the book that stands before the agricultural chemistry the guide's Liebig and Johnston volumes teach.

> The 1770 first edition is scanned from the eighteenth-century collection copy.
