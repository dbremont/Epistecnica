# Jean Haudicquer de Blancourt (1697). De l Art de la Verrerie. Rouen. [English: The Art of Glass, 1699].

```bibtex
@book{blancourt1697verrerie,
  title={The art of glass: shewing how to make all sorts of glass, crystal and enamel},
  author={Blancourt, Haudicquer de},
  year={1697 (tr. 1699)},
  publisher={London: Starkey},
}
```

## Notes

> The French glass tradition set out in three parts — vitreous, crystal and enamel — the practice of the Saint-Gobain era beside the Neri-Kunckel line, with the melting of plate glass and enamel carried in its 1699 English translation.
