## Notes

> Joseph Priestley's history and present state of electricity — the first comprehensive account of the electrical experiments his century had run, together with the original ones Priestley performed at Warrington and Leeds in its preparation. The experimental foundations of the electrical literature the guide's later telegraphy books assume; the science its century had, put in order on the eve of Volta's pile.
