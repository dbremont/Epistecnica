## Notes

> Herman Haupt — Worcester railroad engineer, later chief of the military railroads — printed the truss as a course of demonstration: the strains on the members computed from the applied load, the arch and the suspension worked in the same grammar, the practice attached to the theory in details of joints, chords and spans. His General Theory is the American bridge engineering beside Whipple, the demonstration form the profession taught from.

> The scanned copy is a later (1869) printing of the 1851 treatise.
