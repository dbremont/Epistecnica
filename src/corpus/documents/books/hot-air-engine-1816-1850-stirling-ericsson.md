## Notes

> The Stirling and Ericsson hot-air engine patents and papers — the closed cycle with a regenerator, the external combustion and the working gas kept inside the machine: an engine line that promised safety and quiet and was displaced by steam, then recovered by the twentieth century for its efficiency. The patents document a principle worked out before its materials.
