## Notes

> Ludwig Boltzmann's Vorlesungen über Gastheorie — the kinetic theory taught as a system: the distribution, the collision integral, the H-theorem and the statistical reading of the second law, with the reversibility and recurrence objections met in the text. The theoretical completion of the heat doctrine the engine books assume, and the book from which the twentieth century's statistical mechanics started.
