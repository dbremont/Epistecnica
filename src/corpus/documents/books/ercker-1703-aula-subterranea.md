## Notes

> Lazarus Ercker's Aula Subterranea — the ores and the assays, the smelting and the refining, the mint and the works: the fullest early modern book on the testing and treatment of metals, with the furnaces, the crucibles and the procedures drawn and described. The 1703 German edition is the expanded later form of the 1574 treatise, and the book the assayer used long after Agricola.

> The 1703 edition is scanned; the 1574 treatise is the guide's earlier entry.
