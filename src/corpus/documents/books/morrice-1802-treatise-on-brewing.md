## Notes

> Alexander Morrice's treatise exhibits — as its subtitle claims — the whole process of the brewer's art and mystery: materials, mashing, fermentation, the vessels and the economy of the brewery, four decades after Combrune and on the eve of the industrial common brewery. The process documented end to end, the brewery as a production system rather than a theory-observation pair.
