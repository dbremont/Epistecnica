## Notes

> William Gilbert's De Magnete — the geomagnetic branch's founding text: the earth itself a magnet, the loadstone experiments and the compass observation economy where terrestrial magnetism began. Standing as Telluris's own marked open, a founder beside Gauss's absolute measure and Sabine's series.
