## Notes

> Willem Jacob 's Gravesande's Physices elementa mathematica — the Newtonian philosophy taught through apparatus: each proposition demonstrated with the machine built for it, the air-pump, the pulley, the inclined plane and the optical bench, in the Leiden cabinet that became a model for Europe. The book that documents the instrument cabinet as a teaching technology.

> Both 1720 volumes are scanned from the Wellcome copy, with the 1725 supplement.
