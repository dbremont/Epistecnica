## Notes

> Edward Lewis Attwood's Text-book of Theoretical Naval Architecture — the theory of the ship laid out for the student: the geometry of the hull and the integration of its properties, the stability at all angles, the resistance and the propulsion, the rolling and the strength, and the calculations of the drawing office made a curriculum. The boundary form in which nineteenth-century naval architecture was handed to the twentieth, entered at 1899 as the verified edition.

> The 1899 first edition and a later printing are scanned.
