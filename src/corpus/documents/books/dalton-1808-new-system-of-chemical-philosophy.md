## Notes

> John Dalton's New System — the atomic theory the industrial chemistry practically assumed: the elements' weights, the compounds' proportions and the Manchester tradition alongside the mechanical-arts trades it taught. The theoretical frame beneath Ure's and Knapp's industrial handbooks.
