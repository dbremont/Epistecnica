## Notes

> Vannoccio Biringuccio's Pirotechnia — the founding metallurgical manual of the period, sixteen years ahead of Agricola: the ores and their assays, the smelting and casting of metals, the founders' small-shop practice, gunpowder and fireworks, and the distilling that the book's last books turn to. The work Agricola's systematic survey answered, and the reason the metallurgical branch opens before 1556 rather than with it.

> The guide's lower bound moves to 1500 in this change set so the pre-1600 founding layer is stated rather than smuggled in; the 1540 first edition is scanned from the copy held at the Getty.
