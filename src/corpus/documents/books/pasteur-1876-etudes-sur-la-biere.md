## Notes

> Louis Pasteur's study of beer — the brewer's fermentations and maladies treated as the experimental ground on which the germ theory of fermentation reached its industrial form: the causes of spoilage, the oxygen question, the heating procedure that carried his name into the brewery and demonstrated the commerce of preservation as applied microbiology. The book that turned Appert's verified technique into explained process, at the moment brewing became a science.
