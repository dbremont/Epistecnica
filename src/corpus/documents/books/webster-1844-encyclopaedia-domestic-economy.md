## Notes

> Thomas Webster and Mrs. William Parkes's domestic encyclopædia — the household production, equipment and practical manufactures of the Victorian home: the housekeeping processes, the apparatus and the economy of running a mid-century English house. The domestic-economy literature beside the manufactures — industrial technology's household side, one alphabet of its own.
