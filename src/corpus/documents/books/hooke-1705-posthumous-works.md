## Notes

> Robert Hooke's posthumous works — the Cutlerian lectures and the discourses read before the Royal Society, collected after his death: the spring and the watch escapement, the air-pump and the meteorological instruments, the mechanical inventions and the experiments that the Society's own programme ran on. The collector that holds the mechanical papers the guide's Mechanica and Instrumentalis branches otherwise cite only in fragments.

> The 1705 first edition is scanned from two copies; the modern edition of the same collection is also held.
