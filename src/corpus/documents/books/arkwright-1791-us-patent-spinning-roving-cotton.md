## Notes

> Richard Arkwright's American patent for spinning and roving cotton — the water-frame invention in its primary patent form: the drawing of the machine and the specification of its parts, granted to William Pollard as assignee of Arkwright's English patents. The 1791 grant is the scanned primary document of the spinning frame that founded the factory system — the specification the histories of the cotton manufacture describe but the British Patent Office's own early texts have left unscanned.

> Honest record: the British 1769 water-frame patent, Hargreaves's 1770 jenny and Crompton's 1779 mule specifications are not verified in archive scans here and are recorded as rows in this section.
