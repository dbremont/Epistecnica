## Notes

> Thomas Edward Thorpe's dictionary of the applied chemistry of the 1890s — the new industries of dyes, fertilizers, electrochemistry and industrial synthesis carried into reference form as the century closed. The successor in the Ure tradition, at the far end of the period the guide's third stage names: engineering sciences, industrial chemistry, electrical technology and systematic manufacturing.
