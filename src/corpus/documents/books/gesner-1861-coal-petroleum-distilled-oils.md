## Notes

> Abraham Gesner's practical treatise on coal, petroleum and other distilled oils — the mineral oils described as a manufacture before the Pennsylvania wells made them a flood: the distillation, the refining, the lamps and lubricants, and the kerosene that displaced the whale oil. The founding technical account of the petroleum industry, written by the man who had patented the process a decade earlier.

> The 1861 first edition and the 1865 second are both scanned.
