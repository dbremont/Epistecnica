## Notes

> Marcellin Berthelot's Chimie organique fondée sur la synthèse — organic chemistry rebuilt on synthesis: the compounds made in the laboratory from their elements and simpler bodies, and the argument that the living organism held no special power over matter. The theoretical turn that made the synthetic dye industry and the later industrial organic chemistry conceivable.
