## Notes

> Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel's Gradmessung — the arc-measurement of East Prussia and its connection to the Prussian and Russian triangulation chains, printed at Berlin in 1838: the determination of the figure of the earth from geodetic measurement that the discipline's *Bessel ellipsoid* carried for a century. The authoritative source of the gravimetry-and-geodesy branch's original determination; the user outline's "On the Determination of the Figure of the Earth" resolves here.
