## Notes

> Henry Booth's Account of the Liverpool and Manchester Railway — the first inter-city railway described in its works: the parliamentary fight, the survey and the construction, the locomotives and the traffic, and the organization that made a public railway run. The primary record of the line that opened the railway age, written by its treasurer and locomotive designer.
