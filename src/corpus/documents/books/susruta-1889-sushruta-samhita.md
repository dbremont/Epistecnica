## Notes

> The Susruta Samhita carries the surgical book of the Indian tradition: the instruments — the knives, the saws, the forceps, the tubes and the caustic — the operations of rhinoplasty, lithotomy and the excision of tumours, and the pre- and post-operative regimen. The dating of the text is contested; the 1889 edition is the Sanskrit text scanned here.
