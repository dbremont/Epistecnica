## Notes

> Cipriano Piccolpasso, the Castel Durante potter, wrote the whole majolica shop into three books — the clays, the turning and the moulds, the tin glaze, the lead calcination, the colours and the art of the pigments, the forms of the kilns — all drawn. The manuscript of the sixteenth century was printed in Rome in 1857, the earliest illustrated manual of the terracotta and glaze trades.

> The scanned copy is the 1857 Rome printing.
