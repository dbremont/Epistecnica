## Notes

> James Hutton's Theory of the Earth — the earth read as a system with a history: the erosion and the deposition, the consolidation and the uplift, the cycles that required an unimaginable time, and the argument that the present processes explain the past. The geological founding text the guide's Lyell assumes, and the book that made the earth an object with a history rather than a stage.

> The 1899 edition is scanned; the 1795 first edition is the entry's year.
