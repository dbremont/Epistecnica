## Notes

> Henry Richardson Procter's text-book — the conversion of skins into leather as one documented discipline: hide chemistry, tanning materials, the tannins and the tannery operations, written by the Yorkshire College professor whose leather-chemistry teaching the industry institutionalized. The tanning branch's chemistry decade closing Dussauce's manufacturing treatise thirty years earlier.
