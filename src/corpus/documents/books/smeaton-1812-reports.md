## Notes

> The reports of John Smeaton — the civil engineer's commissioned answers on bridges, harbours, lighthouses, canals and water engineering, collected posthumously in four volumes from 1812: engineering advice as a documented practice, containing the working arithmetic of consulting civil engineering generations before the profession organized. The first English literature of civil engineering practice as advice.
