## Notes

> The Niles general machinery catalogue — the industrial machine tools of the Hamilton, Ohio works: milling, boring, planing, shaping, grinding, punching and gear-cutting machines, priced and illustrated for the buyers the trade ran. The machine-tool trade catalogue beside the instrument catalogues the collection holds — what the machine shops actually bought.
