## Notes

> [**Construction systems and the examination syllabus**] Compares the 1599 masons' ordinances of Mexico City with those of Seville, and analyses the construction systems contained in the chapters that were decreed as examination topics for the certification of master builders. Drawing on architectural treatises known at the time and on records of building activity, it explains the solutions New Spanish builders could give to each technical problem in the ordinances — a technical-historical memoir of architectural practice in the capital until the Real Academia de San Carlos.
