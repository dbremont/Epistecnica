## Notes

> [**Examination practices**] A long-duration social and cultural history of guild examination in New Spain: how artisans entered and left the trades, the means they used to control the examination, and the networks they built against the monopolistic practices of the Mexico City guilds. It is a cross-trade study rather than a monograph on a single guild.

> [**Relevance**] Very high. It describes the everyday workings of apprenticeship, examination, and access to mastery in the capital of New Spain across the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.
