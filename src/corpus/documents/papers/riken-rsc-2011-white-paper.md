## Notes

> The advisory-council white paper and activity report for the RIKEN SPring-8 Center, covering the centre's organisation, the SACLA X-ray free-electron laser project, the photon-science research programme, and the centre's role as a national and international user facility.

> It is a useful primary document for the part of RIKEN that is not a laboratory system but a large-scale infrastructure operator: the centre model, the advisory-council layer, and the way a national facility is justified to users and funders.
