## Notes

> A formal assessment, in a stock–flow-consistent model, of what would happen if the euro area adopted a **euro-bancor**: a clearing mechanism that would recycle the surpluses of the creditor countries and relieve the deflationary pressure on the debtors. The model has the sectors and balance sheets that a stock–flow-consistent framework requires, so the exercise can trace the financial consequences of the reform rather than only its accounting.

> The result is that the clearing mechanism improves the debtors' position and stabilises output, but the magnitude depends on the rules: how much credit the clearing union extends, at what rate, and whether the creditors are obliged to adjust. The paper is the technical companion to the political argument, and it shows that “a clearing union would help” is a claim whose size depends entirely on the parameters.
