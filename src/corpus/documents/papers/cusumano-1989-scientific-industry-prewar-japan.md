## Notes

> A chapter in the standard business-history collection on prewar Japanese enterprise, read here for its account of "scientific industry" as a strategy rather than a slogan. Cusumano connects research organisation, technology strategy and entrepreneurship in firms and institutes that treated science as a source of industrial capability.

> The chapter is a useful external check on the RIKEN story: it places the institute's industrial turn in the wider prewar Japanese pattern of science-based enterprise, where research capability, patenting and commercial ventures were being assembled together.
