## Notes

> A archival reconstruction of Joji Sakurai's role in founding and running RIKEN. The familiar story credits Takamine with the idea and Shibusawa with the mobilisation, but neither was in a position to run the institute: Takamine was based in the United States, and Shibusawa was not a scientist. Yamanaka shows that Sakurai designed the organisational framework, recruited chemists and physicists, solicited industrial donations, negotiated with officials, handled land purchase and personnel, and served as acting director.

> The article is a useful corrective to a purely heroic founding narrative. It also documents the early financial strain and the internal conflicts that followed, which is the background to Okochi's later reforms.
