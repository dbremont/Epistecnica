## Notes

> Ethereum generalises the ledger into a machine: a blockchain that carries a Turing-complete virtual machine, so that the rules attached to an entry can be executed by the ledger itself. A transfer can be made conditional, a payment split automatically, an entitlement extinguished on a date — without an intermediary to perform the step.

For property the move is that the incidents travel inside the instrument. Where a share certificate states rights that a company's law and officers make effective, a token can encode them: transfer restrictions, fractions, royalty splits, voting weights. The register stops merely recording the holding and starts executing it.

The limit is the one the note already states: a machine enforces what the machine can see. Conditions that depend on facts outside the chain — a court's judgment, a boundary, a physical delivery — still need an oracle or an institution, so the code carries the incidents only as far as its inputs reach. That is why the token is a title instrument of unprecedented precision and still not a regime.
