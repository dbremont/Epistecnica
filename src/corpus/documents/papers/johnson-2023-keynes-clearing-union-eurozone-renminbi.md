## Notes

> A survey of the clearing-union idea from Keynes's 1943 proposal through the euro area to the internationalisation of the renminbi. The paper's thread is that the clearing-union design keeps returning whenever a monetary order produces persistent surplus–deficit imbalances that the adjustment mechanism does not correct, and that the renminbi's international role raises the same questions again.

> **Caveat on the venue.** The request cited this as the *Journal of Economic Literature*. The article is in the **Journal of Economics Library** (econsciences.com, ISSN 2149-2379), a different and much smaller journal, not the American Economic Association's *Journal of Economic Literature*. The entry records the venue the article actually carries.
