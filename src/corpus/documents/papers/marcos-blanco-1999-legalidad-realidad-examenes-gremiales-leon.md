## Notes

> [**Legal requirement and production control**] In sixteenth-century León the guild examination was more than a mandatory administrative requirement for practising certain trades: its legal configuration also made it an effective instrument for controlling production, both for guilds and for artisan collectives that were not constituted as corporate associations, at a moment of economic crisis.

> [**Relevance**] High. It shows the distance between the legal rules and the actual examination practices, and how examination served economic as well as professional ends.
