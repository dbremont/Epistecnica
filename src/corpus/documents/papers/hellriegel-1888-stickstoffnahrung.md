## Notes

Hellriegel and Wilfarth demonstrated that legumes obtain nitrogen through root nodules and their bacterial symbionts, solving the puzzle of why legumes thrive on poor soil. It is the discovery that makes the rotation, the green manure and the nitrogen cycle agricultural facts.
