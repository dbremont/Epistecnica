## Notes

> [**An unpublished examination book**] Publishes a partial transcription, preserved in a judicial record, of the Zaragoza guild's examination book: the master's examination requests in the four trades, the examination pieces imposed on candidates, and the oath dates of those admitted between 1718 and 1768. No other registers of the guild survive. The article compares the record with the ordinances and examines the conflict between the guild and the academic sculptors from 1740.

> [**Relevance**] Very high. It gives direct evidence of actual examination pieces and admission to mastery, and contrasts workshop training with the academic model.
