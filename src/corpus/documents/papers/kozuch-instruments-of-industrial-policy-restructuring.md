# Kożuch, M. Instrumenty polityki przemysłowej jako narzędzia restrukturyzacji polskiego przemysłu [Instruments of Industrial Policy as Tools of Restructuring Polish Industry].

> **Bibliographic caveat.** The venue and year of this item are **not verified**: the title does not appear in the standard Polish indexes under this form, and it is most likely a chapter in a collective volume from the Kraków academe of the 1990s. The verified sibling is Kożuch, M. (1997). *Ocena skuteczności funkcjonowania fiskalnych instrumentów polityki przemysłowej w Polsce*, Zeszyty Naukowe Akademii Ekonomicznej w Krakowie, 472, 29–43 — the fiscal-instruments article of the same research programme. Treat the entry as a pointer to the programme, not as a certified citation.

## Notes

> The subject is the **instrument set** of Polish industrial policy in the transformation: the menu of tools a government used to restructure an industry it had just stopped planning — tax preferences and exemptions, subsidies and grants, state guarantees and sureties, tariff and non-tariff protection, sectoral and enterprise restructuring programmes, settlement proceedings with creditors, and the institutions set up to administer them.

> Its analytic value now is as the **Polish-language record**: the transformation was documented extensively in the academic series of the time (Kraków, Wrocław, Warsaw), and much of it is invisible to English-language indexes — which is itself a finding about the literature, and the reason this entry carries a caveat rather than a confident citation.
