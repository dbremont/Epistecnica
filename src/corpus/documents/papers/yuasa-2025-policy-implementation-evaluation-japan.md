# Yuasa, T. (2025). The Current Situations and Issues of Policy Implementation and Evaluation in Japan: What Role Can Public Administration Studies Play?

> Bibliographic record: [CiNii Research — 1390022067668673152](https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390022067668673152).

## Notes

> The paper asks what **public-administration research** can contribute to policy implementation and evaluation in Japan — a question that sounds academic and is not, because it is about whether the field has a role in the machinery at all. Its object is the gap between the **evaluation system** (the ministry-set objectives and the ex-post assessments) and the **implementation practice** (what the agencies actually do), and its argument is that the discipline's contribution is not more indicators but a better account of how implementation works.

> The Japanese setting makes it concrete, and the corpus's [Public Policy Research Actors](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/public/polity/jpn/japan.md) section is its institutional map: GRIPS for policy research, the ministry institutes for the evidence base, and the evaluation regime the paper examines. Read together, the section is the *who* and the paper is the *what* — and the same tension appears in the [incorporated administrative agency](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/public/incorporated-administrative-agency.md) form, where the ministry that sets the objective is also the body that evaluates it.
