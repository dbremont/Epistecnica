## Citation Styles

> …
> 

| **Citation Style** | **In-Text Citation** | **Reference List Format** | **Primary Disciplines** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **APA (7th edition)** | (Author, Year, p. xx) | Author(s). (Year). *Title*. Publisher. DOI or URL (if applicable). | Psychology, Education, Social Sciences |
| **MLA (8th edition)** | (Author xx) | Author(s). *Title*. Publisher, Year. | Literature, Arts, Humanities |
| **Chicago (17th edition)** - **Notes and Bibliography** | Superscript number; Footnotes or Endnotes | Author(s). *Title*. Publisher, Year. | History, Arts, Social Sciences |
| **Chicago (17th edition)** - **Author-Date** | (Author Year, page) | Author(s). Year. *Title*. Publisher. DOI or URL (if applicable). | Sciences, Social Sciences |
| **Harvard** | (Author Year, p. xx) | Author(s). Year. *Title*. Publisher. DOI or URL (if applicable). | Sciences, Social Sciences |
| **Vancouver** | Superscript number or (Number) | Author(s). *Title*. Journal Name. Year;Volume(Issue):pages. DOI/PMID. | Medicine, Health Sciences |
| **IEEE** | [Number] | Author(s). “Title of Article,” *Journal Name*, vol. xx, no. xx, pp. xx-xx, Year. | Engineering, Computer Science |
| **AMA** | Superscript number or (Number) | Author(s). *Title*. Journal Name. Year;Volume(Issue):pages. DOI/PMID. | Medicine, Health Sciences |
| **ACS** | (Author, Year) or Superscript number | Author(s). *Title*. Journal Name. Year, Volume(Issue), pages. DOI. | Chemistry, Biochemistry |
| **Turabian (9th edition)** - **Notes-Bibliography** | Superscript number; Footnotes or Endnotes | Author(s). *Title*. Publisher, Year. | Various disciplines, similar to Chicago |
| **Turabian (9th edition)** - **Author-Date** | (Author Year, page) | Author(s). Year. *Title*. Publisher. DOI or URL (if applicable). | Sciences, Social Sciences |
