# Why study physics?

**Contains:** [How to Study Quantum Mechanics?](/view.html?p=notes/miscellanea/personal/how-to-study-quantum-mechanics.md)

> Physics is the most effective domain for developing **high-fidelity modeling skills** under conditions of strong feedback and constraint.
> 

> Physics is a training ground for rigorous modeling because it enforces precise, testable, and feedback-rich representations of reality. The value lies in transferring this epistemic discipline—rather than its specific models—into more complex domains like social systems.
> 

It provides:

- **Tight model–reality coupling**
    
    Predictions are quantitatively testable, enabling rapid error correction.
    
- **Explicit formalization**
    
    Systems are expressed in mathematical structures (e.g., differential equations, state spaces), forcing clarity in assumptions and mechanisms.
    
- **Controlled abstraction**
    
    One learns how to isolate variables, define idealizations, and progressively reintroduce complexity.
    

Compared to fields like Chemistry and Biology:

- Systems are less noisy and less historically contingent
- Feedback loops are faster and less ambiguous
- Model failure is easier to diagnose

### Transfer Principle

The core objective is not physics per se, but:

> **The acquisition of epistemic rigor in model construction, validation, and revision**
> 

This includes:

- Identifying state variables and constraints
- Distinguishing signal from noise
- Avoiding overfitting narratives to sparse data
- Iteratively refining models under empirical pressure

## How to study physics?

- Used books, papers, and problems; videos only for entertainment.
- Reality → Representation → Reality. (Keep at watch a reality).
