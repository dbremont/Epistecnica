## Structure

| **Date** | **Area (Subject)** | **Tags** | **Entry Type** | **Entry** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | e.g., Mathematics, Programming, Philosophy | #Concept, #Exercise, #Reflection, #Question, #Hard, #MorningSession | e.g., Concept, Exercise, Reflection, Question | Main content: notes, insights, solutions, reflections, or questions |

## Entry Type

| **Entry Type** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| Concept  (Theory) | Notes on key ideas, definitions, frameworks, and principles. |
| Summary | Condensed version of readings, lectures, or videos. |
| Exercise | Solved problems, practice questions, coding exercises, calculations. |
| Experiment | Hands-on activities, lab experiments, simulations, or projects. |
| Reflection | Thoughts on what worked, what was hard, and what you learned. |
| Self-assessment | Evaluate understanding, skill level, or progress on a topic. |
| Question | Unanswered questions, curiosities, or ideas to investigate further. |
| Research note | References, sources, links, or summaries of external materials. |
| Goal / Next Step | Intended objectives for the next session, skill targets, or action items. |
| Milestone | Achievements, completed projects, or skill improvements to track progress. |
|  Mental Representation | Notes or diagrams of internal models, schemas, or conceptual structures that guide understanding and skill development. |
