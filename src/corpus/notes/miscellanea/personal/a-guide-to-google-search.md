# A Guide To  Google Search


> …
> 

Here’s a table of common Google search operators and their functions:

| **Operator** | **Function** | **Example** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `""` (Quotes) | Searches for an exact phrase. | `"climate change"` |
| `-` (Minus) | Excludes a term from the search results. | `apple -fruit` |
| `site:` | Limits search results to a specific site or domain. | `site:example.com` |
| `intitle:` | Finds pages with a specific word in the title. | `intitle:"sustainable living"` |
| `inurl:` | Finds pages with a specific word in the URL. | `inurl:recipe` |
| `filetype:` | Searches for specific types of files. | `filetype:pdf "data analysis"` |
| `related:` | Finds sites related to a specific URL. | `related:bbc.com` |
| `OR` | Searches for pages that have one of several words. | `vacation OR holiday` |
| `*` (Asterisk) | Acts as a wildcard, matching any word. | `"best * for summer"` |
| `define:` | Provides definitions of a word or phrase. | `define:algorithm` |
| `cache:` | Shows the most recent cached version of a page. | `cache:nytimes.com` |
| `info:` | Provides information about a URL. | `info:cnn.com` |
| `allintitle:` | Finds pages with all specified words in the title. | `allintitle:health nutrition` |
| `allinurl:` | Finds pages with all specified words in the URL. | `allinurl:food safety` |
| `allintext:` | Finds pages with all specified words in the text. | `allintext:climate change` |
| `allinanchor:` | Finds pages with all specified words in the anchor text (links). | `allinanchor:solar energy` |
| `A..B` | Searches for a range of numbers. | `camera $50..$100` |
| `before:` | Searches for content published before a specific date. | `before:2024-01-01` |
| `after:` | Searches for content published after a specific date. | `after:2023-01-01` |
| `+` (Plus) | Ensures that a word is included in the search results (note: this is less commonly used now). | `+Google` |
