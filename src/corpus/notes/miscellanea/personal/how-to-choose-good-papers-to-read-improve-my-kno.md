# How to Choose Good Papers to Read & Improve My Knowledge?

**Contains:** [How to Study Quantum Mechanics?](/view.html?p=notes/miscellanea/personal/how-to-study-quantum-mechanics.md)

On choosing papers to read &  improve my Knowledge? 

- Choose papers that enhance and deepen my understanding, compress information, and generalize my current knowledge lattice;  making it more reliable, flexible, and robust.
- Choose papers that minimize the **Kolmogorov complexity** of my knowledge lattice—enhancing and deepening understanding, efficiently compressing information, and uncovering general principles to make it more reliable, flexible, and robust.
- Choose papers that maximize the **information gain** while minimizing redundancy, refining my knowledge lattice into a more reliable, flexible, and robust structure.
