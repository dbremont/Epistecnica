# Why Saying ‘We Will Explore Concept X’ Is Idiotic?


> **Concepts are not explored** - what is explored is what they represented or stand for.
> 

> Note: The same apply for others types of mental representation (ideas, …).
> 

> Concepts are used to parse reality; inquiry concerns the structure, dynamics, and constraints of what is parsed.
> 

> A concept is an epistemic abstraction that enables the identification and organization of real processes, relations, or structures; theoretical inquiry evaluates the adequacy of this abstraction against reality.
> 

## Case

- [ ]  ❌ Wrong : We explore the concept of labor.
- [x]  ✅ Right:  We investigate the real processes and relations denoted by “labor,” and evaluate the adequacy of different conceptualizations used to represent them.
