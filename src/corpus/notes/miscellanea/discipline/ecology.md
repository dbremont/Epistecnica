# Ecology

> **Ecology** is the scientific study of the interactions among organisms and their environments — how populations, communities and ecosystems are structured, how they change, and how energy and matter move through them.

## Scope

Ecology cuts the living world at the level of the **population, community and ecosystem**: the organism in its environment, the interactions among organisms, and the flows of energy, matter and information that sustain them. It is a biological discipline, but it borrows from earth science, statistics and modelling.

## Subfields

| Subfield | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Population ecology** | Population size, growth, regulation, and the interactions that bound them. |
| **Community ecology** | Species composition, competition, predation, mutualism, and community assembly. |
| **Ecosystem ecology** | Energy flow, nutrient cycling, productivity, and decomposition. |
| **Behavioural ecology** | How behaviour contributes to survival and reproduction. |
| **Landscape ecology** | Spatial patterns, connectivity, and processes across heterogeneous land. |
| **Conservation ecology** | Biodiversity loss, habitat fragmentation, restoration, and management. |
| **Evolutionary ecology** | How ecological interactions shape selection, adaptation, and diversification. |

## Methods

Field observation, manipulative field experiments, long-term monitoring, comparative studies, population and community models, and statistical inference from spatial and temporal data.
