# Business

> **Business** is the technical and organizational study of the firm, its management toolkit, and the demand-side relations through which value is produced, exchanged and consumed. It gathers the former `production/firm` and `production/consumption` shelves.

## Shelves

- [Firm](/view.html?p=notes/technica/business/firm.md) — the firm as a productive organization: formalization, agency, organization, modelling, production, value creation and technology.
- [Firm Template](/view.html?p=notes/technica/business/firm-template.md) — the structured template for modelling a firm across its microeconomic, institutional and capability layers.
- [Firm Ecosystem](/view.html?p=notes/technica/business/firm-ecosystem.md) — the supporting systems the firm depends on but does not own.
- [Toolkit](/view.html?p=notes/technica/business/toolkit/readme.md) — the generic and special management techniques used to run a firm.
- [Consumption](/view.html?p=notes/technica/business/consumption/readme.md) — buyers, consumers, value and the customer taxonomy.
