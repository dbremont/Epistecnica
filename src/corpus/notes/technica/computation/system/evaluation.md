# Evaluation

> Evaluation concerns the systematic examination of a software system to determine its behavior, characteristics, and degree of conformance to expected properties. It encompasses activities ranging from **monitoring and debugging** to **performance measurement and benchmarking**, as well as assessing **dependability, specification conformance, and overall quality control**.
