## Production Stages

| Step | Production Stage             |                                                                       Typical Duration |
| ---- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
| 1    | **Breeding**                 |                                                         ~9 months to pregnancy/calving |
| 2    | **Calving**                  |                                                                      Birth → 0–1 month |
| 3    | **Calf Rearing**             |                                                                    Birth → ~6–8 months |
| 4    | **Weaning**                  |                                                                            ~6–8 months |
| 5    | **Growing / stocker**        |                                                                           ~8–18 months |
| 6    | **Finishing**                |                                                                          ~18–24 months |
| 7    | **Slaughter**                |                                                                          ~24–30 months |
| 8    | **Breeding-cow replacement** | Heifers typically enter breeding around ~12–15 months; first calving around ~24 months |
