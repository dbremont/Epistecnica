# Foreign Exchange

> **Foreign exchange** (FX, forex) is the market for currencies, and a currency is the asset it trades: a claim on the issuing central bank, priced in another currency.

## Instruments

| Instrument | What it is | Settles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spot** | Immediate exchange, conventionally T+2 | Two business days |
| **Forward** | A [forward](forward.md) on a currency pair | A fixed future date |
| **FX swap** | A spot purchase with a simultaneous forward sale, or the reverse | The two legs |
| **Currency future** | A [future](future.md) on a currency pair | Exchange, with daily margin |
| **Currency option** | An [option](option.md) to exchange at a strike | At expiry |

A **currency pair** quotes the price of one currency in another (EUR/USD is dollars per euro). The market is the largest and most liquid in the world, and it is overwhelmingly over-the-counter.

## What drives a rate

- **Interest-rate differential** — the forward rate is the spot rate adjusted by the two rates (covered interest parity); the carry trade borrows the low-yielding currency to hold the high-yielding one.
- **Inflation and the real rate** — purchasing-power parity is the long-run anchor.
- **Balance of payments and flows** — trade and capital flows move the spot.
- **Reserve status** — a reserve currency is held by central banks for settlement and safety, which supports its demand.

## Currency and the corpus

The market structure — dealers, venues, settlement — is the [Forex market](/view.html?p=notes/social/coordinator/market/instance/forex/forex.md) in the social shelf; this note is the asset side. Cryptocurrencies are a separate digital asset, not a foreign currency.
