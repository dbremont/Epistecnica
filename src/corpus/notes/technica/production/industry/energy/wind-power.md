# 🌬️ Wind Power Industry

> The **wind power industry** designs, manufactures, installs and services wind turbines: the nacelle (drivetrain, generator, converter, controller), the rotor (blades, hub), the tower and the foundation. It is an assembly industry on top of a specialised supply chain — composites for blades, castings and forgings for the drivetrain, power electronics for the converter, control systems and software for the turbine — and its economics are set by the levelised cost of electricity, by the grid connection, and by the scale of the machine.

## 🏢 Firms

> The value chain has two layers that are often confused: the **OEMs**, who design and assemble the turbine and carry the warranty, and the **specialist suppliers**, who make the components and the control systems and sell to several OEMs. The suppliers are where the industrial capabilities concentrate — and, for a country building the industry, the more attainable entry point.

| Firm | Country | Position in the chain | What it supplies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vestas** | Denmark | OEM | Onshore and offshore turbines, in-house blade production, and the industry's largest installed base for service |
| **Siemens Gamesa** | Germany / Spain | OEM | Onshore and offshore turbines, blades, and service |
| **GE Vernova** | United States | OEM | Onshore and offshore turbines (Haliade-X) and the grid equipment beside them |
| **Nordex** | Germany | OEM | Onshore turbines, with a strong European and Latin American footprint |
| **Enercon** | Germany | OEM | Gearless direct-drive onshore turbines |
| **Goldwind** | China | OEM | Turbines, permanent-magnet direct-drive, and the Chinese market base |
| **ABB** | Switzerland / Sweden | Component supplier | Generators, power converters, grid connection and substations — see [ABB Ltd.](/view.html?p=notes/social/collective/actor/private/firm/abb-ltd.md) |
| **Beckhoff** | Germany | Automation supplier | PC-based control, PLC, I/O and drives — the controller platform a turbine or a blade factory can be built on |
| **Bachmann electronic** | Austria | Automation supplier | Wind turbine controllers, condition monitoring and SCADA, sold as a wind-specific control package |
| **LM Wind Power** | Denmark | Blade supplier | Blades for the OEMs |
| **ZF** | Germany | Drivetrain supplier | Gearboxes and drivetrain components |
| **TPI Composites** | United States | Blade supplier | Composite blades manufactured for OEMs |

> Read the table by position rather than by size. The OEMs compete on the whole machine and carry the balance-sheet risk of a 20-year warranty; the suppliers compete on a component and on the ability to serve several OEMs at once. ABB sits on the electrical side, Beckhoff and Bachmann on the control side — which is why the automation vendors appear in a wind table at all: a modern turbine is a controlled plant before it is a machine.
