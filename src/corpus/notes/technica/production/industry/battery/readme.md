# Battery Industry

> The **battery industry** converts mined and refined materials into electrochemical cells, modules and packs for vehicles, stationary storage, consumer devices and industrial equipment. It is a manufacturing industry built on a long, chemically specialised supply chain: lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite, manganese and phosphate move through refining, precursor and active-material production, cell manufacturing, pack assembly and, increasingly, recycling.

> The industry is not the technology. The technical field is the [Battery Technology Domain](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/battery-technical-domain.md); the industry is the firms, mines, refineries, gigafactories, pack plants, equipment suppliers and recyclers that own and operate it. Its two distinctive organisational forms are the [supply chain](/view.html?p=notes/technica/production/industry/battery/supply-chain.md) and the [industrial cluster](/view.html?p=notes/technica/production/industry/battery/cluster.md).

## Product space

| Product | Description | Main use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lithium-ion cells** | Rechargeable cells using lithium-ion chemistry in cylindrical, prismatic or pouch formats | EVs, storage, consumer electronics |
| **Lithium-iron-phosphate cells** | Lower-cost, safer lithium-ion chemistry with lower energy density | EVs, stationary storage |
| **Sodium-ion cells** | Alternative chemistry using sodium instead of lithium | Cost-sensitive storage and entry EVs |
| **Lead-acid batteries** | Mature, low-cost starting and backup batteries | Vehicles, UPS, telecoms |
| **Flow batteries** | Electrolyte stored in tanks and circulated through a stack | Long-duration stationary storage |
| **Modules and packs** | Cells integrated with cooling, sensing, enclosure and BMS | Vehicle and system integration |
| **Battery management systems** | Electronics and software monitoring voltage, current, temperature and state | Safety, performance and lifetime |
| **Recycled battery materials** | Recovered lithium, nickel, cobalt, copper, aluminium and graphite | Feedstock for new cells |

## Industry segments

| Segment | What it does | Representative firms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mining** | Extracts lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite and other materials | diversified miners and specialised producers |
| **Refining and precursors** | Converts ore and brine into battery-grade chemicals and precursor cathode material | chemical and refining firms |
| **Active materials** | Produces cathode and anode powders | specialty chemical firms |
| **Cell manufacturing** | Coats, calenders, winds or stacks, forms and ages cells | CATL, BYD, LG Energy Solution, Samsung SDI, SK On, Panasonic Energy, Northvolt |
| **Pack assembly** | Integrates cells into modules and packs | automakers, cell makers, Tier 1 suppliers |
| **Equipment** | Supplies coating, calendering, stacking, formation and inspection machines | process-equipment firms |
| **Recycling** | Collects, dismantles and recovers materials | recyclers and cell makers |
| **Testing and certification** | Validates safety, performance, transport and grid compliance | laboratories and certification bodies |
