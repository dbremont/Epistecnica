# Products

The delivered goods of technical production, read at the point where they leave the producer and enter use. A product note types the article itself — what it is made of, what class it belongs to, the properties it is released against and the standards that govern it — and leaves the production system that makes it to the industry family.

- [Fine Chemical](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/cto/product/fine-chemical.md) — the specification-sold substance at the head of the chain.
- [Drug](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/cto/product/drug.md) — the active principle, read by its action on a target.
- [Pharmaceutical Product](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/cto/product/pharmaceutical-product.md) — the formulated, packaged, authorised medicine.
- [Healthcare Product](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/cto/product/healthcare-product.md) — the umbrella over medicines, devices and diagnostics.
