# Separation Science and Technology

> The **Separation Science and Technology** domain is the super-domain that composes every technique for resolving a mixture into fractions of it — one stream made richer, another poorer, one product brought to specification. It is not a practice of its own: it is the scope inside which the member sets sit, and its work is to say what they share, where each one begins, and where the domain stops.

> It is the complement of an observation: the physical chemistry of solutions states how a solute distributes itself between two phases at equilibrium, and the chemistry of complexants states which ion a molecule will hold; separation technology intervenes in that equilibrium — contacting the phases, staging the contacts, sizing the barrier, running the cycle — so that a difference the chemistry allows becomes a product the plant delivers.

> The cut is the mixture. What is in: the techniques that split it, by whatever driving force — a chemical-potential difference between phases (extraction, sorption, membranes), a vapour-pressure difference (distillation, evaporation), a solubility difference (crystallization, precipitation), a mobility difference (chromatography, centrifugation), an interfacial free-energy difference (flotation), or an electric field (electrodialysis, electrorefining). What is out: the mixture itself, which belongs to nature; the thermodynamic and coordination-chemistry knowledge that says what is possible, which belongs to epistemica; the plant, the firm, and the market, which belong to the production and social spaces; and the mineral application of these techniques, which is carried inside [Geological Resource Development Technical Domain](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/cto/es/multinode/geological-resource-development-technical-domain.md) as its Beneficiation And Processing Set. Worked per [Philosophia Artium Technicarum et Operis](/view.html?p=notes/philosophia-artium-technicarum-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What technical element type does this technical instance belong to?

**Separation Science and Technology belongs to the `Technical Element Set` technical element type — a collection of technical elements scoped to one bounded field, which here is the resolution of a mixture into specified fractions.**

```text
Technical Element Set
└── Separation Science and Technology (bounded field: mixture → specified fractions)
```

It is a set rather than a `Production Technical System` because it owns no plant, no column, and no contactor bank: the members own those, and what this scope adds is the family and its boundary. That is the same move as the technical core catalog, which is the corpus's existing precedent for a set whose members are themselves sets; here the member family is the separation techniques rather than the transformation domains.

The `Technical Element Set` grouping below the root is admitted by the No-repetition exception: it scopes the instance family of the ten member sets that would otherwise hang untyped off the root's own segment, and it appears exactly once. Composite members take the `Set` suffix at this depth. No row below carries two types.

**On the name.** The field's own name carries "science", and that half is real: partition equilibria, complexation constants, mass transfer, and the machine-learning models that now predict them are inquiry, and inquiry is epistemic — it lives in [Chemical Engineering](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/epistemic/discipline/chemical-engineering.md) and the chemical sciences, and the four papers cited below are instances of it. What this set composes is the other half: the techniques, the equipment families, and the practices that realize a separation once the physics has said it is possible. The name is kept because it is the field's, not because the note claims the set is a science.

**Why these ten and not others.** Mineral beneficiation is deliberately **not** a member. Crushing, gravity concentration, flotation, and leaching applied to an ore are the *application* of this family to one feed, and they already sit inside the Geological Resource Development domain as its Beneficiation And Processing Set; making beneficiation a member here would file an application inside the family it draws on. Chemical reaction is out for a more basic reason: a reactor changes a substance, a separator only redistributes it, and where a separation is carried out by a reaction — precipitation, electrodeposition, ion exchange with chelation — the reaction is the instrument and the separation is the end. Water treatment is out as a sector frame, though nearly every unit process in a water plant is a member set below.

Secondary readings, kept as prose rather than compromise typing: a single column, contactor bank, membrane skid, or filter press is a `Production Technical System`; one extraction stage, one elution, or one filtration cycle is an `Operative Technique`; the phase-equilibrium and mass-transfer model the field works in is a `Technical Domain Reality Model` (decomposed in the root spine). Each takes its own root in its own decomposition.

### What is this technical instance?

> A super-domain, not a plant: the extraction member moves a solute between two liquids, the sorption and membrane members take it onto or through a solid or a barrier, the thermal and crystallization members exploit a phase change, the chromatographic member exploits a difference in migration rate, and the mechanical, flotation, gas, and electrochemical members split by density, surface chemistry, volatility, and charge; this scope holds them together by the single transformation they perform — a mixture is resolved into fractions, on a schedule, to a specification.

**What the members share, and what they do not.** The shared thing is the transformation, not the mechanism. They are one domain because a kilogram of high-purity europium oxide, a cubic metre of oxygen, and a tonne of clarified process liquor are produced the same way in form — a feed of stated composition, a driving force, a number of stages or a barrier, and a product specification — even though the driving force differs, and with it the energy budget, the failure mode, and the scale at which the technique is built. The one thing no member can do is change the elements it separates: a separator redistributes matter, it does not transform it, and the mass balance is therefore the domain's first constraint and its first warrant. That is why a separation can be improved indefinitely by better staging and never by a better reaction, and why the field's limits are thermodynamic rather than chemical.

**The state of the members, stated plainly.** In this corpus the domain is composed almost entirely of names. No member below has a note of its own; the only separation material in the corpus is natural — [Separation Process (Purification) (Isolation)](/view.html?p=notes/nature/separation-process-purification-isolation.md) is an unfinished natural-process stub and [Chromatography](/view.html?p=notes/nature/chromatography.md) a natural-system stub with no decomposition — plus the mineral application held inside the Geological Resource Development domain, and the discipline note [Chemical Engineering](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/epistemic/discipline/chemical-engineering.md), which names separation as one of the four questions a plant answers (heat, mass, separation, containment) without decomposing it. The honest consequence is that the domain is asserted at the level of its member family and its spine, and the depth beneath it is future work. What depth the corpus does have is bench-scale and one system at a time: the four papers cited below, all on f-element separations.

**The gap this creates, and its remedy.** Every member row names what it holds and what it would decompose into, so each can be written as its own decomposition without changing this note. Until then the Member Coverage Requirement below records the gap as a requirement on the domain rather than leaving it to a reader to notice.

Lineage: fire assay, cupellation, and parting — the noble-metal separations that worked before any theory of why → the alembic and industrial rectification, a phase change made into a unit operation → industrial liquid–liquid extraction for the actinides and the rare earths in the 1940s, with ion exchange following → synthetic resins, zeolites, and the reverse-osmosis membrane, separations that run without a phase change → flowsheet simulation, energy integration, and process intensification, the plant as an optimization object → the data-driven era: predictive models of distribution ratios, automated high-throughput platforms under Bayesian optimization, and extractant molecules generated by language models, which is where the four papers below sit.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this technical instance?

> Boundary: one super-domain, decomposed at shallow depth — the root, its ten direct sub-domain constituents, and the spine that states what the members share (evolution, purpose, reality model, the requirements and standards the whole field is bounded by). No member is re-decomposed here: each has its own note, and repeating a member's contents inside its parent is duplication, not structure.
>
> Typing reads directly from the instance path: the members resolve to the enclosing `Technical Element Set` grouping; the spine lands on flat facet types. Expansion is licensed by `(root) := <<Technical Element>> -> ... -> Technical Element Set`, the recursion rule for that composite. Every backticked type segment groups instances and terminates on none; every leaf resolves to a technical instance.
