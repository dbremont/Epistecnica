# Urbanism

> The technical domain of the built environment: the objects, networks and practices through which a settlement is built, serviced and shaped.

This shelf is the technical reading of urbanism. The social and policy reading lives one shelf over, in [Urbanismo](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/action/problem/urbanism/urbanismo.md) — what makes a city great, how spatial planning should be directed, and what the urban system is. The two shelves point at each other: the policy question there, the technical objects here.

## Technical objects and practices

- [Architecture](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/architecture.md) — architectural style models and the building scale.
- [Residential Building](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/residential-building.md) — the housing typology and its construction solutions.
- [Urban Design](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/urban-design.md) — the design of built form and public space.
- [Urban Mobility](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/urban-mobility.md) — the movement system of the settlement.
- [Urban Drainage System](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/urban-drainage-system.md) — stormwater management as a technical network.
- [Waste Management & Valorisation](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/waste-management-valorisation.md) — the waste system of the settlement.
- [Microclimate Engineering](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/microclimate-engineering.md) — the urban climate the fabric produces.
- [Thermal Power Station](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/thermal-power-station.md) — the energy plant that serves the settlement.
- [Cartogram](/view.html?p=notes/technica/general/urbanism/cartogram.md) — the cartographic technique.
