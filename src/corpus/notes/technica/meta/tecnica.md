# Meta Tecnica

> **Meta Tecnica** is technique taken as the subject of contemplation: not the exercise of a technique, but the reflexive study of what technique is, how it is constituted, how it changes, and what its presence does to the one who knows it and to the world it acts on. It is the technica corpus's counterpart to the epistemica corpus's own reflective scope.

> Where the technica notes ask *what a technical element is and how it works* — the practice, the technique, the object, the system — Meta Tecnica asks what it means that there is technique at all: why the making of things has its own kind of knowledge, whether that knowledge is distinct from science, and what a technical object is when it is considered apart from its use.

## Scope

- **Technique and knowledge** — *techne* against *episteme*: the kind of knowing that belongs to making, and whether it is a lesser or a different knowledge.
- **The technical object** — the object read on its own terms, as a structure with an internal evolution, rather than as a tool for a user.
- **Technical evolution** — how technical objects change: by transfer, by combination, by the resolution of internal contradictions, by the pressure of use.
- **Technique and science** — the direction of the relation, and the claim that modern science is itself a technical practice.
- **Technique and the human** — the maker, the operator, the user; the question of what technique does to the one who exercises it.
- **The limits of technical rationality** — what cannot be decided technically, and what the attempt to decide it technically costs.

> The scope is deliberately thin at present: it names the questions and holds the place. The substantive work sits in the notes it points to — [Technique](/view.html?p=notes/technica/meta/technique.md), the [technical-object taxonomy](/view.html?p=notes/technica/technical-object-taxonomy.html), and the [atlas](/view.html?p=notes/technica/atlas.html) — and in the philosophia that governs them.

## Terminology

> The full vocabulary of the technica corpus — 173 terms in twelve groups: the three structural concepts, the ten domains of technical-element vocabulary, and the social-technical terms of the facet. Each is defined in the Glossarium, where selecting it in any note also opens its definition. The element's own account — its realization chain, space evolution and life cycle — is [Meta Technical Element](/view.html?p=notes/technica/meta/technical-element.md).

### The three structural concepts

> The concepts that organize the rest: how an element is realized, how its space changes, and how its existence is phased.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Element Realization Chain](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-realization-chain)** | The sequence through which a technical possibility becomes a working element — conception, invention, research, design, development, production and deployment. |
| **[Technical Element Space Evolution](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-space-evolution)** | The change over time of the set of technical elements available in a domain, and of the relations that make that set a space rather than a list. |
| **[Technical Element Life Cycle](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-life-cycle)** | The sequence of developmental states and transitions through which an element passes, from emergence to discontinuation — analytical phases, not universal or strictly sequential stages. |
| **[Technical Genesis](/glossarium/?t=technical-genesis)** | The process through which a technical possibility originates and becomes an identifiable technical element. |

### 1. Technical Ontology

> What technical elements are, and the kinds of thing an element can be.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Element](/glossarium/?t=technical-element)** | Anything that occupies a role in a purposeful transformation of reality — the reality transformed, the operation that transforms it, or anything that enables, constrains, regulates or evaluates that operation. |
| **[Technical Element Space](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-space)** | The landscape of technical elements available within a domain, against which a practice selects what it works with. |
| **[Technical Object](/glossarium/?t=technical-object)** | A structural artifact that embodies operational techniques into a stable, reproducible system with defined interfaces and invariants. |
| **[Technical System](/glossarium/?t=technical-system)** | Organized set of technical objects whose interaction realizes a technical capability. |
| **[Technical Artifact](/glossarium/?t=technical-artifact)** | An intentionally produced material or symbolic technical entity — the element read as something made. |
| **[Technical Component](/glossarium/?t=technical-component)** | A constituent part of a technical object or system, identified by the role it plays in the whole. |
| **[Technical Material](/glossarium/?t=technical-material)** | Matter selected or transformed for technical purposes: a substance plus its processing history and specification. |
| **[Technical Structure](/glossarium/?t=structure)** | The organization and arrangement of components, relations and dependencies that constitutes an object, and that stays the same across configurations. |
| **[Technical Property](/glossarium/?t=property)** | An identifiable characteristic of a technical element that a specification can state, a test can measure and a design can trade. |
| **[Technical Configuration](/glossarium/?t=technical-configuration)** | A particular arrangement of technical components and their relations, at a moment or as a design. |
| **[Tool](/glossarium/?t=tool)** | An implement that bears directly on the workpiece or target, shaping, holding, cutting or joining it. |
| **[Machine](/glossarium/?t=machine)** | A drive, motion or power package that actuates a tool or equipment. |
| **[Instrument](/glossarium/?t=instrument)** | An implement that measures, indicates, regulates or monitors a variable. |
| **[Technical Possibility](/glossarium/?t=technical-possibility)** | A conceivable technical configuration or transformation that could be realized under specified conditions. |
| **[Technical Potential](/glossarium/?t=technical-potential)** | The latent capacity of existing knowledge, materials, techniques and components to enable new technical configurations. |
| **[Technical Operand](/glossarium/?t=technical-operand)** | The entity, material or phenomenon upon which a technical operation acts. |
| **[Technical Input](/glossarium/?t=technical-input)** | Material, energy, information or another element introduced into a technical operation or process. |
| **[Technical Output](/glossarium/?t=technical-output)** | Material, energy, information or another result produced by a technical operation or process. |

### 2. Technical Knowledge

> The knowledge, procedure and pattern a technique carries.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Principle](/glossarium/?t=technical-principle)** | General rule governing the construction or operation of technical systems. |
| **[Technical Knowledge](/glossarium/?t=technical-knowledge)** | Knowledge of how to make, transform, maintain or evaluate technical elements. |
| **[Technical Method](/glossarium/?t=method)** | An organized procedure for investigating, constructing, testing or solving a technical problem — the order of operations, the checks and the stopping rule. |
| **[Technical Technique](/glossarium/?t=technique)** | The reproducible way of performing a technical operation or achieving a transformation. |
| **[Know-how](/glossarium/?t=know-how)** | Procedural knowledge of how to do something, carried in practice rather than in propositions. |
| **[Technical Analysis](/glossarium/?t=technical-analysis)** | Examination of the structure, properties, behaviour or performance of an element, undertaken to explain or predict it. |
| **[Technical Design](/glossarium/?t=technical-design)** | Deliberate specification of the structure, properties and organization of a technical object or process. |
| **[Technical Invention](/glossarium/?t=invention)** | The first conception or realization of a new technical element, before adoption. |
| **[Technical Reverse Engineering](/glossarium/?t=technical-reverse-engineering)** | Analysis of an existing system to recover its components, techniques and operational principles. |
| **[Technical Representation](/glossarium/?t=technical-representation)** | The representation of the structure, properties, behaviour or operation of a technical element. |
| **[Technical Model](/glossarium/?t=technical-model)** | A simplified representation of a technical element or phenomenon used to explain, predict or investigate its behaviour. |
| **[Engineering Knowledge](/glossarium/?t=engineering-knowledge)** | Organized knowledge used to analyze, design, construct and optimize technical objects and systems. |

### 3. Technical Activity

> What agents do: practice, activity, operation and process — with the research kinds.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Practice](/glossarium/?t=technical-practice)** | Repeatable, organized pattern of technical work integrating activities, techniques, principles, standards and tools. |
| **[Technical Activity](/glossarium/?t=technical-activity)** | Situated bundle of technical doings carried on within a practice. |
| **[Technical Operation](/glossarium/?t=technical-operation)** | An individual action or transformation performed within a technical process or system. |
| **[Technical Process](/glossarium/?t=technical-process)** | An ordered sequence of operations through which a technical transformation occurs. |
| **[Technical Research](/glossarium/?t=technical-research)** | Systematic investigation that turns foundational knowledge into technical objects, techniques and methods. |
| **[Foundational Research](/glossarium/?t=foundational-research)** | Research aimed at new scientific knowledge without immediate practical application. |
| **[Applied Research](/glossarium/?t=applied-research)** | Research aimed at solving practical problems or developing new products, processes or techniques. |
| **[Research and Development](/glossarium/?t=research-and-development)** | The organized activity spanning foundational, applied and development research. |
| **[Industrial Research](/glossarium/?t=industrial-research)** | Firm-conducted R&D to develop new products or improve production processes. |
| **[Innovation](/glossarium/?t=innovation)** | The process by which a novelty is brought into use and diffuses through a production system or market. |
| **[Prototype](/glossarium/?t=prototype)** | A first working model built to test a design hypothesis before production. |
| **[Pilot Plant](/glossarium/?t=pilot-plant)** | A small-scale facility that tests a process under near-industrial conditions before scale-up. |
| **[Technical Production](/glossarium/?t=technical-production)** | The organized realization of technical processes to produce specified outputs. |

### 4. Technical Constitution

> How an element is organized: function, mechanism, architecture, interface, and the descriptions that specify them.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Function](/glossarium/?t=technical-function)** | A transformation, service or effect that an element is designed or capable of producing. |
| **[Technical Mechanism](/glossarium/?t=technical-mechanism)** | Physical, procedural or logical arrangement through which a function or transformation is produced. |
| **[Technical Architecture](/glossarium/?t=technical-architecture)** | The fundamental organization of a system: its components, their interfaces, their dependencies and the principles that hold them together. |
| **[Technical Interface](/glossarium/?t=technical-interface)** | Defined boundary through which technical elements exchange matter, energy, information or control. |
| **[Technical Parameter](/glossarium/?t=technical-parameter)** | A variable whose value characterizes or controls a technical object or process. |
| **[Technical Requirement](/glossarium/?t=technical-requirement)** | A formalized desired state or performance objective a solution must satisfy. |
| **[Technical Blueprint](/glossarium/?t=technical-blueprint)** | A generative description prescribing how to create, assemble or configure an artifact. |
| **[Technical Specification](/glossarium/?t=technical-specification)** | A precise statement of requirements, performance and interface conditions used to verify a solution. |
| **[Technical Constraint](/glossarium/?t=technical-constraint)** | A bound on technical action imposed by reality, resources, regulations or other conditions. |
| **[Technical Control](/glossarium/?t=technical-control)** | The regulation of a technical process or system to maintain or achieve a specified state or behaviour. |
| **[Technical Feedback](/glossarium/?t=technical-feedback)** | The return of information about an operation's results or current state to influence its subsequent behaviour. |

### 5. Technical Research

> The inquiry vocabulary: problem, question, investigation, experiment, test, validation.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Problem](/glossarium/?t=technical-problem)** | A discrepancy between a current or projected state and a desired technical state that calls for intervention. |
| **[Technical Research Problem](/glossarium/?t=technical-research-problem)** | A specific unresolved problem concerning the constitution, behaviour, performance or transformation of an element. |
| **[Technical Research Question](/glossarium/?t=technical-research-question)** | An interrogative formulation specifying what must be investigated to address a research problem. |
| **[Technical Investigation](/glossarium/?t=technical-investigation)** | Systematic inquiry into the properties, behaviour, mechanisms or possibilities of technical elements. |
| **[Technical Experimentation](/glossarium/?t=technical-experimentation)** | Controlled intervention designed to investigate or establish technical relationships and outcomes. |
| **[Technical Testing](/glossarium/?t=technical-testing)** | Empirical examination of whether an element satisfies specified requirements or exhibits expected behaviour. |
| **[Technical Validation](/glossarium/?t=validation)** | Establishment that an object, method or system satisfies its intended requirements and conditions of use. |
| **[Technical Simulation](/glossarium/?t=technical-simulation)** | The execution of a model to reproduce or investigate the behaviour of a technical element under specified conditions. |

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |

| **[Technical State](/glossarium/?t=technical-state)** | The identifiable configuration of an element at a particular point in time, including the values of its parameters. |
| **[Technical Change (Δ)](/glossarium/?t=technical-change)** | A difference between two technical states or configurations. |
| **[Technical Element Transition](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-transition)** | The passage of an element from one technical state to another, by adjustment, modification or replacement. |
| **[Technical Transformation](/glossarium/?t=technical-transformation)** | A substantive alteration of the structure, properties, functions or capabilities of an element. |
| **[Technical Element Trajectory](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-trajectory)** | The historically extended sequence of changes through which one element develops. |
| **[Technical Adaptation](/glossarium/?t=adaptation)** | Modification of an existing element to accommodate different conditions or requirements. |
| **[Technical Combination](/glossarium/?t=technical-combination)** | The formation of a new technical configuration by combining existing elements. |
| **[Technical Displacement](/glossarium/?t=technical-displacement)** | The replacement or supersession of an element by another in the role the first occupied. |
| **[Technical Obsolescence](/glossarium/?t=technical-obsolescence)** | The loss of technical relevance or adequacy of an element under changing conditions. |
| **[Technical Diffusion](/glossarium/?t=technical-diffusion)** | The spread and adoption of technical knowledge, techniques and objects across agents and societies. |
| **[Technical Element Emergence](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-emergence)** | The initial appearance of an element — a new configuration recognized as a distinct possibility. |
| **[Technical Element Maturation](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-maturation)** | The achievement of a relatively established and stable configuration, with its dominant design settled. |
| **[Technical Element Persistence](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-persistence)** | The continuation of an element across time, through maintenance, repair and institutional support. |
| **[Technical Element Replacement](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-replacement)** | The substitution of one element by another in the role the first occupied. |
| **[Technical Element Discontinuation](/glossarium/?t=technical-element-discontinuation)** | The termination of an element's production, operation or institutional support. |
| **[Technical Lineage](/glossarium/?t=technical-lineage)** | The historically traceable relationship between technical elements derived from earlier configurations. |
| **[Technical Accumulation](/glossarium/?t=technical-accumulation)** | The progressive accumulation of technical knowledge, components, methods and capabilities. |

### 7. Technical Capability

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Capability](/glossarium/?t=technical-capability)** | Value-producing possibility enabled by a technical function. |
| **[Technical Competence](/glossarium/?t=technical-competence)** | Acquired capacity to reliably execute technical processes and techniques. |

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |

| **[Technical Efficiency](/glossarium/?t=technical-efficiency)** | The relation between the resources consumed and the results produced by an element. |
| **[Technical Effectiveness](/glossarium/?t=technical-effectiveness)** | The degree to which the intended technical objectives are achieved. |
| **[Technical Reliability](/glossarium/?t=technical-reliability)** | The capacity to perform the required function consistently, over a stated period, under stated conditions. |
| **[Technical Robustness](/glossarium/?t=robustness)** | The capacity of an element to maintain acceptable behaviour under variation and disturbance. |
| **[Technical Precision](/glossarium/?t=precision)** | The degree of conformity between a technical result and a specified reference or target. |

| **[Technical Maintenance](/glossarium/?t=technical-maintenance)** | Activities intended to preserve or restore the operational condition of an element. |
| **[Technical Risk](/glossarium/?t=technical-risk)** | The possibility and consequence of an undesirable technical outcome under uncertainty. |
| **[Technical Verification](/glossarium/?t=technical-verification)** | The determination of whether a technical element conforms to its specified design requirements. |

### 10. Technical–Human Relations

> The maker's side: technicity, technē, poiesis, praxis, judgment, learning and transmission.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **[Technical Labor](/glossarium/?t=technical-labor)** | Purposive expenditure of human cognitive or physical effort in technical activity. |
| **[Craft](/glossarium/?t=craft)** | Skilled making governed by practical knowledge, carried by a practitioner and transmitted through apprenticeship. |
| **[Skill](/glossarium/?t=skill)** | Acquired capacity to perform a class of tasks with a given level of proficiency. |
| **[Tacit Knowledge](/glossarium/?t=tacit-knowledge)** | Knowledge held in practice that is difficult to codify, articulate or transfer explicitly. |
| **[Apprenticeship](/glossarium/?t=apprenticeship)** | Transmission of a craft or technique through supervised practice under a master. |
| **[Homo Faber](/glossarium/?t=homo-faber)** | The human read as maker: the being whose relation to the world is constituted in part by making. |
| **[Technical Education](/glossarium/?t=technical-education)** | Formation of technical competence through instruction, vocational training, apprenticeship and practice. |
| **[Technicity](/glossarium/?t=technicity)** | The condition or characteristic of being technical — what makes something technical rather than merely physical. |
| **[Technical Mediation](/glossarium/?t=technical-mediation)** | Technique as the medium through which humans relate to the world, not a neutral instrument between them. |
| **[Technē](/glossarium/?t=techne)** | Knowledge and capacity associated with making and producing, as distinct from theory and from practical wisdom. |
| **[Poiesis](/glossarium/?t=poiesis)** | Production — the bringing-forth of something into existence, whose end lies in the product rather than the activity. |
| **[Praxis](/glossarium/?t=praxis)** | Action or practice considered in its own right, whose end lies in the acting rather than in a product. |
| **[Instrumentality](/glossarium/?t=instrumentality)** | The relation between technical means and intended ends, and the reduction of the world to means when it becomes exclusive. |
| **[Affordance](/glossarium/?t=affordance)** | The possibility for action an object or environment offers an agent — a relation between properties and capacities, not a property of either. |
| **[Technical Agency](/glossarium/?t=technical-agency)** | The capacity of technical arrangements to condition or influence action and outcomes. |
| **[Technological Autonomy](/glossarium/?t=technological-autonomy)** | The thesis that technical development follows a dynamic partially independent of deliberate human direction. |
| **[Technical Judgment](/glossarium/?t=technical-judgment)** | The capacity to evaluate a technical situation and determine an appropriate action under uncertainty. |
| **[Technical Learning](/glossarium/?t=technical-learning)** | The acquisition and transformation of technical knowledge and competence by an agent or organization. |
| **[Technical Transmission](/glossarium/?t=technical-transmission)** | The communication and reproduction of technical knowledge between agents, by instruction, demonstration, text or artifact. |
| **[Technical Externalization](/glossarium/?t=technical-externalization)** | The transfer of human capacities, knowledge or operations into external technical arrangements. |

### The technical facet of social reality

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |

| **[Sociotechnical System](/glossarium/?t=sociotechnical-system)** | The joint system of technical and social elements, irreducible to either side alone. |
| **[Technical Code](/glossarium/?t=technical-code)** | The technical realization of a social interest or ideology in the design of an artifact. |
| **[Actor-Network](/glossarium/?t=actor-network)** | A heterogeneous association of human and non-human actants that holds together by translation. |
| **[Sociotechnical Imaginary](/glossarium/?t=sociotechnical-imaginary)** | A collectively held vision of a good society realized through technology. |
| **[Technological Trajectory](/glossarium/?t=technological-trajectory)** | The direction a technical field follows under a shared paradigm, bounded by its heuristics. |
| **[Path Dependence](/glossarium/?t=path-dependence)** | The dependence of present technical possibilities on the sequence of past choices. |
| **[Lock-in](/glossarium/?t=lock-in)** | The persistence of an incumbent option because switching costs and increasing returns outweigh its alternatives. |
| **[Technological Momentum](/glossarium/?t=technological-momentum)** | The inertia a maturing technical system acquires from its invested capital, institutions and users. |
| **[Large Technical System](/glossarium/?t=large-technical-system)** | A networked system of artifacts, organizations and rules — a railway, a grid, a telecommunication network. |
| **[Technological Transition](/glossarium/?t=technological-transition)** | A shift from one sociotechnical regime to another, through niche experimentation and landscape pressure. |
