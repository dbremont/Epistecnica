# Generalized Function


> A generalized function is a mathematical concept representing distributions or objects that generalize the notion of traditional functions to include distributions, such as Dirac delta function or step functions, used extensively in mathematical physics and engineering.
> 

The genus covers several species, and they are not interchangeable: Schwartz [distributions](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/mathematical/mapping/distribution/distribution.md) (test-function functionals) are the working case, hyperfunctions and Colombeau algebras are the alternatives built when multiplication is needed, and ultradistributions are the restriction to smaller classes of probes.
