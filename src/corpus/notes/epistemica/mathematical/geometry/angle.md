# Angle

> An **angle measures** the amount of **rotation or the separation between two intersecting lines** or rays, quantified as the degree of turning from one direction to another, typically expressed in degrees or radians.
> 

## Notion

> Analogous to the concept of number, there exists an abstract underlying idea that unifies various types of angles, though in practice we must work with each specific notion individually.

| **Concept** | **Definition** | **Measure** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Geometric Angle** | Figure formed by two rays with a common vertex | Degrees (°), Radians (rad) |
| **Angular Measure** | Quantitative representation of an angle | Degrees, Radians, Gradians |
| **Solid Angle** | 3D angle measuring how large an object appears | Steradian (sr) |
| **Complex Plane** | 2D plane representing complex numbers | Angle = argument of complex number |
| **Hyperbolic Angle** | Area under unit hyperbola corresponding to rotation | Radians (hyperbolic) |

## Categorization

| Category | Type of Angle | Description | Mathematical Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| By Measure | **Zero Angle** | Two rays coincide. | \(\theta=0^\circ\) |
| By Measure | **Acute Angle** | Smaller than a right angle. | \(0^\circ<\theta<90^\circ\) |
| By Measure | **Right Angle** | Forms a perpendicular relationship. | \(\theta=90^\circ\) |
| By Measure | **Obtuse Angle** | Greater than 90° but smaller than 180°. | \(90^\circ<\theta<180^\circ\) |
| By Measure | **Straight Angle** | Forms a straight line. | \(\theta=180^\circ\) |
| By Measure | **Reflex Angle** | Greater than 180° but smaller than 360°. | \(180^\circ<\theta<360^\circ\) |
| By Measure | **Full Angle** | Represents one complete rotation. | \(\theta=360^\circ\) |
| By Relationship | **Complementary** | Two angles whose measures sum to 90°. | \(A+B=90^\circ\) |
| By Relationship | **Supplementary** | Two angles whose measures sum to 180°. | \(A+B=180^\circ\) |
| By Relationship | **Adjacent** | Two angles sharing a vertex and one side, without overlapping interiors. | — |
| By Relationship | **Vertical (Opposite)** | Opposite angles formed by intersecting lines. | \(A=B\) |
| By Relationship | **Congruent** | Two angles with equal measures. | \(A=B\) |
| By Relationship | **Linear Pair** | Adjacent angles whose non-common sides form a straight line. | \(A+B=180^\circ\) |
| By Position | **Corresponding** | Angles occupying matching positions when a transversal intersects two lines. | Equal if lines are parallel. |
| By Position | **Alternate Interior** | Angles between two lines, on opposite sides of a transversal. | Equal if lines are parallel. |
| By Position | **Alternate Exterior** | Angles outside two lines, on opposite sides of a transversal. | Equal if lines are parallel. |
| By Position | **Same-Side Interior** | Interior angles on the same side of a transversal. | Sum to \(180^\circ\) if lines are parallel. |
| By Position | **Same-Side Exterior** | Exterior angles on the same side of a transversal. | Sum to \(180^\circ\) if lines are parallel. |

## Measurement System

| Measurement System | Description |
|---|---|
| **Degree System** | Divides a complete revolution into 360 equal parts, each called a degree (°). |
| **Radian System** | Measures angles using the ratio between the arc length and the radius of a circle. A complete revolution is \(2\pi\) radians. |
| **Gradian System** | Divides a complete revolution into 400 equal parts, each called a gradian (gon). |
