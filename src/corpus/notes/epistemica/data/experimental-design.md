# Experimental Design

> A structured approach to planning, conducting, and analyzing experiments.

> The design decides which condition is applied to which unit, in what order and how many times. It does not decide how a condition is made to hold: that is the routine's job, and the routines live in the [experimental-method scope](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/experimental-method/experimental-method.md). The techniques the conditions are applied with are tabulated in [data collection](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/data/data-collection.md); the dated cases are the [natural-science collection](/corpus/notes/epistemica/data/natural-science-data-collection-experimentation.csv).
