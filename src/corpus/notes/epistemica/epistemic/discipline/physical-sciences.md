# Physical Sciences

> In this set of notes, Physical Sciences is treated as a **discipline family** — an `Epistemic Practice` whose members are the practices that inquire into matter, radiation, and their interactions.

> **Physical Sciences** is the family of practices that describe the world as matter and radiation under law: they measure quantities, state mathematical laws that relate the measurements, and test those laws by experiment.

> Its members differ in the segment they cut — motion, light, charge, heat, quanta, spacetime, condensed matter — not in the kind of warrant they seek. That is why the family is one practice and its disciplines are members rather than separate practices: a measurement in optics and a measurement in mechanics are judged by the same standard and written in the same formalism.

> Optics is carried as a member in its own right, not as a chapter of electromagnetism. It split from it historically, keeps its own approximations (rays, then waves, then photons), and keeps its own instruments; it also appears inside Atomic, Molecular, and Optical Physics, which is how the field actually divides.

This note is the family-level decomposition; each member discipline is a practice in its own right and can be promoted to its own note without rewriting this table. The schema it instantiates is owned by the canonical [Philosophia Artium Epistemicarum et Operis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/csiglab/Naturgnosis/refs/heads/main/app/note/data/meta/philosophia-artium-epistemicarum-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What is this epistemic instance?

> Physical Sciences is the organized inquiry into matter, radiation, and their interactions: it registers what the world does, states laws that relate the registrations, and tests those laws by experiment. Its product is a warranted law together with the measurement that supports it; its warrant is reproducibility under controlled observation, not proof.

### What epistemic element type does this epistemic instance belong to?

> `Epistemic Practice` — the family is a doing: a set of practices carried by agents, applying methods and acts, producing constructions, and judged by its own standard. It is not an `Epistemic Element Set` read as a catalogue, and not an `Epistemic Framework` read as a lens; both are secondary readings, recorded below.

### Is the type of this `epistemic element instance` ambiguous?

> No at the root: Physical Sciences is one organized doing, so `Epistemic Practice` is its primary type. Secondary readings stay prose: `readable as` an `Epistemic Element Set` when the family is taken as a catalogue of disciplines rather than as a doing, and `readable as` an `Epistemic Framework` when its shared methods are read as one lens over the target. Neither is a second primary type, and no row below carries two types.

> The boundary is drawn by segment and warrant, not by vocabulary. Mathematics supplies the formalism and statistics supplies the inference, but neither measures the world; engineering uses a law without warranting it; chemistry and biology cut neighbouring segments at the molecular and organismal scale and are separate families.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this epistemic instance?

> **Boundary.** This table decomposes one epistemic instance — the family as an organized doing — and names its member disciplines as `Member` sub-practices. The scaffolding the members share (orientation, access, representation, evaluation, infrastructure) is typed once at the family root, because the same instruments, standards, and notations serve every member; repeating it per discipline would restate one element once per discipline. Sub-fields are grown only where a discipline genuinely divides, and each remains a `Member` practice, never a second type.

> **Stopping rule.** A row is terminal when it names a discipline or a sub-field. No discipline is decomposed into its own theories, and the segment it addresses is named here but decomposed only in the naturalis grammar.

> **Identity.** Instance Tree Path holds instances only; every path is unique, and no type segment terminates a branch. A discipline that also belongs to a neighbouring family — physical chemistry, biochemistry, biophysics, mathematical physics — is carried by the family whose question it asks, and the overlap is recorded in its description rather than by giving the row two parents.

### What Is a critical conceptual evaluation of this `epistemic element instance`?

> **Verdict.** The family is unusually determinate: its members share one segment, one formalism, and one warrant, so membership is decided by the segment a practice cuts rather than by a contested definition. Its weakness is the same uniformity — the boundary with mathematics and with engineering is a boundary of warrant, not of vocabulary, and a formalism can migrate into physics without its measurement coming along.

| Criterion | Verdict | Diagnostic Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conceptual Identification | Strong | Distinguished from mathematics (supplies the formalism, does not measure) and from engineering (applies the law, does not warrant it); the segment and the warrant are both stated. |
| Definitional Clarity | Strong | "Matter, radiation, and their interactions" states the cut; "reproducibility under controlled observation" states the test. |
| Conceptual Determinacy | Moderate–Strong | Boundary rule: a claim is physical iff it is a law or measurement about matter or radiation testable by experiment. Borderline cases — mathematical physics, physical chemistry — are placed by the question the practice asks. |
| Internal Consistency | Strong | Members differ in segment, not in type or warrant, so membership and typing do not conflict; optics carries two homes without carrying two parents. |
| Representational Adequacy | Strong | The shared scaffolding is typed once and each discipline is a member, so the family is not restated fifteen times; the two optics occurrences are distinguished by their sub-fields. |
| Purpose Adequacy | Strong | Law-seeking, mechanism-answering, and instrument-mediated access are all served; the note never claims a law holds beyond the measurements that support it. |
