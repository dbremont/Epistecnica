# Density Functional Theory (DFT)


> A computational quantum mechanical modeling method used to investigate the electronic structure of many-body systems.
> 

> DFT simulations are a type of QM simulation that uses density functional theory to investigate the electronic structure and properties of molecules. They investigate the structure and properties of large molecules and materials.
>
