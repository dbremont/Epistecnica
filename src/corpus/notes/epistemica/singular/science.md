# Science

> **Science** is the organized practice of constructing, testing, and revising warranted descriptions of reality — the domain in which observation is turned into theories, laws, models, and measurements that explain, predict, and enable intervention.
> 

> A **scientific theory** is a system of constructs and claims about a bounded segment of reality, stated in a representational system and answerable, at least in principle, to observation. Its strength is compression under warrant: a few statements must carry many observations, and the observations must be able to overturn them.

> **Simulation** is the process of creating a model or imitation of a real-world system or process to study its behavior under various conditions without the need to experiment on the actual system.
> 

> **Numerical Analysis** is the **mathematical study** of algorithms for approximating solutions to mathematical problems, especially those arising in engineering, science, and computational mathematics.
> 

> This scope holds the corpus of scientific inquiry: the physical theories and frameworks (quantum field theory, statistical mechanics, gauge theory, the classical field theories, the mechanical formulations), the domain's spaces, systems, and elements, and the computational and numerical methods by which its constructions are evaluated. The named constructions — the theories, laws, and models stated about particular systems — live in the `construction/` scope.
> 

## Scopes

> The domain is read in four parts, each reached through this scope.

- **Physical theory** — [Quantum Field Theory (QFT)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/quantum-field-theory-qft.md), [Statistical Mechanics](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/statistical-mechanics.md), [Quantum Electrodynamics (QED)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/quantum-electrodynamics-qed.md), [Gauge Theory](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/gauge-theory.md), [String Theory](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/string-theory.md), [Conformal Field Theory](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/conformal-field-theory.md), [Topological Quantum Field Theory (TQFT)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/topological-quantum-field-theory-tqft.md), [Classical Field Theory (CFT)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/classical-field-theory-cft.md), [Electromagnetism](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/electromagnetism.md), [Newtonian Mechanics](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/newtonian-mechanics.md), [Lagrangian Mechanics](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/lagrangian-mechanics.md), [Hamiltonian Mechanics](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/hamiltonian-mechanics.md), [Kinetic Theory](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/kinetic-theory.md), [Rigid Body Mechanics](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/rigid-body-mechanics.md).
- **Named constructions** — the theories, laws, and models about particular systems: [Navier–Stokes Equations](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/construction/navierstokes-equations.md), [Fick's Laws of Diffusion](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/construction/fick-s-laws-of-diffusion.md), [Lotka–Volterra](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/construction/lotka-volterra.md), [General Equilibrium Theory](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/construction/general-equilibrium-theory.md), [Density Functional Theory (DFT)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/construction/density-functional-theory-dft.md), [Molecular Orbital Theory](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/construction/molecular-orbital-theory.md), [Mori–Zwanzig Formalism](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/construction/mori-zwanzig-formalism.md).
- **Systems and spaces** — the targets a theory cuts: [Physical System](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/physical-system.md), [Phase Space](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/phase-space.md), [Configuration Space](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/configuration-space.md), [Reference Frame](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/reference-frame.md), [Material Space](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/material-space.md), [Chemical Space](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/chemical-space.md), [Multibody System](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/multibody-system.md).
- **Methods** — how a construction is computed and checked: simulation, discretization, and numerical analysis — [Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/computational-fluid-dynamics-cfd.md), [Finite Element Method (FEM)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/finite-element-method-fem.md), [Finite Difference Method (FDM)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/finite-difference-method-fdm.md), [Spectral Method Toolbox](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/spectral-method-toolbox.md), [Molecular Dynamics (MD)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/molecular-dynamics-md.md), [Discrete-Event Simulation](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/discrete-event-simulation.md), [Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)](/note.html?p=notes/epistemica/singular/fast-fourier-transform-fft.md).
