# Indicators

> This is the entry point to the indicator shelf: the measured series through which the state of an economy is observed — prices, confidence, labour and trade.

> An indicator is not the phenomenon itself. It is a constructed measure: a survey, a basket, a ratio, a balance. Read each entry for what it counts, how it is built, and where it stops being informative.

## Prices and confidence

- [Consumer Confidence](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/consumer-confidence.md) — households' reported optimism about their finances and the economy.
- [Consumer Confidence Index](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/consumer-confidence-index.md) — the index number built from the confidence survey.
- [Consumer Price Index (CPI)](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/consumer-price-index.md) — the price of a consumer basket, and the headline inflation measure.

## Labour

- [Employment Rate](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/employment-rate.md) — the employed share of the working-age population.
- [Unemployment Rate](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/unemployment-rate.md) — the unemployed share of the labour force.
- [Labor Force Participation Rate](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/labor-force-participation-rate.md) — the share of the working-age population in the labour force.

## Trade

- [Trade as % of GDP](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/trade-as-percent-of-gdp.md) — openness, measured as exports plus imports over output.
- [Trade Balance](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/indicator/trade-balance.md) — exports minus imports of goods and services.
- [Terms of Trade](/view.html?p=notes/social/dynamic/trade/terms-of-trade.md) — export prices relative to import prices (a trade note, not an indicator-shelf member).
