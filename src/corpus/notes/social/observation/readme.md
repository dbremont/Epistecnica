# Observation

> This is the entry point to the observation shelf: the notes that describe what a social system *is* at a given moment, rather than how it changes.

> The shelf holds observed states and conditions. The economic states — business climate, demand, infrastructure, investment, labour, country risk, hyperinflation, the middle-income trap, monopoly, efficiency, the production matrix, productivity, utility, rent, scarcity, income, poverty, stagflation, technology and trade — sit under `state/`, beside the system-state and wealth notes.

## Members

- [State](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/state/state.md) — the state of a social system.
- [State (Snapshot)](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/state/state-snapshot.md) — a complete description of the relevant variables at a moment.
- [Wealth](/view.html?p=notes/social/observation/state/wealth.md) — the stock reading of the economic condition.
