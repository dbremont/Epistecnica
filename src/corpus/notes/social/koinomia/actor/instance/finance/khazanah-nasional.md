# Khazanah Nasional

> Malaysia's sovereign wealth fund: the state's strategic investment arm, holding and building Malaysian companies across aviation, telecommunications, healthcare, finance, energy and infrastructure.

**Khazanah Nasional Berhad** is Malaysia's sovereign wealth fund, incorporated on 3 May 1993 and wholly owned by the Minister of Finance (Incorporated). It invests in strategic Malaysian companies and sectors, holds the state's legacy corporate stakes, and manages the Government-Linked Companies transformation.

## What it is

- **Strategic holdings** — majority and significant stakes in companies that carry national infrastructure and services: Telekom Malaysia, Axiata, CIMB Group, IHH Healthcare, Tenaga Nasional, UEM Group, and Malaysia Airlines through Malaysia Aviation Group.
- **Sector mandate** — aviation, telecommunications, healthcare, financial institutions, energy, infrastructure, real estate, and technology.
- **GLC transformation** — from 2004 Khazanah served as the secretariat of the Government-Linked Companies Transformation Programme, the reform of state-controlled listed companies run with the Putrajaya Committee on GLC High Performance.
- **Research** — the Khazanah Research Institute, its policy research arm, publishing *The State of Households*.

It invests as an owner, not as a fund of funds: it takes positions in companies it controls or anchors, and manages them through board and strategy rather than delegating a sector mandate to private managers. It is Malaysia's instance of a [State Holding and Investment Company](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/finance.md), beside [SFPIM](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/actor/instance/finance/federal-holding-and-investment-company.md), Temasek and the historical industrial holdings (INI, IRI).
