# Royal Dublin Society

> The **Royal Dublin Society** (RDS) was founded in **1731** as the *Dublin Society for Improving Husbandry, Manufactures and Other Useful Arts* — arguably the **first organisation dedicated explicitly to technical-productive improvement** rather than to science as such. It is the earliest instance of the [promotion agency](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/actor/instance/development/promotion-agency.md) form, and the Irish case runs unbroken from it to IDA Ireland.

## What it did

| Instrument | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Premiums** | Prizes for crops, manufactures, fishing gear, weaving — money for a demonstrated improvement, the ancestor of the innovation grant |
| **Model farms and shows** | Demonstration rather than instruction: the improvement is shown to work |
| **Drawing schools** | Design and draughtsmanship for manufactures, the technical-education arm |
| **Botanic gardens** | Plant introduction and trials |
| **Library and publications** | The circulation of useful knowledge |
| **Standards and testing** | Later, agricultural and industrial testing — the RDS was the body Irish industry consulted |

## The claim

> **"The first agency to promote technical-productive development"** is a defensible claim with a caveat. Defensible, because the 1731 foundation is explicitly about *husbandry and manufactures* — production, not natural philosophy — and its instruments (premium, school, trial, publication) are the ancestor of the development agency's. Caveated, because earlier bodies did parts of the job: the [Inspection des manufactures](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/actor/instance/development/france-inspection-des-manufactures.md) (1669) regulated, but it did not promote.

> The distinction that makes the RDS the first of its kind: it **paid** for improvement, voluntarily, with a chartered society's own funds and subscriptions, rather than ordering it by regulation.

## The line to the modern agency

> The RDS's functions were eventually split: technical education went to the state, agricultural research to the institutes, the show to the society itself. What remained of the *promotional* function was taken up by the state's own agencies in the twentieth century — the [IDA](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/actor/instance/development/ida-ireland-industrial-development-authority.md) for investment, Enterprise Ireland for exports and firm development. The same country, the same function, a different carrier.
