# Czech Academy of Sciences

> The **Czech Academy of Sciences** (Akademie věd České republiky, AV ČR) is the Czech Republic's public basic-research institution: a body of some fifty institutes, founded in 1992 as the successor to the Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences, which had absorbed the Royal Bohemian Society of Sciences in 1952. It is the state's own research arm — funded from the state budget, governed by an academy assembly and a president, and evaluated as an institution rather than project by project.

## What it is

| Dimension | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Legal form** | A public research institution established by statute, not a ministry and not a university |
| **Scope** | Natural, technical, social and life sciences, plus the humanities — the institutes span mathematics and physics to history and philosophy |
| **Funding** | State budget, with a research-plan and institutional-evaluation regime; competitive project funding on top |
| **Governance** | Academy assembly, president, and institute directors; scientific councils at both levels |
| **Role in the system** | The basic-research counterpart to the universities and the applied-research agencies — the place where long-horizon work without a teaching load is done |

## The lineage

> The Academy's institutional ancestry runs through three bodies, and the sequence is the interesting part. The **Royal Bohemian Society of Sciences** (1784) was a private learned society of elected members; the **Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences** (1952) was a state academy that took over the society's tradition and turned research into a national system; the **Czech Academy of Sciences** (1992) is the post-communist reconstruction of that system into a public institution with its own evaluation.

> Read as a form, it is the classic **state academy**: unlike a university it does not teach as its primary function, and unlike a research council it does not only fund — it employs. The tension of the form is the familiar one: institutional funding protects long-horizon work, and the same protection makes it harder to redirect when the science moves.
