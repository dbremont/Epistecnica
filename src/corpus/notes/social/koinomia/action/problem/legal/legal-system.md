# Legal System

> The **legal system** is the order that makes claims enforceable: property, contract, insolvency, and the courts that adjudicate them.

## Domain space

- **Property rights** — who owns what, and how ownership is recorded and transferred.
- **Contract enforcement** — whether a promise can be relied on without personal trust, and how long enforcement takes.
- **Insolvency and secured credit** — what a lender can recover, which sets the price and availability of credit.
- **Business law** — incorporation, governance, minority protection.
- **Courts and adjudication** — the institution that hears and decides (see [Justice](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/action/problem/state/justice/justice.md)).
- **Legal certainty** — the stability and predictability of the rules themselves.

## The growth question

Investment is a bet on the future enforceability of a claim. Where contracts are slow or uncertain, firms stay small and trade with people they know; where titles are insecure, land is not used as collateral and credit stays informal. The legal system is a deep determinant because it prices risk for every other domain.
