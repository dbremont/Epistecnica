# Space

> The spatial frame of the public order: the ground a polity holds, the regions it delimits, and the world that contains both.

This shelf is the spatial organ of the koinomia domain, alongside [Polity](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/collective/polity/polity.md), [Actor](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/actor/readme.md) and [Action](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/action/readme.md). It is not the representation-of-space scope under `epistemica/representation/space/` — that shelf asks how space is represented — and it is not the corpus-wide *Social Space*, which is the whole social corpus read spatially. The question here is narrower: what ground does a public order occupy, how is it delimited, and at what scale?

## Levels

- [Territory](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/space/territory.md) — the bounded domain over which a polity claims authority; the ground itself, read politically.
- [Region](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/space/region.md) — the delimitation concept: a bounded area of denser internal interaction, owned by no one.
- [World](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/space/world.md) — Planet Earth, the planetary frame that contains every territory and region; still unfinished.

## Read it with

- [Polity](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/collective/polity/polity.md) — the authority that holds a territory.
- [City](/view.html?p=notes/social/koinomia/collective/city.md) — a region read as an inhabited, governed settlement.
