# Social Reality

> Social reality is the part of reality that exists through agents, their bonds and their institutions — the patterns, structures and norms that emerge from interaction and are sustained by shared recognition.

A **social element** is anything the social corpus can name: an actor, a collective, an action, a relation, a norm, a property, a representation. The shelves below file each element by **what it is**, not by the domain it happens to sit in; the public/commons domain is one shelf (`collective/`), not the umbrella.

## The guide

The schema owner is [Philosophia Socialium et Operis](/view.html?p=notes/philosophia-socialium-et-operis.md): the element type, the permitted link forms, the decomposition contract, and the QA.

## How to read it

- **By type, then by domain.** A note is filed where its element type lives; domain shelves (like `collective/` for the public order) are readings across types.
- **Markdown notes vs live notes.** The `.md` files are the corpus; `.html` pages under `representation/` are the live atlases and viewers.
- **The corpus is a web, not a tree.** Start at a shelf readme, follow the links, and use the universal search (`@tag` filters) to cross it.
