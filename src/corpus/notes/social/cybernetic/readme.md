# The Cybernetic World

> The **cybernetic world** is the part of social reality that runs on designed control: units that sense, decide and act, and the people and organizations that delegate to them.

This is the main entry for the study of that world. Its lens is the [Cybernetic Facet](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/facet/cybernetic.md) — control and delegation through designed systems, a mechanism that cuts across sectors rather than a sector of its own.

## The facet

> What does the explanatory work here? Not *where* the machine sits and not *who* uses it, but the loop: a reference is set, a state is measured, an action is selected, and the consequence returns as the next reading. The social question is the delegation — who set the reference, who can stop the unit, who bears the consequence.

The cybernetic world is not a place in the corpus; it is a reading that cuts across the actor, action, relation, normative and policy shelves. An algorithm on a trading desk is economic by its market, technical by its implementation and cybernetic by its delegation; no one of those readings replaces the others.

## How to read it

- **Keep the element's type.** A cybernetic unit is still an actor; an automated desk is still an organization; the facet scopes the inquiry and changes no type.
- **Follow the delegation.** For any system, name the principal, the reference it was set to, the stop condition and the accountable party. Where one is missing, the reading has found the failure.
- **Expect competing readings.** The same configuration answers to the technical, economic, political and normative mechanisms at once; take the cybernetic reading only where it explains something the others do not.

## QA

### Is the cybernetic world a sector?

No. It is a facet: it cuts across firms, states, markets and households. A hospital, a bank, a farm and a ministry can each carry it, and none of them is it.

### Does every automated system belong here?

Only where delegation and control explain the configuration. A machine that transforms material without a delegated decision is a technical object; the cybernetic reading begins when someone delegates a choice to it and remains answerable.

### What is new about it?

The scale and autonomy of the delegation, not the existence of control. Thermostats, autopilots and trading rules are old; what is new is the number of decisions delegated to systems that select their own subgoals, and the accountability gap that follows.
