# Human Formation Facet

> The **human formation facet** — the catalog's **Educational** facet — is the dimension of social reality whose dominant causal mechanism is the *formation of persons*: the cultivation of cognitive, moral, practical and cultural capacity across a life, by family, school, university and workplace, under whatever pedagogy each carries.

> It answers the question *where does education go?* Into this facet, and not into one of its own: facets are distinguished by dominant causal mechanism, and formation names that mechanism exactly as the [Facet](/view.html?p=notes/social/framework/facet.md) catalog's Educational row states.

## The mechanism is formation, not the content

> Every facet is assigned by the mechanism doing the explanatory work, never by the subject taught. An industrial school training machinists and a business school training managers are in the same facet, because in each the mechanism is the making of a person's capacity; the machine and the balance sheet are content, and content is typed by its own elements elsewhere.

- **Content belongs to the facet that owns it** — engineering to the Technical facet, law to the Legal, commerce to the Economic. A subject does not drag its teaching along with it.
- **What this facet owns is transmission**: the ordered passage of a person from capacity to capacity — instruction, exercise, examination — and the warrant that the passage happened. A degree is this facet's certificate, not the knowledge's.
- **Warrant is borrowed, not produced here.** The truth of what is taught is certified next door, in the [Research Facet](/view.html?p=notes/social/facet/research.md); this facet certifies the competence of the one who learned, and the two certificates are routinely confused.

## What it is not

- **Not the education actor.** The [Education Actor](/view.html?p=notes/social/collective/actor/education-actor.md) is the warranted body that teaches — court, temple, mosque, university, ministry. The facet is the mechanism such a body carries. Actors, activities and compounds keep their own element types and are merely *read* under this facet; the facet owns no nodes.
- **Not a stage of life.** Schooling is the concentrated, institutionalised part of it. The formation of a person continues through apprenticeship, work, family and reading long after the last examination.
- **Not training alone.** Drill that produces a repeatable act is the narrowest case. The facet also carries the formation of judgement, taste and character — the part the pedagogues argue about in [Educational Field](/view.html?p=notes/social/problem/dimension/education/educational-field.md).

## Where its elements live in the corpus

- **Field**: [Educational Field](/view.html?p=notes/social/problem/dimension/education/educational-field.md) — the three ecosystems (technical training, science and engineering preparation, general formation) and the twelve theories of formation from Kant to Freire.
- **Actors**: the [Education Actor](/view.html?p=notes/social/collective/actor/education-actor.md) type note, [University](/view.html?p=notes/social/collective/actor/university.md) as its chartered kind, and the country-coded shelves under `social/collective/actor/private/education/<code>/` — industrial schools, medical and business schools, technical universities.
- **Stratum**: [Bildungsbürgertum](/view.html?p=notes/social/structural/stratum/bildungsburgertum.md) — the stratum the *Bildung* reading of this facet produced in one society.
- **Policies**: the ministries and programmes that steer it are filed under `social/problem/dimension/education/`, with the polity shelves that carry them.
