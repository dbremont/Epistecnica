# Cultural Activity

> **Cultural Activity** is the recurring, organized doing through which a collective produces, transmits and performs meaning together — convivial conversation, reading and recitation, ceremony, performance. It is the doing; the pattern the doing enacts is [Culture](/view.html?p=notes/social/synontic/culture/culture.md), and the two must not be confused.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Cultural Activity belongs to the `Activity` social element type (Action category)** — a coarse bundle of granular social actions performed as one situated doing. Its layer is **Ontic**: the gathering, the recitation and the performance exist extra-mentally. Its facet is cultural. It is the third family of the activity tree, beside [Economic Activity](/view.html?p=notes/social/action/activity/economic/economic-activity.md) and [Research Activity](/view.html?p=notes/social/action/activity/research/research.md).

Readable secondarily as the transmission of a **Noetic** stock (the canon, the doctrine, the repertoire it carries) and as reproducing a **Synontic** pattern (the meanings whose shared recognition makes the occasion what it is). Those readings are prose; the tree below types the doing once. [Culture](/view.html?p=notes/social/synontic/culture/culture.md) names the stable pattern — the design for living — that these doings enact and re-enact; a cultural *activity* is one of the occasions on which the pattern is performed.

### What is this social instance?

> The doing-family of meaning-production: the occasions on which people assemble to make, transmit, or display significance together, together with the conventions that govern those occasions. The family is organized by what the doing does with meaning: **sociability** produces it in exchange (the salon, the club, the coffee house), **cultivation** transmits it into persons (the reading circle, the lecture, the concert), and **ceremony** displays it as binding form (the rite, the festival, the procession).

> The branch is newly opened and thin: it currently holds the [Salon](/view.html?p=notes/social/action/activity/cultural/salon.md) as its first worked instance. Commemoration is *not* here — the corpus keeps its memorial material in a separate home (`public/memorial/`), which owns the doings that fix a collective's relation to its past.

## QA

### Is a salon an activity or a compound?

> Both readings are available and the tree takes the first. As an `Activity` a salon is the recurring doing — the gathering, its conversation, its conventions. As a `Social Compound` it is host, habitués, rules and room taken together as one institution. The corpus files the doing because a salon's unity is what it *repeats*: take the repetition away and what remains is a room, a host, and a set of acquaintances, not a salon.

### Where does commemoration go?

> Not here. A memorial day, a monument's unveiling, and a funeral are doings that fix a collective's relation to its past, and the corpus keeps that material in its own home rather than opening a second branch for it. The cut is the object of the meaning: this branch performs present significance, commemoration fixes past significance.
