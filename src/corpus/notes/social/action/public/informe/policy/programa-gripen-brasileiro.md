# Programa Gripen Brasileiro

> The **Programa Gripen Brasileiro** is Brazil's fighter acquisition and industrial programme: the F-X2 selection of the Gripen NG in December 2013, the contract of 24 October 2014 for 28 Gripen E and 8 Gripen F aircraft with full transfer of technology, and the build-out that followed — a production line at Embraer's Gavião Peixoto site, Brazilian participation in the aircraft's development, and a batch plan that has grown from 36 aircraft to 45 and beyond against an Air Force requirement of 108. It is the defence offset as industrial policy: the purchase is the instrument, the domestic technological capability is the objective.

### What is this social instance?

> The programme began as a competition. In October 2008 Brazil selected three finalists for its F-X2 fighter programme — the Dassault Rafale, the Boeing F/A-18E/F Super Hornet, and the Gripen NG — to replace its Northrop F-5EM and Mirage 2000C aircraft, with an initial plan for at least 36 and possibly up to 120. Saab submitted its tender for 36 Gripen NGs in February 2009; in early 2010 the Air Force's final evaluation reportedly placed the Gripen ahead, on lower unit and operational cost. After delays over financial constraints, President Dilma Rousseff announced the Gripen NG's selection on 18 December 2013. The decisive factors were domestic manufacturing opportunities, full transfer of technology, participation in the aircraft's development, and potential exports to Africa, Asia, and Latin America; the distrust of the United States after the 2013 surveillance disclosures weighed in the choice.
>
> On 24 October 2014 Brazil and Sweden signed the contract: 39.3 billion SEK — US$5.44 billion — for 28 single-seat Gripen E and eight two-seat Gripen F, delivered from 2019 to 2024 and sustained until 2050, financed by a Swedish government loan at a subsidised 2.19 per cent over 25 years. At least 15 aircraft were to be assembled in Brazil, Brazilian companies were to take part in production, and the F models were to come later. Almost US$1 billion of the price increase since selection paid for Brazil-requested development, the Wide Area Display — a panoramic 19 by 8 inch touchscreen — among it; AEL Sistemas delivered that display software and other avionics. The compensation package was set at US$9 billion, 1.7 times the order value. In 2015 the two countries finalised the joint development of the Gripen F, designated F-39 in Brazil.
>
> Lineage: the F-X2 competition (2008–2013) → the contract and the transfer package (2014–2015) → the flight-test and delivery phase (2019–2020), the first Brazilian F-39E flown at Linköping on 26 August 2019 and handed over on 10 September, reaching Brazil on 20 September 2020 and Gavião Peixoto for testing four days later → the operational basing of the 1st Air Defense Group at Anápolis and the first series aircraft received on 1 April 2022 → the second batch decision (26 aircraft at about US$85 million each) and the four added in April 2022, bringing the first batch to 40 → the inauguration of the Gripen E production line in Brazil on 9 May 2023 → the letter of intent of 9 November 2024 raising the contract by 25 per cent to 45 aircraft, paid in part by Sweden's selection of four Embraer C-390 airlifters → the September 2025 negotiations for six more Es and twelve second-hand Gripen C/Ds from Swedish inventory → the 12th and first domestically produced aircraft presented on 25 March 2026, and a June 2026 memorandum for 20 more Es to be built in Brazil and based at Santa Maria.
