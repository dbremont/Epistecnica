# Publishing

> The publishing organizations: the compound units that produce and circulate publications — publishers, learned-society presses, editorial offices, scholarly journals, and preprint servers.

This shelf is a kind family under the [Organization](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/organization.md) hub — the compound Interaction Unit that acts as one — beside the actor shelf's other `private/` families (firm, research, education, finance, health, party). It files the *organization*, not the artifact: a book, an article, or an issue is an epistemic or technical object handled by the research space and by technique/production, reachable from here by association only.

## The shelf

- **Publishing Organization** — the kind: an organization whose constitutive output is the production and circulation of publications. Its identity is a title and masthead, its membership a board, editors, contributors and subscribers, its rules a submission and refereeing regime, and its agency an editor-in-chief and editorial board.
- **Scholarly Journal** — the scientific sub-kind: a serial, refereed publication organization holding the priority-and-refereeing regime through which a finding is told first and told as settled.
- **Instances** — [Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/private/publishing/philosophical-transactions-of-the-royal-society.md), the founding scholarly journal (London, 1665).
