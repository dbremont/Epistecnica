# Reckitt

> **Reckitt** is the British consumer-health and household-products company whose business is **brands, not processes**: Dettol, Lysol, Harpic, Vanish, Finish, Air Wick, Nurofen, Gaviscon, Strepsils, Durex. Founded as a Hull starch business in 1840, merged with Colman's in 1938, merged again with the Dutch chemicals firm Benckiser in 1999, and renamed from Reckitt Benckiser to Reckitt in 2021.

## What kind of company it is

| Dimension | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Portfolio** | A set of **powerbrands** in three segments — hygiene, health, home — each a category leader rather than a technology |
| **Growth model** | Buy a brand, extend it geographically, spend on marketing and shelf space; organic growth is a few percent |
| **Cost structure** | Low R&D relative to revenue (a few percent), high gross margin, and a marketing line that is the real investment |
| **Moat** | Distribution and **brand recall**, not patents — a consumer will not switch disinfectant to save pennies |

## The opposite of a capability company

> The corpus's [Corning](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/private/firm/corning-incorporated.md) note is the case of **process leverage**: one competence, pointed at many markets. Reckitt is the mirror image — **brand leverage**: one distribution machine, pointed at many products, where the product itself is replaceable. Neither company invents much; they capitalise different assets. Corning's asset is what it can make, Reckitt's is what a shopper will reach for.

> That difference explains the acquisitions. Reckitt buys **brands**, not laboratories: SSL (Durex, Scholl) in 2010, and Mead Johnson — infant formula — in 2017, the largest and most consequential. It also explains the fragility: a brand portfolio has no technical head start to fall back on, so a reputational shock is a direct hit on the asset itself.

> The infant-formula litigation that began in the United States in the 2020s (claims linking preterm-infant formula to necrotising enterocolitis) is the case in point, and it drove a portfolio review in the mid-2020s. It belongs in the note because it is not an accident of one product: it is what happens when the company's whole value is confidence in a name.
