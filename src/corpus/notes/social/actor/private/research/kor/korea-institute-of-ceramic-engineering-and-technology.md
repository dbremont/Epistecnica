# Korea Institute of Ceramic Engineering and Technology (KICET)

> The **Korea Institute of Ceramic Engineering and Technology** is Korea's dedicated ceramics institute. Its lineage runs back to the ceramics department of the Central Testing Laboratory, established in 1912; it was government-funded from 2000 and made an independent corporation on 4 May 2009. It supports the ceramic industry with research and development, testing, evaluation and certification.

## Ceramic focus

- **Ceramic materials and processes** — powder synthesis, shaping, sintering and machining.
- **Electronic and display ceramics** — substrates, dielectric and piezoelectric components.
- **Energy ceramics** — materials for batteries, fuel cells and energy conversion.
- **Bioceramics** — dental and orthopaedic ceramics.
- **Structural and refractory ceramics** — the industrial base of the Korean ceramics sector.
- **Testing, evaluation and certification** — the institute's public gatekeeping function.

## Place in the field

Korea built a ceramics industry — sanitary ware, tile, electronic components — faster than it built the laboratories to support it, and KICET is the public laboratory that closed the gap. Its certification role makes it more than a developer: a test result from KICET is a market document. That combination of research and conformity assessment is the Korean model, and it is why the institute's name says engineering and technology rather than science.
