# Center for Advanced Ceramic Technology (CACT)

> The **Center for Advanced Ceramic Technology** is a New York State Center for Advanced Technology hosted by Alfred University, first designated in 1987 and renewed for a further ten years in 2018. Alfred is the American university that built ceramic engineering as a degree subject, and CACT is its industry-facing arm: state money to put a university's ceramics capability at the service of firms.

## Ceramic focus

- **Electronic ceramics** — dielectrics, piezoelectrics and magnetic ceramics.
- **High-temperature structural ceramics** — materials for severe service.
- **Ceramic additive manufacturing** — printing and sintering of ceramic parts.
- **Glass and photonic ceramics** — optical and photonic glasses and glass-ceramics.
- **Testing and analysis** — the analytical services a small firm cannot staff itself.
- **Workforce** — the ceramics teaching tradition the centre draws on.

## Place in the field

CACT is a state technology centre, not a federal laboratory and not a company: its mandate is economic development through a university's specialism, and its instrument is access — to equipment, to students, to a professor who has seen the failure before. The model is the American answer to the German Fraunhofer institutes, run at the scale of one state and one material.
