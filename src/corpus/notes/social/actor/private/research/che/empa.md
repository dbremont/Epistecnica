# Empa (Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology)

> **Empa** is the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology, based in Dübendorf with sites in St. Gallen and Thun. It began in 1880 as the federal material-testing laboratory, was renamed the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Testing and Research in 1988, and now carries its present name. Its ceramics work sits in the High Performance Ceramics laboratory and in the thin-film groups.

## Ceramic focus

- **High-performance ceramics** — structural and functional ceramics, damage-tolerant grades.
- **Nanostructured and nanocomposite ceramics** — thin films and coatings with ceramic phases.
- **Thin films and photovoltaics** — deposition, sintering and interface control.
- **Joining and corrosion** — ceramic-metal joints and high-temperature degradation.
- **Mechanical and thermal testing** — the testing inheritance of a materials laboratory.
- **Materials for energy** — coatings and ceramics for energy conversion.

## Place in the field

Empa is an applied federal laboratory in the ETH domain: part of its budget comes from a mandate, part from contracts and from the Swiss innovation system. Its ceramics work is smaller in headcount than IKTS or NIMS but concentrated where ceramics meet thin films and interfaces — the physics of a coating that must survive thermal cycling. It also carries a testing lineage that predates the research institute: the laboratory was founded to test materials for Swiss industry, and that duty has not disappeared.
