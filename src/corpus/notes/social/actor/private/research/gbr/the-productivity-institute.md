# The Productivity Institute (TPI)

> The **Productivity Institute** is a UK-wide research organisation studying the causes of the UK's productivity problem and the policies and firm practices that could address it. It was established in September 2020 with funding from the Economic and Social Research Council and is headquartered at Alliance Manchester Business School, University of Manchester.

The institute is not a regulator, a statistical agency or a lobby group. It is a research organisation that produces evidence, frameworks and policy analysis on productivity, and it works through a network of academic partners, regional Productivity Forums and a policy unit.

## Research programme

TPI's research is organised around:

- the **Manchester Model** and the relationship between firms, places and productivity;
- **people** — skills, work, health and labour-market participation;
- **firms** — management, technology adoption, investment and business performance;
- **institutions and the economy** — finance, governance and the macro-institutional conditions;
- **sector-specific research**;
- the **International Productivity Monitor**.

It also runs a **Productivity Lab** for data and measurement, a policy unit, and nine regional Productivity Forums covering the UK's nations and English regions.

## Place in the UK research system

TPI is a publicly funded research institute hosted by a university and organised as a UK-wide consortium. It occupies the space between academic research, policy advice and regional engagement. Its work is relevant to industrial policy, regional policy and the measurement of public and private productivity.
