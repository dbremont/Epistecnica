# Institute for Manufacturing (IfM)

> The **Institute for Manufacturing** is the manufacturing research, education and engagement institute of the University of Cambridge's Department of Engineering. It was established in 1998, building on a manufacturing-engineering teaching tradition that began at Cambridge in 1966.

The IfM describes itself as a place for new thinking about manufacturing and for putting those ideas into practice. Its work spans advanced manufacturing, industrial systems, technology management, supply chains, sustainability and manufacturing policy.

## Research and engagement

The institute's research groups and centres include:

- industrial resilience and international manufacturing;
- digital manufacturing and industrial automation;
- technology management, innovation and intellectual-property management;
- strategy and performance;
- industrial sustainability, healthcare and advanced materials;
- science, technology and innovation policy;
- the Cambridge Service Alliance and related industry programmes.

Its engagement arm, **IfM Engage**, works with firms, governments and universities on manufacturing strategy, technology roadmapping and capability building. The institute also runs manufacturing education and executive courses, including a long-standing manufacturing-engineering programme.

## Place in the UK research system

The IfM is a university-hosted research institute, not a public agency. It combines engineering research, management research and direct firm engagement, and it is one of the UK institutions that connects manufacturing capability to industrial policy and firm-level practice.
