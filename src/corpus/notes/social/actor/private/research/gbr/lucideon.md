# Lucideon

> **Lucideon** is a materials development, testing and assurance company in Stoke-on-Trent, and the direct descendant of the British Ceramic Research Association. The British Refractories Research Association (1920) and the British Pottery Research Association (1937) merged in April 1948 as the BCRA; it became British Ceramic Research Ltd, traded as CERAM, and was rebranded Lucideon on 1 February 2014.

## Ceramic focus

- **Ceramic testing and analysis** — the services a pottery or a technical-ceramics plant cannot staff in-house.
- **Materials development** — body and glaze formulation, process troubleshooting.
- **Advanced ceramics** — technical ceramics beyond the traditional whiteware base.
- **Assurance and certification** — conformity assessment against standards.
- **Failure investigation** — why a fired body cracked, a glaze crazed, a refractory spalled.
- **Information services** — the ceramics library and abstracts built by the association.

## Place in the field

Lucideon is the industrial-research model that predates the modern institute: a sector-wide research association, funded by the industry it served, that later became a company. The BCRA's work in the 1950s on sintered oxides and dielectric materials is the point where the pottery association turns into a technical-ceramics laboratory, which is why the same institution can test a sanitary-ware glaze and a ceramic substrate. It is not a university and not a manufacturer; it is the testing and assurance layer of a ceramics industry, kept alive as a business after the industry that funded it contracted.
