# Centre for Advanced Structural Ceramics (CASC)

> The **Centre for Advanced Structural Ceramics** is a research centre in the Department of Materials at Imperial College London, started in July 2008 with a five-year EPSRC grant of £5.5 million to build a UK focus for structural ceramics. It runs an industry consortium and an annual summer school alongside the research, so the centre is a meeting point as much as a laboratory.

## Ceramic focus

- **Structural ceramics** — the load-bearing end of the class, where brittleness is the design problem.
- **Ultra-high-temperature ceramics** — borides, carbides and nitrides for extreme environments.
- **Ceramic matrix composites** — fibre-reinforced ceramics and their interfaces.
- **Mechanical behaviour** — fracture, creep, fatigue, strength distributions and proof testing.
- **Processing and joining** — powder routes, densification, and the joints that usually fail first.
- **Coatings** — environmental and thermal barrier systems.

## Place in the field

CASC is the UK's structural-ceramics centre, and the choice of "structural" is the whole programme: not whiteware, not electronic ceramics, but the materials that must carry load at temperature — aerospace, energy and defence. Its funding model is the consortium: member firms pay in and get access to the laboratory and to the students it trains, which keeps the research tied to a requirement rather than to a grant cycle.
