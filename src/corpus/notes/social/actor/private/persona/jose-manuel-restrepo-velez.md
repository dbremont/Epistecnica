# José Manuel Restrepo Vélez

> Colombian politician, historian, geographer and botanist (Envigado, 1781 – Bogotá, 1863), first Secretary of the Interior of Gran Colombia and author of the *Ensayo sobre la geografía, producciones, industria y población de la provincia de Antioquia* (1809) and the *Historia de la Revolución de la República de Colombia* (1858).

## Life and work

> Restrepo studied at the Colegio de San Bartolomé in Bogotá and worked with José Celestino Mutis and Francisco José de Caldas on the botanical and geographical surveys of the viceroyalty. His first work, the 1809 *Ensayo sobre la geografía, producciones, industria y población de la provincia de Antioquia*, was published in Caldas's *Semanario del Nuevo Reino de Granada* and accompanied the first technically surveyed map of the province.

> He was governor of Antioquia in 1819 and, from 1821 to 1830, Secretary of the Interior of Gran Colombia under Bolívar and Santander — the first holder of the office, and the organizer of the national archive. His later histories, the *Historia de la Revolución de la República de Colombia* and the *Historia de la Nueva Granada*, became the founding works of republican Colombian historiography.

## The mining argument

> In the *Ensayo*, Restrepo argues that mining wealth produces only an ephemeral prosperity, while agriculture, the arts and commerce are the true sources of a nation's wealth. The passage is a classic statement of the case later called the resource curse — that a mineral endowment can fail to make a country opulent, and can even divert it from the productive activities that do.
