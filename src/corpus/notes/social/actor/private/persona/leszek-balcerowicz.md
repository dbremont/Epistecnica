# Leszek Balcerowicz

> Polish economist who designed the "Balcerowicz Plan" — the 1990 shock-therapy package that moved Poland from a centrally planned to a market economy. Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance (1989–1991, 1997–2000), then President of the National Bank of Poland (2001–2007).
