# Ehrenfried Walther von Tschirnhaus


> **Ehrenfried Walther von Tschirnhaus** (1651–1708) — German mathematician, physicist and philosopher, correspondent of Spinoza, Leibniz and Newton. Known for the *Tschirnhaus transformation*, the change of variable that removes the intermediate terms from an equation of degree *n* (published in the *Acta Eruditorum*, 1683), and for his optical researches on burning mirrors and lenses; author of the *Medicina mentis* (Amsterdam, 1687), the logic of discovery that sets out his art of invention. With Johann Friedrich Böttger he shares the European invention of hard-paste porcelain, though he died in October 1708, before the Meissen manufactory opened.
