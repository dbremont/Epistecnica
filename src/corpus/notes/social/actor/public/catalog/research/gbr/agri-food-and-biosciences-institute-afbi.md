# Agri-Food and Biosciences Institute (AFBI)

> Northern Ireland's agri-food and biosciences institute, providing research, diagnostics and scientific advice to the agri-food sector.

## Focus

- Animal health and welfare
- Plant science and crop production
- Food science and safety
- Environmental and marine science
