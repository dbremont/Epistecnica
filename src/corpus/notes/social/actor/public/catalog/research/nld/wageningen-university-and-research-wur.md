# Wageningen University & Research (WUR)

> The Dutch university and research organisation for food, agriculture, environment and health, with a long-standing international role in agricultural science.

## Focus

- Plant and crop science
- Animal science and aquaculture
- Food technology and nutrition
- Environmental and climate research
