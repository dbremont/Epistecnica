# Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences

> The **Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences** (Koninklijke Nederlandse Akademie van Wetenschappen, **KNAW**) is the Dutch national academy: founded in **1808** by Louis Napoleon as the *Koninklijk Instituut van Wetenschappen, Letterkunde en Schoone Kunsten*, an academy since 1851, and today an elected body of members with an advisory role and a set of research institutes under it.

## What it is

| Dimension | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Legal form** | A statutory body (*openbaar lichaam*) with its own governance, independent of the ministries it advises |
| **Membership** | Elected members — the Dutch equivalent of the academy's *fellows*, not a funding body |
| **Function** | Advice to government on science and scholarship, prizes and recognition, and the running of research institutes |
| **Institutes** | A portfolio of national institutes — demography, social history, language and literature, the humanities, and others — hosted under the academy |
| **Position** | The **academy** pillar of the Dutch research system, beside the research council ([NWO](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/public/catalog/research/nld/nwo/dutch-research-council-nwo.md)) and the applied-research organisation (TNO) |

## The triad, and why the position matters

> The Dutch system is the clearest European case of a **three-pillar** research architecture: a council that funds (**NWO**), an organisation that does applied research (**TNO**), and an academy that recognises, advises and hosts institutes (**KNAW**). The three are distinct because they answer different questions — who gets the money, who solves the practical problem, who certifies the standing of the field — and the corpus keeps all three for that reason.

> Read against the [Czech](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/public/catalog/research/cze/czech-academy-of-sciences.md) and [Polish](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/public/catalog/research/pol/polish-academy-of-sciences.md) academies, the Dutch case is the **advisory** variant: the same form, but with the funding and the applied research deliberately placed outside it. That is the design decision that distinguishes the Netherlands from the state-academy model, and the one worth comparing in the [public organization](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/public/public-organization.md) typology.
