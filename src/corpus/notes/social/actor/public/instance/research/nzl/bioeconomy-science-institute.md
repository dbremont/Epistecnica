# Bioeconomy Science Institute

> The New Zealand institute that brings together AgResearch, Manaaki Whenua – Landcare Research, Plant & Food Research and Scion to work on bioeconomy science.

## Focus

- Bioenergy and biomaterials
- Bioprotection and biosecurity
- Climate resilience
- Land-based production
- Foods and ingredients
