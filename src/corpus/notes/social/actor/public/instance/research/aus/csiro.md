# CSIRO

> Australia's national science agency; agriculture and food is one of its principal domains, from crop genetics to farming systems and food processing.

## Focus

- Crop and pasture science
- Livestock and animal health
- Digital agriculture and sensing
- Food innovation and bioproducts
