# Social Meta

> **Social Meta** is the reflexive shelf of the social corpus: the notes that state the schema its elements are written against — the element types, the layers, the facets, and the analytical apparatus those rest on — rather than describing an instance of social reality.

> Nothing on this shelf is an element. Each note here answers a question the element notes must presuppose — *what kind of thing is this? by which mechanism is it scoped? in which stratum does it exist?* — and the notes it governs point back to it. The technica corpus keeps the same shelf in [Meta Tecnica](/view.html?p=notes/technica/meta/tecnica.md), and the epistemica corpus in [`epistemica/meta/`](/view.html?p=notes/epistemica/meta/theory/philosophy.md).

## What the shelf holds

| Note | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| [Facet](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/facet.md) | What a facet is, the authoritative catalogue of facets in use, facet against field, and facets against layers. |
| [Social Ontology](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/social-ontology.md) | What the elements of social reality are, and the formalism that types them. |
| [Multi Layer](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/multi-layer.md) | The element that inhabits more than one analytical layer at once — ontic and synontic, for instance. |
| [Reflexivity](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/reflexivity.md) | The fact that agents interpret the structures they are part of, and thereby alter them. |
| [Contingency](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/contingency.md) | The fact that a social arrangement could have been otherwise, so that its persistence needs explaining. |
| [Facet Scope](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/facet/facet.md) | The contract for a per-facet note, and the three in use. |

The last three are placeholders: they are named and held in the shelf so the schema has a home, and they still wait for the note that states them.

## The rule of the shelf

- **Schema, not instance.** No note here decomposes a social element into its parts; that is the element notes' work, and it is why nothing on this shelf carries an `Instance Tree Path` table.
- **The catalogue is single.** Facets are listed once, in [Facet](/view.html?p=notes/social/meta/facet.md). The per-facet notes develop its rows; they do not restate the list.
- **Cited, not attached.** A note belongs here when other notes have to be read against it. A definition that only one note uses belongs in that note.

## Why a section shelf rather than a single theory note

> The apparatus is not one doctrine but several axes that are used separately: a definition of the element types, an axis of layers, a test for facets, a claim about contingency. Each is consulted on its own, by different notes, and each can be revised without the others.

Keeping them as separate notes also keeps the facet catalogue usable as a catalogue: a list of facets is a reference table, not an argument, and it should be readable without the rest of the ontology in front of it.
