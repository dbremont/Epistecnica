# University

> The **University** is the self-governing corporation of teachers and scholars — *universitas magistrorum et scholarium* — chartered to teach an advanced curriculum, examine students, and confer degrees in several academic disciplines. It is a `Collective` read as `Education Actor`: where the older education actor is warranted by a court, a temple, or a mosque, the university is warranted by a charter and governs itself. It is the institutional form the European Middle Ages added to the transmission of knowledge, and the one that spread to the rest of the world.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**University belongs to the `Collective / Organization` social element type, Kind of `Education Actor`** — a staffed body that transmits and certifies knowledge, acting as one through membership and rules. Its layer is **Ontic** where it exists extra-mentally (a charter, faculties, masters, students, buildings, an endowment) and **Synontic** where it exists through shared recognition (the degree that is honoured abroad, the *ius ubique docendi* that makes a master teachable anywhere). Its facet is ideational-educational: the dominant mechanism is the production and certification of learned persons under a warrant the body itself holds.

It is distinct from the older **Education Actor** (the warranted teaching body bound to a sponsoring court, temple, or mosque, governed by that sponsor rather than by its own masters and students), from the **Firm** (the school run for fees, which sells teaching rather than warranting it), from the **Research Actor** (the institute that produces warranted knowledge without conferring degrees), and from the **learned society** (the academy that recognizes scholars it does not teach or examine). The university both teaches and examines; the prerogative to confer a degree that its holder may use elsewhere is what it holds that its neighbours do not.

Readable secondarily as a `Research Actor` — from the nineteenth century the professoriate's seminar and laboratory become a research component of the same corporation — and as an `Institution` (the charter, the statutes, and the degree authority as stabilized rules). Those readings are prose, not second roots; the tree below is typed once.

### What is this social instance?

> The university as a *kind*: the corporation of masters and students that holds a charter, works through faculties, and confers degrees. Its oldest instance is Bologna, where teaching began around 1088 and the corporation was organized as *universitas scholarium* — guilds of *students* hiring and paying their masters — by the late twelfth century; Paris, emerging around 1150 as a corporation attached to the cathedral school and chartered by King Philip II in 1200 and recognized by Pope Innocent III in 1215, organized as the reverse: a guild of *masters* whose chancellor conferred the licence to teach. The two arrangements — student-run and master-run — are the two constitutions the form began with, and the rest of Europe's universities were founded on one of them.
>
> Lineage: the cathedral and monastic schools, which taught without conferring → the *studium generale*, the customary name for the medieval university, whose degree was recognized beyond its own city → the chartered corporation with its four faculties (arts, theology, law, medicine) and its privileges of self-government, exemption, and the *ius ubique docendi* → the colleges and their endowments → the Humboldtian research university, in which the seminar and the laboratory joined teaching as the institution's second function → the state-funded and state-regulated mass university, in which the charter's privileges give way to public funding and accreditation.
