# Fraunhofer Institute for Ceramic Technologies and Systems (IKTS)

> The **Fraunhofer Institute for Ceramic Technologies and Systems** is the ceramics institute of the Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft, founded in Dresden in 1992 and now run from Dresden and Hermsdorf in Thuringia. It is an applied-research institute in the Fraunhofer mould: a large part of its work is contract research for industry and public clients, so its agenda is set by what a manufacturer is willing to pay to have solved.

## Ceramic focus

- **Advanced and structural ceramics** — oxide and non-oxide ceramics from powder to finished component.
- **Ceramic composites** — fibre- and particle-reinforced ceramics for wear, friction and high-temperature duty.
- **Manufacturing** — shaping, sintering, machining, joining and additive manufacturing of ceramic parts.
- **Energy materials** — solid-oxide fuel cells and electrolysers, gas-separation membranes, battery materials.
- **Electronic ceramics** — sensors, actuators, piezoelectric and dielectric components.
- **Characterisation** — microstructure, mechanical and thermophysical properties, non-destructive evaluation.

## Place in the field

IKTS is the translation institute of the German ceramic system. The universities produce the science, the ceramic industry is fragmented and mid-sized, and IKTS is where a process is scaled to a pilot line: the Hermsdorf site carries the technical and transparent ceramics, Dresden carries energy, membranes and electronics. It is not a standards body and not a university — it sells development work, so its portfolio is a readable statement of what German industry could not solve alone.
