# Leiden University

> **Leiden University** (Dutch: *Universiteit Leiden*) is the oldest university in the Netherlands, founded in Leiden in 1575 by William of Orange. It became the principal centre of Dutch learning in theology, law, medicine, and the natural sciences, and has remained a public research university funded by the Dutch state.

## Formulation

### What is this social instance?

> Leiden was chartered in 1575, in the first years of the Dutch Revolt, as a Calvinist academy for the new Dutch Republic; its founding statutes gave it a theological faculty and the right to grant degrees. It grew into the Republic's leading university: the Leiden botanical garden (1587), the anatomical theatre, the observatory, and the university press made it a European centre of natural philosophy and medicine.
>
> Its scholars included Joseph Scaliger, Daniel Heinsius, and Hugo Grotius in the humanities; Herman Boerhaave in medicine and chemistry; and Christiaan Huygens and Willem 's Gravesande in the mathematical sciences. The university survived the Napoleonic period, was reorganised under the Kingdom of the Netherlands, and today is a state-funded public university with faculties spanning the humanities, law, medicine, the sciences, and the social sciences.

### Is the type of this `social element instance` ambiguous?

Yes, in three readings:

- **Education actor vs. research actor.** Leiden teaches and certifies, but from the seventeenth century its professors also produced the Republic's natural-philosophical and legal scholarship. The education reading is primary; the research activity is a component of the same compound, not a second actor.
- **Private corporation vs. public body.** The 1575 university was a chartered corporation with its own statutes and revenues; the modern university is state-funded and state-regulated. The type reading is the same — an education actor — while its ownership and governance changed.
- **The university vs. its constituent faculties and institutes.** The faculties, the botanical garden, the observatory, and the press are parts of the compound, not separate actors; their own decompositions live in their own notes.

The education-actor reading is primary: it is the one the other readings presuppose.

### Which facet does this element belong to, if applicable?
