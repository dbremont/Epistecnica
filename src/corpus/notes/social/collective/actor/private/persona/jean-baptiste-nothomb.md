# Jean-Baptiste Nothomb

> **Jean-Baptiste Nothomb** (1805–1881) was a Belgian statesman and diplomat, one of the architects of the new Belgian state after 1830 and a promoter of railway construction and industrial development.

## Life and work

Nothomb was born in Luxembourg and trained as a lawyer. He played a central role in the negotiations that secured Belgian independence and later served as minister of public works and prime minister.

His economic policy focused on infrastructure, especially the state-built railway network, and on the integration of the Belgian economy. He belongs to the generation of liberal statesmen who used public investment to support industrial development.
