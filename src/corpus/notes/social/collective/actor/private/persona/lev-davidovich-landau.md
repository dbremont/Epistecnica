# Lev Davidovich Landau

> Soviet theoretical physicist who led the Landau school and co-authored the multi-volume Course of Theoretical Physics. He introduced the order-parameter theory of second-order phase transitions, explained superfluidity in liquid helium, and predicted Landau damping; he received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1962.
