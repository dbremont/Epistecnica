# Otto Diderik Lütken

> **Otto Diderik Lütken** (1713–1776) was a Danish official and economic writer. His *Undersøgninger* (1760) examined the national economy, production, and the circulation of goods within the Danish monarchy.

## Life and work

Lütken worked in the Danish administrative system and wrote within the cameralist and patriotic-improvement tradition of the eighteenth century. His *Undersøgninger* addressed the relationship between production, consumption, and the circulation of wealth, and argued for policies that would strengthen domestic economic activity.

He belongs to the group of Danish officials who used economic writing to press for administrative reform and the better use of the kingdom’s resources.
