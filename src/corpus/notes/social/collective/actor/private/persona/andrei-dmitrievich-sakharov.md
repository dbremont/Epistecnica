# Andrei Dmitrievich Sakharov

> Soviet nuclear physicist who helped design the first Soviet hydrogen bomb and later became a leading human-rights dissident. He proposed the Sakharov conditions for baryogenesis and received the 1975 Nobel Peace Prize.
