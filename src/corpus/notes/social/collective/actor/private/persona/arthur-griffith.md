# Arthur Griffith

> **Arthur Griffith** (1871–1922) was an Irish nationalist politician and writer, founder of Sinn Féin and a leading advocate of Irish economic and political autonomy.

## Life and work

Griffith worked as a journalist and founded the *United Irishman*. His *The Resurrection of Hungary* (1904) proposed a dual monarchy for Ireland on the Hungarian model, and his programme combined political independence with economic protection and the development of Irish industry.

He served as president of Dáil Éireann during the Anglo-Irish Treaty negotiations and died in 1922. His economic nationalism shaped the early policy of the Irish Free State.

## Selected works

- *The Resurrection of Hungary* (1904).
- Political and economic journalism.
