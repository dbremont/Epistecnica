# Tomáš Baťa

> **Tomáš Baťa** (1876–1932) was a Czech industrialist and the founder of the Bata shoe company, one of the most successful mass-production enterprises of interwar Europe.

## Life and work

Baťa built a global shoe business from his family workshop in Zlín. He introduced assembly-line methods, standardized sizes, company schools, and a paternalist welfare system, and transformed Zlín into a planned industrial town.

His model combined mass production, low prices, and export-led growth. It became a reference point for industrial organization and regional development in interwar Czechoslovakia.
