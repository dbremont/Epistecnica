# Ole Stockfleth Pihl

> **Ole Stockfleth Pihl** (d. 1775) was a Danish-Norwegian clergyman and economic writer, remembered as the editor of the *Oeconomisk Journal* (1757–1758), a periodical devoted to economic policy, fiscal questions, and practical economic knowledge.

## Life and work

Pihl belonged to the circle of Danish and Norwegian officials and clergymen who promoted economic improvement in the mid-eighteenth century. The *Oeconomisk Journal* published short essays on agriculture, manufactures, trade, taxation, and the management of natural resources, and it helped circulate cameralist and patriotic ideas in the Danish monarchy.

His editorial work places him among the intermediaries who translated the era’s economic literature into practical proposals for Danish and Norwegian readers.
