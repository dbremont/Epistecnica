# Pierre Le Pesant de Boisguilbert

> **Pierre Le Pesant, sieur de Boisguilbert** (1646–1714) was a French magistrate and economic writer, one of the earliest critics of Colbertist mercantilism and a precursor of the idea of a self-regulating market.

## Life and work

Boisguilbert was born in Rouen into a family of magistrates, studied with the Jansenists, and became lieutenant-general of the bailliage of Rouen. His administrative experience gave him a close view of the misery caused by taxation and the depression of French agriculture.

*Le Détail de la France* (1695) described the ruin of the kingdom and proposed a fundamental reform of taxes, the free circulation of grain, and the removal of obstacles to internal commerce. He argued that money was only a means of exchange and that wealth depended on production and consumption. His writings were condemned, and he was exiled in 1707.

## Selected works

- *Le Détail de la France* (1695).
- *Supplément au Détail de la France* (1707).
- *Dissertation sur la nature des richesses, de l’argent et des tributs* (1707).
