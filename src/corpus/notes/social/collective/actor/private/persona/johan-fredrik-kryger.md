# Johan Fredrik Kryger

> **Johan Fredrik Kryger** (1707–1777) was a Swedish merchant and economic writer. His *Tankar om swenske fabriquerne* (1755) discussed Swedish manufactures and the policy best suited to their development.

## Life and work

Kryger wrote from the perspective of a practising merchant with an interest in industrial policy. His tract examined the condition of Swedish manufactures and argued for measures that would protect and improve domestic production.

He belongs to the mid-eighteenth-century Swedish debate over manufactures, foreign trade, and the balance of economic power in northern Europe.

## Selected works
