# Claude-Frédéric Bastiat

> **Claude-Frédéric Bastiat** (1801–1850) was a French economist, magistrate, and deputy whose method was not the treatise but the **sophism exposed** — the same demolition applied to the tariff, the subsidy, the broken window, and the legislator who believes himself made of finer clay. He argued that exchange is harmonious and that the state's benevolent interventions are a "great fiction by which everybody tries to live at the expense of everybody else", a claim he tested in the one place where it bit: the wine trade of his own Landes.

## Formulation

### What is this social instance?

> This instance is the person Bastiat as a participant in social reality: a Gascon landowner and magistrate who became the most widely read economic pamphleteer of his century, and whose arguments were made in the Chamber as much as in print, and against socialists in Paris as much as against protectionists in the Landes.
>
> Born **30 June 1801** in Bayonne, died **24 December 1850** in Rome of tuberculosis, he was the son of a merchant family that had bought the estate of Sengresse near **Mugron**, in the Landes, at auction in 1795 — property acquired out of the Revolution, in a region whose wine economy the Empire's blockade and the Republic's taxes had broken. After the July Revolution of 1830 he circulated a pamphlet to the Landes electors criticising government taxation and appealing to the voters' own rationality on behalf of the general interest; he was elected **justice of the peace of Mugron in 1831**, sat on the **Conseil général of the Landes from 1832**, and entered the **National Assembly after the revolution of 1848**.

### What type of social element does this instance belong to?

> A **Persona**, typed through `Interaction Unit (→ Kind) Social Actor (→ Kind) Societal Actor`: an individual actor constituted outside public authority. His magistracy and his seat are offices held within a societal life, not a change of kind — the state-actor reading does not arise for a *juge de paix* whose jurisdiction was a canton and a deputy whose power was a vote in a chamber he did not command.
>
> Its dominant layer is **Agentic** where his argument, his journalism, and his parliamentary interventions are at issue, with **Ontic** aspects (his body, his manuscripts, the Landes estate, the journey to Rome) and **Synontic** aspects (the classifications through which he was recognized: *juge de paix*, deputy, "the most brilliant economic journalist who ever lived").

### Is the type of this `social element instance` unambiguous?

> Yes. The person as societal actor is the unambiguous root. Three neighbouring readings are secondary aspects, recorded as prose rather than grown as second roots: (1) Bastiat as the *producer of epistemic elements* — the essays, the treatises, the *Harmonies* — which belong to the epistemic decomposition and are linked by reference; (2) Bastiat as a *member of a doctrinal school*, the French liberal school, which is an association and an intellectual lineage rather than a kind; and (3) Bastiat as an *instrument of a social class*, the wine-growing bourgeoisie of the Landes, which is a relation he carried, not a thing he was.

### Which facet does this element belong to, if applicable?

> Economical first — tariffs, taxation, exchange, and the incidence of state action are his whole subject — with **ideational** aspects where the doctrine of harmony and the theory of legal plunder are at issue, and a **normative-legal** face where *The Law* argues about what legislation may rightly do. ### What is a critical conceptual evaluation of this `social element instance`?

> The verdicts first. **Conceptual identification**: adequate — the persona-concept locates him as a societal actor whose offices are activities and associations rather than a second kind. **Definitional clarity**: strained precisely where his fame lies, since "economic journalist" names a role in a market for argument rather than a constituent of the person; the tree keeps it as an **attribute**, which is the right level. **Conceptual determinacy**: adequate, with each row naming one thing — instrument, guidance, activity — and no row doing two jobs. **Internal consistency**: strained where the note must decide whether the *sophism* is his instrument or an epistemic form; it is placed as instrumentation, and the essays themselves are referred to the epistemic decomposition. **Representational adequacy**: adequate for the actor and deliberately silent on the truth of his economics. **Purpose adequacy**: adequate — the note exists to make him citable as an actor in a corpus that argues about industrial policy, and the rows do that without adjudicating the argument.
>
> The deeper strain is one the concept cannot remove, only record. Bastiat's own career shows the objection to his theory from inside: the free-trader began as a **producer** complaining about the tax on his region's wine, and the harmony he later asserted between producer and consumer is a claim about the long run that the producer in the short run does not experience. That is the reason the note puts the Landes crisis in the constitution and the associations rather than in a preface — the argument has a place, and the place explains something about the argument.
