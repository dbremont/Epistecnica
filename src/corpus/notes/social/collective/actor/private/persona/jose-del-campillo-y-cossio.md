# José del Campillo y Cossío

> **José del Campillo y Cossío** (1693–1743) was a Spanish statesman and economic reformer. His *Nuevo sistema de gobierno económico para la América* (written c. 1743, published 1789) proposed a comprehensive reform of colonial administration, commerce, and productive development.

## Life and work

Campillo served the Spanish crown in war and finance, becoming secretary of state for war and finance under Philip V. His American programme argued for the economic development of the colonies, the reform of trade, and the integration of colonial production into a renewed imperial economy.

The *Nuevo sistema* circulated in manuscript before its late publication and influenced later Bourbon reformers. It belongs to the eighteenth-century Spanish attempt to combine imperial administration with economic improvement.
