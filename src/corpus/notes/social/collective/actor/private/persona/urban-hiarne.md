# Urban Hiärne

> **Urban Hiärne** (1641–1724) was a Swedish physician, chemist, and mining official. He served on the Swedish Board of Mines and wrote on mineralogy, metallurgy, and the exploitation of natural resources.

## Life and work

Hiärne studied medicine and natural philosophy and became one of the leading scientific officials of the Swedish state. His work on mineralogy and mining chemistry was connected to the Board of Mines and to the practical needs of Sweden’s metal industries.

He also wrote on natural history and on the early history of Sweden. His combination of chemical, mineralogical, and administrative work makes him a representative of the seventeenth-century alliance between natural knowledge and the exploitation of natural resources.

## Selected works

- Mineralogical, chemical, and mining writings.
- Natural-historical and antiquarian works.
