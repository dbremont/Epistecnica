# František Palacký

> **František Palacký** (1798–1876) was a Czech historian and politician, the central figure of the Czech national revival and author of the *History of the Czech Nation in Bohemia and Moravia*.

## Life and work

Palacký was born in Moravia, studied in Bratislava and Vienna, and became the official historian of the Bohemian estates. His multi-volume history interpreted Czech history as a struggle for liberty and constitutional order.

In politics he advocated federalism within the Habsburg monarchy and the cultural and political emancipation of the Czech nation. His work shaped the national consciousness of the nineteenth-century Czech middle class.

## Selected works

- *History of the Czech Nation in Bohemia and Moravia* (1836–1867).
- Political and historical essays.
