# Erik Pontoppidan

> **Erik Pontoppidan** (1698–1764) was a Danish-Norwegian bishop, historian, and economic writer. A prolific figure of the Danish Enlightenment, he is best known in economic history for *Eutropii Philadelphi Oeconomiske Balance* (1759) and for editing *Danmarks og Norges oeconomiske Magazin* (1757–1764).

## Life and work

Pontoppidan was born in Aarhus and educated in theology. He worked as a tutor, pastor, and professor before becoming bishop of Bergen in 1747 and later bishop of Zealand. His ecclesiastical career did not confine his interests: he wrote on history, natural theology, geography, and the economic improvement of Denmark-Norway.

The *Oeconomiske Balance* argued for the use of domestic raw materials, Danish labour, and economic self-sufficiency. The *oeconomiske Magazin* collected essays on agriculture, manufactures, natural resources, and practical improvement, and became one of the main vehicles of cameralist and patriotic economic debate in the Danish monarchy.

## Selected works

- *Eutropii Philadelphi Oeconomiske Balance* (1759).
- *Danmarks og Norges oeconomiske Magazin* (editor, 1757–1764).
- *Danske Atlas* (1763–1781).
