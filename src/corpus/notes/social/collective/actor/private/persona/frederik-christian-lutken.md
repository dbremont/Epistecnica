# Frederik Christian Lütken

> **Frederik Christian Lütken** was an eighteenth-century Danish agricultural and economic writer. His work belongs to the practical-improvement literature that sought to raise agricultural productivity and strengthen the rural economy of the Danish monarchy.

## Life and work

The biographical record for Lütken is thin. He is known chiefly through his agricultural and economic writings, which discussed cultivation, estate management, and the improvement of rural resources in the tradition of Danish patriotic and cameralist reform.

His contribution is best understood as part of the wider eighteenth-century effort to apply economic knowledge to agriculture and local administration.
