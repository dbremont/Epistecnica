# David Riazanov

> Born David Borisovich Goldendakh; Russian revolutionary, Marxologist, bibliographer and archivist. He founded the Marx–Engels Institute in Moscow and edited the first scholarly editions of Marx and Engels, and was executed in the Great Purge in 1938.
