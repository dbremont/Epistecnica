# Jonathan Swift

> **Jonathan Swift** (1667–1745) was an Anglo-Irish cleric, satirist, and political writer, and one of the most influential critics of colonial economic policy in eighteenth-century Ireland.

## Life and work

Swift was born in Dublin, educated at Trinity College, and served as dean of St Patrick’s Cathedral. His *Drapier’s Letters* (1724) attacked the English patent for Wood’s halfpence and became a symbol of Irish resistance to colonial exploitation.

*A Modest Proposal* (1729) used savage irony to expose the poverty and misgovernment of Ireland. Swift’s economic writings defended Irish autonomy and attacked the absentee landlords, restrictive trade laws, and dependence on England.

## Selected works

- *The Drapier’s Letters* (1724).
- *A Modest Proposal* (1729).
