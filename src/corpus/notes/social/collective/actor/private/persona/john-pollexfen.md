# John Pollexfen

> **John Pollexfen** (1636–1715) was an English merchant, MP, and economic writer. His *A Discourse of Trade, Coyn, and Paper Credit* (1697) examined trade, money, credit, and the recoinage crisis of the 1690s.

## Life and work

Pollexfen traded in wine and other commodities and later served as a commissioner of the Board of Trade. He wrote in defence of a mercantilist understanding of national wealth, arguing that excessive paper credit and the export of bullion could damage the coinage and the balance of trade.

His *Discourse* was one of the works to which Isaac Newton responded in his capacity as Master of the Mint. It is a useful source for the monetary and commercial debates of the 1690s.
