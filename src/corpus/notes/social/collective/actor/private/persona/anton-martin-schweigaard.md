# Anton Martin Schweigaard

> **Anton Martin Schweigaard** (1808–1870) was a Norwegian jurist, economist, and politician, a central figure in the modernization of Norwegian law and economic policy in the nineteenth century.

## Life and work

Schweigaard taught law and economics at the University of Christiania and sat in the Storting. He argued for economic liberalism, the reform of the legal system, and the development of a modern administrative state.

His work helped shape the legal and economic framework of nineteenth-century Norway, and he is remembered as one of the most influential intellectuals of the Norwegian national awakening.
