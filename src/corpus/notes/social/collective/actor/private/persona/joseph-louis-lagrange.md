# Joseph-Louis Lagrange

> *“Those who love analysis will see with pleasure that mechanics has now become a branch of it, and will be grateful to me for having thus extended its domain.”*

> **Joseph-Louis Lagrange** — born **Giuseppe Lodovico Lagrangia** at Turin on 25 January 1736 and died in Paris on 10 April 1813 — gave mechanics its analytic form, in the *Mécanique analytique*, the treatise that abolished the diagram and made general equations the whole of statics and dynamics; and gave the theory of equations the direction it traveled until Galois: the resolvent, the permutation, the method of reading a polynomial through the behavior of combinations of its roots. The name of the discipline, *analytical mechanics*, is his; so are the Lagrangian, the Lagrange points of the three-body problem, and the calculus of variations he organized as a branch of the calculus in its own right. Professor of mathematics at the Turin artillery school at nineteen, director of the mathematics class of Frederick the Great's Berlin academy for two decades, then the anchor of the Paris institutions his last years were written into — the century's arithmetic, metric and teaching owed its shape to him as much as its wings to Euler and d'Alembert.

### What is a critical conceptual evaluation of this `social element instance`?

> Evaluating the person-concept applied to Lagrange: it locates him cleanly (embodied individual, societal actor, honorific political layer), and the tree's rows separate constitution, agency, activity, association and attribute instead of conflating the person with the treatise — the *Mécanique analytique* is linked, not integrated into his social being, and mechanics, the theory of equations and the calculus of variations are activities he performed, not elements he is made of. The concept strains where the *attribute* rows claim a standing inseparable from the epistemic content that grounds it: "equations authority" is how others place him, but the warrant lives in the resolvent — content of the corpus's algebra, read out of the cossica technique tree and only cross-linked from here. The diagramless discipline strains the tree in the other direction: it reads first as a thesis about mechanics, which is content of his noetic occupation, not a constituent of his person — the tree types the man; the thesis belongs to the work.
