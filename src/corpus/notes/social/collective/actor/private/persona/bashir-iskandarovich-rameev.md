# Bashir Iskandarovich Rameev


> **Bashir Iskandarovich Rameev** (1918–1994) — Soviet inventor and scientist, one of the founders of Soviet computing: author of 23 patents, including the first patent in the field of electronic computing registered in the USSR (the Automatic Electronic Digital Machine, 1948), and the designer of the Strela, the first serially manufactured Soviet mainframe, and of the Ural computer series.
