# James Steuart

> **James Steuart** (1712–1780) was a Scottish economist, the author of *An Inquiry into the Principles of Political Oeconomy* (1767), the first systematic treatise in English to use “political oeconomy” in its title.

## Life and work

Steuart was a Jacobite who spent years in exile in France, Germany, and Italy. His *Principles* offered a comprehensive account of population, agriculture, manufactures, trade, money, and economic policy.

He defended a managed economy in which the state guided development and demand, and he is often read as the systematic alternative to Adam Smith. His work was influential in Germany and in the cameralist tradition.
