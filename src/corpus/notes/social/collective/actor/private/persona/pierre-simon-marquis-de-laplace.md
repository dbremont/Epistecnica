# Pierre Simon Marquis de Laplace

> *“We ought to regard the present state of the universe as the effect of its antecedent state and as the cause of the state that is to follow. An intelligence knowing all the forces acting in nature at a given instant, as well as the momentary positions of all things in the universe, would be able to comprehend in one single formula the motions of the largest bodies as well as the lightest atoms in the world, provided that its intellect were sufficiently powerful to subject all data to analysis; to it nothing would be uncertain, the future as well as the past would be present to its eyes. The perfection that the human mind has been able to give to astronomy affords but a feeble outline of such an intelligence.”*

> **Pierre-Simon, marquis de Laplace** — mathematician, astronomer and physicist, born at Beaumont-en-Auge in Normandy on 23 March 1749 and died in Paris on 5 March 1827 — gave the solar system its analytic form, the *Mécanique céleste* that Newtonian gravitation had awaited for a century, and then gave chance its analytic form in the *Théorie analytique des probabilités*. The invented name of his science, "celestial mechanics", is his; so is the demon above, and the reading of probability as partial knowledge. He held in his lifetime nearly every scientific institution of France worth the name and, briefly, one seat in its government.

### What is a critical conceptual evaluation of this `social element instance`?

> Evaluating the person-concept applied to Laplace: it locates him cleanly (embodied individual, societal actor, no state mandate), and the tree's rows separate constitution, agency, activity, association and attribute instead of conflating the person with the treatises — the documents are linked, not integrated into his social being, and celestial mechanics and probability are activities he performed, not elements he is made of. The concept strains where the *attribute* rows claim a standing that is inseparable from the epistemic content that grounds it: "probability authority" is how others place him, but the warrant lives in the *Théorie analytique*, an element of the corpus's epistemic space and only cross-linked from here. The determinism quote strains the tree in the other direction: it reads first as a thesis about the universe, which is content of his noetic occupation, not a constituent of his person — the tree types the man; the quote belongs to the work.
