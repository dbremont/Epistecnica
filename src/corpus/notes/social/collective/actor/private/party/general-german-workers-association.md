# General German Workers' Association

> German political party founded in Leipzig on 23 May 1863 by Ferdinand Lassalle, the first organized mass workers' party in Germany. It merged with the Social Democratic Workers' Party in 1875 to form the Socialist Workers' Party of Germany, the predecessor of the Social Democratic Party of Germany.
