# Legislature

> A **Legislature** is the assembly through which a polity deliberates and enacts the binding rules it will live under: the public organization whose warrant is election rather than appointment or inheritance, whose procedure *is* the making of law, and whose votes fix the budget, authorize taxation, ratify treaties, confirm officers, and hold the executive to account. It is the organ in which a political community's authority is exercised as deliberation and decision rather than as command.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Legislature belongs to the `Public Organization` social element type (Collective / Organization branch), Kind of `Legislature`** — an organization wielding delegated public authority under law, whose specific warrant is the enactment of binding rules for a polity.

It is not a `Polity` — the political community whose authority the assembly exercises, not the assembly itself; not `Government` — the executive organ, which in parliamentary systems arises *from* the legislature and in presidential ones is separately constituted, so the two must not be collapsed; not a `Council` or a `Commission` — bodies appointed to advise, coordinate, investigate, or regulate without the power to enact; and not a `Party` — the electoral organization that fields candidates into it.

Readable secondarily as a `Deliberative Agency` — the assembly is the structured capacity in which a polity weighs alternatives and commits to a course, and that is the right reading of what it does — and, for an assembly of estates, as a `Collegial Body` of orders rather than of persons. Those readings are prose, not second roots.

The type is also not uniform in structure: a **unicameral** and a **bicameral** legislature are the same kind under two compositions, and the difference (a second chamber that revises, or that represents territories, or that blocks) changes procedure without changing what the organization is.

### What is this social instance?

> The assembly at work. A constitutional or statutory warrant fixes that this body, and no other, may make law; an electoral mandate seats its members for a term and grants them immunity in the exercise of the office; a presiding officer and a business committee order its time; standing committees hold the domain jurisdictions in which most of the actual drafting, evidence-taking, and amendment happens; procedure — agenda, debate, quorum, vote — converts argument into decision; and the products are the statute, the appropriation, the ratification, and the confirmation, alongside the less visible output of oversight: the question, the inquiry, the audit, the confidence vote.
>
> The anatomy has to carry the two things a legislature is often confused about. It is a **place of speech**, and the record of that speech is an institution of its own — the official record, the library, and the research service that let a member speak with something behind the speech. And it is a **place of numbers**: the budget and the tax schedule are legislative products, which is why the finance and revenue committees of a legislature carry weight out of proportion to their membership.
>
> Lineage: the assembly of estates, where orders deliberate and vote as orders → the representative assembly, where districts and populations are represented by number → the committee-centric legislature, in which the chamber ratifies what its committees have already settled → and, in the other direction, the delegating legislature, which transfers rule-making to executives and agencies and keeps only the frame. The direction of travel is not one-way, and the committee-centred form is what makes a modern assembly able to legislate at all.
