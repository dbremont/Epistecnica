# Public Inquiry

> A **Public Inquiry** is a temporary public organization appointed to investigate a matter of public concern, establish facts, and report. It is constituted by a warrant and terms of reference, holds only the powers those instruments confer, and dissolves when its report is delivered.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Public Inquiry belongs to the `Public Organization` social element type, Kind of `Task Group`** — an organization wielding delegated public authority for a bounded mandate and dissolving on delivery. Its layer is **Ontic**: commissioners, counsel, a secretariat, hearings, exhibits and transcripts exist extra-mentally. Its facet is political: the dominant mechanism is authoritative inquiry on behalf of public power.

It is not a **Committee** — a committee is drawn from a parent assembly and reports back into that assembly's decision. It is not a **Court** — a court adjudicates a dispute and its judgment binds; an inquiry finds facts and recommends. It is not a standing **Regulator** — it holds no continuing jurisdiction. It is not a **Department** — it holds no continuing programme or budget line.

Readable secondarily as an `Action Instrument` of the appointing authority, and as a `Forum` in which the state, firms and citizens are made to appear and account. Those readings are prose, not second roots.

### What is this social instance?

> An inquiry fixes *who investigates, under what warrant, with what powers, and to whom it reports*: the appointing authority and terms of reference, the commissioner or panel, counsel assisting and secretariat, the powers to summon and compel, the hearings and research, the findings, and the report that ends the body's life.

> The genus includes the royal commission, the statutory commission of inquiry, the parliamentary inquiry and the departmental inquiry. They differ in warrant, powers and addressee; they share the same form: a temporary body, a bounded question, a public report.
