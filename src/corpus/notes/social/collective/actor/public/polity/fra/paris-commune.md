# Paris Commune

> Revolutionary government that held Paris from 18 March to 28 May 1871, elected by universal male suffrage and organized around recallable delegates on workers' wages. It separated church and state and became the reference experiment in working-class self-government.

## Pattern

Direct democracy through mandated, recallable delegates: legislative and executive power fused in a single council, with officials paid workers' wages to prevent a professional political class.

## Sources

The *Journal officiel* of the Commune, the records of the Commune Council and its commissions, and the contemporary press and memoirs of participants and exiles.
