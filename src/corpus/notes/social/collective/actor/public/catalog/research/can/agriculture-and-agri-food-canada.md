# Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada

> The federal department responsible for agriculture in Canada: policy, programmes, and a network of research centres from the Atlantic to the Pacific.

## Focus

- Crop and livestock research
- Agri-environmental science and soil health
- Food safety and plant health
- Market and trade analysis
