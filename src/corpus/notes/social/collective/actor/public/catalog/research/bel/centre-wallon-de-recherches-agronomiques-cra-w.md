# Centre wallon de Recherches agronomiques (CRA-W)

> The Walloon agricultural research centre: applied research for crop and animal production, food technology and the rural environment.

## Focus

- Crop production and protection
- Animal science and welfare
- Food technology and quality
- Environment and rural development
