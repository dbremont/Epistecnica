# P. L. Kapitza Institute for Physical Problems

> The **Institute for Physical Problems** (Институт физических проблем, IPP) was founded in Moscow in **1934** — at Kapitsa's request, through the USSR Academy of Sciences' charter — to give Pyotr L. Kapitsa a laboratory after his return from England. Its name was chosen by him for what it refused to be: not a low-temperature institute, not a microphysics institute, but *"known scientific problems, the scope of which will be determined by … the personnel."* It is now the **P. L. Kapitza Institute for Physical Problems of the Russian Academy of Sciences** (IPP RAS).

> The record's own curiosity: an institute that never grew — begun with fewer than ten researchers, staffed around fifty in the twenty-first century — inside whose walls three Nobel prizes in physics were carried out, and whose work survived seven re-namings, a house arrest, a war, and the end of the state that chartered it.

### Is the type of this `social element instance` unambiguous?

Yes. One institute, one continuity — the record itself insists on it, tracing the seven names as *the same body* renamed, not seven bodies succeeded. Three neighboring readings are secondary aspects, not rival roots, and are recorded here as prose:

- **Continuity vs. succession.** The 1934 founding, the 1946 removal and the 1991 succession from the USSR Academy to the RAS are one institute in three states, not seven bodies in succession. The tree binds the continuity and carries the years as attributes of it.
- **Person vs. institute.** Kapitsa's name, his program and his standing are inseparable from the institute's identity — but he is the institute's founding *director*, an association row, and the body outlived his tenure twice over.
- **Defense-actor reading.** The 1946–55 atomic work was performed by the institute's staff without its charter becoming a defense-lab charter — the sanction is a *tie-state*, not a re-typing.

### Which facet does this element belong to, if applicable?

**Facet: scientific (research)**, with a state-application face. The mechanism is experimental physics — low-temperature, then atomic, then plasma — the instrumented pursuit of warranted knowledge. The **application face**: the helium turboexpander was an invention of practical value, the hydrogen-bomb calculation was the state's most urgent problem, and the sanctions list of 2023 names the defense sector again. **Political face**: the institute's continuity was guaranteed by the Academy of Sciences and broken twice from above — 1946 and 2023.
