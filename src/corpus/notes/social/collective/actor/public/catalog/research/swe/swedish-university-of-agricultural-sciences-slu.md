# Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU)

> The Swedish university of agricultural sciences at Uppsala: research and education across agriculture, forestry, animal science, food and landscape.

## Focus

- Agricultural and horticultural science
- Forestry and forest ecology
- Animal science and veterinary medicine
- Food and landscape planning
