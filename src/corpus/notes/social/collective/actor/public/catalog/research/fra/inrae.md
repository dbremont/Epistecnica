# INRAE

> The French national research institute for agriculture, food and environment, created in 2020 by merging INRA and IRSTEA; one of the largest agricultural research organisations in Europe.

## Focus

- Agroecology and cropping systems
- Food systems and nutrition
- Biodiversity and ecosystem services
- Water, soils and climate
- Bioeconomy and biomass
