# Rothamsted Research

> The oldest agricultural research station in the world, founded in 1843, and the home of the Broadbalk and Park Grass long-term experiments.

## Focus

- Long-term field experiments
- Soil science and nutrient cycling
- Crop genetics and plant science
- Sustainable and digital agriculture
