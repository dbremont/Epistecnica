# Swedish Defence Research Agency (FOI)

> The **Swedish Defence Research Agency** — *Totalförsvarets forskningsinstitut* (**FOI**) — is Sweden's government agency for research, development and analysis in support of total defence. It works on military technology, security policy, CBRN protection, command-and-control systems and civil emergencies, and it is organised under the Ministry of Defence.

FOI is a public research actor rather than a firm or a university. Its output is research, technology development and analysis for defence and security customers, with published reports in Swedish and English.

## History

FOI was created in **2001** by combining two older agencies:

- **FOA** — the Swedish National Defence Research Institute, created in 1945 from the Swedish Armed Forces Chemical Institute, the Military Physics Institute and the radar section of the Swedish Board of Inventions.
- **FFA** — the National Aeronautical Research Institute, created in 1940 in Bromma to support the Swedish aviation industry, much of it military.

FOA's early work included protection against chemical weapons and, in the 1950s and 1960s, nuclear-weapons-related research and protection. After Sweden signed the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty in 1968, the remaining nuclear development work was dismantled and protection research continued.

The agency's Stockholm activities were consolidated at Kista in 2005. FOI's staff peaked at about 1,300 around the time of its creation and was reduced to roughly 1,000 by 2007.

## Research areas

FOI's research is organised into four research sections plus administration:

- **C4ISR** — command, control, communications, computers, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance: radar, optronic sensors, communication networks, artificial intelligence and information security.
- **CBRN Defence and Security** — chemical, biological, radioactive and nuclear materials, incidents and protection.
- **Defence and Security, Systems and Technology** — aeronautics, sonar, jet propulsion, missile technology, nuclear weapons, arms control, ballistic protection and related technology.
- **Defence Analysis** — crisis management, conflict, international security, strategy and regional or thematic studies.
- **Research Support and Administration** — management and support for the research divisions.

The agency also works on civil emergencies, natural disasters and security policy, which makes it a dual-use research body rather than a purely military laboratory.
