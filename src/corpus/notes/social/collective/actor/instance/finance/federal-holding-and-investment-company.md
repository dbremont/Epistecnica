# Federal Holding and Investment Company

> Belgium's federal investment and holding company: the state's direct capital allocator, taking positions in strategic companies and managing the federal shareholdings.

The **Federal Holding and Investment Company** (SFPIM, formerly SFPI-FPIM) is the Belgian federal state's investment and holding vehicle, created in 2006 from the merger of the Federal Participation Company and the Federal Investment Company. It manages roughly **€14 billion** of assets on behalf of the federal state across **189** portfolio companies, and reported a €211.2 million profit in 2025.

## What it is

The portfolio separates three allocation modes:

- **On its own behalf** — direct equity and debt positions in Belgian companies: Ageas (6.44%), Air Belgium (12.15%), Addax Motors (26.51%), Accessia Pharma (25.00%), and others across life sciences, finance, aviation, energy and utilities, transport and mobility, and impact investing.
- **On behalf of the federal government** — the state's legacy shareholdings, held and managed for the federal authority.
- **Delegated assignments** — missions assigned by the government.

It invests directly: it takes positions itself, in equity and debt, rather than acting as a fund of funds that steers private capital into a sector mandate — the contrast with a [Government Guidance Fund](/view.html?p=notes/social/collective/actor/instance/finance/government-guidance-fund.md). Its subsidiaries carry the specialist vehicles: SFPIM International (founded 1971), SFPIM Real Estate, SFPIM Relaunch and SFPIM Defence.

The company is the Belgian instance of a [State Holding and Investment Company](/view.html?p=notes/social/actor/finance.md) — the finance-actor kind that manages public shareholdings and invests directly, alongside the historical industrial holdings (INI, IRI) and the development corporations.
