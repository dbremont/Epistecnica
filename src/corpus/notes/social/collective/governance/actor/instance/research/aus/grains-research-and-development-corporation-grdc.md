# Grains Research and Development Corporation (GRDC)

> The statutory corporation that invests grower levies and government funds in grains research and development for Australian grain growers.

## Focus

- Variety improvement and agronomy
- Farming systems and soils
- Pests, weeds and diseases
- Extension and grower adoption
