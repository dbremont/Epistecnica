# Computation Policy

> **Computation policy** is the public stake in the digital substrate: connectivity, compute, data, and the systems of government that run on them.

## Domain space

- **Connectivity** — broadband, mobile, and the networks that carry data.
- **Compute** — data centres, cloud capacity, and the energy and land they need.
- **Data governance** — rights, openness, privacy, and interoperability.
- **Digital public infrastructure** — identity, payments, registries, e-government.
- **Digital skills** — the capability to use and build on the substrate.
- **Cybersecurity** — the security of the systems the economy depends on.

## The growth question

Computation is a general-purpose technology, and the policy question is access: whether firms and citizens can reach affordable connectivity, compute and data, or whether the substrate is a bottleneck owned by one incumbent. Digital public infrastructure is the part the state can build directly, and the part with the widest spillovers.
