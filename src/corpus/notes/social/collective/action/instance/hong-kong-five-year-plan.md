# Hong Kong Five-Year Plan

> The **Hong Kong Five-Year Plan** is the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region's own five-year development plan, published for 2026–2030 as a full text with highlights, infographics, fact sheets and a webcast. It arranges the region's development under seven parts, from the strengthening of the Four Centres to the institutional safeguards for implementation.

> What makes it worth a note is not the document but the commitment: a polity whose governing doctrine for half a century was **positive non-interventionism** has adopted a five-year development horizon, aligned with the national plan of the People's Republic. Whether that becomes an instrument or stays a document is the question the note leaves open.

> One round is a plan, not yet an instrument. The [China Five-Year Plan](/view.html?p=notes/social/collective/action/instance/china-five-year-plan.md) is seventy years of composed rounds; this is the first, and the note treats it as such.

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**The Hong Kong Five-Year Plan belongs to the `Plan` social element type** — an arrangement composing ends, means and sequence over a fixed horizon, in the Action Guidance branch of the social ontology: a plan orients the actions of the units that adopt it, and the guidance is its mode of existence.

Readable secondarily as a `Strategic Planning Instrument` **if and only if** the round is renewed — the instrument is the machinery that composes rounds, and one round has not yet demonstrated the renewal. That reading is kept as prose, not as a second tree.

The document is also readable as a `Policy` where its parts authorise courses of action. What it is, is a plan; what it does, is policy.

### What is this social instance?

> A seven-part arrangement for 2026–2030: the Four Centres and the high-calibre talent hub as the competitive core, the Northern Metropolis as the spatial instrument, the Greater Bay Area as the collaborative frame, social protection as the welfare floor, development and security as the governance posture, and implementation safeguards as the part that decides whether any of it happens.

It is published as a package — full text, highlights, infographics, fact sheets, webcast, press and speeches — and the package is itself part of the arrangement: a plan of this kind has to be read by the ministries, the legislature, the business associations and the public that will be asked to deliver it.

## The institutional turn

Hong Kong's post-war economic doctrine was **positive non-interventionism**: the administration set the conditions and left allocation to the market, and the doctrine was defended as the reason the city worked. Industrial policy existed at the edges — the productivity council, the trade development council, the science park — but a five-year development plan is a different object: it composes ends and sequence, and it commits an administration to be judged against them.

Three things make the adoption legible. The national plan assigns the region a position — the Four Centres and the talent hub — and a region with an assigned position is expected to say how it will fill it. The Northern Metropolis is a land and housing programme of a scale that no annual budget cycle can carry. And the Greater Bay Area makes integration a standing commitment rather than a case-by-case negotiation. Each pulls in the same direction: a horizon longer than the fiscal year.

## What to watch

The plan's own seventh part is the test. A development plan is a document until an administration is accountable for its targets, and the note therefore watches three things rather than the seven parts:

- **Whether the targets are quantified** — and where, since a plan whose numbers live only in the highlights is a communication product.
- **Whether the machinery is named** — an office, a reporting line, a review point; Part 7 either names them or the plan remains a declaration.
- **Whether the next round happens** — an instrument is proven by renewal, and the second edition is the evidence that the first was more than a statement of intent.

The figures themselves are not reproduced here. They are in the documents, and a note that paraphrased them would be inventing numbers it cannot check.
