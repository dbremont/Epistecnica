# Telecom Product Space

> The goods whose function is to carry a signal between places: the module that puts a signal onto a medium, the medium itself, and the equipment that terminates, repeats or switches it. The space is filed under the Good, beside the semiconductor space that builds these modules and the software space that runs the networks they serve.

| --- | --- | --- |
| [Transceiver](/view.html?p=notes/social/good/telecom/transceiver.md) | One unit that transmits and receives over a shared antenna, line or port | duplex, radio, module |
| [Optical Transceiver](/view.html?p=notes/social/good/telecom/optical-transceiver.md) | A pluggable module that converts an electrical signal to light and back for a fibre link | optics, module, datacenter |
