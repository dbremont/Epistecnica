Good-Producing Goods

The goods whose product is another good — the capital and intermediate side of the shelf, filed by the kind of good each one delivers and by its implement role. Each card is a type: the Good-Producing Technical Element branch of the Philosophia Artium Technicarum et Operis, with the virtual producers added from the technica corpus. The goods themselves are classified in Good and Product.

The test. An element is a producer when the transformation is the delivered Good. A conveyor that relocates goods it delivers is a producer (material/service good); the same belt moving another producer's output to the next station is a support implement, not a producer. Kinds: material, energy, information, service, virtual — the virtual good is the design, model or document a Production Virtual Technical Object carries. Roles: machine, equipment, tool, facility, instrument, system.
