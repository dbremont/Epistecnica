# Agricultural Product Space

> The goods that come off cultivated land: the harvested crop, the animal output, and the processed foodstuff that leaves the farm as the product it is sold as. The space is filed under the Good, beside the production scope that grows and harvests them — a good is what is exchanged, the industry is what makes it.
