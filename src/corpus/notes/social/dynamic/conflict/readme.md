# Conflict

> This is the entry point to the conflict shelf: the doings through which actors — persons, groups, organizations, polities — pursue incompatible goals by coercive, adversarial or disruptive means.

> The shelf holds the general forms of organized conflict, not the particular wars and disputes that instance them. Three forms are already worked here: [Violence](/view.html?p=notes/social/dynamic/conflict/violence.md) (the direct infliction of harm), [State Adversarial Interaction Phenomena](/view.html?p=notes/social/dynamic/conflict/state-adversarial-phenomena.md) (emergent rivalry between polities across domains and scales), and [Society Polarization](/view.html?p=notes/social/dynamic/conflict/polarization.md) (the divergence of a society's positions into opposed camps). War, rebellion, civil war and revolution are named as members of the shelf and still wait for their notes.
