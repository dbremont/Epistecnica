# Instrument

> **Instrument** is the means an agent deploys to carry out an action — the
> artifact, device, procedure or vehicle that extends or mediates its capacity
> to act, distinct from guidance (what it should do) and organization (how it
> is arranged).

## Formulation

### What social element type does this social instance belong to?

**Instrument belongs to the `Instrument` social element type (Action category,
`Action Instrument` branch, Technic layer)** — a recognition-constituted means
that mediates action. It is distinct from `Rule` / `Policy` (the course it
serves), from `Program` / `Project` (the organization that deploys it), and from
`Social Action` itself (the act it carries out).

### What is this social instance?

> The mediated means: an agent acts through something — a tool, a form, a fund,
> a register, a machine. Instruments compose into toolkits and regimes; a
> regime's anatomy is purpose, means, operator, and material.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this social instance?

> Boundary: one instrument family, decomposed at middle depth — kinds and anatomy only.
>
> Stopping rule: a row is terminal when it names an instrument kind or an anatomy element.
