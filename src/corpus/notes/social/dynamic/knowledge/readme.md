# Knowledge

> This is the entry point to the knowledge shelf: the social systems through which knowledge is produced, held, diffused and used.

> Knowledge is read here as a social system, not as an epistemic state. The shelf carries the inventory of the system — `Knowledge = {Technology, Know-how, Know-what}` — and the production side: the arrangements through which inquiry becomes warranted, transferable knowledge.

## Members

- [Knowledge System](/view.html?p=notes/social/dynamic/knowledge/knowledge-system.md) — the inventory: the disciplinary areas a knowledge system draws on, and the `Technology / Know-how / Know-what` composition.
- [Research System](/view.html?p=notes/social/dynamic/knowledge/research-system.md) — the Knowledge Production System: recursive interaction units and the layered mechanisms of validation, reproduction and symbolic control over truth claims.
