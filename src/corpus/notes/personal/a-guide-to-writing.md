# A Guide To Writing


> **Writing**: Writing is a medium of human communication that involves representing a language through a system of physically inscribed, mechanically transferred, or digitally represented symbols.
> 

> **Writing**: Writing is the process of **serializing** one's thoughts into a **symbolic or visual form**.
> 

## The Power of Text: Why It’s the Ultimate Communication Medium

"Why choose text to consume information? 

Text is precise, compact, indexable, transmissible, translatable, asynchronous, and quick to absorb, making it the preferred choice for busy individuals.

Information absorption through text is spatial. Texts can be arranged in three-dimensional space, allowing for scanning and skimming to selectively absorb information.

In contrast, audio is temporally linear. You cannot skim through live audio, nor can you easily navigate back and forth."

As example; Amazon has a writing / text culture to communicate internally.
