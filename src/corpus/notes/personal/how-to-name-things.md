> **Project Naming Method for Recurring Initiatives**
> 

> For projects that have a **temporal, repetitive nature** (e.g., ongoing improvements, cyclical updates, or annual initiatives), it’s important to establish a clear and consistent naming convention. This ensures easy identification, organization, and tracking over time.
> 

### **Proposed Naming Convention**

Use the following format:

**`[YEAR]-[V]`**

- **`[YEAR]`**: The year the project iteration begins or is relevant.
- **`[V]`**: A version or iteration indicator (e.g., V1, V2, V3).

### **Examples**

1. **Annual Improvement Projects**:
    - `2023-V1`: First iteration of improvements in 2023.
    - `2024-V1`: First iteration of improvements in 2024.
2. **Quarterly Updates**:
    - `2023-V1`: Q1 improvements.
    - `2023-V2`: Q2 improvements.
3. **Multi-Year Initiatives**:
    - `2023-V1`: Initial phase in 2023.
    - `2024-V2`: Second phase in 2024.

### **Benefits of This Method**

1. **Clarity**: Clearly indicates the timeline and iteration of the project.
2. **Consistency**: Provides a standardized format for all recurring projects.
3. **Scalability**: Easily accommodates future iterations or updates.
4. **Tracking**: Simplifies tracking progress over multiple years or versions.

### **Implementation Steps**

1. Define the scope of the project and its recurring nature.
2. Assign the `[YEAR]-[V]` format at the start of each iteration.
3. Document the naming convention in project guidelines for team reference.
4. Use the name consistently in communications, documentation, and tracking tools.
