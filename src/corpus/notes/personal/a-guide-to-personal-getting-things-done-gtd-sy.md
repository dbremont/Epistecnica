## **A Guide To Getting Things Done (GTD) System**

> **Getting Things Done (GTD)**: Developed by David Allen, this method emphasizes task management through five key stages: capture, clarify, organize, reflect, and engage, to achieve a "mind like water" state.
> 

> All of the work of **“Daniel de Victoriano y Bremont“**  is organized from ‘Bremontix’ the holding enterprise.
> 

> **Ideas**, tasks, projects etc should be collected in an append only list (https://github.com/breflexio/append-only-incomming-work);  then process and enter the life-cycle.
> 

> **Recurrent Tasks**:  Like daily reflection; weekly reflection; montly reflection, yearly reflection, ….
