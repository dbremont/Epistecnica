# How to profile an algorithm?


> A Guide to profile algorithms.
> 

> **To study an algorithm** is to unravel the fabric of a problem, dissecting its formal essence and reconstructing it into a symphony of states and transformations. It is a journey through the abstract and the concrete, where the problem's soul is captured in its formal specification, and its body is forged in the crucible of implementation.
> 

> But **mastery demands more than understanding**; it requires questioning. What is the cost of this elegance? What trade-offs lie hidden in the shadows of time and space? And how does this algorithm, this particular path to truth, compare to the myriad others that could have been taken? To study algorithms is to embrace this duality—the technical precision of analysis and the philosophical depth of inquiry. It is to see not just the code, but the cosmos it represents.
>

Ask yourself:

## Problem Spec

- Which are the related problems.
- What is the formal problem specification?
- What fundamental principles or algorithms relate to this?

## Solution

- How is the solution represented?
- What is the algorithms state? What is the semantics of the state?
- How does the algorithm evolve the state?
- What are the big design ideas behind this implementation?

## Verification

> …
> 
- Does the algorithm work correctly?

## Analysis

- Are there trade-offs?
- What is the time complexity?
- What is the space complexity?

## Alternatives

- Are there other algorithms that solve the same problem?
- Why choose this algorithm over others?
