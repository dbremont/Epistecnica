# Knowledge System

> Note:  How to facilitate distributed work & cognition and problem-solving.
> 

> A knowledge base is a centralized repository of information, resources, and documentation designed to store, organize, and provide easy access to relevant knowledge for users or systems.
> 

## Personal Knowledge System

| **Model** | **Description** | **Tools/Methods** | **Application** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal Knowledge Management (PKM)** | A comprehensive approach to collect, categorize, store, search, retrieve, and share knowledge. | Digital note-taking apps, databases, cloud storage | Systematic knowledge collection and retrieval. |
| **Zettelkasten Method** | A network of interconnected notes, each containing a single piece of information, linked to related notes. | Index cards, digital tools (e.g., Obsidian, Roam) | Mimicking associative thinking, creating a web of knowledge. |
| **Mind Mapping** | A visual form of organizing information around a central concept, with branches representing hierarchical information and associations. | Mind mapping software (e.g., MindMeister), paper | Brainstorming, visually organizing ideas. |
| **Commonplace Books** | An analog or digital collection of ideas, quotes, observations, and information categorized by themes or subjects. | Notebooks, digital documents | Collecting and referencing diverse information sources. |
| **Digital Note-taking with Tagging/Search** | Creating, tagging, and searching notes using digital tools, often organized into notebooks or databases. | Evernote, OneNote, Notion | Efficiently categorizing and linking related information. |
| **Wiki Systems** | Personal wikis or wiki-like databases for creating interconnected pages, facilitating easy linking of related concepts. | Notion, TiddlyWiki, personal wiki software | Building a web of personal knowledge. |
| **Learning Management Systems (LMS)** | Adapting LMS platforms for personal organization of courses, resources, and learning paths. | Moodle, Canvas, Google Classroom | Structured learning and resource management. |
| **Bullet Journaling** | A flexible analog system combining a diary, to-do list, and note-taking with an emphasis on indexing for retrieval. | Notebooks, pens, bullet journal templates | Free-form knowledge capture and task management. |
| **File Hierarchy Systems** | Organizing digital files in a structured directory system, using folders and subfolders. | Computer directories, cloud storage systems | Categorizing information by topic, project, or type. |
| **Concept Maps** | Visual organization of knowledge showing connections and hierarchies between complex ideas. | Concept mapping tools, paper | Structuring and displaying relationships between concepts. |
