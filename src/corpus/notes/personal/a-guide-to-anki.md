# A Guide to Anki


> **Note:** **Anki** is a tool designed to improve the recall of specific information, but it's only a small part of the broader set of practices required for deep understanding.
> 

> Anki is a powerful tool for improving memory through spaced repetition and active recall. However, it's just one piece of a larger learning process. Deep understanding requires additional strategies—such as concept mapping, problem-solving, synthesis, and teaching the material to others.
>
