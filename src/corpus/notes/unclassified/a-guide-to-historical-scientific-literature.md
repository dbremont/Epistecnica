# Historical Scientific Literature Review (Pre-1900)

> A catalog of documents intended to reconstruct the **scientific knowledge, the methods of inquiry, the classifications, measurements and theories** available before 1900 D.C. — the *artes* of knowing nature, beside the *artes* of making it.

> **Logia** — from the Greek *logia*, "the studies of" — is the science-side counterpart of the **Ars** guide. Each section is titled `Logia <object in the genitive>`: *Logia Vitae* (the studies of life), *Logia Terrae* (of the earth), *Logia Caeli* (of the sky), and so on. Where the Ars guide catalogs what was *made*, this one catalogs what was *known* — and the method by which it came to be known.

## Logia Naturae

> Natural philosophy and natural history before the special sciences — the Greek inquiry into nature, the encyclopaedists of the classical and medieval world, the Renaissance natural historians and the great syntheses that carried the natural world as one subject.
