# Historical Technical Library: Technology and Engineering, 1600–1900

> The library is organized as a **multidisciplinary collection** rather than a mechanical-engineering one. Its objective is to reconstruct the **technical knowledge, engineering methods, productive processes, machinery and technological capabilities** available during the early modern and industrial periods.
