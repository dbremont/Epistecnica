# Physical Object

> A **physical object** is a substantive natural object: a fully specified material particular — a bounded piece of matter with persistence, extension, and a location — that can be counted, referred to, and decomposed into its building blocks. It is not a substance (the stuff it is made of), not a property (what it carries), and not a process (what it undergoes).

This note is the directory index and the classification of the physical-object family under `nature/physical-object/`: the notes whose instance is a *body* — discrete or continuous, single or multiple, single-phase or many-phase. Particles, atoms, fields and phases are physical objects too, but they stay at the `nature/` root because their identity is set by the particle and matter notes, not by the body criteria below.

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Natural Object` at meso level of organization (Ontic; Existential), per the Tabular view — read as *parsed reality*, never as inquiry.

A physical object is a `Substantive` specialized to `Natural Object`: "fully specified material particular". Its parts are `Building Block`s and its stuff is `Substance / Matter` — the guide licenses both on `Natural Object` (`→ Part`, `→ Has`). It is **not** a `Substance / Matter` (the stuff-kind, individuated by composition and phase, not by being a particular), **not** a `Natural System` (the system is the members plus their interactions; the body is a member), and **not** a `Property` or `State` (those are carried by it).

### What is this natural instance?

> The material particulars reality is made of, taken at the level at which they are counted and moved: a stone, a planet, a drop of water, a steel beam, a gas volume, a swarm of orbiting planets. The unifying question of the family is **what makes two samples or two bodies the same individual** — bounded persistence, spatial extent, composition — and how far that individuation can be pushed before the object dissolves into a continuum or a mere set of interacting others.

## Taxonomy of physical objects

The table states the family split and the criterion each type is individuated by; each note is the decomposition of its type.

| Physical Object Type | Identity criterion | Counting | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discrete body | Held-together parts; you can count the object off | One body | [Structure](/view.html?p=notes/nature/physical-object/structure.md) |
| Continuous body (medium) | A deformation/stress field over a volume; no privileged parts | An extent, not a count | [Continuous Medium](/view.html?p=notes/nature/physical-object/continuous-medium.md) |
| Fluid body | Cannot sustain static shear; takes the container's shape | An extent, not a count | [Fluid](/view.html?p=notes/nature/physical-object/fluid.md) |
| Multiple body | A set whose members interact; the set is the object | Many bodies, one set | [N-body system](/view.html?p=notes/nature/physical-object/n-body-system.md) |
| Composite body | One volume holding several substances or phases | One body, several components | — |

Types marked — are stated for the classification; they have no note of their own yet, and their rows in the decomposition above stop at the type.

**Why these four criteria.** They are the ways "the same object" can be settled: by **parts** (discrete), by **field** (continuous), by **set membership** (multiple) and by **composition** (composite). A description that does not know which criterion it is using will conflate a body with its stuff, a body with its parts, or one body with a system of bodies — the three collapses this taxonomy exists to prevent.

**Not in this directory.** *Particles* — [Particle](/view.html?p=notes/nature/physical-object/particle.md), [Classical Particle](/view.html?p=notes/nature/classical-particle.md), [Elementary Particle](/view.html?p=notes/nature/elementary-particle.md), [Quantum Particle](/view.html?p=notes/nature/quantum-particle.md), [Composite Particle (Hadron)](/view.html?p=notes/nature/composite-particle-hadron.md) — are physical objects whose identity is set by the particle notes, not by a body criterion. *Atoms* — [Atom](/view.html?p=notes/nature/atom.md), [Atomic Nucleus](/view.html?p=notes/nature/atomic-nucleus.md) — are bound systems at the micro level. *Phases* — [Phase](/view.html?p=notes/nature/phase.md) — are states of a substance, carried by a body, never a body itself.

**Limitation checklist.** Every type here exhibits *Scale* (the discrete/continuous split is a modelling choice at a resolution, not a property of matter), *Many-Body coupling* (true of the multiple and composite types by construction) and *Computational Complexity* (all four criteria are decided by computation in practice). The continuous and multi-component types additionally exhibit *Emergence* — the constitutive laws cannot be read off the parts.
