# Aluminium Oxide

> **Aluminium oxide** (Al₂O₃), **alumina**, is the compound of aluminium and oxygen: a white, refractory, electrically insulating solid that is amphoteric — dissolving in acid and in base — and the raw material of every aluminium smelter. In nature it is corundum, the mineral of ruby and sapphire; in industry it is a powder whose grade is fixed by a calcination temperature.

> The formula is the least interesting thing about it. The same composition is a gemstone at one particle size and a catalyst support at another; α-alumina is dense, inert and hard, γ-alumina is porous, reactive and used to hold other catalysts. What distinguishes the grades is not chemistry but structure and surface.

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this aluminium oxide instance belong to?

**Aluminium oxide belongs to `Ontic Element (→ Kind) Substance` — specifically to the `Compound` kind: a substance of fixed elemental ratio, Al:O = 2:3, whose identity is that ratio and the bonding that realises it.**

The boundary against `Mineral` is the first. Corundum is the mineral; aluminium oxide is the compound, and the same compound occurs as several polymorphs of which corundum is only one. The boundary against the element is the second: aluminium is the metal the compound is reduced to, and the note keeps the two apart. The boundary against `Ore` is the third: [bauxite](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/geological/bauxite.md) is the rock, alumina is what the rock is refined into.

Readable secondarily as `readable as technique` — smelter-grade, calcined, tabular, fused and activated aluminas are industrial artefacts specified by purity, particle size and surface area. That reading is prose here and is not a second root.

### What is this natural instance?

> A compound of aluminium and oxygen, Al₂O₃, molar mass 101.96 g/mol: white when pure, coloured by trace chromium (ruby) or iron and titanium (sapphire) in its natural mineral form. It melts near 2072 °C, boils near 2977 °C, has a Mohs hardness of 9 in the corundum form, a density near 3.95 g/cm³, and a band gap wide enough to make it an insulator. Chemically it is amphoteric and, once calcined, extremely inert.
>
> Its polymorphs are the practical substance: **α-alumina** (corundum) is the thermodynamically stable, dense, hard form; **γ-alumina** is a defect spinel with a high surface area, used as a catalyst support and adsorbent; η, θ, δ, κ and χ are transition forms reached at intermediate calcination temperatures. Which one a powder is — and therefore what it is good for — is decided by the thermal history, not by the formula.

## Production

| Step | What happens | What it fixes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bayer digestion** | Bauxite is digested in hot sodium hydroxide; alumina dissolves as sodium aluminate and the residue becomes red mud | The available-alumina yield and the caustic balance |
| **Clarification** | The liquor is separated from the mud and filtered | Product iron and soda content |
| **Precipitation** | Aluminium hydroxide is crystallised from the cooled liquor, seeded with fine crystals | Particle size and crystal form |
| **Calcination** | The hydroxide is heated; temperature and residence time select the phase | α versus γ, surface area, density, hardness |
| **Hall–Héroult** | The smelter-grade alumina is dissolved in molten cryolite and reduced electrolytically to aluminium | The metal, not the oxide — the boundary of this note |

> The Bayer process is the reason alumina is cheap enough to be a commodity, and the reason its specification is a refinery problem rather than a chemical one. The product is not one substance but a family: the same plant makes smelter-grade and hydrate, and the calciner decides which alumina leaves.

## What is a critical conceptual evaluation of this natural element instance?

The concept under evaluation is **Aluminium Oxide**, defined above as *the compound Al₂O₃, with the polymorph and the industrial grade as the attributes that decide use*. The evaluation applies the criteria of [Philosophia Conceptuum et Operis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/csiglab/Naturgnosis/refs/heads/main/app/note/data/meta/philosophia-conceptuum-et-operis.md).

**Verdicts.** Conceptual Identification — **pass**: the compound is separated from the element it is reduced to, from the mineral it occurs as (corundum), from the rock it is refined from (bauxite) and from the metal it becomes. Definitional Clarity — **pass**: the formula, the polymorphs and the two surface properties that define a grade are stated operationally. Conceptual Determinacy — **pass with qualification**: "aluminium oxide" names one composition but several substances in use, so a grade specification must fix the phase and the surface area; the note records that the name alone underdetermines the object. Internal Consistency — **pass**: polymorphs, attributes, the mineral varieties, the industrial grades and the bound are distinct branches. Representational Adequacy — **qualified**: complete for the compound and its grades; the refinery, the smelter and the markets are linked. Purpose Adequacy — **pass**: shaped for material selection — every leaf is a structure, a property, a grade or a use.
