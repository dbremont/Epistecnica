References

Data compiled from standard chemistry references. Atomic masses follow IUPAC standard values; for radioactive elements the mass number of the most stable isotope is shown. Atom diagrams are schematic Bohr-model illustrations, not photographs. The chemical-origin notes summarize each element's dominant nucleosynthesis channel: Big Bang, cosmic-ray spallation, stellar fusion, supernovae, slow or rapid neutron capture, decay chains, or man-made production.
