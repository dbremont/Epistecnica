# Rydberg Matter

> **Rydberg matter** is an exotic state of matter composed of atoms in **highly excited electronic states** — Rydberg atoms, whose outer electron orbits at a very large radius — interacting strongly enough through those extended orbits to exhibit **collective quantum effects** rather than behaving as a gas of independent atoms.

> A Rydberg atom is an atom whose valence electron sits in a state of very high principal quantum number: its orbital radius and polarizability grow enormously, making it the nearest thing in nature to a classical orbit. Pack a gas of such giant, weakly bound atoms and they begin to act on one another through their extended orbits — not a gas any more, but a collective phase. The state is the laboratory's alkali-metal gas, at the edge of atomic and plasma physics; it matters to quantum optics as the strongest long-range dipole–dipole interaction a neutral atom can offer.
