# Photonic Matter

> **Photonic matter** is a quasi-phase of matter in which **light itself behaves like a fluid** — photons, normally non-interacting, acquire effective mutual interactions in nonlinear media and organize as a collective photonic state.

> Photons carry no charge and normally pass through one another; the state is reached by engineering the medium — in optical cavities and nonlinear media — so that photons acquire an effective mass and interact through photon–photon effective potentials. In that regime a photon gas reaches thermal equilibrium and behaves like a fluid: the photonic matter of laboratory optical systems. It is the quasi-phase of the state table — the case whose particles carry no rest mass at all.
