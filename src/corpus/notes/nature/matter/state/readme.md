# State — the States of Matter

> Notes for the **states of matter** — how matter arranges itself and how the arrangement gives the phase its behavior: solid, liquid, gas, plasma, and the quantum and exotic states from the condensates to the time crystal. Each note lives in this directory; the states are deliberately not rows of the matter-type table — they own [Matter](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/matter.md)'s state table instead.

> The shelf also carries the **concept and its sub-notes**: [State of Matter](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/state-of-matter.md) (the general instance the table's rows are cases of), [Phase](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/phase.md) (the thermodynamic reading of a state), and the state-specific notes — [Type of Gas](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/type-of-gas.md), [Viscous Liquid](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/viscous-liquid.md), [Non-conductive Liquid](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/non-conductive-liquid.md), [Crystalline Solids](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/crystalline-solids.md) and [Liquid Crystal](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/liquid-crystal-lc.md). Anything about how matter is arranged is filed here, not at the `nature/` root.
