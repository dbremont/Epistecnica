# State — the States of Matter

> Notes for the **states of matter** — how matter arranges itself and how the arrangement gives the phase its behavior: solid, liquid, gas, plasma, and the quantum and exotic states from the condensates to the time crystal. Each note lives in this directory; the states are deliberately not rows of the matter-type table — they own [Matter](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/matter.md)'s state table instead.

| State | Substratum | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solid | atomic or molecular lattices with strong cohesive forces | [Solid](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/solid.md) |
| Liquid | molecules bound by intermolecular forces | [Liquid](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/liquid.md) |
| Gas | individual atoms or molecules with weak interactions | [Gas](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/gas.md) |
| Plasma | charged particles and electromagnetic fields | [Plasma](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/plasma.md) |
| Bose–Einstein Condensate | bosonic atoms under quantum degeneracy | [Bose–Einstein Condensate](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/bose-einstein-condensate.md) |
| Fermionic Condensate | paired fermions under quantum degeneracy | [Fermionic Condensate](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/fermionic-condensate.md) |
| Superfluid | quantum-coherent atomic ensemble | [Superfluid](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/superfluid.md) |
| Superconductor | electron pairs forming a quantum condensate | [Superconductor](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/superconductor.md) |
| Quark–Gluon Plasma | fundamental quarks and gluons | [Quark–Gluon Plasma](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/quark-gluon-plasma.md) |
| Degenerate Matter | fermions compressed to near-nuclear densities | [Degenerate Matter](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/degenerate-matter.md) |

| Photonic Matter | photons interacting through effective potentials | [Photonic Matter](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/photonic-matter.md) |
| Time Crystal | coherently driven quantum states breaking temporal symmetry | [Time Crystal](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/state/time-crystal.md) |
