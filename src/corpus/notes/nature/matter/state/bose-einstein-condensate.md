# Bose–Einstein Condensate

> A **Bose–Einstein condensate (BEC)** is a state of matter in which bosonic atoms are cooled so near absolute zero that a large fraction of them occupy the **same quantum state** — the wavefunctions merge, and the collection behaves coherently, as one entity rather than as independent particles.

> The condensate is the state where the **quantum wave nature of matter stops being a correction and becomes the whole description**: with thermal motion drained away, nothing distinguishes one atom's quantum state from its neighbour's, so the ensemble must be described by a single collective wavefunction. Predicted by Bose and Einstein in the 1920s from the statistics of photons, first produced in the laboratory in 1995 with ultracold rubidium gas, and since studied in more than a dozen atomic species.

Read per [Matter](/view.html?p=notes/nature/matter/matter.md), whose state table carries this row: a BEC is not a new *type* of matter but a new *arrangement* of it — bosonic atoms under quantum degeneracy, the substratum the table names — emergent from collective behavior, organization and energy distribution.
