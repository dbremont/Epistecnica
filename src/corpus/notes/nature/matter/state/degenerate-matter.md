# Degenerate Matter

> **Degenerate matter** is matter whose state is dominated by **quantum degeneracy pressure** rather than by thermal motion — the regime fermions enter when compressed to near-nuclear densities: it holds itself up not by heat but by the exclusion principle that forbids fermions from sharing quantum states.

> The state's distinction is mechanical: squeeze fermionic matter — electrons, neutrons — and the pressure that stops the collapse is **the requirement that no two fermions occupy the same state**. It thus does not cool away: a white dwarf is held up by electron degeneracy pressure, and a neutron star by neutron degeneracy pressure, at densities far past chemical binding. Degenerate matter is the *compressible-but-not-collapseable* state — the state of the compact astronomical objects.
