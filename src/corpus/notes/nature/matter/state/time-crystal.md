# Time Crystal

> A **time crystal** is a state of matter that exhibits **periodic motion in time while maintaining spatial order** — a structure whose pattern repeats not in space alone, as an ordinary crystal does, but in **time**: a phase that breaks **temporal symmetry**, returning periodically to an earlier state as part of its own dynamics.

> Ordinary crystals break translational symmetry in space: their order is their lattice, repeated in the spatial directions. A time crystal is the temporal analogue, a many-body state in which some observable returns periodically **without being pushed around each cycle** — the system returns to its earlier state as part of its own equilibrium dynamics, breaking time-translation symmetry the way a lattice breaks space-translation symmetry. Realized in driven quantum systems with separated degrees of freedom: the driven chain of spins or ions whose response alternates at a fraction of the drive frequency, the state that repeats *out of* step with the push that drives it.
