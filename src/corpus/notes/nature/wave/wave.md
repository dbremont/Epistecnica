# Wave

> **Waves**: the study of the properties of locks, such as their frequency, wavelength, and amplitude
>

> **Note: In Spanish Onda.**
>

## Properties

| Term | Definition | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frequency (f)** | Number of complete **oscillations** or **cycles** that occur per unit of time | Hertz (Hz) = cycles per second (1/s) |
| **Period (T)** | Time it takes for one complete cycle or oscillation to occur | Seconds (s) |
| **Wavelength (λ)** | Distance between two consecutive points in phase on the wave (e.g., peak to peak) | Meters (m) |
| **Amplitude (A)** | Maximum displacement from the equilibrium or rest position | Depends on context (e.g., meters for displacement, volts for voltage) |
| **Wave Speed (v)** | How fast the wave propagates through the medium | meters per second (m/s) |
| **Phase** | The position within a cycle of the wave, often measured in degrees (0°–360°) or radians. Determines where in the oscillation the wave is at a given point in time. |  |
| **Smoothness & Regularity** | Differentiability and integrability properties of wave functions, studied in Sobolev spaces. |  |

## QA

### Which are some instances of waves found in nature?

> **Note:** This list is neither exhaustive nor representative; it is simply a collection of examples of wave phenomena found in nature.

| Wave Type | Wave | Description | Physical Substrate | Propagation Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanical | Sound Wave | Pressure disturbance propagating through a medium. | Air, water, or solid matter | Particle interactions and elastic pressure variations. |
| Mechanical | Surface Water Wave | Disturbance propagating along a liquid surface. | Water surface | Gravity and surface tension. |
| Mechanical | Capillary Wave | Small-scale ripple propagating across a liquid surface. | Liquid surface | Surface tension. |
| Mechanical | Gravity–Capillary Wave | Surface wave influenced by gravity and surface tension. | Liquid surface | Gravity and surface tension. |
| Mechanical | Solitary Water Wave | Localized wave that approximately maintains its shape while propagating. | Water | Balance between nonlinearity and dispersion. |
| Mechanical | Tsunami Wave | Long-wavelength ocean wave generated by sudden water displacement. | Seawater | Gravity and fluid inertia. |
| Mechanical | Infragravity Wave | Long-period ocean surface wave. | Seawater | Gravity and nonlinear interactions between surface waves. |
| Mechanical | Internal Ocean Wave | Wave propagating within stratified ocean water. | Stratified seawater | Buoyancy and gravity. |
| Mechanical | Internal Tide | Internal wave generated by tidal currents interacting with underwater topography. | Stratified seawater | Buoyancy and tidal forcing. |
| Mechanical | Edge Wave | Surface wave trapped near a coastline. | Coastal water | Gravity and coastal boundary conditions. |
| Mechanical | Hydraulic Wave | Disturbance propagating through flowing water. | Flowing liquid | Gravity, pressure gradients, and fluid inertia. |
| Mechanical | Flood Wave | Propagating rise in water level along a river. | River water | Gravity and fluid dynamics. |
| Mechanical | Seiche Wave | Standing oscillation within an enclosed or partially enclosed body of water. | Lake, reservoir, or enclosed sea | Gravity and basin geometry. |
| Mechanical | Seismic P-Wave | Longitudinal seismic wave propagating through Earth's interior. | Geological materials | Compression and expansion. |
| Mechanical | Seismic S-Wave | Transverse seismic wave propagating through Earth's interior. | Solid geological materials | Shear deformation. |
| Mechanical | Rayleigh Wave | Surface seismic wave involving elliptical particle motion. | Earth's crust | Coupled vertical and horizontal elastic deformation. |
| Mechanical | Love Wave | Surface seismic wave involving horizontal transverse motion. | Earth's layered crust | Shear deformation and waveguide effects. |
| Mechanical | Stoneley Wave | Interfacial elastic wave propagating along a boundary between solids. | Solid–solid interface | Coupled elastic deformation. |
| Mechanical | Scholte Wave | Interfacial wave propagating along a solid–fluid boundary. | Solid–fluid interface | Coupled acoustic and elastic motion. |
| Mechanical | Flexural Wave | Wave involving bending deformation of a structure. | Elastic plates, beams, or shells | Elastic bending stiffness and inertia. |
| Mechanical | Torsional Wave | Wave involving propagating twisting deformation. | Elastic rods and solid structures | Shear elasticity and rotational inertia. |
| Mechanical | Lamb Wave | Guided elastic wave propagating through a thin plate. | Solid plate | Coupled longitudinal and transverse deformation. |
| Mechanical | Crack Wave | Localized wave propagating along a crack or fracture. | Fractured solid material | Elastic deformation and stress interactions. |
| Mechanical | Atmospheric Gravity Wave | Atmospheric oscillation caused by displaced air parcels. | Atmospheric gases | Gravity and buoyancy. |
| Mechanical | Mountain Wave | Atmospheric wave generated by airflow over mountainous terrain. | Atmospheric gases | Gravity and buoyancy. |
| Mechanical | Lee Wave | Atmospheric wave forming downstream of an obstacle. | Atmospheric gases | Gravity and buoyancy. |
| Mechanical | Acoustic-Gravity Wave | Atmospheric disturbance involving compressibility and gravitational restoring forces. | Atmospheric gases | Pressure and gravity. |
| Mechanical | Lamb Wave | Large-scale atmospheric wave with vertically structured horizontal propagation. | Atmospheric gases | Atmospheric compressibility and pressure dynamics. |
| Mechanical | Rossby Wave | Large-scale planetary wave associated with variations in planetary vorticity. | Ocean or atmosphere | Conservation of potential vorticity. |
| Mechanical | Kelvin Wave | Wave trapped along a boundary or equatorial region. | Ocean or atmosphere | Gravity and Coriolis force. |
| Mechanical | Poincaré Wave | Oscillation in a rotating fluid influenced by gravity and planetary rotation. | Ocean or atmosphere | Gravity and Coriolis force. |
| Mechanical | Inertial Wave | Wave associated with oscillations in a rotating fluid. | Rotating fluid | Coriolis force. |
| Mechanical | Baroclinic Wave | Wave associated with horizontal density gradients in a rotating fluid. | Stratified ocean or atmosphere | Buoyancy and rotational dynamics. |
| Mechanical | Mixed Rossby–Gravity Wave | Equatorial wave combining rotational and gravity-wave characteristics. | Atmosphere | Gravity and Coriolis force. |
| Electromagnetic | Radio Wave | Electromagnetic radiation associated with natural and artificial sources. | Electromagnetic field | Coupled electric and magnetic field oscillations. |
| Electromagnetic | Microwave | Electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths shorter than radio waves. | Electromagnetic field | Electromagnetic field propagation. |
| Electromagnetic | Infrared Wave | Electromagnetic radiation associated with thermal emission. | Electromagnetic field | Electromagnetic field propagation. |
| Electromagnetic | Visible Light Wave | Electromagnetic radiation detectable by the human eye. | Electromagnetic field | Electromagnetic field propagation. |
| Electromagnetic | Ultraviolet Wave | High-frequency electromagnetic radiation. | Electromagnetic field | Electromagnetic field propagation. |
| Electromagnetic | X-ray Wave | High-energy electromagnetic radiation. | Electromagnetic field | Electromagnetic field propagation. |
| Electromagnetic | Gamma-Ray Wave | Very-high-frequency electromagnetic radiation. | Electromagnetic field | Electromagnetic field propagation. |
| Gravitational | Gravitational Wave | Disturbance in spacetime geometry generated by accelerating massive objects. | Spacetime geometry | Propagation of metric disturbances. |
| Plasma | Langmuir Wave | High-frequency collective oscillation of electron density. | Plasma | Electrostatic interactions among electrons. |
| Plasma | Ion-Acoustic Wave | Low-frequency wave involving collective ion motion and electron pressure. | Plasma | Ion inertia and electron pressure. |
| Plasma | Alfvén Wave | Transverse disturbance propagating through magnetized plasma. | Magnetized plasma | Magnetic tension and plasma inertia. |
| Plasma | Torsional Alfvén Wave | Twisting disturbance propagating along magnetic field lines. | Magnetized plasma | Magnetic tension and plasma inertia. |
| Plasma | Fast Magnetosonic Wave | Compressional disturbance propagating through magnetized plasma. | Magnetized plasma | Magnetic pressure, plasma pressure, and inertia. |
| Plasma | Slow Magnetosonic Wave | Compressional wave propagating under magnetic and plasma pressure constraints. | Magnetized plasma | Magnetic tension and plasma pressure. |
| Plasma | Whistler Wave | Electromagnetic wave commonly observed in Earth's magnetosphere. | Magnetized plasma | Electron dynamics and magnetic fields. |
| Plasma | Ion-Cyclotron Wave | Oscillation associated with ion gyromotion. | Magnetized plasma | Ion gyromotion and electromagnetic forces. |
| Plasma | Electron-Cyclotron Wave | Wave associated with electron gyromotion. | Magnetized plasma | Electron gyromotion and electromagnetic forces. |
| Plasma | Kink Wave | Transverse displacement of a magnetic structure, such as a solar coronal loop. | Magnetized plasma | Magnetic tension. |
| Astrophysical | Solar Acoustic Wave | Acoustic oscillation propagating through the Sun. | Solar plasma | Pressure and compressibility. |
| Astrophysical | Solar Gravity Wave | Internal oscillation propagating through the Sun. | Solar plasma | Buoyancy. |
| Astrophysical | Density Wave | Propagating density enhancement in galactic structures. | Interstellar gas and stellar distribution | Gravitational interactions and collective dynamics. |
| Astrophysical | Accretion-Disk Density Wave | Density disturbance propagating through a rotating accretion disk. | Accretion-disk plasma and gas | Pressure, gravity, and rotational dynamics. |
| Astrophysical | Bow Shock Wave | Shock structure produced by an object moving through a surrounding medium. | Interstellar gas or plasma | Nonlinear compression and fluid dynamics. |
| Biological | Action Potential Wave | Propagating electrical excitation along a neuron. | Neuronal membrane and ionic medium | Voltage-gated ion channels and membrane dynamics. |
| Biological | Calcium Wave | Propagating intracellular increase in calcium concentration. | Cytoplasm | Calcium release, diffusion, and intracellular signaling. |
| Biological | Cardiac Excitation Wave | Electrical excitation propagating through cardiac tissue. | Cardiac tissue and extracellular ionic medium | Cellular coupling and ionic currents. |
| Biological | Cortical Spreading Wave | Slowly propagating wave of neuronal and glial depolarization. | Brain tissue | Cellular excitation and ionic redistribution. |
| Biological | Metachronal Wave | Coordinated propagating pattern of ciliary motion. | Ciliated tissue and surrounding fluid | Mechanical coupling and coordinated oscillation. |
| Chemical | Reaction-Diffusion Wave | Propagating chemical concentration pattern generated by chemical reactions and diffusion. | Reactive chemical medium | Reaction kinetics and molecular diffusion. |
| Chemical | Chemical Autowave | Self-sustaining propagating chemical excitation. | Reactive chemical medium | Nonlinear chemical kinetics and diffusion. |
| Quantum | Matter Wave | Wave-like behavior associated with quantum particles. | Quantum state or wavefunction | Quantum mechanical evolution. |
