# Pontic–Caspian Steppe

> The **Pontic–Caspian steppe** is the grassland belt running from the northern Black Sea (the Pontic part) eastward to the Caspian: southern Ukraine, Crimea, the North Caucasus and Kalmykia, and into western Kazakhstan. It is a **temperate grassland** — a biome instance, not a political region — and one of the great continuous corridors of the Eurasian steppe.

## As a natural system

| Dimension | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Climate** | Continental and semi-arid: cold winters, hot dry summers, precipitation below what forests require |
| **Soil** | **Chernozem** — the deep dark mollisol that makes the region one of the world's agricultural cores |
| **Vegetation** | Bunch grasses and feather grass; trees only along rivers, which is why the steppe is a *grassland* and not a savanna |
| **Fauna** | Grazing and burrowing animals — saiga antelope, marmots, ground squirrels — and the raptors and steppe eagles above them |
| **Land use now** | Largely ploughed: wheat and sunflower in the Pontic part, pasture in the drier Caspian part |

> Its dynamic is **disturbance-maintained**: grassland persists because grazing and fire suppress woody growth, so removing the grazers does not preserve the steppe — it converts it, slowly, into scrub. That is the ecological reading of the same fact the historians use: the steppe is a system organised around a herbivore layer, and when the herbivore layer is replaced by a plough, the system changes type rather than merely losing species.

## As a corridor

> The same belt is the **steppe road**: the corridor along which mounted pastoralism, trade and invasion moved between the Danube and the Altai for three millennia — Scythians, Sarmatians, Huns, Mongols. The corpus keeps the natural element and the historical use together because the second is only possible given the first: a continuous grassland with fodder on the hoof is a route, and a forest is not.

> Read against the [biome](/view.html?p=notes/nature/ecosystem/biome.md) and [ecology](/view.html?p=notes/nature/ecosystem/ecology.md) notes, it is the case where the **physical** classification (climate, soil, vegetation) and the **historical** one (a corridor) pick out the same object — which is the test of whether a natural unit is real or an artefact of the classifier.
