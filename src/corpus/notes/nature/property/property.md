# Property

> A **property** is a determinable feature instantiated by an element or state, intrinsically or relationally — the mass a body carries, the charge a particle holds, the temperature a volume is at. It is not the element that bears it and not the state the element is in: it is what the element can be said to *have*.

This note is the directory index of the property family under `nature/property/`: the notes whose instance is a *feature carried by a physical element* rather than the element itself. The shelf is newly opened and thin — [Inertia](/view.html?p=notes/nature/property/inertia.md) is its first member; the other physical quantities in the corpus ([Mass](/view.html?p=notes/nature/mass.md), [Force](/view.html?p=notes/nature/force.md), [Momentum](/view.html?p=notes/nature/momentum.md), [Energy](/view.html?p=notes/nature/energy.md), [Acceleration](/view.html?p=notes/nature/acceleration.md), [Pressure](/view.html?p=notes/nature/pressure.md), [Work](/view.html?p=notes/nature/work.md), [Power](/view.html?p=notes/nature/power.md), [Spin](/view.html?p=notes/nature/spin.md)) still sit at the `nature/` root and move here as each is formulated.

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Property` (Ontic; Qualitative), per the Tabular view — read as *parsed reality*, never as inquiry. It is one of the qualitative kinds of `Ontic Element`, alongside `Aspect` (a dimension under which an element is considered) and `State` (the configuration an element is in at a moment). A property is carried: it appears in a tree as the child of `<<Ontic Element>> (→ Attribute) Property`, and its bearer is the note that carries it.

Two specializations matter here. `Property (→ Kind) Interaction-Derived Quantity` covers quantities defined only over two or more elements or variables — momentum, force, energy — and `Property (→ Kind) Adaptation` covers the heritable fit of a lineage. It is **not** a `Constraint` (a bound imposed on behaviour) and **not** an `Aspect` (a way of considering an element, not a feature it has).

### What is this natural instance?

> The features a body carries, taken at the level at which they are quantified: resistance to a change of motion, inertia's measure, the capacity to do work, the push a field exerts. The family's question is **what makes a property the same property across bearers** — whether a quantity is defined by the bearer alone (an intrinsic property) or only by a joint state of several elements (an interaction-derived quantity) — and where the line between a property and a constraint falls when the property is read as a law.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this natural instance?

> Boundary: the property family only, one level per type, each leaf naming the note that decomposes it further. Types introduced by specialization carry the links licensed on `Property`; no branch is closed here.
>
> Identity: instance tree paths begin at `Ontic Element` and step down through one labelled link per row. Grouping types are `` `code` ``, instances are Title Case, and no type terminates a branch.

## Taxonomy of properties

The table states the family split and the criterion each type is individuated by.

| Property Type | Criterion | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intrinsic property | Quantified over the bearer alone; no second element enters the definition | [Inertia](/view.html?p=notes/nature/property/inertia.md) |
| Interaction-derived quantity | Quantified only over a joint state of two or more elements or variables | [Momentum](/view.html?p=notes/nature/momentum.md), [Force](/view.html?p=notes/nature/force.md), [Energy](/view.html?p=notes/nature/energy.md) — at the root |
| Adaptation | Heritable fit between a lineage and its environment | [Adaptation](/view.html?p=notes/nature/adaptation.md) — living, not physical |

**Limitation checklist.** The family exhibits *Scale* (a quantity is intrinsic or interaction-derived only at a resolution — pressure is a property of a continuum, not of its molecules) and *Measurement Disturbance* (every quantity here is read by acting on the bearer: inertia by accelerating it, charge by moving it, temperature by contact).

## QA

### Why is inertia here while momentum stays at the root?

> Because the cut is the one the guide draws between a `Property` and an `Interaction-Derived Quantity`. Inertia is carried by a single body and quantified without reference to anything else; momentum is mass × velocity, force is a relation between bodies, energy is a bookkeeping over a system — each is a joint-state quantifier. The root notes stay until they are formulated; when they are, the ones that qualify move here under the interaction-derived kind.

### Is the law of inertia a property?

> No, and the word hides two elements. *Inertia* as resistance is a `Property` instantiated by a body. The **law** of inertia — a body persists in its state of motion unless acted upon — is a bound imposed by physical law, i.e. `Constraint (→ Kind) Natural Constraint`, and reading it as a property of individual bodies is a category slip. A note that wants the principle must say so and change its root type.
