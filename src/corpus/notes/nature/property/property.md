# Property

> A **property** is a determinable feature instantiated by an element or state, intrinsically or relationally — the mass a body carries, the charge a particle holds, the temperature a volume is at. It is not the element that bears it and not the state the element is in: it is what the element can be said to *have*.

This note is the directory index of the property family under `nature/property/`: the notes whose instance is a *feature carried by a physical element* rather than the element itself. The shelf holds the canonical quantities of the corpus, filed by the cut below — the ones a body carries by itself, and the ones defined only over a joint state of several elements or variables. Membership is a filing decision, not a claim that a quantity is simple: each member keeps its own decomposition, and the ones the corpus has not formulated yet stay stubs.

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

`Property` (Ontic; Qualitative), per the Tabular view — read as *parsed reality*, never as inquiry. It is one of the qualitative kinds of `Ontic Element`, alongside `Aspect` (a dimension under which an element is considered) and `State` (the configuration an element is in at a moment). A property is carried: it appears in a tree as the child of `<<Ontic Element>> (→ Attribute) Property`, and its bearer is the note that carries it.

Two specializations matter here. `Property (→ Kind) Interaction-Derived Quantity` covers quantities defined only over two or more elements or variables — momentum, force, energy — and `Property (→ Kind) Adaptation` covers the heritable fit of a lineage. It is **not** a `Constraint` (a bound imposed on behaviour) and **not** an `Aspect` (a way of considering an element, not a feature it has).

### What is this natural instance?

> The features a body carries, taken at the level at which they are quantified: resistance to a change of motion, inertia's measure, the capacity to do work, the push a field exerts. The family's question is **what makes a property the same property across bearers** — whether a quantity is defined by the bearer alone (an intrinsic property) or only by a joint state of several elements (an interaction-derived quantity) — and where the line between a property and a constraint falls when the property is read as a law.

**Limitation checklist.** The family exhibits *Scale* (a quantity is intrinsic or interaction-derived only at a resolution — pressure is a property of a continuum, not of its molecules) and *Measurement Disturbance* (every quantity here is read by acting on the bearer: inertia by accelerating it, charge by moving it, temperature by contact).

## QA

### Why does the shelf split into intrinsic and interaction-derived kinds?

> Because the cut is the one the guide draws between a `Property` and an `Interaction-Derived Quantity`. Inertia is carried by a single body and quantified without reference to anything else; momentum is mass × velocity, force is a relation between bodies, energy is a bookkeeping over a system — each is a joint-state quantifier, so each is filed under the interaction-derived kind rather than among the features a body carries alone. The split is not a rank: both kinds are `Property`, and the interaction-derived ones are the ones whose definition names a second element.

### Is the law of inertia a property?

> No, and the word hides two elements. *Inertia* as resistance is a `Property` instantiated by a body. The **law** of inertia — a body persists in its state of motion unless acted upon — is a bound imposed by physical law, i.e. `Constraint (→ Kind) Natural Constraint`, and reading it as a property of individual bodies is a category slip. A note that wants the principle must say so and change its root type.

### Is heat a property or a transfer?

> Open question, and the shelf's weakest filing. Heat is admitted here because the corpus types it as a quantity, but classical thermodynamics separates the *transfer* (heat, a process quantity) from the *state* a body is in (its internal energy) — and a quantity that names a crossing between two bodies is not obviously a feature either one carries. If the note is reformulated as a transfer, it leaves the property family for the interaction or process scope and internal energy takes its place among the members. Until then it stays filed under the interaction-derived kind, where at least the joint-state requirement is honest.
