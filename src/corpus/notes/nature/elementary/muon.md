`Substance / Matter` (Ontic; Existential) at **micro** level of organization, per the Tabular view — read as *parsed reality*, never as inquiry. ### What is this natural instance?

**Muon**: the second-generation charged lepton — an electron's charge with 207 times its mass, unstable with a lifetime long enough (2.2 µs) that cosmic-ray muons reach the ground before decaying. It is an elementary particle: nothing in the standard model composes it, and its decomposition stops where its kind is individuated (mass, charge, spin, lepton number).
