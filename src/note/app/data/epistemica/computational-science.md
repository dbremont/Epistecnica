# Computational Science

> **Computational Science**: Computational science is an interdisciplinary field that uses computational techniques, algorithms, and models to solve complex scientific and engineering problems, often involving simulations, data analysis, and numerical methods to study phenomena that are difficult or impossible to observe directly.
> 

> **Simulation** is the process of creating a model or imitation of a real-world system or process to study its behavior under various conditions without the need to experiment on the actual system.
> 

> **Numerical Analysis** is the **mathematical study** of algorithms for approximating solutions to mathematical problems, especially those arising in engineering, science, and computational mathematics.
