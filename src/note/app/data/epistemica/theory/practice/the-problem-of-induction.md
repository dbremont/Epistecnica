# The Problem of Induction

Related: [Bayesian Epistemology](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/bayesian-epistemology.md)

> Induction is the inference process by which, given a finite body of evidence E and a hypothesis space H, one selects the hypothesis h∈H that maximizes the posterior probability
> 

$P(h\mid E)=\frac{P(E\mid h)\,P(h)}{\sum_{h'\in H}P(E\mid h')\,P(h')},$ thereby generalizing from observed instances to unobserved cases.
