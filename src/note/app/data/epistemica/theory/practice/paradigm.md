### **🌌** Signature

> A comprehensive list of aspects to cover in order to understand a topic.
> 

| Aspect | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Nucleus (Deepest Essence) 🧬** | What is the deepest essence? |
| **Evidentia (Evidence) 📊** | What proofs, data, or logical arguments support it? How can its claims or performance be verified? |
| **Telos (Intent) 🎯** | What is it for? Toward what end is it directed? What tools does it offer, if any? What outcomes does it aim to achieve? |
| **Gnosis (Understanding) 🧠** | How to grow a **mental represented** <u>(compress, fluid,  hierarchical (depth, abstraction), meta-awareness, flexibility, minimal sensitivity, well-connected)</u> that capture this topic both its declarative aspects and procedural aspects?  How can we develop an **explicit representation & organization** of this topic that supports the growth of a strong **mental representation**? What **previous structure**  must be in place in order to make the growth of the new representation feasible? |
| **Epignosis (Application) 🛠️** | How is this knowledge applied in practice? What advanced skills or insights emerge from mastery? What real-world problems does it solve? |
| **Relatio (Connections) 🔗** | How does this topic relate to other subjects or fields? What are the interdisciplinary connections? |
| **Idoneitas (Suitability) 📐** | How effectively does it achieve its purpose? What metrics prove its value? |
| **Limites (Limits) ⛔** | What are its boundaries? What constraints define its operation? |
| **Contextus (Context) 🌍** | What is the historical, cultural, or situational context in which this study occurs? |
| **Dynamis (Evolution) 🌱** | How might it transform? What emerging influences could redefine it? |
| **Critica (Critique) 🔍** | What are the common criticisms or challenges associated with this topic? How have these been addressed or debated? |
| **Verbum Usus (Language Use) 🗣️** | How is the word/concept used in language and discourse? What are the typical semantic, pragmatic, and stylistic patterns surrounding its use? How do people’s usage contexts, connotations, and variations influence understanding? What shifts or trends in language use reflect its evolving meaning or social significance? |

## Taxonomy

| Category | Paradigm | Description | Era | Main Thinkers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metaphysics | Idealism | Reality is fundamentally mental or spiritual. | Ancient to Modern | Plato, Berkeley, Hegel |
| Metaphysics | Materialism | Reality is fundamentally material or physical. | Ancient to Modern | Democritus, Marx, Engels |
| Epistemology | Rationalism | Knowledge primarily through reason and innate ideas. | 17th-18th Century | Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz |
| Epistemology | Empiricism | Knowledge through sensory experience and observation. | 17th-18th Century | Locke, Hume, Berkeley |
| Ethics | Utilitarianism | Morality based on the greatest happiness or utility. | 18th-19th Century | Bentham, Mill |
| Ethics | Deontology | Morality based on duty, rules, and intentions rather than consequences. | 18th-19th Century | Kant |
| Logic | Formal Logic | Study of valid inference using symbolic systems. | 19th Century | Frege, Boole, Russell |
| Philosophy of Science | Positivism | Science based only on observable phenomena and empirical data. | 19th-20th Century | Comte, Mach, Ayer |
| Philosophy of Science | Falsificationism | Scientific theories should be falsifiable to be considered scientific. | 20th Century | Popper |
| Phenomenology | Phenomenology | Study of structures of consciousness and experience from the first-person perspective. | 20th Century | Husserl, Heidegger |
| Existentialism | Existentialism | Focus on individual freedom, choice, and subjective meaning in an absurd or indifferent world. | 19th-20th Century | Kierkegaard, Sartre, Camus |
| Structuralism | Structuralism | Human culture and cognition understood as systems of relationships and structures. | 20th Century | Saussure, Lévi-Strauss |
| Post-Structuralism | Post-Structuralism | Critique and deconstruction of structures, emphasizing instability of meaning. | Late 20th Century | Derrida, Foucault |
| Pragmatism | Pragmatism | Truth is what works practically; knowledge is judged by its effects. | Late 19th - 20th C | Peirce, James, Dewey |
| Analytic Philosophy | Analytic Philosophy | Focus on language analysis, logic, and clarity of argumentation. | 20th Century | Russell, Wittgenstein, Quine |
| Continental Philosophy | Critical Theory | Social critique focusing on power, ideology, and emancipation. | 20th Century | Adorno, Horkheimer, Habermas |
