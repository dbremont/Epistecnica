# Representation

Related: [Modelling](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/modelling.md)

> The form (representation) of the representation cannot be confuse the representation with the represented: a mathematical model can be dynamic or static. A simple equation capturing structural aspects can be considered less vivid, whereas a differential equation model is vivid.
> 

**Guiding Questions:**

- What does **representation** denote in the context of *Ars Reali Describendi*?
- What are the types of representation? For example:
    - **Vivid representations** (models, simulations, or concrete visualizations)
    - **Interpolative / less vivid representations** (state patterns, historical dynamic patterns, abstract summaries)
