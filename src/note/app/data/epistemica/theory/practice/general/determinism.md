# Determinism

> The belief that all events are determined completely by previously existing causes.
> 

> Pierre-Simon Laplace, Baruch Spinoza
> 

> Determinism is the philosophical doctrine that all events, including moral choices, are determined completely by previously existing causes and natural laws.
> 

> Systems whose future states are entirely determined by their initial conditions and the laws governing their dynamics. The system’s behavior can be precisely predicted given complete knowledge of the initial conditions.
>
