# Objectivity

> **Objectivity** denotes a mode of existence, judgment, or  representation that is independent of individual perceptions, biases, or  interpretive frameworks. It presupposes a reality or truth that is  invariant across observers, grounded in mind-independent criteria, and  verifiable through intersubjective agreement or empirical validation.
