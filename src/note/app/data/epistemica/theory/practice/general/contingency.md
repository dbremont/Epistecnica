# Contingency

> **Determinism** and **contingency** both belong to the realm of metaphysics and philosophy, specifically within the study of the nature of reality, causality, and the principles governing the occurrence of events.
> 

> In philosophy, **contingency** refers to the concept that certain events or states of affairs are not necessarily determined or inevitable but could have been otherwise depending on various conditions or circumstances.
