# Epistemic System

Related: [Bayesian Epistemology](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/bayesian-epistemology.md)

> An **epistemic system** is a complex socio-technical framework designed to **produce, validate, and disseminate knowledge or reliable information** within a community or organization. It encompasses the collective processes, structures, and norms through which groups generate shared understanding and truths.
> 

> **An epistemic system is any organized set of agents, practices, technologies, and institutional mechanisms that collectively enable the acquisition, verification, and transmission of knowledge.**
> 

### Key Features:

- **Knowledge Production:** Facilitates generation of new insights or information (e.g., research, observation, forecasting).
- **Validation:** Includes processes to evaluate and verify knowledge claims (e.g., peer review, testing, consensus-building).
- **Distribution:** Knowledge is shared and propagated across participants or components.
- **Adaptation:** Incorporates feedback to refine or update knowledge over time.
- **Social and Technical Components:** Combines human actors (experts, communities) with tools and infrastructures (databases, communication platforms).

### Examples:

- Scientific research communities
- Academic publishing and peer review
- Prediction markets
- Collaborative knowledge bases like Wikipedia
