# Context-State Relation

Related: [Epistemic Artifact](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/epistemic-artifact.md)

> The *Context–State Relation* denotes the representation of a snapshot of a piece of reality as a **state**, where this state is explicitly indexed by a specific **context**.
> 

> The **context** refers to the underlying ontic and operational substrate that conditions the observation—i.e., the set of background assumptions, constraints, mechanisms, and environmental factors that stabilize both the interpretation and the distribution of the state.
> 

> **Note:** This structure is typically overlooked in high-theory formalizations of modeling; however, in practice, it is one of the most consequential elements, as it implicitly grounds  of the resulting model.
>
