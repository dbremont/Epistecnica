# Activity

Related: [Modelling](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/modelling.md)

> An epistemic activity is any operation performed by an observer to construct, transform, evaluate, or apply knowledge about reality.
> 

## Activity Type Space

> Which are the types of activities?
> 

| **Activity** | **Description** | **Tags** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Observation** | Acquisition of raw phenomenological input from the world via sensing or instrumentation. | sensing, acquisition, empirical, **grounding** |
| **Recording** | Fixation of observations into a persistent phenomenological record (e.g., logs, traces). | capture, storage, temporal, **grounding** |
| **Encoding** | Transformation of raw records into structured data representations. | structuring, formatting, abstraction, **representational** |
| **Operationalization** | Mapping abstract concepts or variables into measurable or observable proxies. | measurement, proxy, mapping, **operational** |
| **Measurement** | Assignment of values to variables according to a measurement model and instrumentation. | quantification, instrumentation, error, **operational** |
| **Specification** | Definition of variables, domains, constraints, and structural relations (schema construction). | parameterization, constraints, structure, **structural** |
| **Modeling** | Construction of representations that map selected aspects of reality into formal or semi-formal systems. | abstraction, representation, assumptions, **representational** |
| **Formalization** | Expression of models or relations in a precise symbolic or mathematical language. | symbolic, exactness, syntax, **representational** |
| **Simulation** | Execution of a model to generate system behavior under specified conditions. | execution, dynamics, generative, **operational** |
| **Inference** | Derivation of conclusions, estimates, or predictions from models and data. | estimation, prediction, reasoning, **inferential** |
| **Hypothesizing** | Formulation of testable propositions about relationships or mechanisms. | conjecture, testable, provisional, **inferential** |
| **Explanation** | Construction of accounts linking phenomena to causes, mechanisms, or principles. | causal, mechanistic, interpretive, **explanatory** |
| **Validation** | Evaluation of epistemic artifacts against empirical evidence or consistency criteria. | testing, falsification, empirical-fit, **evaluative** |
| **Verification** | Checking internal correctness, consistency, or conformance to formal specifications. | consistency, correctness, formal, **evaluative** |
| **Comparison** | Systematic evaluation of competing models, hypotheses, or explanations. | benchmarking, selection, contrast, **evaluative** |
| **Generalization** | Extension of results or models beyond specific instances to broader domains. | abstraction, scaling, transfer, **inferential** |
| **Synthesis** | Integration of multiple artifacts into a coherent structure (e.g., theory building). | integration, coherence, unification, **explanatory** |
| **Decomposition** | Breaking down systems or problems into constituent parts for analysis. | reduction, modularization, analysis, **structural** |
| **Classification** | Assignment of entities into categories or types within a taxonomy. | categorization, labeling, semantic, **structural** |
| **Abstraction** | Suppression of detail to focus on relevant structural or relational aspects. | reduction, relevance, simplification, **representational** |
| **Algorithmization** | Conversion of models or procedures into executable algorithms. | proceduralization, computation, **operational** |
| **Computation** | Execution of algorithms to transform inputs into outputs. | execution, processing, automation, **operational** |
| **Communication** | Externalization and transmission of epistemic artifacts (e.g., papers, diagrams). | transmission, representation, dissemination, **meta-epistemic** |
| **Critique** | Systematic analysis of assumptions, limitations, and failure modes of artifacts. | analysis, limitation, rigor, **evaluative** |
| **Revision** | Modification of artifacts in response to evidence, critique, or new constraints. | iteration, refinement, update, **meta-epistemic** |
