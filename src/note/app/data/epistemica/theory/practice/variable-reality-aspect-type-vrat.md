# Variable–Reality Aspect Type (VRAT)

Related: [Variable](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/variable.md), [State Descriptor](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/general/state-descriptor.md)

> In this note, we explore the notion of **Variable–Reality Aspect Type (VRAT)** as an intellectual mechanism for separating the aspect of reality under investigation from its representation.
> 

## Formulation

> What is Variable–Reality Aspect Type (VRAT)? A **Variable–Reality Aspect Type (VRAT)** is a *type-level abstraction* that denotes a **class of admissible variation modes** in a **piece of reality**, which can be consistently identified, stabilized, and formalized into variables.
> 

> Why the Concept of Variable–Reality Aspect Type (VRAT)?  It introduces a *platonic layer* that separates reality from its representation (variables), allowing us to define constraints on which variables can validly capture a given aspect of reality. In this way, a VRAT determines the set of admissible representations.
> 

## Which are the types of Variable–Reality Aspect Type (VRAT) (Properties)?
