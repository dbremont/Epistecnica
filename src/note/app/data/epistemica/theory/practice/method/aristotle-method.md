# Aristotle's Method

Related: [Philosophical System](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/method/philosophical-system.md), [Plato’s Method](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/method/plato-method.md)

> Aristotle’s philosophical system is a hylomorphic, teleological, empirically grounded framework in which reality consists of individual substances composed of matter and form, explained through a four-cause ontology, organized into a geocentric cosmos, and integrated with an epistemology that derives universal knowledge from sensory experience via abstraction and demonstrative reasoning.
> 

## Philosophical System

| **Dimension** | **Criterion** | **Description** | **Aristotle’s Position** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Metaphysics** | **Ontological Commitment** | What kinds of entities are said to exist fundamentally? | Substances (ousia) are primary; individual entities composed of form (morphē) and matter (hylē) are fundamental. Universals exist only in particulars. |
|  | **Substance Theory** | How are objects or substances conceived? | Hylomorphic theory: every substance is a composite of matter (potentiality) and form (actuality). |
|  | **Modality** | How does the system treat necessity and possibility? | Distinction between potentiality (dynamis) and actuality (energeia); necessity applies to essential attributes, possibility to contingent attributes. |
|  | **Causality** | What is the nature of causation? | Four causes: material, formal, efficient, and final causes. Causation is teleological as well as mechanistic. |
|  | **Time and Space** | How are time and space understood ontologically? | Time is the measure of change in respect of before and after; space (place) is the innermost motionless boundary of what contains an object. |
|  | **Mind–Body Relation** | How are mental and physical substances related? | The soul (psychē) is the form of a living body; not a separate substance except for the active intellect (nous poietikos), which may be immortal. |
|  | **Unity and Plurality** | Is reality one or many? | Reality is plural: many substances exist, each a unity of form and matter. |
|  | **World Structure** | How is the cosmos organized? | Geocentric, finite cosmos with concentric celestial spheres; the unmoved mover is the ultimate cause of motion. |
|  | **Change** | How does this metaphysics view change? | Change is the actualization of potentiality; explained through hylomorphism and the four causes. |
| **Epistemology** | **Source of Knowledge** | Where does knowledge come from? | From experience (sense perception) which provides the basis for abstraction of universals by the intellect. |
|  | **Theory of Truth** | What makes a belief true? | Correspondence theory: truth is saying of what is that it is, and of what is not that it is not. |
|  | **Justification** | How are beliefs justified? | Through demonstration (apodeixis) from first principles, which are grasped by nous (intuitive intellect). |
|  | **Innate Ideas** | Are some ideas present in the mind from birth? | No innate ideas; all knowledge begins with the senses. |
|  | **Limits of Knowledge** | What are the constraints on what can be known? | The finite capacity of human senses and intellect; divine things are knowable only indirectly. |
|  | **Role of Reason vs. Experience** | What is the balance between rational and empirical sources? | Empirical observation provides data; reason organizes and abstracts to form scientific knowledge. |
|  | **Degrees of Knowledge** | Is knowledge binary or graded? | Graded: from perception → memory → experience → craft (technē) → scientific knowledge (epistēmē) → wisdom (sophia). |
|  | **Knowledge Objects** | What can be known? | Substances, their attributes, and causes; both the natural and metaphysical realms. |

## Work

| **Area / Dimension** | **Work** | **Main Focus** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logic / Metaphysics | **Categories** | Classification of beings, predication, substance-accident distinction. |
| Logic / Epistemology | **On Interpretation** | Propositions, truth, modality. |
| Logic | **Prior Analytics** | Syllogistic reasoning, deductive structure. |
| Epistemology / Logic | **Posterior Analytics** | Demonstration, scientific knowledge, first principles. |
| Logic | **Topics** | Dialectical reasoning, disputation methods. |
| Logic | **Sophistical Refutations** | Fallacies and refutation techniques. |
| Metaphysics / Natural Philosophy | **Physics** | Nature, change, motion, time, causes. |
| Metaphysics / Cosmology | **On the Heavens** | Structure of the cosmos, celestial motions. |
| Natural Philosophy | **On Generation and Corruption** | Change in the sublunary realm, elements. |
| Natural Philosophy | **Meteorology** | Weather, geology, climate phenomena. |
| Biology | **History of Animals** | Empirical study of animal species. |
| Biology | **Parts of Animals** | Anatomy and functional explanation. |
| Biology / Natural Philosophy | **Movement of Animals** | Locomotion and its causes. |
| Biology | **Progression of Animals** | Anatomical adaptation to movement. |
| Biology | **Generation of Animals** | Reproduction, development, inheritance. |
| Metaphysics | **Metaphysics** | Being qua being, substance, causes, unmoved mover. |
| Ethics / Political Philosophy | **Nicomachean Ethics** | Virtue ethics, eudaimonia, moral reasoning. |
| Ethics | **Eudemian Ethics** | Alternate formulation of virtue ethics. |
| Ethics | **Magna Moralia** | Concise ethical treatise (possibly spurious). |
| Political Philosophy / Ethics | **Politics** | Structure of the polis, constitutions, citizenship. |
| Ethics / Household Management | **Economics** | Household economy, management (disputed authorship). |
| Rhetoric / Practical Philosophy | **Rhetoric** | Persuasion, rhetorical appeals, public speech. |
| Aesthetics | **Poetics** | Theory of tragedy, epic, dramatic art. |
| Psychology / Metaphysics | **On the Soul** | Soul as form, perception, intellect. |
| Psychology | **On Sense and the Sensible** | Sensory processes. |
| Psychology | **On Memory and Recollection** | Memory mechanisms, retrieval. |
| Psychology / Biology | **On Sleep and Waking** | Biological basis of sleep cycles. |
| Psychology | **On Dreams** | Nature and causes of dreams. |
| Psychology | **On Divination in Sleep** | Dreams as potential signs. |
| Biology | **On Length and Shortness of Life** | Factors determining lifespan. |
| Biology / Psychology | **On Youth, Old Age, Life and Death, and Respiration** | Physiological aging, life functions. |
