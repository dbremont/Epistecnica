# Interaction

> In ontological terms, an *interaction* refers to a relationship or a set of relationships between two or more entities (concepts, objects, or individuals) within an ontological framework. Specifically, it can be defined as a formalized, often bidirectional, association that is represented as a role or property in the context of ontology. These interactions typically define the dynamic relationships or dependencies that arise from the behaviors, states, or functions of the involved entities.
