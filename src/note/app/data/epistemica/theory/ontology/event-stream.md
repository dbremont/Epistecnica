# Event Stream

Related: [Ontology](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/practice/general/ontology.md), [Quasi-Open Interaction Field](note.html?n=epistemica/theory/ontology/quasi-open-interaction-field.md)

> A ‘event stream’ is a  highly abstract notion that captures the **manifestation (i.e., events)** of a portion of reality (a system, ecosystem, etc.).
> 

> Note: The term phenomenon captures the idea of  a pattern in a stream of events - so it at structure, we if  don’t have a pattern  - or don’t know then using the term phenomenon is not right.
> 

> **Note:** The indiscriminate use of the term *phenomenology* is often treated as equivalent to the idea of an *event stream*. However, this is conceptually imprecise. The linguistics of the term *phenomenology* denote the **study of phenomena**, not the phenomena themselves, nor a given stream of events.
