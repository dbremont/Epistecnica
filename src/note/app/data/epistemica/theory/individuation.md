# Individuation

> The “principium individuationis” is the principle or process that determines the formation of individual identities and distinct entities in the world.
> 

> Concretization: This concept refers to the process through which an abstract potential becomes actualized in a concrete form. It is closely related to individuation and how entities become fully realized.
>
