# Occam’s Razor

> **Simplicity and Occam’s Razor**: Models should strive for simplicity and avoid unnecessary complexity. The principle of Occam’s Razor suggests that the simplest one is usually preferred among competing models that explain the same observations.
> 

> Simpler explanations are more likely to be true than complicated ones. This is the essence of Occam’s Razor, a classic principle of logic and problem-solving. Instead of wasting your time trying to disprove complex scenarios, you can make decisions more confidently by basing them on the explanation that has the fewest moving parts. - Occam’s Razor
> 

> What is simple is always wrong. What is not is unusable. - Paul Valéry
