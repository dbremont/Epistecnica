# Finite Element Method (FEM)


> **Finite Element Method (FEM):** Solves partial differential equations by dividing a system into more minor elements, commonly applied to study the mechanical behavior of materials and structures.
> 

> Divide the problem domain into smaller subdomains (finite elements) and approximate the solution using piecewise functions.
> 

> Structural analysis, heat transfer, and fluid dynamics.
>
