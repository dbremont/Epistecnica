## Kernel Abstraction

| **Abstraction** | **Description** | **Use** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Electric Charge (q)** | Scalar quantity representing the source of electromagnetic forces | Fundamental property causing fields and forces |
| **Electric Field ($\mathbf{E}$)** | Vector field representing force per unit charge at each point in space | Describes how charges interact at a distance |
| **Magnetic Field ($\mathbf{B}$)** | Vector field representing magnetic influence on moving charges and magnetic moments | Describes magnetic effects and dynamics |
| **Lorentz Force ($\mathbf{F}$)** | Force on a charged particle due to electric and magnetic fields: $\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}$) | Governs particle dynamics in EM fields |
| **Maxwell’s Equations** | Set of four coupled partial differential equations governing $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ fields | Foundation of classical electromagnetism |
| **Electromagnetic Potentials ($\phi, \mathbf{A}$)** | Scalar and vector potentials from which $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ can be derived | Simplify calculations and connect to quantum theory |
| **Electromagnetic Wave** | Coupled oscillations of $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ propagating through space | Models light, radio waves, and all EM radiation |
| **Poynting Vector ($\mathbf{S}$)** | Represents energy flux (power per unit area) carried by EM fields | Describes energy transfer and power flow |
| **Field Tensor ($F_{\mu\nu}$)** | Antisymmetric rank-2 tensor combining $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ fields in special relativity | Encodes EM fields in a relativistically invariant way |
| **Permittivity ($\varepsilon$) & Permeability ($\mu$)** | Material constants describing response to $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ fields | Characterize medium effects on EM wave propagation |
| **Charge and Current Densities ($\rho, \mathbf{J}$)** | Distributions of charge and current in space and time | Sources for the fields in Maxwell’s equations |
| **Gauge Symmetry** | Freedom to transform potentials without changing physical fields | Fundamental symmetry underlying EM theory and quantum electrodynamics |
| **Quantum Photon** | Quantum of the electromagnetic field; discrete particle of light | Fundamental particle mediating EM interactions in quantum theory |
