# Classical Field Theory (CFT)


> **Classical Field Theory (CFT)** is a framework in physics that describes the behavior of fields (e.g., electromagnetic, gravitational) as continuous entities governed by deterministic equations, such as Maxwell's equations or Einstein's field equations, without incorporating quantum effects.
> 

## List of Fields

> 

| **Category** | **Field** | **Description** | **Governing Equations** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Electromagnetism** | Electric Field $\mathbf{E}$ | Describes the force experienced by charged particles due to electric charges. | Maxwell's equations (Gauss's law for electricity). |
|  | Magnetic Field $\mathbf{B}$ | Describes the force experienced by moving charges or magnetic dipoles. | Maxwell's equations (Gauss's law for magnetism, Faraday's law, Ampère's law). |
|  | Electromagnetic Potential $\phi, \mathbf{A}$ | Describes scalar and vector potentials in electromagnetism. | Maxwell's equations (in terms of potentials). |
| **Gravitation** | Gravitational Field $\mathbf{g}$ | Describes the force experienced by masses due to gravity. | Newton's law of gravitation or Einstein's field equations (general relativity). |
|  | Spacetime Metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ | Describes the curvature of spacetime due to mass and energy. | Einstein's field equations (general relativity). |
| **Fluid Dynamics** | Velocity Field $\mathbf{v}$ | Describes the flow of fluids or the motion of particles in a continuous medium. | Navier-Stokes equations. |
|  | Pressure Field $P$ | Describes the distribution of pressure within a fluid or gas. | Euler equations or Navier-Stokes equations. |
| **Solid Mechanics** | Displacement Field $\mathbf{u}$ | Describes the deformation of solid materials under stress. | Elasticity equations (Hooke's law, strain-displacement relations). |
|  | Stress Field $\sigma$ | Describes internal forces within a solid material. | Cauchy stress equations. |
|  | Strain Field $\epsilon$ | Describes the deformation of a solid material. | Strain-displacement relations. |
| **Thermodynamics** | Temperature Field $T$ | Describes the distribution of temperature in a medium. | Heat equation (Fourier's law). |
|  | Heat Flux Field $\mathbf{q}$ | Describes the flow of thermal energy in a medium. | Fourier's law of heat conduction. |
| **Continuum Mechanics** | Density Field $\rho$ | Describes the distribution of mass in a continuous medium. | Continuity equation (mass conservation). |
|  | Velocity Gradient Field $\nabla \mathbf{v}$ | Describes the rate of deformation in fluids or solids. | Navier-Stokes equations or strain rate tensor. |
| **Acoustics** | Pressure Wave Field $p$ | Describes the propagation of sound waves in a medium. | Wave equation (acoustics). |
| **Material Science** | Phase Field $\phi$ | Describes the evolution of phase boundaries in materials (e.g., solidification). | Phase field equations (e.g., Allen-Cahn or Cahn-Hilliard equations). |
