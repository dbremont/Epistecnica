# Observational

> In these notes, we will explore techniques for building models and drawing inferences from data (observations). Rather than adhering to arbitrary distinctions between fields, we adopt a unified approach, integrating concepts and methods across disciplines to create a cohesive framework for understanding and working with data. Our study will be guided by three key questions:
> 
> - What is the **representational capacity** of a learning model $L_m$?
> - What kinds of questions can the **learning model** $L_m$ answer, and how does it answer them?
> - What are the practical limitations or constraints of the learning model $L_m$ in real-world applications?

> An **observational model** is a representation of the underlying process that generates the observed data.
> 

> ⚠️ **DREAD WARNING**: Never ever forget that your models are not the sections reality;  and the goal is to represent a section of reality; and remember if you don’t  have experience with the problem domain; fooling yourself is as easier as it can get.
>
