Rotation Playground

live noteplayground#geometry#transformation

A rotation turns a figure about a fixed point by a fixed angle without changing its size or shape: every vertex travels along a circle centred on that point, and the angle between any two of them is preserved. Pick a figure, pick a centre, and turn it. Every coordinate, arc, and angle below is computed in the browser from the state you set.

The lab

Drag the red centre point, or switch the centre to the origin, the figure's own centre, or vertex A. The grey ghost is the original figure; the blue figure is its image after the rotation. The gold dashed arcs are the paths traced by each vertex.

The matrix is orthogonal with determinant , so distances, angles, and orientation are preserved — only position and direction change. A positive angle turns counter-clockwise; a negative one turns clockwise. With the centre at the origin the terms vanish and the map is a pure linear transformation.

Because every point keeps its distance to the centre, each vertex traces a circle of radius ; the lab draws those arcs in gold. Rotating by returns the figure to itself, and the point fixed by every rotation is the centre.
