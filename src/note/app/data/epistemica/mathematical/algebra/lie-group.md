# Lie Group


> **Lie group** (pronounced [/liː/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English) [*LEE*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key)) is a [group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_(mathematics)) that is also a [differentiable manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differentiable_manifold), such that group multiplication and taking inverses are both differentiable.
