# Topological Space

 : Manifold (Manifold%200a4e49f73dc6401b986c5ea752d80e82.md)

> Sets equipped with a topology, a collection of open sets that define a structure on the set.
> 

### Topological Spaces

| Subtype | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Manifold** | Differential geometry and topology use a topological space that locally resembles Euclidean space near each point. |
| **Metric Space** | A space where distances between points are defined, inducing a topology that satisfies properties such as the triangle inequality. |
| **Euclidean Space** | The familiar space of classical geometry is also a metric space with topology induced by the Euclidean metric. |
| **Hausdorff Space** | A topological space where distinct points have disjoint neighborhoods, ensuring the uniqueness of limits. |
| **Compact Space** | A space where every open cover has a finite subcover, generalizing the concept of closed and bounded subsets of Euclidean space. |
| **Connected Space** | A space that cannot be represented as the union of two disjoint non-empty open sets, indicating it cannot be split into two "pieces." |
| **Locally Compact Space** | A space where every point has a compact neighborhood. |
| **Normed Space** | In functional analysis, a vector space with a norm assigns a positive actual number to each vector. |
| **Banach Space** | A complete normed vector space, where every Cauchy sequence converges to a limit within the space. |
| **Topological Manifold** | Locally homeomorphic spaces to Euclidean space, beyond differentiable manifolds. |
| **Product Topology** | A topology on the Cartesian product of two or more topological spaces defines a new topological space. |
| **Quotient Topology** | It is obtained by identifying points in a topological space, often used to construct new spaces. |
