> A map between a function to  scalar.
> 

## Definition

A **functional** is a special kind of function that maps functions to scalars: $F: \mathcal{F} \to \mathbb{R} \quad \text{or} \quad \mathbb{C}$

- Here, $\mathcal{F}$ is a function space (e.g., $L^2([a,b])$).
- Example: the **integral functional**: $F(f) = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx$
- Functionals are central in variational calculus, optimization, and quantum mechanics (e.g., expectation values).
