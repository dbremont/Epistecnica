# Differential Geometry

 : Geometry (Geometry%201acc0f5171ec809ab11ad964fbff6df1.md), Space (Space%2019fc0f5171ec80a88882f0b8b4d96331.md)

> **Differential Geometry** is a discipline that studies **smooth geometric structures** on manifolds. It encompasses **many types of geometries**, each defined by additional structures (like metrics, connections, or symplectic forms) that allow one to measure, compare, or relate points, curves, surfaces, and higher-dimensional objects. Rather than being one fixed geometry, it is a **meta-geometry** or a **toolkit for defining and analyzing multiple geometries** on differentiable spaces.
> 

> **Differential Geometry**:  Geometry - Object * Technique * Problem  → Result (Analysis).
> 

## Formulation

> **Geometry:** A system for describing and analyzing the spatial relationships, shapes, and structures of objects, defined by the combination of **geometry objects** and the **techniques or structures** used to study them.
> 

> **Space:** The underlying set or manifold where geometric objects exist; can include additional structures such as metrics, connections, or symplectic forms that define specific geometries.
> 

> **Geometry Object:** Any entity being studied in a geometry, e.g., points, curves, surfaces, vector fields, tensors, submanifolds, or other constructs defined on the space.
> 

## Problem Space

> Which kinds of question do we answer in Differential Geometry?
> 

| **Question Type** | Instance(s) |
| --- | --- |
| Curve properties | What is the length of a curve? Which curves are geodesics? |
| Surface properties | What is the curvature of a surface? How does it embed in space? |
| Manifold structure | What are the topological invariants? Is the manifold orientable? |
| Vector fields | How do vector fields behave along curves or surfaces? |
| Global properties | How do geometric quantities relate to topology (Gauss-Bonnet, etc.)? |

## Technique Space

> Which techniques do we used to solve problems in differential geometry?
> 

| **Technique** | **Use** |
| --- | --- |
| Calculus on manifolds | Compute derivatives, integrals, and rates of change of fields and forms |
| Coordinate charts & transition maps | Express objects locally for computation and analysis |
| Covariant derivatives & connections | Define parallel transport, geodesics, and curvature |
| Metrics & Riemannian structures | Measure distances, angles, areas, volumes |
| Differential forms & exterior calculus | Integrate over manifolds, study flux, divergence, curl |
| Lie derivatives & flows | Study the change of fields along vector flows |

## Result Space

> Which kinds of results do we get by a differential geometric analysis?
> 

| **Result Type** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| Lengths & angles | Quantitative measurements along curves or between vectors |
| Curvature | Riemann curvature tensor, Ricci tensor, scalar curvature |
| Geodesics | Shortest paths, locally straight curves |
| Topological invariants | Euler characteristic, genus, Betti numbers |
| Manifold classification | Local vs. global properties, embedding possibilities |
| Behavior of flows | Integral curves, vector field dynamics, Lie group actions |
