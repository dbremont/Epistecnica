Markov Chain Representational System

live noteinteractive#markov-chain#representation

A Markov Chain representational system writes change as a distribution of mass hopping between named states. The whole model is one transition matrix , , where is the probability of moving from state to . It is how probabilistic succession is stated, and the stationary distribution is the way it is read.
