Ordinary Differential Equation Representational System

live note interactive #dynamical-systems #representation

An Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE) representational system writes continuous-time change as a rate law: a finite state vector evolves under a rule . It is the canonical way dynamical behaviour is stated, and the phase portrait is the way it is read — a vector field whose flow lines are the possible histories of a system.

Below, this one representation is instantiated in five domains. Pick an instance to load it; its rate law and description appear above the lab. Then play — drag the parameter sliders, drop trajectories by clicking the phase plane, and watch the time series.

Playground

Pick an instance, then play with it. The phase plane shows the vector field (pale arrows), the trajectories (flow lines), and the equilibria (numeric, coloured by stability). Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, ⌂ to fit, click anywhere to launch a trajectory from that point.

  1. Every pale arrow is the rate law sampled at a point; together they are the vector field.
  2. The coloured curves are trajectories obtained by integrating the rate law with a fixed-step RK4 scheme — they are the histories the representation permits.
  3. Equilibria are found numerically and classified by the eigenvalues of the Jacobian; the time series below shows the selected trajectory's states against time.
