Coupled Oscillator Network Representational System

live noteinteractive#coupled-oscillators#representation

A Coupled Oscillator Network representational system writes change as many local clocks coupled to their neighbours. Each oscillator has a natural frequency and a phase ; coupling pulls neighbours together, . It is how collective timing is stated, and the order parameter is the way it is read.

Below, this one representation is instantiated in four domains. Pick an instance to load it; its coupling and description appear above the lab. Then play — turn up the coupling and watch a scattered crowd of phases lock into one.

Pick an instance, then play with it. Each dot is one oscillator; its angle is its phase, and the gold arrow is the mean phase whose length is the order parameter . Drag a dot (or click the field) to kick the crowd, and watch climb on the trace.
