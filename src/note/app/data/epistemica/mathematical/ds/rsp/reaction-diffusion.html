Reaction–Diffusion Representational System

live noteinteractive#reaction-diffusion#representation

A Reaction–Diffusion representational system writes change as local reaction coupled to spatial diffusion: two concentrations evolve under and the same for . It is how continuous fields are stated, and the evolving pattern is the way it is read.

Below, this one representation is instantiated in four domains. Pick an instance to load it; its kinetics and description appear above the lab. Then play — let the field evolve, click to inject chemical, and watch the mean values.
