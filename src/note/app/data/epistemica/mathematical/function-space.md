### Lists of Functional Spaces

In mathematics, functional spaces (often referred to as function spaces) are sets of functions that have certain properties and are studied with various mathematical tools. Here's a table listing some important functional spaces, their properties, and example applications:

| Functional Space | Description | Example Applications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $L^p$ spaces | Spaces of functions for which the p-th power of the absolute value is Lebesgue integrable. $L^2$ is particularly important for its connection to Hilbert spaces. | Quantum mechanics, signal processing, Fourier analysis |
| Sobolev spaces $W^{k,p}$ | Spaces of functions that have weak derivatives up to a certain order that are in $L^p$. | Partial differential equations, numerical analysis, variational problems |
| Hilbert spaces | Complete inner product spaces. They generalize the notion of Euclidean space to infinite dimensions. | Quantum mechanics, functional analysis, optimization |
| Banach spaces | Complete normed vector spaces. They generalize the concept of Euclidean spaces to infinite-dimensional spaces. | Functional analysis, differential equations, economics |
| Hölder spaces $C^{k,\alpha}$ | Spaces of functions that have derivatives up to order $k$ that are $\alpha$-Hölder continuous. | Regularity theory of partial differential equations, geometric analysis |
| $C^k$ spaces | Spaces of functions with continuous derivatives up to order  $k$. | Differential equations, differential geometry, approximation theory |
| Hardy spaces $H^p$ | Spaces of holomorphic functions on the unit disk in the complex plane that are $p$-integrable on the boundary. | Complex analysis, harmonic analysis, control theory |
| Bergman spaces | Spaces of square-integrable holomorphic functions on a domain. | Complex analysis, operator theory, mathematical physics |
| Besov spaces | Function spaces that generalize Sobolev and Hölder spaces and are used to study properties of functions in terms of smoothness and integrability. | Harmonic analysis, partial differential equations, image processing |
| Orlicz spaces | Generalizations of  $L^p$ spaces using more general convex functions to define the norm. | Functional analysis, probability theory, optimization |
| $L^\infty$ space | Space of essentially bounded functions. | Measure theory, functional analysis, optimization |
| Schwartz space $\mathcal{S}$ | Space of rapidly decreasing smooth functions, used particularly in the context of Fourier transforms. | Harmonic analysis, distribution theory, quantum field theory |
| Distribution spaces | Generalized function spaces used to extend the concept of derivatives to non-smooth functions. | Theory of partial differential equations, signal processing |
