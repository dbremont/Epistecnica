# Conjugate


> A **conjugate** is a generic conceptual abstraction—or even an abstract technique—that captures a **pairing of an object $X$ with another object $X′$**, such that certain **structural, algebraic, or functional properties are preserved** between them.
> 

**Key points:**

1. The pairing often reflects **symmetry, duality, or inverse-like relationships**.
2. Conjugates appear across many areas of mathematics (numbers, algebra, matrices, groups, functions) but **share the common idea of mirrored or complementary structure**.
3. They are **not necessarily unique**, but their usefulness comes from preserving the properties of interest.
