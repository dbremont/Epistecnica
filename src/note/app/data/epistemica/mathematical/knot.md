# Knot

 : Function (Function%20049537a4fd564eaaac4bae443839b0c0.md), Equivalence Relation (Equivalence%20Relation%20185c0f5171ec809b8792d30f0d629151.md)

> A **knot** is a **smooth embedding** of the circle $S^1$ into 3-dimensional Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^3$, i.e., $K : S^1 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{R}^3$ such that $K$ is a **smooth (or piecewise-linear) injective map**, and the image $K(S^1) \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ is considered **up to ambient isotopy** — that is, two knots $K_1$ and $K_2$ are equivalent if there exists a smooth isotopy of  $\mathbb{R}^3$ (a continuous deformation of the ambient space) that carries $K_1$ to $K_2$.
> 

> **Related Topics**: Knot group (fundamental group of complement), Alexander polynomial, Jones polynomial, Vassiliev invariants, etc.
>
