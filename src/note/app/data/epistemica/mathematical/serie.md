# Serie

 : Sequence (Sequence%20c94965faab844ad0b6d456021a27f952.md), Recurrence Equation (Recurrence%20Equation%20122c0f5171ec8029b62aebd9ef062806.md)

> A **series** is, roughly speaking, a description of the operation of adding infinitely many quantities, one after the other, to a given starting quantity.
> 

> **Convergent Series**: A series whose terms approach a finite limit as more terms are added. For example, the geometric series with a ratio less than one converges.
> 

> **Divergent Series**: A series that does not converge; its terms do not approach a finite limit. Examples include the harmonic series and the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}$.
> 

## List of Series

> **Arithmetic Series**: A series in which each term after the first is obtained by adding a constant difference to the previous term. $a+ (a+d) + (a+2d) + ...$
> 

> **Geometric Series**: A series where each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by a constant ratio.  $a+ar+ar^2+…$
> 

> **Power Series**: A series of the form $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n (x - c)^n$, where $a_a$ are coefficients, $c$ is a constant, and $x$ is a variable.
> 

> **Taylor Series**: A specific type of power series that represents a function as an infinite sum of terms calculated from the values of its derivatives at a single point.
> 

> **Fourier Series**: A way to represent a function as a sum of sine and cosine functions, often used in signal processing.
> 

> **Telescoping Series**: A **telescoping series** is a series whose terms mostly cancel out when summed, leaving only a few terms, much like a collapsing telescope.
> 

## QA

### Why a telescoping series is called telescoping?

> Telescoping in the informal language means expanding and contracting (collapsing) a telescope - seems this series can be expanded and collapse or contracted; they are described as telescoping.
