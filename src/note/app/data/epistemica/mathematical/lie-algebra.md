# Lie Algebra


> An algebra is classified as a **Lie algebra** if it is a vector space equipped with a binary operation called the **Lie bracket**, which satisfies the properties of **bilinearity**, alternativity (i.e., the bracket of an element with itself is zero), and the **Jacobi** identity.
> 

## List of Lie Algebras

| **Lie Algebra** | **Definition** | **Applications** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **$\mathfrak{gl}(n)$** | The general linear Lie algebra of all $n \times n$ matrices with entries from a field, usually $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. | Linear algebra, quantum mechanics, and representation theory. |
| **$\mathfrak{sl}(n)$** | The special linear Lie algebra of all $n \times n$ matrices with trace zero. | Quantum mechanics, particle physics, and string theory. |
| **$\mathfrak{so}(n)$** | The special orthogonal Lie algebra of all $n \times n$skew-symmetric matrices. | Theoretical physics, relativity, and gauge theory. |
| **$\mathfrak{su}(n)$** | The special unitary Lie algebra of all $n \times n$ skew-Hermitian matrices with trace zero. | Quantum mechanics, particle physics, and quantum field theory. |
| $\mathfrak{sp}(n)$ | The symplectic Lie algebra of all $2n \times 2n$ matrices that preserve a symplectic form. | Hamiltonian mechanics, symplectic geometry, and quantum mechanics. |
| **Heisenberg algebra** | The Lie algebra generated by the position and momentum operators in quantum mechanics. | Quantum mechanics, representation theory, and harmonic analysis. |
| **Poincaré algebra** | The Lie algebra of the Poincaré group, representing the symmetries of special relativity. | Special relativity, quantum field theory, and particle physics. |
| **Virasoro algebra** | An extension of the Witt algebra with a central charge term, important in string theory. | String theory, conformal field theory, and statistical mechanics. |
| **Kac-Moody algebra** | A generalization of finite-dimensional Lie algebras, including affine Lie algebras. | String theory, conformal field theory, and integrable systems. |
| **Cartan algebra** | The Lie algebra generated by Cartan matrices, important in the classification of simple Lie algebras. | Theoretical physics, representation theory, and algebraic geometry. |
