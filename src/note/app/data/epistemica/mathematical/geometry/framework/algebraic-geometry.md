# Algebraic Geometry

 : Algebraic Variety (Algebraic%20Variety%20178c0f5171ec80b6905af23118f21fe4.md)

> Algebraic Geometry is the branch of mathematics that studies **geometric structures** defined by **polynomial equations**. It focuses on the solution sets of systems of polynomial equations—known as algebraic varieties—and explores their geometric, topological, and algebraic properties using geometric, topological, and even algebraic tools.
> 

> Reader: We study the solutions of polynomial equations—the geometric structures they define—using a continually expanding and virtually limitless toolbox of mathematical techniques.
> 

## Formulation

Study Mechanisms:

- Relating algebraic properties of polynomials (like factorization, ideals) to geometric structures.
- Studying families of solutions, intersections, singularities, and dimensionality.
- Using tools from commutative algebra (rings, ideals, modules) to understand geometry.

**Objects of study:**

- **Algebraic varieties:** Sets of solutions to one or more polynomial equations over a field (e.g., real numbers ℝ, complex numbers ℂ).
- **Schemes (modern view):** A generalization of varieties that allows “gluing” of local solutions and incorporates more abstract algebraic structures.

**Applications:**

- Robotics (configuration spaces), coding theory, cryptography (elliptic curves), optimization, and physics (string theory, moduli spaces).
