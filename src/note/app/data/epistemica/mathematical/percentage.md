# Percentage


> A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100, representing a proportionate part of a whole.
> 

## Usage

| **Use** | **Description** | **Case** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Financial Calculations** | Used to determine interest rates, fees, or tax rates in financial transactions. | A bank offers a savings account with a 3% interest rate. |
| **Discounts and Markups** | Commonly used in retail to calculate sales prices after discounts or price increases. | An item originally priced at $100 with a 20% discount is sold for $80. |
| **Statistics** | Utilized in statistical reports to express data in a more comprehensible way, like proportions and comparisons. | 75% of survey respondents prefer product A over product B. |
| **Performance Evaluation** | Used to assess performance metrics, such as grades or completion rates. | A student scores 85% on an exam, indicating they answered 85 out of 100 questions correctly. |
| **Population Studies** | Expresses demographic data in terms of the percentage of a population exhibiting certain characteristics. | 60% of the population is vaccinated against a disease. |
| **Health Metrics** | Often used to report health statistics, such as BMI or prevalence rates of diseases. | 30% of adults are classified as overweight based on BMI. |
| **Surveys and Polls** | Indicates the proportion of respondents who hold specific opinions or preferences. | 50% of participants prefer option A in a poll. |
| **Budgeting and Financial Planning** | Helps individuals and organizations allocate budgets based on percentage of total income or expenses. | Allocating 30% of income to housing expenses. |
| **Investment Returns** | Represents the gains or losses on investments relative to the original amount invested. | An investment returns 10% over one year. |
| **Growth Rates** | Expresses changes over time, such as economic growth or population increases. | The economy grew by 2% last year. |
| **Comparison of Data** | Facilitates the comparison of different datasets or groups on a common scale. | Comparing the percentage of students passing a test in different schools. |
| **Risk Assessment** | Used to express the likelihood of an event occurring, such as in insurance policies. | A policy might state a 5% chance of a natural disaster occurring. |
| **Sales Performance** | Measures sales targets achieved as a percentage of the goal. | A sales team met 90% of its quarterly target. |
| **Cost of Living Adjustments** | Often used to adjust salaries or benefits according to inflation rates. | A salary increase of 4% to account for inflation. |
