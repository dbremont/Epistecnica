# Exponentiation

 : Logarithm (Logarithm%20150c0f5171ec809ea1ebe2a4ee071f13.md)

> **Exponentiation** is a mathematical operation where a base is multiplied by itself a specified number of times, or more generally, defined as $a^b = \exp(b \cdot \ln a)$ for real or complex values.
> 

## Order of Magnitude

> An **order of magnitude** is a way to describe a **scale** or **size difference** between quantities using **powers of 10**. It helps compare values that differ by factors of 10, 100, 1000, and so on — that is, exponentially rather than linearly.
> 

> Order of Magnitude=⌊log10(x)⌋
> 

> **"Order"** → from Latin *ordo*, meaning *rank* or *level*.  Refers to the **position in a hierarchy** or **exponential scale**.
> 

> **"Magnitude"** → from Latin *magnitudo*, meaning *greatness* or *size*. Refers to the **amount or scale** of the quantity.
> 

**Size Difference:**

| Quantity A | Quantity B | Ratio A:B | Orders of Magnitude Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 100 | 1:10 | 1 order of magnitude |
| 1,000 | 1,000,000 | 1:1,000 | 3 orders of magnitude |
| 0.01 | 100 | 1:10,000 | 4 orders of magnitude |
| 2,000,000 | 20,000,000 | 1:10 | 1 order of magnitude |

**Scale:**

| **Order of Magnitude** | **Approximate Scale** | **Example** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10010^0 | 1 | 1 meter |
| 10110^1 | 10 | Room length |
| 10310^3 | 1,000 | Small city population |
| 10610^6 | 1,000,000 | Large city |
| 10910^9 | 1,000,000,000 | National budget or population |
