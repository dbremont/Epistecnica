# Signal


> A **function** that conveys information about a phenomenon or system.
> 

## Complex Systems

> In complex adaptive systems, **signals** are pieces of information or stimuli that help agents (or components) within the system make decisions, adjust behavior, and adapt to their environment.
> 

> **Signals** contribute to **self-organizing** behaviors within the system, where local interactions based on shared signals lead to emergent patterns or structures without a central controlling authority.
> 

> Feedback loops, which can be either positive (reinforcing) or negative (balancing), are essential in complex adaptive systems. **Signals often carry feedback** that agents interpret to modify their behavior.
> 

> Signals carry **information** that agents use to guide their actions and interactions. For example, in financial markets, price changes act as signals that influence buying and selling decisions among traders.
> 

> Agents within the system respond to signals by adjusting their behavior or learning from past interactions, allowing the system to adapt to changing conditions over time.
> 

> Signals help synchronize and coordinate activities across agents, fostering cooperation or competition within the system.
>
