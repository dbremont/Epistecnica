# Manifold

 : Topological Space (Topological%20Space%20a2ad890d81b84a3bb674e007bbdc8f78.md), Smoothness (Smoothness%2019fc0f5171ec80089ecae31fc54e509d.md), Geometry (Geometry%201acc0f5171ec809ab11ad964fbff6df1.md), Differential Form (Differential%20Form%20e30db9e63d3840ba9aa2b1f929d36d50.md), Space (Space%2019fc0f5171ec80a88882f0b8b4d96331.md)

> A **topological space** that locally resembles **Euclidean space** and allows for calculus.
> 

> **Topological Manifold** – A space that is locally like $\mathbb{R}^n$, but without requiring smooth structure.
> 

> **Differentiable Manifold** – A manifold where functions are differentiable, allowing calculus to be done.
> 

> **Riemannian Manifold** – A differentiable manifold with a metric that allows measurement of distances and angles.
> 

> **Complex Manifold** – A manifold where charts map to $\mathbb{C}^n$ instead of $\mathbb{R}^n$.
> 

> **Lorentzian Manifold** – A manifold with a metric signature that allows the study of spacetime in general relativity.
