# Logistic Function

 : Function (Function%20049537a4fd564eaaac4bae443839b0c0.md)

> The **function models** how a system, such as population growth or neural activation, evolves over time, initially growing rapidly and then gradually slowing as it approaches a **limiting capacity** (often referred to as the carrying capacity in biological systems). It reflects a system's **saturation point**, where growth levels off as it reaches its **maximum potential**.
> 

> It represents the relationship between a system's current state and its potential capacity, illustrating how the system evolves toward its limiting boundary.
>
