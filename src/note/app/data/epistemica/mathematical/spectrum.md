# Spectrum


> In mathematics, particularly in functional analysis and linear algebra, the **spectrum** of a linear operator $T$ on a Banach or Hilbert space is the set of complex numbers $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}$ for which the operator $T - \lambda I$ is not invertible, encompassing the point spectrum (eigenvalues), continuous spectrum, and residual spectrum, and generalizing the notion of eigenvalues for infinite-dimensional or non-diagonalizable operators.
> 

Notation:

- **$I$**: the identity operator.
- **$T$**: a linear operator on a Banach or Hilbert space
