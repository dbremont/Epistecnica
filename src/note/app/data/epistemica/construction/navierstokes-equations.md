# Navier–Stokes Equations

   : Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) (Computational%20Fluid%20Dynamics%20(CFD)%2049aa01db8f824c00a226bb0cf4ccbca4.md)

> The **Navier–Stokes equations** describe how the velocity, pressure, and density of a fluid evolve over time due to internal stresses, viscosity, and external forces, embodying the conservation of momentum in a flowing medium.
