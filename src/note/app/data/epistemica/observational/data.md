## I.I.D Data

> "I.I.D." stands for Independent and Identically Distributed, a common assumption in probability and statistics. It means that the random variables you are working with have two key properties:
> 
- **Independent:** Each random variable does not influence the others. Knowing the outcome of one gives no information about the outcome of another.
- **Identically Distributed:** Each random variable follows the same probability distribution (same mean, variance, and overall shape).
