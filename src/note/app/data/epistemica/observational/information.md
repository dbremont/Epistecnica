 : Shannon Information Theory (Shannon%20Information%20Theory%20a22d92f3a8b845698e10e8039544dad3.md), Coding (Coding%2021b82ffc9433484ca5e7a8aa98f32825.md), Information Geometry (Information%20Geometry%209e8f47513e9c4b8ba9e9450c52dced78.md), Fisher Information (Fisher%20Information%204cefa509a9c94f7b912cfbdc477adbec.md), Algorithmic Information Theory (AIT) (Algorithmic%20Information%20Theory%20(AIT)%2002ef38459a5743eeb0726c479584010a.md)

> In this note, we will explore the multi-aspect concept of “Information.”
> 

> **Information** is any distinguishable pattern, signal, or state that, when received or interpreted by a system, constrains its possible beliefs, states, or actions.
> 

> **Información** del latin informatio - “acción y efecto de dar una noticia”.
>

## Space of Information Theory

| **Theory** | **Focus** | **Key Contributors** | **Deals with meaning?** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shannon's Information Theory** | Measures information as reduction of uncertainty (entropy) | Claude Shannon (1948) | ❌ No |
| **Kolmogorov Complexity** | Measures information as the shortest algorithm that produces a string | Andrey Kolmogorov, Gregory Chaitin | ❌ No |
| **Fisher Information** | Measures information in statistical estimation | Ronald Fisher | ❌ No |
| **Algorithmic Information Theory** | Merges Shannon’s entropy and Kolmogorov complexity to measure randomness in data | Chaitin, Solomonoff, Kolmogorov | ❌ No |
| **Dretske’s Semantic Information Theory** | Considers information as meaningful data that reduces uncertainty | Fred Dretske | ✅ Yes |
| **Floridi’s Theory of Strongly Semantic Information** | Defines information as data + truthfulness | Luciano Floridi | ✅ Yes |
| **Bar-Hillel & Carnap Semantic Theory** | Uses probability to define semantic information | Yehoshua Bar-Hillel, Rudolf Carnap | ✅ Yes |
| **Quantum Information Theory** | Studies information in quantum systems (qubits, superposition) | John von Neumann, Charles Bennett | ❌ No |

## It From Bit Claim

> **“Every ‘it’—every particle, every field of force, even the spacetime continuum itself—derives its function, its meaning, its very existence entirely… from the answers to yes-or-no questions, binary choices, bits.”** — *John Archibald Wheeler*
> 

> Reality ("it") arises from information ("bit").
> 

> **Wheeler’s language is philosophical**, it sparked **technical developments** in how we **quantify and manipulate information in physics**.
> 

> dbremont: Sees “It from Bit” as philosophical overreach — bits may model systems but don’t constitute them.
> 

> “It from Bit” provides a unifying language for physics and computation. It's influential in quantum information, black hole entropy, and simulation theory.
> 

> **Limits**  It risks collapsing into metaphor if not backed by physical models. Bits can encode **descriptions** , but whether they *constitute* reality is unproven.
> 

How ***Wheeler** back this claim?*

- Quantum Measurement Creates Reality ?? This seems to me like a philosophical interpretation.
- Wheeler was inspired by **Claude Shannon's information theory,** He observed that many physical quantities (e.g. entropy, state, position) can be defined in **bits.**
    - He extended this to propose that the **structure of the universe itself** might emerge from binary distinctions.

## What do scientists mean when they refer to ‘the information theory of a given set of phenomena’? Are they talking about properties of  that determine compressibility, describability, regularities, or something else?

> Scientists invoking “the information theory of phenomena” are usually referring to **the properties of those phenomena as data**—how compressible, describable, patterned, or predictable they are—not directly to the physical phenomena themselves.
>
