# AIXI


> **AIXI** is a theoretical model of an **idealized superintelligent agent** in artificial intelligence, proposed by Marcus Hutter.
> 

Formally, **AIXI** combines:

- **Solomonoff induction** (for prediction)
- **Sequential decision theory** (for choosing optimal actions)

In simple terms:

- **It predicts** all possible future observations and rewards by considering all computable hypotheses, weighted by their simplicity (shorter programs are more likely).
- **It acts** to maximize expected rewards over time based on these predictions.
