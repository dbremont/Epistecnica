# Chi-Squared Distribution

   : Gamma Distribution (Gamma%20Distribution%20dea9bef79a3842f9b45335592819bee8.md)

> The **Chi-squared distribution** is a continuous probability distribution that describes the sum of the squares of $k$ independent standard normal random variables.
> 

> **Chi-squared distribution is a special case of the Gamma Distribution Family;**
> 
> 
> **Gamma Distribution:**  $\chi^2(k) \sim \text{Gamma}(\alpha = k/2, \beta = 2)$    or $\chi^2(k) \sim \text{Gamma}(\alpha = k/2, \beta = \frac{1}{2})$. Note: $k$ refers here to the degrees of freedom.
> 

> **The Chi-squared distribution can be defined as the distribution of the square of a standard normal random variable.**  For example, let $X \sim N(0,1)$. If we set $Y = X^2$, then $Y \sim \chi^2(1)$, that is, $Y$ follows a Chi-squared distribution with 1 degree of freedom.
