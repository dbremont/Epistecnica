# Bootstrapping


> A resampling technique is used to estimate the **distribution of a statistic or estimator**.
> 

> **Bootstrapping** is a resampling-based statistical method that estimates the sampling distribution of an estimator by repeatedly drawing random samples with replacement from the observed data, allowing for inference about population parameters without relying on asymptotic approximations or parametric assumptions.
>
