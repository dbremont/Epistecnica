## Betting Methods

> 

| **Strategy** | **Description** | **Pros** | **Cons** | **Best For** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Kelly Criterion** | Bets a fraction of bankroll based on edge and odds to maximize long-term growth. | Maximizes growth, balances risk and reward. | High volatility, requires accurate probability estimates. | Bettors with a clear edge and accurate probability estimates. |
| **Fixed Fractional** | Bets a fixed percentage of bankroll on each wager (e.g., 1-5%). | Simple, reduces risk of ruin. | Slower growth compared to Kelly. | Conservative bettors or those with uncertain edge. |
| **Martingale** | Doubles the bet after each loss to recover losses with a single win. | Guarantees recovery of losses with infinite bankroll. | Exponential growth of bets, high risk of ruin. | Short-term play with small, frequent wins (e.g., roulette). |
| **Fibonacci** | Follows the Fibonacci sequence (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ...) to determine bet sizes. | Slower progression than Martingale, less aggressive. | Still risky, requires long winning streaks to recover losses. | Bettors looking for a less aggressive progression than Martingale. |
| **Flat Betting** | Bets the same fixed amount on every wager. | Simple, predictable, no risk of ruin. | No adaptation to edge or bankroll size. | Beginners or those with no edge. |
| **Risk of Ruin (RoR)** | Adjusts bet sizes based on the probability of losing the entire bankroll. | Focuses on preserving bankroll, customizable. | Complex calculations, may limit growth. | Bettors prioritizing capital preservation. |
| **Anti-Martingale** | Increases bets after wins and decreases after losses (e.g., "let profits ride"). | Capitalizes on winning streaks, limits losses. | Misses opportunities during losing streaks. | Bettors with winning streaks or momentum-based strategies. |
| **Value Betting** | Bets only when the odds are in your favor (positive expected value). | Focuses on long-term profitability. | Requires identifying value opportunities, not always available. | Experienced bettors who can identify mispriced odds. |
| **D'Alembert** | Increases bets by one unit after a loss and decreases by one unit after a win. | Less aggressive than Martingale, easier to manage. | Still risky, relies on eventual wins to recover losses. | Bettors looking for a moderate progression system. |
| **Paroli** | Doubles bets after wins to capitalize on winning streaks. | Limits losses, focuses on winning streaks. | Requires consecutive wins to be effective. | Bettors with a winning streak or positive momentum. |
| **Oscar's Grind** | Increases bets by one unit after a win and resets after a loss. | Slow and steady progression, low risk. | Slow growth, requires patience. | Bettors looking for a low-risk, gradual progression. |
| **1-3-2-6 System** | A positive progression system based on a 4-bet sequence (1x, 3x, 2x, 6x). | Limits losses, focuses on winning streaks. | Requires consecutive wins to be effective. | Bettors with a winning streak or positive momentum. |
| **Labouchere** | Uses a sequence of numbers to determine bet sizes (e.g., 1-2-3). | Flexible, customizable progression. | Complex, requires careful planning. | Experienced bettors with a structured approach. |
| **Reverse Labouchere** | Similar to Labouchere but adds numbers after wins and removes after losses. | Limits losses, focuses on winning streaks. | Complex, requires careful planning. | Experienced bettors with a structured approach. |
| **Dutching** | Spreads bets across multiple outcomes to guarantee a profit. | Reduces risk, ensures profit if any outcome wins. | Requires precise calculations, limits upside. | Bettors looking to hedge or guarantee a profit. |
| **Hedging** | Places additional bets to reduce risk or lock in profits. | Reduces risk, ensures profit in certain scenarios. | Limits potential profits, requires precise calculations. | Bettors looking to minimize risk or lock in profits. |
| **Arbitrage Betting** | Exploits differences in odds across bookmakers to guarantee a profit. | Risk-free profit if done correctly. | Requires multiple accounts, time-consuming. | Advanced bettors with access to multiple bookmakers. |
| **Trading (Betfair)** | Buys and sells bets on betting exchanges to lock in profits. | Can guarantee profits, flexible. | Requires knowledge of betting exchanges, time-consuming. | Advanced bettors familiar with betting exchanges. |
| **Monte Carlo Simulation** | Uses random sampling to simulate outcomes and optimize bet sizes. | Provides insights into risk and reward. | Requires computational resources, complex. | Advanced bettors with technical expertise. |
| **Expected Value (EV)** | Bets only when the expected value is positive. | Focuses on long-term profitability. | Requires identifying value opportunities, not always available. | Experienced bettors who can identify mispriced odds. |
| **Bankroll Management** | Focuses on preserving and growing bankroll through disciplined betting. | Reduces risk of ruin, sustainable. | No specific strategy for individual bets. | All bettors, especially beginners. |
