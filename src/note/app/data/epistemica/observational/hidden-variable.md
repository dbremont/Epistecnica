# Hidden Variable


> A **latent variable**, also known as a hidden or unobservable variable, is a variable that cannot be directly measured or observed but is inferred from other variables that are measured.
> 

> Note: Hidden Value = Latent Variable.
> 

## Taxonomy

| **Latent Variable** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Intelligence** | It cannot be measured directly or inferred through IQ tests and assessments. |
| **Attitude** | A latent construct inferred from surveys or questionnaires regarding beliefs and preferences. |
| **Emotional Well-being** | Involves a person’s emotional state, happiness, and life satisfaction, assessed through self-report surveys. |
| **Personality Traits** | Traits like extroversion and conscientiousness are inferred through personality assessments. |
| **Socioeconomic Status (SES)** | Represents an individual’s economic and social position, often estimated using income, education, and occupation data. |
| **Market Demand** | Reflects consumer preferences and willingness to purchase, inferred from sales data and surveys. |
| **Quality of Life** | A complex construct involving physical health and psychological well-being is estimated through surveys and assessments. |
| **Customer Satisfaction** | I inferred from customer feedback, reviews, and surveys in business and marketing contexts. |
| **Depression** | I inferred from symptoms assessed through psychological evaluations and questionnaires. |
| **Academic Achievement** | A student’s performance is typically assessed through grades and test scores. |
| **Dark Matter and Dark Energy (Cosmology)** | I postulated in astrophysics to explain the motion and expansion of the universe, though not directly observable. |
| **Wavefunction (Quantum Mechanics)** | Describes the probability distribution of a particle’s position and momentum in quantum mechanics, a complex-valued function used to make predictions. |
| **Temperature (Statistical Mechanics)** | Considered a latent variable related to the average kinetic energy of particles in large ensembles, it is not directly observable at the individual particle level. |
| **Gravitational Potential (General Relativity)** | It represents the curvature of spacetime, which is used to describe gravity; it is inferred from the motion of objects in its presence. |
| **String Tension (String Theory)** | A latent variable in string theory, strings are postulated as the fundamental building blocks of the universe, with their tension related to phenomena that are not directly measurable. |
| **Phase Transition Order Parameter (Condensed Matter Physics)** | Characterizes the state and transition of a system during phase transitions (e.g., solid to liquid), inferred from other measurements. |

## **Best Practices**

- **Triangulation**: Combine multiple methods (e.g., surveys + behavioral data) for robustness.
- **Validation**: Ensure measures are reliable (consistent) and valid (reflect the true construct).
- **Contextual Adaptation**: Tailor tools to the specific domain (e.g., psychology vs. economics).

## Case Studies

- Assessing "organizational culture" across different branches of a company.
- Measuring "risk aversion" through economic choice tasks.
- Measuring "anxiety" through physiological responses (heart rate, sweating).
- Using "years of education" as a proxy for "socioeconomic status.”
- Track actions linked to the construct (e.g., "engagement" via click-through rates).
