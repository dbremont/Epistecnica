# Principle of Sufficient Reason (PSR)

> The **Principle of Sufficient Reason (PSR)** is the meta-ontological principle that asserts **everything that exists or occurs has a sufficient reason or explanation for why it is so rather than not**.
> 

## Formulation

- **Causal:** Every event has a cause.
- **Logical:** Every proposition has a reason why it is true.
- **Existential:** Every entity exists for a reason.

## History

- Formulated by **Leibniz** in the 17th century.
- It underlies much of classical metaphysics and rationalist thought.

## **Schopenhauer’s Extension**

> In *On the Fourfold Root of the Principle of Sufficient Reason*, Schopenhauer divides the principle into four kinds of reasoning:
> 
1. **Becoming (causal law in nature)**
2. **Knowing (logical justification)**
3. **Being (mathematical or spatial relations)**
4. **Acting (human motivation and volition)**
