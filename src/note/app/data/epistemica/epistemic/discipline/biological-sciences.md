# Biological Sciences

> In this set of notes, Biological Sciences is treated as a **discipline family** — an `Epistemic Practice` whose members are the practices that inquire into living systems: their molecules, cells, organisms, lineages, and ecosystems.

> **Biological Sciences** is the family of practices that treat the world as alive: how living systems are built, how they work, how they vary, and how they descend.

> Its members cut the same living segment at different levels of organization — molecule, cell, organ, organism, population, ecosystem — which is why the family is held together by level and by descent rather than by a single method. A molecular biologist and an ecologist share the standard of replication and very little else.

> Biophysics, biochemistry, and bioinformatics borrow the instruments of physics, chemistry, and computation and formalism, but each asks a biological question — how a living system is built, works, varies, or descends — and so is carried here.

This note is the family-level decomposition; each member discipline is a practice in its own right and can be promoted to its own note without rewriting this table. The schema it instantiates is owned by the canonical [Philosophia Artium Epistemicarum et Operis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/csiglab/Naturgnosis/refs/heads/main/app/note/data/meta/philosophia-artium-epistemicarum-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What is this epistemic instance?

> Biological Sciences is the organized inquiry into living systems: how they are built, how they function, how they vary, and how they descend. Its product is a warranted account of a living system at a stated level of organization; its warrant is replication in independent organisms and the reproducibility of the assay.

### What epistemic element type does this epistemic instance belong to?

> `Epistemic Practice` — the family is a doing: a set of practices carried by agents, applying methods and acts, producing constructions, and judged by its own standard. It is not an `Epistemic Element Set` read as a catalogue, and not an `Epistemic Framework` read as a lens; both are secondary readings, recorded below.

### Is the type of this `epistemic element instance` ambiguous?

> No at the root: Biological Sciences is one organized doing, so `Epistemic Practice` is its primary type. Secondary readings stay prose: `readable as` an `Epistemic Element Set` when the family is taken as a catalogue of disciplines rather than as a doing, and `readable as` an `Epistemic Framework` when its shared methods are read as one lens over the target. Neither is a second primary type, and no row below carries two types.

> The boundary is drawn by segment and warrant, not by vocabulary. Chemistry studies the molecule without the organism; medicine intervenes on the organism rather than only warranting claims about it; paleontology reads the record of life but is carried by earth science where the question is stratigraphic.

### What is the recursive instance decomposition of this epistemic instance?

> **Boundary.** This table decomposes one epistemic instance — the family as an organized doing — and names its member disciplines as `Member` sub-practices. The scaffolding the members share (orientation, access, representation, evaluation, infrastructure) is typed once at the family root, because the same instruments, standards, and notations serve every member; repeating it per discipline would restate one element once per discipline. Sub-fields are grown only where a discipline genuinely divides, and each remains a `Member` practice, never a second type.

> **Stopping rule.** A row is terminal when it names a discipline or a sub-field. No discipline is decomposed into its own theories, and the segment it addresses is named here but decomposed only in the naturalis grammar.

> **Identity.** Instance Tree Path holds instances only; every path is unique, and no type segment terminates a branch. A discipline that also belongs to a neighbouring family — physical chemistry, biochemistry, biophysics, mathematical physics — is carried by the family whose question it asks, and the overlap is recorded in its description rather than by giving the row two parents.

> **Typing.** The root path declares the role once: `Epistemic Element` (→ Kind) `Epistemic Element Set` (→ Kind) `Epistemic Practice` (→ Instance) Biological Sciences. Members hang by `Member`, not by `Part`: the family is an aggregate of practices, not a whole with components.

### What Is a critical conceptual evaluation of this `epistemic element instance`?

> **Verdict.** The family is broad and internally heterogeneous: its members share a warrant but not a method, so coherence comes from the levels of organization rather than from a common instrument. That makes the decomposition honest and the boundary soft — the same phenomenon can be described at four levels, and which one counts depends on the question asked. Its strength is coverage; its limit is that mechanism is often inferred from correlation that the assay cannot separate.

| Criterion | Verdict | Diagnostic Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conceptual Identification | Strong | Distinguished from chemistry (molecule, not organism) and from medicine (intervenes, rather than only warrants); the level of organization is stated as the ordering principle. |
| Definitional Clarity | Moderate–Strong | "Living systems" plus the level list states the cut; replication in independent organisms states the warrant. |
| Conceptual Determinacy | Moderate | Boundary rule: a claim is biological iff it concerns a living system or its parts at a stated level of organization. Borderline cases — biochemistry, biophysics, bioinformatics, paleontology — are placed by the question the practice asks. |
| Internal Consistency | Strong | Level of organization orders the members without giving any of them a second type; the shared scaffolding is typed once at the root. |
| Representational Adequacy | Moderate–Strong | Nomenclature, the model organism, and the assay record cover descent, structure, and function; behaviour is carried as a discipline, not as a row type. |
| Purpose Adequacy | Strong | Function, origin, and variation are all served; the note does not claim mechanism where only correlation has been measured. |
