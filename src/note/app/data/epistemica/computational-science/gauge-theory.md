# Gauge Theory


> In this note we will explore the notion of ‘Gauge Theory’ and  the set of techniques related with the creation and analysis of such theories.
> 

> `A` **gauge theory** is a **theory that possesses a local symmetry (gauge invariance)**—it is not merely the property itself, but a class of theories **defined by** that property. Note `gauge theory` without the a - means nothing.
> 

> Gauge theory toolbox involves a pipeline of tasks: **construction, symmetry analysis, gauge fixing, dynamical derivation, quantization, consistency validation, and solution extraction**, all centered around enforcing and exploiting local invariance.
>
