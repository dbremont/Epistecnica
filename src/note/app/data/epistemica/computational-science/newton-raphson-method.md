# Newton-Raphson Method


> **Newton's Method** (also called the **Newton-Raphson Method**) is an **iterative numerical optimization algorithm** used to find **roots** (solutions) of real-valued functions.
>

## Formulation

> $x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$
