# Boolean Network


> Boolean networks can model systems with binary states and interactions, such as genetic regulatory networks, neural networks, epidemiological models, social dynamics, ecological systems, and technological networks like power grids.
> 

> The **dynamics of networks** refer to the study of how the states and interactions within a network evolve over time, influenced by the structure and function of its interconnected elements.
