# Antenna

- [Networking Hardware](note.html?n=technica/computation/telecom/networking-hardware.md)

> An antenna is a **transducer** that converts **guided** **electromagnetic waves** into **free-space radiation** and vice versa, operating based on Maxwell’s equations, with characteristics defined by parameters such as radiation pattern, gain, directivity, impedance matching, polarization, and efficiency across specific frequency bands.
