# Bluetooth


> 

Guiding questions:

- **What are the components of the technical objects in a Bluetooth communication system?**
    - **Physical Decomposition** – hardware elements such as antennas, transceivers, controllers, power supply, and sensors.
    - **Software Decomposition** – firmware, protocol stack (L2CAP, HCI, SDP, etc.), drivers, and application interfaces.
- **What is the electromagnetic wave mechanism used?**
    - Frequency bands, modulation schemes (e.g., GFSK), spread spectrum techniques (FHSS), and how signals propagate in space.
- How does the protocol ensure reliable data transfer?
- What are the power and energy constraints?
- What are the interference and coexistence challenges?
- How is security and encryption handled?
- How does the system scale with multiple devices?
- What are the latency and throughput characteristics?
- How is the hardware optimized for miniaturization and cost?
- What is a **Bluetooth** profile?
- **What other questions should I be asking?**
