# Interface Design Principle


## On The Subjectivity of Design

> Is design really subjective, or just hard to ground on analytical principles? Aka,  you know; when you see it.
> 

> **Design** is the deliberate structuring of perception and action, exploiting fundamental cognitive and neurological rules to **create systems** that are both intuitive and expressive.
> 

**Core Principles:**

- **Cognitively Rooted** – Grounded in how the human brain naturally perceives and processes information (e.g., Gestalt grouping, pattern recognition, spatial memory).
- **Minimal Universal Constraints** – Respects perceptual rules the brain handles with ease, such as contrast, visual hierarchy, and affordance cues.
- **Flexibility Within Structure** – Allows for a high degree of variation and creativity within a framework of perceptual coherence.
- **Dual Depth: Functional & Emotional** – Balances utility (clarity, predictability) with affective impact, creating resonance beyond mere usability.
