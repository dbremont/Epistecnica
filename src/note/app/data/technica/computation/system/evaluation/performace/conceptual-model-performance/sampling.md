# Sampling


> **Sampling models** are foundational for making inferences about a population from a sample, especially under conditions where studying the entire population is impractical or impossible.
> 

> **Sampling** is selecting a subset of data to collect and analyze, helping manage the volume of telemetry data generated by a system.
> 

Types of Data:

- **Logs are** text-based records of events, activities, and messages the system generates. They are often used for troubleshooting and debugging.
- **Metrics**: Numerical data and statistics provide quantifiable information about system performance, resource utilization, and application behavior.
- **Traces**: A representation of the path and timing of requests or transactions as they traverse various components in a distributed system, allowing for end-to-end visibility.=
