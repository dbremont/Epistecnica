What are the dimensions of Quality Control in a Software Production Firm?

- **Interaction Design Quality** — how well users can perceive, understand, learn, and operate the system.
- **Functional Quality** — whether the software correctly implements its intended functions and domain rules.
- **Structural Quality** — internal organization of the software: modularity, cohesion, coupling, orthogonality,  separation of concerns, architectural integrity.
- **Reliability Quality** — ability to operate correctly and consistently over time and under expected conditions.
- **Performance Quality** — responsiveness, throughput, latency, resource consumption, and scalability.
- **Security Quality** — resistance to unauthorized access, misuse, data compromise, and other security failures.
- **Compatibility / Interoperability Quality** — ability to interact correctly with other systems, platforms, protocols, and data formats.
- **Maintainability Quality** — ease of understanding, modifying, debugging, testing, and extending the software.
- **Testability Quality** — how effectively the system can be verified through automated and manual testing.
- **Deployability Quality** — ease and safety of building, packaging, releasing, configuring, upgrading, and rolling back the software.
- **Observability Quality** — ability to determine the system's internal state and diagnose failures through logs, metrics, traces, etc.
- **Data Quality** — correctness, consistency, integrity, completeness, and appropriate handling of data.
- **Portability Quality** — ability to operate across environments, operating systems, architectures, or infrastructure.
- **Accessibility Quality** — ability of people with different abilities to effectively use the system.
- **Documentation Quality** — accuracy, completeness, discoverability, and usefulness of technical and user documentation.
