# Computation Trade-Off


> A **Computation Trade-Off** occurs when optimizing one aspect of a computational process (such as speed, memory usage, accuracy, or energy consumption) leads to compromises or reductions in another aspect. In other words, improving performance in one metric typically incurs costs or sacrifices in others.
> 

## Case Studies

- **Time vs. Space:** Using more memory can reduce computation time, or vice versa.
- **Accuracy vs. Speed:** More precise algorithms may require longer execution times.
- **Energy vs. Performance:** Increasing processing speed often raises energy consumption.
