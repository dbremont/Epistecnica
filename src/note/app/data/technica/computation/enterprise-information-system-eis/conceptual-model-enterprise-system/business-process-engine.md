# Business Process Engine


> **Workflow engines** are software tools that automate and manage the sequence of tasks, actions, and processes within an organization, facilitating efficient and streamlined workflow management.
> 

> **Workflow automation** refers to the use of technology to streamline, automate, and optimize repetitive tasks, processes, and workflows within an organization.
> 

> Aka Workflow Management System.
