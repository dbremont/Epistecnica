## Digital Transformation: Providers

> An **ERP provider** is a company that offers integrated software solutions designed to manage and automate core business processes across finance, human resources, procurement, distribution, and other departments within an organization.
> 

| ERP System | Core Features | Pricing Flexibility | User Interface | Customer Support | Scalability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ADP** | Payroll, HR management, tax, compliance | Customized based on services and scale | User-friendly, intuitive | 24/7 phone support, online resources | Highly scalable, suitable for SMEs to large enterprises |
| **FreshBooks** | Invoicing, expenses tracking, time tracking | Plans for freelancers to small businesses | Very intuitive, clean design | Extensive online resources, email, and phone support | Suitable for freelancers and small businesses, limited for larger enterprises |
| **QuickBooks** | Accounting, invoicing, payroll, payments | Tiered pricing, relatively flexible | Easy to use for beginners | Phone, chat support, and online resources | Primarily for small to medium businesses, limited scalability for very large enterprises |
| **Quicken** | Personal and basic business finance management | Mostly fixed pricing for different tiers | Straightforward, dated interface | Phone support and online forums | Primarily for personal or small business use, not suitable for larger businesses |
| Alegra | Accounting, invoicing, payroll, payments | Mostly fixed pricing for different tiers | … | … | … |
