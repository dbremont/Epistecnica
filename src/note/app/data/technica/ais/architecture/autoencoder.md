> In this note we will explore the idea of an auto-encoder.
> 

> For a comprehensive view on re-representation see ([Representation](note.html?n=epistemica/mathematical/representation.md)).
> 

Guiding questions:

- How should I think about an autoencoder? Is it a **functional epistemic tool concept** or a **concrete epistemic tool**?
- What is the structure of an autoencoder?
    - What types of encoders exist?
    - What types of decoders exist?
- What role does an autoencoder play in a modeling or learning system?
- How are autoencoder outputs validated?
- In which applications is this procedure used?
- What additional deep questions should I be asking?
- **The Information Bottleneck Trade-off:** At what precise point does a bottleneck transition from "Extracting Essence" to "Losing Critical Signal"?
- **Manifold Hypothesis:** Does the autoencoder truly discover a natural manifold, or does the architecture of the encoder impose a synthetic geometry on the data? Is there even a manifold - or the manifold can be temporal, and regimes changes that create new manifold will not be predicted?
