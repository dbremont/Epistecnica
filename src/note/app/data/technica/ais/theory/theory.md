## Reflection

Opinions:

- Intelligence is more mechanistic than we thought.
- We did not have a clue on how biological intelligence works.
- In reality, reality is not that regular, but the subset we care -- and  about—our mental representations of it, almost by tautological. See Schopenhauer. 
- Now that we have a system—or at least a class of systems—that exhibits similar phenomena, it is a good time to investigate intelligence more deeply from a mechanistic perspective, starting with nonbiological systems. But lets remove the conceptuals fog,  and start from the very beginning.
- It is important to recognize that biological intelligence must also rely on minimal-action mechanisms, although these need not be similar in any way to those found in artificial systems.
- Each type of continuation (taking summarization as an example) is enabled by the context → next-token mechanism's ability to exploit and capture certain regularities, which the model itself does not necessarily need to know explicitly.
- When analyzing an LLM, the central question is: How are the regularities present in a domain captured and exploited, and how does the system use them, alongside context-extension mechanisms, to generate the right trajectory?
