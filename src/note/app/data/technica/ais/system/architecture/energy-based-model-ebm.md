# Energy-Based Model (EBM)


> In this note we will explore a class of **Learning Systems called Energy-Based Models (EBMs)** that **represent relationships between variables through an energy function, assigning low energy to compatible configurations and higher energy to incompatible ones**. EBMs provide a **flexible, general framework** for modeling complex dependencies, enabling tasks such as **state estimation, structured prediction, density modeling, and generative modeling**.
> 

> The term “energy” in **EBMs** comes from physics, where energy is a measure of the ability to do work. In the context of **EBMs**, the energy function is a mathematical representation of the “work” required to match the input data to the model’s internal representation. The goal of an **EBM** is to find the configuration of model parameters that minimizes the energy function, which corresponds to the best match between the input data and the model’s representation.
