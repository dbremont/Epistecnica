# Minimal Principle

> Mother Nature usually finds the simplest solutions.

> **Note on scope:** Saying that nature tends to find the simplest solutions to problems does not mean that nature optimizes everything. Optimization at the level of a part does not necessarily imply optimization at the level of the whole. In complex systems, redundancy and other forms of apparent inefficiency may be essential for robustness, resilience, and overall system performance.
