# Compositionality

> A principle of reality with a twofold nature: read ontically, what exists at one level is
> constituted by its parts and the way they compose; read cognitively, the same composition is
> how a mind parses and builds its representations of that reality. Both faces are one
> principle — reality composes, and minds that track it compose too. Decomposed per
> [How to decompose any natural instance?](note.html?n=meta/philosophia-naturalis-et-operis.md).

## Formulation

### What natural element type does this natural instance belong to?

**Principle** — deliberately not classified into a single element type. Principles have a
twofold nature: this one reads both as an ontic constraint on how reality is constituted and
as a cognitive form on how observers parse that reality. One root binds `(root) := Principle`
and the faces decompose as branches; the epistemic and technical readings stay secondary
prose (see References), never merged into the tree.

### What is this natural instance?

**Compositionality**: the constitution of a whole is fixed by its parts and their mode of
composition — structure propagates upward from micro through meso to macro, so the macro is
assembled rather than merely aggregated. Where determination fails — where coupling yields
what no part carries — compositionality gives way to emergence, the limitation factor
bounding its strict reading.

The twofold nature: ontically, reality composes; cognitively, meaning and models compose the
same way, which is why construction can mirror reality in modules. What survives a change of
instruments is the ontic face — the composition of the segment itself; the cognitive and
construction faces are readings of it, not further furniture.

Limitation checklist (Complexity, Nonlinearity, Uncertainty, Chaos, Emergence, Scale, Data
Availability, Computational Complexity, Irreducibility, Partial Observability, Non-Repeatability,
Measurement Disturbance, Many-Body coupling): **Emergence** and **Irreducibility** bound the
principle's strict reading — where coupling defeats part-wise determination, composition stops
and the whole outresolves its parts. **Nonlinearity** and **Many-Body coupling** mark where
the composition terms stop adding.

Secondary readings (kept as prose, never merged into the tree): readable as epistemic — the
cognitive face is meaning-compositionality (a representation's content composed from the
content of its parts), the operator-side reading under the epistemicarum grammar; readable as
technique — construction realizes the principle as modular design with interface contracts,
decomposed under the technicarum grammar; readable as social — institutions compose from
actors and roles under the socialium grammar.
