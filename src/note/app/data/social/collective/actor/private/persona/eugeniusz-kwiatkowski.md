# Eugeniusz Kwiatkowski

> **Eugeniusz Kwiatkowski** (1888–1974) was a Polish engineer, economist, and politician, the chief architect of the Central Industrial Region and the modernization of the Polish economy in the 1930s.

## Life and work

Kwiatkowski trained as a chemist and engineer and served in the Polish government as minister of trade and industry. His Four-Year Plan (1936–1939) concentrated investment in the Central Industrial Region, developed the port of Gdynia, and expanded heavy industry and armaments.

He was interned during the Second World War and later returned to public life in the Polish People’s Republic. His economic policy is remembered as an attempt to build a modern industrial base in a newly independent state.
