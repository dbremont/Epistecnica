# Stanisław Leszczyński

> **Stanisław Leszczyński** (1677–1766) was king of Poland in 1704–1709 and 1733–1736 and later duke of Lorraine. He is associated with *Głos wolny wolność ubezpieczający* (1749), a programme of political and economic reform attributed to his circle.

## Life and work

Leszczyński’s reign was shaped by the Great Northern War and the War of the Polish Succession. Exiled from Poland, he received the Duchy of Lorraine from France and made Nancy a centre of enlightened patronage.

The *Głos wolny* argued for the reform of the Polish constitution, the strengthening of the central authority, the improvement of agriculture, and the reform of taxation and serfdom. It belongs to the eighteenth-century Polish debate on how to repair the Commonwealth.
