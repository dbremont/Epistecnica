# Anders Chydenius

> **Anders Chydenius** (1729–1803) was a Finnish-Swedish priest, politician, and economist, and one of the most important liberal voices of the Swedish Age of Liberty. His *Källan Til Rikets Wan-Magt* (1765) attacked trade restrictions and privileges; *Den nationnale winsten* (1765) set out the positive case for commercial freedom.

## Life and work

Chydenius was born in Ostrobothnia, studied at Åbo, and served as a parish priest. He represented the clergy in the Swedish Riksdag and used his parliamentary platform to attack monopolies, sumptuary laws, and restrictions on labour and trade.

His argument was both economic and moral: privileges enriched the few at the expense of the many, and freedom of trade and occupation would increase national wealth and population. He is often described as a precursor of classical liberalism, though his thought remained rooted in Lutheran theology and the Swedish improvement tradition.
