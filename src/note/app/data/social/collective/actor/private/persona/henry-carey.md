# Henry Carey

> **Henry Carey** (1793–1879) was an American economist and publisher, a leading advocate of protectionism and national economic development. His *Principles of Political Economy* (1837–1840) and *The Harmony of Interests* (1851) argued that protective tariffs served the harmony of agricultural and industrial interests.

## Life and work

Carey was born in Philadelphia and took over his father’s publishing business. He wrote on political economy, slavery, and social harmony, and became one of the most influential American protectionists of the nineteenth century.

His system combined a rejection of Ricardo’s rent theory with a defence of a balanced national economy in which agriculture and manufactures supported one another. He influenced later American economic nationalism and the German historical school.
