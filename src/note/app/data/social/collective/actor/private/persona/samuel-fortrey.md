# Samuel Fortrey

> **Samuel Fortrey** (1622–1681) was an English landowner, fen drainer, and economic writer, author of *England’s Interest and Improvement* (1663). The tract is a mercantilist defence of domestic manufactures, employment, and a favourable balance of trade.

## Life and work

Fortrey belonged to the gentry and served as a bailiff of the Bedford Level Corporation, the body responsible for draining the Fens. His economic pamphlet argued that England should encourage immigration, enclosure, and home manufactures, and that the crown should set an example by consuming domestic goods.

The book is best known for its estimate of a large English trade deficit with France, a figure that was widely repeated and helped shape later seventeenth-century commercial policy. Its combination of economic argument and practical improvement makes it a representative text of Restoration mercantilism.
