# Johan Fischerström

> **Johan Fischerström** (1735–1796) was a Swedish naturalist and economic writer. His work on agriculture, fishing, and natural resources belongs to the eighteenth-century Swedish improvement literature.

## Life and work

Fischerström wrote on the practical exploitation of natural resources, including agriculture, fishing, and rural industry. His economic writings were framed by the patriotic and cameralist concerns of the Swedish Enlightenment: population, food supply, and the better use of the kingdom’s land and waters.

He is a representative of the naturalist-economic current that connected the observation of nature with proposals for economic improvement.
