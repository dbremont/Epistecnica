# Károly Kautz

> **Károly Kautz** (1829–1902) was a Hungarian economist and professor, a representative of national political economy and an advocate of industrial development in the Habsburg monarchy.

## Life and work

Kautz taught at the University of Pest and wrote on national economy, finance, and economic policy. His work belongs to the nineteenth-century Hungarian effort to create a national economic science suited to the country’s agrarian and industrial conditions.

He combined liberal economic ideas with a concern for national development and the role of the state in supporting industry and infrastructure.
