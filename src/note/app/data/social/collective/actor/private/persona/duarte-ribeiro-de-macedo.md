# Duarte Ribeiro de Macedo

> **Duarte Ribeiro de Macedo** (1618–1680) was a Portuguese diplomat and economic writer. His *Discurso sobre a introdução das artes* (1675) argued for the introduction of arts and manufactures as a means of national economic development.

## Life and work

Macedo served the Portuguese crown as a diplomat in Paris and other European courts. His economic writing drew on the example of Colbert’s France and argued that Portugal should develop its own manufactures rather than rely on imported goods and the export of raw materials.

His *Discurso* is an early Portuguese statement of the case for industrial policy and for the coordination of economic reform by the state.
