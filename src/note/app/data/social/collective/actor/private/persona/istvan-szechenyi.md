# István Széchenyi

> **István Széchenyi** (1791–1860) was a Hungarian aristocrat, statesman, and reformer, one of the leading figures of the Hungarian national revival and economic modernization before 1848.

## Life and work

Széchenyi travelled widely in Europe and returned convinced that Hungary needed infrastructure, education, and economic reform. His *Hitel* (Credit, 1830) argued for the abolition of feudal restrictions and for the creation of a modern credit system.

He promoted the regulation of the Danube, the construction of the Chain Bridge in Budapest, the Hungarian Academy of Sciences, and agricultural improvement. His reformist liberalism contrasted with the more radical nationalism of Kossuth, and his later years were marked by political isolation and depression.
