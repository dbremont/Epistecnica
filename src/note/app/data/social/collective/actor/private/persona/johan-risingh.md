# Johan Risingh

> **Johan Risingh** (1617–1672) was a Swedish mercantilist, colonial official, and economic writer. He served as secretary of the Swedish Board of Trade and as governor of New Sweden on the Delaware, and wrote on Swedish commerce, manufacturing, and economic development.

## Life and work

Risingh was educated in Sweden and became a civil servant in the commercial administration. His *Itt swenskt crönings- och förmyndare-regemente* and related writings argued for the development of Swedish manufactures and commerce, and for the colonial and commercial expansion of the kingdom.

As governor of New Sweden in the 1650s he attempted to strengthen the colony before it was lost to the Dutch. His economic writing belongs to the seventeenth-century Swedish mercantilist programme that linked manufactures, navigation, and state power.
