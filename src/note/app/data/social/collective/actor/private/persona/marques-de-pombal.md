# Marquês de Pombal

> **Sebastião José de Carvalho e Melo, Marquês de Pombal** (1699–1782) was the chief minister of Portugal under Joseph I and the dominant figure of Portuguese enlightened reform. His programme combined state-directed economic development, imperial reform, and administrative centralization.

## Life and work

Pombal rose through diplomacy and the royal administration, and after the 1755 Lisbon earthquake he directed the reconstruction of the city and a broad programme of reform. He reorganized the wine trade, promoted chartered companies, strengthened the navy, and sought to reduce Portugal’s dependence on British commerce.

His economic policy was mercantilist and interventionist: the state was to discipline privileged interests, develop manufactures, and integrate the Brazilian and Portuguese economies. His rule was also authoritarian, and his fall after Joseph I’s death was followed by a reaction against his methods.
