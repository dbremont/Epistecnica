# Eric Salander

> **Eric Salander** (1709–1770) was a Swedish jurist and economist. He taught at Uppsala, served on the Board of Commerce, and wrote on crafts, manufactures, and industrial organization.

## Life and work

Salander’s *Tal, om slögder* (1754) examined the role of the crafts and manufactures in the Swedish economy. Like many of his contemporaries, he treated the organization of production as a matter of public policy and sought to improve the quality and competitiveness of Swedish goods.

His work belongs to the Swedish utilitarian and cameralist literature of the mid-eighteenth century, which combined legal administration with economic improvement.
