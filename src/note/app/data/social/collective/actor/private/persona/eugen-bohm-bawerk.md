# Eugen Böhm-Bawerk

> **Eugen Böhm-Bawerk** (1851–1914) was an Austrian economist, a leading figure of the Austrian School, and a finance minister of the Habsburg monarchy. He is best known for his theory of capital and interest.

## Life and work

Böhm-Bawerk studied at Vienna and Heidelberg and taught at Innsbruck and Vienna. His *Capital and Interest* (1884–1889) developed the theory of roundabout production and the time-preference explanation of interest, while *Karl Marx and the Close of His System* (1896) was one of the most influential critiques of Marx’s value theory.

He served as Austrian finance minister in the 1890s and later returned to teaching. His work shaped the Austrian School’s account of capital, interest, and economic calculation.

## Selected works

- *Capital and Interest* (1884–1889).
- *The Positive Theory of Capital* (1888).
- *Karl Marx and the Close of His System* (1896).
