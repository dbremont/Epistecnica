# Camillo Benso di Cavour

> **Camillo Benso, Count of Cavour** (1810–1861) was a Piedmontese statesman and the chief architect of Italian unification. As prime minister of Sardinia-Piedmont he promoted railways, free trade, and administrative modernization.

## Life and work

Cavour was born in Turin and trained as a military engineer before turning to agriculture, journalism, and politics. He founded *Il Risorgimento* and became prime minister of Piedmont in 1852.

His economic policy combined liberal free trade with state investment in infrastructure and a strong fiscal administration. He used diplomacy and war to expel Austria from Italy and to bring the peninsula under the House of Savoy.
