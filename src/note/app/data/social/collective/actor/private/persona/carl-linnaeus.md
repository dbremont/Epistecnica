# Carl Linnaeus

> **Carl Linnaeus** (1707–1778) was a Swedish botanist, physician, and naturalist, and the central figure of eighteenth-century biological classification. His economic significance lies in economic botany, agricultural improvement, and the effort to reduce Sweden’s dependence on imported commodities.

## Life and work

Linnaeus studied at Lund and Uppsala, travelled through Lapland and the Swedish provinces, and published *Systema Naturae* (1735) and *Species Plantarum* (1753). His students and correspondents formed a global network of botanical observation.

His economic programme was practical and patriotic: acclimatizing useful plants, improving agriculture, finding domestic substitutes for tea, coffee, silk, dyes, and medicines, and turning natural knowledge into national wealth. His travels and *Instructio peregrinatoris* were designed to collect economically useful information as well as specimens.

## Selected works

- *Systema Naturae* (1735; later editions).
- *Species Plantarum* (1753).
- Travel journals and economic-botanical writings.
