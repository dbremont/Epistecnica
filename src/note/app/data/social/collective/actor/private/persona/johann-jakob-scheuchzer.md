# Johann Jakob Scheuchzer

> **Johann Jakob Scheuchzer** (1672–1733) was a Swiss naturalist and physician, best known for *Physica Sacra* (1731–1735), a monumental attempt to harmonize Scripture with natural history.

## Life and work

Scheuchzer studied in Zurich and Altdorf and became a physician and professor of mathematics in Zurich. He travelled extensively in the Alps and wrote on Swiss natural history, geology, and meteorology.

*Physica Sacra* combined biblical commentary with copperplate illustrations of natural phenomena, fossils, and antiquities. His work belongs to the early Enlightenment effort to reconcile revealed religion with the new natural philosophy.

## Selected works

- *Physica Sacra* (1731–1735).
- *Itinera alpina* (1706–1708).
