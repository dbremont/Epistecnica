# Patrick Geddes

> **Patrick Geddes** (1854–1932) was a Scottish biologist, sociologist, and town planner, a pioneer of regional survey and the idea of the city-region.

## Life and work

Geddes taught botany at Edinburgh and Dundee and became a leading figure in the British sociological and planning movements. He coined the term “conurbation” and developed the valley-section method of regional analysis.

His work combined biology, geography, and civic activism, and he influenced the garden-city and regional-planning movements. He worked on urban renewal in Edinburgh and on planning projects in India and Palestine.
