# Luis Ortiz

> **Luis Ortiz** was a sixteenth-century Spanish economic writer, remembered for his *Memorial* (1558) to Philip II, an early plea for import substitution, domestic manufactures, and the protection of Spanish industry.

## Life and work

Little is known of Ortiz’s life beyond his career as a royal official and his memorial to the crown. The *Memorial* described the outflow of Spanish money to foreign manufactures and argued that the kingdom should develop its own industries and reduce dependence on imported goods.

His programme belongs to the early Spanish arbitrista tradition: a diagnosis of economic decline combined with practical proposals for state action, population growth, and the promotion of productive work.
