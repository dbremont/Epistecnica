# Ivan Pososhkov

> **Ivan Pososhkov** (1652–1726) was a Russian merchant and self-taught economic writer, author of the *Book of Poverty and Wealth* (1724), one of the earliest Russian works of political economy.

## Life and work

Pososhkov was born into a family of silversmiths and became a merchant and entrepreneur. His *Book of Poverty and Wealth* argued for state-led development, the promotion of manufactures, the reform of taxation and money, and the improvement of agriculture.

He presented the book to Peter the Great and was arrested soon afterward; he died in the Peter and Paul Fortress. The work was not published until 1842, but it became a central text in the history of Russian economic thought.

## Selected works

- *Book of Poverty and Wealth* (1724; published 1842).
