# Irineu Evangelista de Sousa, Viscount of Mauá

> **Irineu Evangelista de Sousa, Viscount of Mauá** (1813–1889), was a Brazilian industrialist, banker, and railway builder, one of the leading entrepreneurs of the Brazilian Empire.

## Life and work

Mauá began as a merchant in Rio de Janeiro and went on to build railways, shipyards, steamship lines, and banks. He founded the Mauá Bank and promoted the industrialization and infrastructure of the Brazilian Empire.

His enterprises connected Rio de Janeiro, the Paraíba Valley, and the south, and he was an early advocate of industrial development in a slave-based, export-oriented economy. His later career was marked by financial difficulties and political opposition.
