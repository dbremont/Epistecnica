# José de Páez

> **José de Páez** (Mexico City, 7 December 1721 – c. 1780) was a Novohispano painter of the eighteenth century and one of the most prolific religious painters of New Spain. He specialized in Marian devotions, followed the style of Miguel Cabrera, and his works circulated in New Spain, the Viceroyalty of Peru, and Spain.

## Life

Páez was born in Mexico City, then the capital of New Spain, the son of Baltazar de Páez. The exact year of his birth is uncertain: the article's sources place it anywhere between 1714 and 1727, though 1721 is the date most often given. He was a pupil of the painter Nicolás Enríquez and married Rosalía Caballero in 1753.

His death is likewise recorded imprecisely, as around 1780 in some sources and 1790 in others.

## Work

Páez painted religious subjects, with a marked preference for Marian advocations, and produced an exceptionally large body of work. His biographer Manuel Toussaint wrote that «José de Páez inundó la segunda mitad del siglo XVIII con sus pinturas» — he flooded the second half of the eighteenth century with his paintings. His paintings reached fame in New Spain, the Viceroyalty of Peru, and Spain, and his style follows that of Miguel Cabrera.
