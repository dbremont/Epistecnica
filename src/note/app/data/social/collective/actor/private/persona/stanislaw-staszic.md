# Stanisław Staszic

> **Stanisław Staszic** (1755–1826) was a Polish priest, geologist, writer, and statesman, a leading figure of the Polish Enlightenment and an advocate of industrial and educational reform.

## Life and work

Staszic studied in Leipzig and Paris and became one of the most active reformers in the last decades of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth. His *Przestrogi dla Polski* (1790) attacked the defects of the old order and called for the emancipation of the peasantry, the reform of education, and the development of industry.

After the partitions he worked in the Duchy of Warsaw and the Kingdom of Poland, where he helped organize mining, geology, and technical education. He was a founder of the Polish geological survey and a patron of scientific institutions.
