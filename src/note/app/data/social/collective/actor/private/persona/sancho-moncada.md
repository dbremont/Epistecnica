# Sancho de Moncada

> **Sancho de Moncada** (c. 1580–1638) was a Spanish economist and cleric, best known for *Restablecimiento de España* (1619), a programme of proto-industrial planning and national economic reform.

## Life and work

Moncada wrote during the economic crisis of the Spanish Habsburg monarchy. He argued that Spain’s decline came from the export of raw materials and the import of foreign manufactures, and he proposed measures to restore agriculture, population, and domestic industry.

His work belongs to the arbitrista literature that combined fiscal diagnosis with projects for economic renewal. It is an early statement of the case for manufactures and for the coordination of economic policy by the state.
