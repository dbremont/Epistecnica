# Anders Nordencrantz

> **Anders Nordencrantz** (1697–1772) was a Swedish official and economic writer. His *Arcana oeconomiae et commercii* (1730) examined commerce, manufactures, and the principles of national economic policy.

## Life and work

Nordencrantz worked in the Swedish administrative and commercial apparatus during the Age of Liberty. His economic writings defended a managed commercial order in which the state supported manufactures, navigation, and the balance of trade.

He belonged to the mercantilist generation that shaped Swedish economic debate before the more liberal arguments of Anders Chydenius and his allies in the 1760s.
