# Abram Fyodorovich Ioffe

> Russian and Soviet physicist who pioneered solid-state physics and electromagnetism in the USSR. He founded the Leningrad Physico-Technical Institute and mentored a generation of Soviet physicists, including Kapitsa, Landau, and Tamm.
