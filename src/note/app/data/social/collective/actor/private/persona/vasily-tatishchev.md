# Vasily Tatishchev

> **Vasily Tatishchev** (1686–1750) was a Russian historian, administrator, and economic modernizer, known for his *History of Russia* and for his work in the Urals mining administration.

## Life and work

Tatishchev served Peter the Great and his successors as a military officer, diplomat, and provincial administrator. He founded the mining administration of the Urals and promoted the development of metallurgy and industry in the region.

His *History of Russia* was the first systematic attempt to write a continuous Russian history on the basis of chronicles and documents. His administrative writings combined economic development with state-building.

## Selected works

- *History of Russia* (written 18th c.).
- Administrative and mining reports.
