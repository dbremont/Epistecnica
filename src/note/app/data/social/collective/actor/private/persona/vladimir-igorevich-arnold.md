# Vladimir Igorevich Arnold

> Soviet and Russian mathematician known for the Kolmogorov–Arnold–Moser theorem, singularity theory, and the Arnold conjectures in symplectic topology. He was awarded the Wolf Prize in Mathematics in 2001.
