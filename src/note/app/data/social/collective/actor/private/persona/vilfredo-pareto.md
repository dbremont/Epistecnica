# Vilfredo Pareto

> **Vilfredo Pareto** (1848–1923) was an Italian engineer, economist, and sociologist, known for the concept of Pareto efficiency, the Pareto distribution, and the theory of elites.

## Life and work

Pareto trained as an engineer and worked in industry before turning to economics. He taught at Lausanne and wrote *Manual of Political Economy* (1906), a foundational text of general equilibrium and welfare economics.

He introduced the idea that an allocation is efficient when no one can be made better off without making someone else worse off. In sociology he developed the theory of the circulation of elites and analysed the role of non-logical action in social life.

## Selected works

- *The Mind and Society* (1916).
