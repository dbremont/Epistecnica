## Anglo-American Philosophy Conduct of Life Theories

- **Deontology:** Rooted in Immanuel Kant's philosophy, it emphasizes following universal moral laws, often expressed as categorical imperatives.
- **Consequentialism (Utilitarianism):** Best exemplified by Jeremy Bentham, this position focuses on maximizing overall happiness or good, often summarized as "the greatest happiness for the greatest number".
- **Contractualism:** This approach, associated with Jean-Jacques Rousseau, posits that moral principles arise from a social contract or agreement among rational individuals.
