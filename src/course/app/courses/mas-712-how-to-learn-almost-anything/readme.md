# MAS.712 How to Learn (Almost) Anything

> The course on learning itself: as the digital revolution changes how and what we learn, it explores the new opportunities for learning — new approaches, contexts, tools, and ideas — with a special focus on what can be learned through immersive, hands-on activities. Students participate in and reflect on a variety of learning situations, develop new workshops with Media Lab technologies, iteratively run and refine them, and analyze how and what the participants learn.

**MIT OpenCourseWare** — Spring 2001, graduate (Media Arts and Sciences), Prof. Mitchel Resnick and Prof. Bakhtiar Mikhak.
