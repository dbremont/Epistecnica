Atlas of Courses

atlas @mathematics @computation @ml @science

Every course of the two source lists — courses.md (awesome-courses + csdiy.wiki picks) and science-courses.md — merged with the courses corpus into one map. Where the same course appears twice — another offering, another year, another source — it is listed once: the primary row opens the in-corpus course page when a directory exists, and every other occurrence rides along as a reference. The seven anchor courses of this atlas carry the main badge. Same course number with a different title (14.451, 14.452, 9.98, 18.S096) means a different course — both are kept, noted in place.
