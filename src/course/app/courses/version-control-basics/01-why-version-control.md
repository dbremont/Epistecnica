# Lecture 1 — Why Version Control?

> Before commands: the problems version control solves, and the properties every solution shares.

## Formulation

### What problem does version control solve?

> Collaborative editing over time: several agents change the same files, mistakes happen, and past states must stay recoverable. Without history, the work exists only in its latest, most fragile form.

### What properties must a solution have?

> Identity (every state addressable), history (states linked in order), branching (parallel lines of work), and merging (recombining lines). Centralized and distributed tools differ only in where history lives.

## Takeaway

> Version control turns a pile of files into a queryable history of intentions — `who changed what, when, and why`.
