## Lecture 1: Introduction and Scope

> MIT: Build models of the world to control it.
> 

> So here we build models  of thinking, perception and action.
> 

> In order to have a model we need representation.
> 

> So AI is about building **‘representations’** that support the modelling (simulation) of **thinking, perception and action**.
> 

> The notion of **representation** here is ambiguous, as it may refer either to the formal **representation of the computational system that simulates thinking, perception and action** or to the **internal representations constructed by the system of itself** and of the world it interacts with.
>
